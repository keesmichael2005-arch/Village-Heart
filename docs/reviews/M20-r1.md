# M20 review, round 1 (verifier)

Range reviewed: `88cdd09..74e10a1` on `m20` (START = the merge-base with origin/dev), merged with origin/dev (`3025c23`; dev only added three lines to `docs/agents/builder.md`, no conflict).

Most of M20 holds up. All roadmap criteria have tests, and every test I mutated went red. Layer 2 was stable over 9 runs. The FAIL is one
safety hole: the design's central promise, "never break player-placed blocks", fails for blocks that are attached to a natural block in the site.

## Fix list

1. **Clearing a natural block destroys the player-placed blocks attached to it from outside the site** (`build/BuildExecutor.clear`,
   `core/blueprint/SiteRules.check`, `build/SiteValidation`).
   `SiteRules` only classifies the touched cells and checks fluids next to them. `BuildExecutor.clear` then calls `level.destroyBlock(..., UPDATE_ALL)`.
   This shape-updates the neighbours, so any block whose support was the cleared cell pops, even though it stands outside the box and is
   clearly not natural. I tested it with a temporary GameTest (removed afterwards) on the standard layout: Small House, `NONE`, `STORAGE_NO_HUT`.
   It put dirt in three touched cells (local (0,1,3), (3,4,3) and (6,2,3)), then added a wall torch on the west face of the first (outside the box),
   a standing torch on top of the roof-layer dirt (above the box) and a ladder on the east face of the third (outside the box).
   `SiteValidation.check` returned **OK**. After `BuildJobs.advance(..., 246)` (the clear steps), all three were `minecraft:air`:
   ```
   [probe] site check: OK
   [probe] wall torch after clear: Block{minecraft:air}
   [probe] standing torch after clear: Block{minecraft:air}
   [probe] ladder after clear: Block{minecraft:air}
   ```
   This is more than the documented gap. That gap covers player-placed dirt or stone that looks natural, but a torch, a ladder, a sign, a
   button, a rail, a door, a carpet or a lantern is never natural. The same mechanism also reaches doors and beds standing on a natural cell,
   hanging lanterns, and hanging entities (item frames and paintings on a touched face). Sand and gravel resting on a cleared cell above the box
   will also fall, but those are natural, so that part is acceptable.
   Expected: the site check refuses (a new ordered rule, for example "torch depends on a block at ...") when a non-natural block outside the touched
   cells would lose its support. A generic way is `!neighbour.canSurvive(...)` with the touched cell treated as air, for each of the 6 neighbours.
   Hanging entities whose support face is a touched cell should also be refused. The builder needs the same check per CLEAR step, because a
   player can attach a torch after the confirm: it should stop with the usual BLOCKED text instead of clearing. Add a GameTest like the probe
   above (wall torch, standing torch, ladder, item frame) that asserts the refusal, and one that asserts a mid-build attachment stops the builder
   and the attached block survives. Add an L1 `SiteRules` case if the rule lives in core.
