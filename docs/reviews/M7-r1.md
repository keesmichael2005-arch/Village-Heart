Range reviewed: `0344442..26f8fd1` on `m7` (merge-base with `origin/dev` to the M7 head; 14 commits, 99 files).

The milestone is close. Every roadmap acceptance criterion has a test, and each test I mutation-checked fails when its
feature is broken (table below). The full `scripts/verify.sh` passes. The blocker is one Layer 2 test that fails

## Fix list

1. **Recurring flake: `WorkerGameTests.depositTripWhenFull`, in `DepositTrip` / `GoToJobBlock`**
   (`src/main/java/dev/villageheart/worker/DepositTrip.java`, `GoToJobBlock.java`, `WorkerBrain.java`).
   - **Failures:** in full `runGameTest` runs, 2 of 17 failed:
     `the 64 cobblestone reach Storage took 942 ticks, bound 600` and `... took 1114 ticks, bound 600`.
     It passed 8 of 8 times when run alone, so the failure needs the parallel load of the full suite.
   - **Why it looks like a real bug:** GameTest bounds count game ticks, so lag alone cannot make a delivery that
     normally takes about 100 ticks take 942. The 942 and 1114 match a trip that hit its `TIMEOUT` (600), waited
     out `COOLDOWN` (200), and then succeeded on a second trip.
   - **What the instrumented runs show:** I added temporary logging and ran the suite 9 more times (it did not fail
     again). Two things showed up:
     - Trips do lose their walk target partway. I saw `WALK_TARGET` empty and `CANT_REACH_WALK_TARGET_SINCE` set
       around 200 ticks into a trip.
     - `DepositTrip` only puts the target back every 20 ticks. Meanwhile `GoToJobBlock` (WORK, priority 2) starts on
       any tick where `WALK_TARGET` is absent, once its 100-tick back-off has passed. It can take the villager back to
       the composter while the trip keeps "running" until the 600-tick timeout.
     - Separately, two `DepositTrip` instances (the WORK one and the go_home one) can run at the same time on one
       villager. Vanilla does not stop running behaviours when the activity changes. I saw doubled `t=0` traces for one
       villager in `depositTripAtEndOfDay`.
   - **Expected:**
     - While a deposit trip runs, nothing else in the worker brain replaces or steals its walk target. For example,
       gate `GoToJobBlock` on "no trip in progress", or have the trip put its target back on every tick it is missing.
     - At most one trip runs per worker.
     - Then show the test is stable: run the full Layer 2 at least 10 times with no failures, and record the counts
       in PROGRESS.
   - **Rules:** do not raise the 600-tick bound, which would weaken the test. If you find a different root cause,
     explain it in the commit message.

2. **Releasing the Heart leaks POI tickets in unloaded chunks** (`JobSiteReservations.releaseAll(server, held)` and
   `VillageClaims.releaseAt`).
   - **The bug:** `releaseAll` skips every held block whose chunk is not loaded (`!level.isLoaded(bp)`), but
     `WorldClaim.releaseAt` clears `reservedJobBlocks` either way. Nothing remembers that those tickets are still held.
   - **Effect:** the POI file keeps the ticket taken forever. That composter or smoker can never be claimed by a
     vanilla villager again. This breaks "outside a building, vanilla behaves exactly as in vanilla" (DESIGN §3.4).
   - **When it happens:** the claim radius is 80 blocks, and a release can happen with parts of the claim unloaded:
     `healStaleClaim`, a `/setblock` from far away, or a low view distance.
   - **Expected:** keep releases for unloaded blocks pending in saved data and finish them when the chunk loads,
     plus a GameTest. A cheaper fix is fine if it is equally correct.

## Deviations judged

- **T-L2-5 without "stays inside the room": acceptable.** The roadmap criterion is "does not take the farmer
  profession within 1 day", and the test samples exactly that 120 times over 24000 ticks. It also asserts that the
  villager stays alive, adult and within 44 blocks (inside the 48-block job-site search range), so a villager that
  wandered off cannot pass by accident. The same test then breaks the marker, and the villager becomes a farmer
  within 1200 ticks. That control proves the villager would have taken the composter, and the mutation above turns the
  test red.
- **T-L2-14 retry windows: acceptable, not weakened.**
  - The test re-seeds `BREED_TARGET` and gets up to three wake-up windows. That absorbs vanilla's randomness
    (`InteractWith`, the gate losing its target).
  - It still fails when the worker brain cannot breed (mutation above).
  - Breeding is not a roadmap acceptance criterion for M7.
- **Iron-door pen for T-L2-2: acceptable.** Villagers cannot open iron doors. So "not at the job block before
  work hours" becomes an exact position assertion, where it would otherwise be a race with a random stroll. The
  arrival bound (≤400 ticks after the door opens, and day time ≤3000) is unchanged.
