## Fix list

1. **`one_birth_per_day` loses a second-pair adult's HOME across the clock jump.** Files: `src/gametest/java/dev/villageheart/gametest/GrowthCourtshipGameTests.java` (`oneBirthPerDay`) and `GrowthTestSupport.toDawn`.

   *Symptom.* This is the builder's unexplained failure, with the same signature in every case: `day 2: Dawn[reason=TOO_FEW_READY, freeBeds=2, ready=1]`.
   - Alone: 1 of 5 runs (run 1; runs 2–5 green).
   - Stress: 4 of 192 copies (details below).

   *Cause, measured.* I took a diagnostic snapshot just before the second `toDawn` and again at the day-2 dawn (stress launch 8, copy 9):
   - Before the jump, all four adults are alive, fed (`fed=true`), housed and have a HOME. Adult `594a7437` (second pair, age 0) stands 18 blocks from its bed. Its last stroll target was below the floor, so it carries `CANT_REACH_WALK_TARGET_SINCE=20138` at game time 21609. The stamp is 1471 ticks old.
   - `toDawn` sets the day time to 23980. That starts REST without advancing game time.
   - At the dawn (game time 21630), that adult has `home=NONE` and its bed's ticket is free, so `freeBeds=2` and `ready=1`.
   - A first-pair adult with a stamp 895 ticks old (under 1200) kept its HOME.
   - That is vanilla's REST `SetWalkTargetFromBlockMemory(HOME, …, 1200)`: a cannot-reach stamp older than 1200 ticks releases the POI and erases HOME.
   - Nobody died, nobody was hurt (`hp=20`, `lastHurt=null`), and no make-love run touched that villager.

   *Why only sometimes.* It needs a stroll on day 1 that fails to reach its target and is not followed by a successful walk. In normal play the day between morning and REST has thousands of ticks of other walks that clear the stamp. The test skips them with the clock jump.

   *Expected.* Erase `CANT_REACH_WALK_TARGET_SINCE` on the test's villagers before each clock jump, as `noFreeBedNoBabyInThreeDays` already does at line 73. Doing it in `GrowthTestSupport.toDawn` covers every growth test. Explain why in a comment. Keep the assertion `day 2: LICENSED` as it is: do not tolerate `TOO_FEW_READY`.

   *Proof expected.* A repeated run with zero failures of this signature. For example, 24 copies of `oneBirthPerDay`, each in its own `growth_*` environment, run in one server for at least 6 launches (≥144 copies). That is how I reproduced it; see "Stress harness".

2. **A licensed pair can waste its day on three `NOT_VISIBLE` aborts while standing next to each other.** Files: `growth/Courtship.java` and `mixin/VillagerMakeLoveMixin.java` (the abort classification).

   *Evidence.* Stress launch 6, copy 5, 1 of 192 copies. Day 3 was `LICENSED` with ready 4. Then:
   - seed 1 → `NOT_VISIBLE` at distance 2.01
   - seed 2 → `NOT_VISIBLE` at 1.61
   - seed 3 → `NOT_VISIBLE` at 1.96
   - the licence `LAPSED/MAX_SEEDS`, so there was no baby that day and the test failed with "exactly one baby on day 1: was 0".
   - At the end all four villagers were alive, fed and housed. `BreedTrace` also showed `plain NOT_VISIBLE=1, plain PARTNER_GONE=1`.

   *Why it matters.* Vanilla's make-love stops when the partner drops out of `NEAREST_VISIBLE_LIVING_ENTITIES`, and each such stop costs a seed. Here the pair stood within the birth distance (√5) every time, so the licence was spent on a sensor gap, not on a real failure to meet.
   - At this rate the test flakes about 0.5 % of the time.
   - The rule this protects is "free bed + food → baby within 2 days". Two such days in a row would break it.

   *Expected.* Find out why the partner is not "visible" at under 2 blocks: the sensor refresh, line of sight through the House's beds or walls, or a stroll target. Then either:
   - do not count a `NOT_VISIBLE` abort within √5 against `COURTSHIP_MAX_SEEDS` (re-seed once the sensor has seen the partner again), or
   - show it cannot be fixed in the mod and say why in plan section 8.

   *Test expected.* A GameTest that seeds a licensed pair at distance ≤ 2 with the partner missing from `NEAREST_VISIBLE_LIVING_ENTITIES` (the inverse of the `nearby()` helper). It asserts the licence is not lapsed `MAX_SEEDS` and that a baby follows, and it must be red without the fix. Plus the same stress proof as item 1.

### Stress harness (for the builder)

The harness is not committed. It lived in my scratchpad and can be rebuilt in about 5 minutes:
- `StressGameTests` holds 24 `@GameTest` methods `stress0..23`. Each has its own `growth_stress_<k>` environment (`{"type": "minecraft:all_of", "definitions": []}`) and calls one copy of the `oneBirthPerDay` body.
- Run it with `-Dfabric-api.gametest.filter=villageheart-gametest:stress_game_tests_stress*`.
- Its diagnostics print, for each adult: `HOME`, whether the bed is occupied and its ticket free, `fed`/`housed` as the dawn computes them, age, position, `CANT_REACH_WALK_TARGET_SINCE`, game time, walk target and activity. They print just before the second `toDawn` and at the day-2 dawn.
