  - **Walls.** It acts only when the worker's box is inside a block's collision shape, and then moves it at most 3 blocks, to the nearest cell where it fits with ground under it. So it can put a worker on either side of the wall it was stuck in, or on a low roof. ## Fix list

1. **`src/main/java/dev/villageheart/worker/job/WorkerUnstick.java`: hidden away workers (M16 miner, M17 hunter) are "rescued".**
   - **What's wrong.** `WorkerSystem.tick` calls `rescue` for every loaded worker, including a hidden (`NoAI`, invisible) miner or hunter.
     - If anything solid ends up in the hold cell, `rescue` teleports the hidden villager up to 3 blocks away. This can be gravel falling down the shaft, a block a piston pushes, or a tree growing at a hunting ground.
     - `AwayTrips.repin` (every 20 ticks in `MinerSystem`/`HunterSystem`) then teleports it back.
     - For the hunter, which does not surface on a blocked hold, this can ping-pong every 20 ticks with an `[unstick]` log line each time.
   - **Evidence.** Probe `probeHiddenMinerStaysPinned` (a copy of `minerVanishesAndReturns`, not committed): once the miner is down, gravel is set in its hold cell and the one above.
     - The hidden, still-away miner was moved 1.0 block off its hold for **15 of 200 ticks** (`[unstick] ... moved to <hold - 1 x>`) before M16 surfaced it.
     - With `if (AwayTrips.isAway(v)) return false;` it is never moved while away. The surfacing itself is M16's own response to the blocked shaft.
   - **Expected.**
     - Skip away workers in `rescue` (`AwayTrips.isAway(v)` → `false`); their position belongs to `AwayTrips`.
     - Add an L2 test (the probe above is a ready start; a hunter version is better because the hunter does not surface).
2. **The farmer can stop working for good with status `ok` (`starter` run 4).**
   - **Evidence.** In `scripts/simulate.sh starter 10`, run 4 of 9, the farmer's `work.farmer.harvested` per day was 80, 6, then **0 on all of days 3-10**.
     - `worker_status` was `ok` (plus the lumberjack's `no_work`) the whole time.
     - The worker stayed employed and alive (population 8, no deaths).
     - Carrots in Storage went 215 → 3, and `food.days_left` went 9.2 → 0.1.
     - The lumberjack was fine the whole time.
     - The server log has no farmer line at all: no trip failure, no `[unstick]` for the farmer (the 6 `[unstick]` lines were all the lumberjack at its camp), and no fire.
   - **Repro attempt.** The world was overwritten by the next run before I could read the farmer's position. I reran 4 more times with `-Dvillageheart.farm.trace=true` and did not reproduce it (runs 6-9 all harvested every day). The rate is about 1 in 9 here, consistent with the builder's "run 7".
   - **Expected.**
     - Find the cause. Candidates: a farmer stuck somewhere that `WorkerUnstick` does not cover (inside a House or the Storage, behind a door, on a roof), or a walk that never ends because the planner keeps `CONTINUE`.
     - A farmer that cannot make progress must not report `ok`.
     - Make `starter` catch it with an assertion such as "farmer harvests on every day from day 3" (`work.farmer.harvested > 0 on every day from day 3`, or what the assertion language allows), or with a food-days-left floor.
     - Then run `starter` at least 10 times and report the per-day harvest counts.
3. **`LeafEscape`'s `mobGriefing` rule has no test.**
   - **What's wrong.** It is new behaviour (CLAUDE.md done-item 2), and nothing goes red if the gamerule check is removed.
   - **Expected.** An L2 test: the `lumberjackBoxedInByLeavesGetsOut` setup with `mobGriefing` false. No leaf breaks, and the trip simply fails. Restore the gamerule after the test.
