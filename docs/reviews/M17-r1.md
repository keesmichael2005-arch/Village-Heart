## Changed existing assertions (weakening check)
1. **`SiteRulesTest.huntingGround`:** the message changed from "No Hunting Lodge in the village" to "No Hunting Lodge within 40 blocks".
   This is the approved M5 rule change (DESIGN 5.4). A new reason pin with `Balance.HUNTING_GROUND_LINK_RANGE` was added, so the test is stricter, not weaker.
2. **`HeartTextsActivityTest`:** the generic-fallback cases used "hunter" as their example of a job with no specific words. They now use
   `unlisted_job` and check the same fallback. A new test, `theHunterHasItsOwnWords`, pins all four hunter texts. Not weakened.
3. **`minerHaulsFortyCobblestone` stray-item box:** narrowed from a 40-block cube around the test to its own 32 x 32 footprint, from `dy-14` to `dy+14`.
   I checked whether this could hide a real M16 drop, such as a pickaxe dropped at day end:
   - Every place in `src/main` that spawns items is one of these: `AwayTrips.release`, `MinerJob.dropPending`, `HunterJob.dropPending`
     (all three drop *pending haul or yield items* from the saved map, and that map never holds a tool), the lumberjack and farmer
     `popResource` (crops and logs), `BuildJobs` and `VillageClaims` (emeralds). `CampTools.use` damages or empties the chest slot in place.
     **No code path in the mod can drop a pickaxe.** A stone pickaxe can only come from a chest being removed. `MineTestSupport.clearHigh`
     fills a neighbouring high scene with air, and since 1.21.5 removing a container block spills its contents. Every miner scene keeps a stone pickaxe in its Mine chest.
   - "Age 0" does not prove the item was just dropped: an item entity in a chunk that is loaded but not entity-ticking never ages.
   - The new box still contains everything the test's own miner can drop: the whole platform, the shaft floor at `dy-12`, the Mine and Storage rooms, and the surface spot.
     The old box was no deeper (abs y 24 to 104 against 50 to 78 now: nothing on the real ground was ever inside either one).
   Conclusion: the narrowing excludes only items from neighbouring tests. It cannot hide a real miner drop. Not a fix item. A better root fix would be for `clearHigh` to empty the
   chests before it removes them, so a high scene never spills items (note 2).
4. `AwayState` gained fields 9 and 10 through a delegating 8-argument constructor, `MinerHauls` now delegates to `AwayHauls`, and `CampTools.lease` takes an optional

## The touch rule
"A hunter that touches a hidden hunter within 6 blocks of the marker counts as arrived." Without it, the corridor test still arrives, but only
after 152 ticks: `WalkTo`'s "can get no nearer, within 6" acceptance eventually fires while the hunter pushes against the solid NoAI villager.
The builder measured up to 2600 ticks under CPU load. The rule is narrow: it applies only while walking, within `HUNT_ENTRY_CLOSEST`
(the same 6 blocks that the "closest" acceptance already allows), and only on contact with an *away* villager. `holdCell` then keeps the two
hunters on different cells (asserted). It is a sensible game rule (the hunters spread out in the field), not a mask on a broken path search.
A hidden villager stays solid, though, for every other walker too (note 1).
