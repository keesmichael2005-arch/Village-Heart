## Exploits
- **Emeralds or items from nothing:** none found.
  - `Markets.trySell` is the only writer. In one tick it plans on a fresh view, withdraws exactly `lot` (or puts the partial take back:
    first the stockpile, through `deposit`, which shrinks the stack; then the merchant's inventory; then the ground), deposits 1 emerald,
    and puts the goods back if the treasury refuses.
  - Both conservation mutations go red.
  - The emerald output is bounded by the per-merchant, per-stall and village caps (32/day). Firing and re-hiring at the same stall is
    capped by `byStall`. Day rollback through `/time set` resets the ledger; that is documented (`!=`).
- **Non-Steward setting reserves:** `Permission.SET_RESERVES(STEWARD)`, through the standard `ActionGate`. That gate checks the rate limit,
  that the menu is open and near the Heart, the epoch, the village, then `malformed()`, then the rank. Mutation-tested at L1 and L2.
- **Malformed payloads:**
  - C2S: `SetReservesPayload` decodes any VAR_INT. The gate rejects out-of-range values with `BAD_RESERVE` before the permission check,
    and `Reserves` itself throws out of range, so no bad value can reach saved data.
  - S2C: `HeartCodecs.decodeReserves` clamps, and `MarketCodecsTest.aHostileStockCannotMakeTheClientThrow` covers it.
  - The admin command uses brigadier ranges plus `Reserves.valid`.
- Client code: `HeartScreen` (src/client) is the only client change. No client classes are referenced from `main`. Every lang key used by
  `market/` and `HeartActions` is in `en_us.json`.

## Saved data
- `WorldClaim.market` is written through `MARKET.lenientOptionalFieldOf("market")`, and only when it differs from `MarketState.initial()`.
- Old saves: `aPreM22SaveHasDefaultMarketAndDefaultsAreNotWritten` passes.
- Leniency:
  - out-of-range reserves are clamped (`marketReservesOutOfRangeAreClamped`, 99/9999 → 30/1024);
  - one bad ledger entry drops only that entry (`oneBadLedgerEntryDropsThatEntryOnly`);
  - a broken `market` (bad `day`, or `market: 5`) reads as absent and the claim survives (`aBrokenMarketReadsAsAbsentAndNeverLosesTheClaim`).

  All of these are lenient as required. A broken ledger also resets the reserves to the defaults, as the plan accepts.
- `releaseAt`/`clear` reset the market: `WorldClaimTest.releaseAndClearForgetTheMarket`.
- What was not proven in-world is fix 1: the GameTests never read the saved copy.
