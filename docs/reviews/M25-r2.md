## Owner decision 2026-11-12: announcements

The decision says: lead-up warnings are gold chat lines. A raid start shows the big title, sends a bold red chat line and rings the
bell, and the title must fit at the smallest GUI scale. A siege start sends a bold red chat line and rings the bell, with **no** title.

Built: `ThreatWarnings.Tone` (WARNING gold, ALERT bold red, NOTICE plain). `Kind.title()` is `Optional` and empty for a siege.
`Kind.start()` is "Raiders are attacking the village!" for a raid and "The dead are coming!" for a siege. `ThreatMessages.warn`
sends lead-up lines in gold. `ThreatMessages.started` sends the red line, plus the title for a raid only. The real starts
(`VillageRaids`, `ZombieSiege.start`) call `started` and then `VillageBell.ring`. `ThreatScheduler` calls `warn`. The lang key
`title.siege` is gone, and `LangCoverageTest` asserts it stays gone. Stuck-raider lines stay plain chat. The decision does not mention
them.
