## Fix list

1. **`src/main/java/dev/villageheart/growth/Growth.java` `onBirth` (line ~291): vanilla births outside the claim log ERROR.**
   - What happens: `VillagerMakeLoveMixin.villageheart$born` calls `Growth.onBirth` for every villager birth on the server. With no matching licence it counts `GrowthStats.unlicensedBirth()` and logs `[growth] a baby (…) was born without a licence` at **ERROR**. That covers births in other villages, far outside the claim, and worlds with no claim at all.
   - Evidence: the plain `breedingAbortProbe` (`-Dvillageheart.probe=true`, no claim) produced 99 vanilla births and 99 `[Server thread/ERROR] (villageheart) [growth] a baby … was born without a licence` lines.
   - Why it matters: that is legal vanilla breeding the mod promises not to touch. The ERROR fails the v1.0 soak gate ("no error logs from `villageheart`") as soon as any other village breeds. It also makes the `unlicensedBirths` counter meaningless as a gate alarm.
   - Expected: count and log an unlicensed birth only when the child (or the bed) is inside the claim (`BreedingGate.claimContains`). Ignore it elsewhere, or log it at DEBUG.
   - Test: a GameTest with a vanilla pair breeding **outside** the claim (or with no claim). It asserts `GrowthStats.unlicensedBirths() == 0` and that no ERROR is logged, or uses a counter that only in-claim births can raise.

2. **No test proves a BORN licence stops exempting its parents (one baby per day).**
   - What happens: `BreedingGate.licensed` returns `l.active() && l.pair().contains(…) && !stale`. Mutant F drops `l.active()`, so a BORN licence still exempts its pair, and **all 17 growth GameTests stay green**.
   - Why the tests miss it: vanilla's 6000-tick cooldown hides the mutant. T-L2-3 watches only 1500 ticks after the birth, and T-L2-1 next checks at the next dawn, which ends the licence anyway. A day is 24000 ticks, so with the mutant (or a regression in `License.born`/`active`) the same pair can breed again the same afternoon if it holds food. Vanilla food sharing between villagers gives it that food.
   - Expected: a GameTest that, after a licensed birth and still on the same day, sets both parents' age to 0, gives them bread and asserts `canBreed()` is false and `BreedingGate.blocks` is true. Optionally it also seeds `BREED_TARGET` and asserts no second baby within one make-love run. It must turn red under mutant F.

3. **`Growth.mustDropBed` / `bedForBirth`: a licensed parent can still get a baby into a bed that is not a House bed** (found by reading the code; not reproduced).
   - What happens: the gate exempts **both** parents from the dawn on (WAITING, APPROACHING and SEEDED alike). Only the SEEDED initiator `a` gets the reserved bed in `bedForBirth`. If vanilla's own `InteractWith` gives `b` (or `a` before seeding) a `BREED_TARGET`, that run uses vanilla `takeVacantBed`. `mustDropBed` drops only beds **inside** the claim. A licensed pair near the claim edge (the sphere is 80 blocks, vanilla searches 48) can therefore take a free bed **outside** the claim and have a baby there.
   - Consequences: the birth does not match the licence, so it is counted as "unlicensed" (with the ERROR of item 1). The licence stays active. The owner's rule "only with a free House bed" is broken.
   - Expected: for any licensed parent that is not the seeded initiator, drop every bed vanilla takes, wherever it is. Equivalently: `mustDropBed` returns true when `BreedingGate.licenceOf(v)` is present and `seededLicenceOf(v)` is empty. Alternatively, `bedForBirth` hands the reserved bed (or empty) to both parents.
   - Test: a licensed pair in WAITING, with `BREED_TARGET` seeded by hand on `b`, a free bed outside the claim within 48 blocks, and the reserved House bed held. It asserts the outside bed keeps its free ticket and no baby is born from that run.
