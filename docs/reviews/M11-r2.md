## Fix list

1. **`src/main/java/dev/villageheart/worker/job/LeafEscape.java` destroys player-placed (persistent) leaves.**
   - **What's wrong.**
     - `free()` breaks every `BlockTags.LEAVES` block in the 3x3x3 around the worker.
     - It runs on *every* failed deposit trip (`DepositTrip`, for all job kinds) and on *every* tree give-up (`LumberJob.giveUpTree`). It never checks whether the worker is actually boxed in.
     - So a decorative hedge next to a worker whose trip times out, or next to a tree it cannot reach, is chopped. After each 6000-tick blacklist the lumberjack retries and chops further, so it tunnels into a hedged garden.
   - **Evidence.** Probe `probeHedgeSurvivesGiveUp`, not committed, a copy of `radiusAndUnreachable`:
     - Setup: the glass box around tree A is replaced by a 3-high ring of `OAK_LEAVES[persistent=true]`, 9 blocks from the trunk.
     - Result: `Expected player hedge blocks broken to be 0: was 14 on tick 361`, with `[probe] hedge blocks broken: 14 of 216`.
   - **Expected.**
     - Never break persistent leaves: check `!s.getValue(LeavesBlock.PERSISTENT)`. Persistent leaves are what a player places, and what vanilla never decays.
     - Preferably break only when the worker really is enclosed. For example: no neighbouring cell at feet level is passable and the cells that block it are natural leaves.
     - `lumberjackBoxedInByLeavesGetsOut` should then use **natural** leaves, kept from decaying (a log within distance 6, or `DISTANCE` set and no random ticks). It currently uses `PERSISTENT=true`, so it would block the correct fix.
     - Add an L2 test that a persistent-leaf hedge survives a give-up and a failed trip. The probe above is a ready start.
   - The other two checks:
     - **Drops.** `destroyBlock(p, true, v)` drops the leaves' loot on the ground. The lumberjack's `findItem` collects saplings, sticks and apples within its radius, so that is fine. A farmer's failed trip leaves them lying until a lumberjack is near or they despawn. That is acceptable.
     - **Looping.** `free()` is a single bounded pass (at most 27 blocks). It is called at most once per failed trip, which has a cooldown, and once per give-up, so it cannot loop forever.
2. **`StarterActions.ramp` (harness): the javadoc's invariant is false, and canopies leave floating blocks.**
   - **What's wrong.**
     - `target = clamp(e, floor - k, floor + k)` bounds each column's distance from the floor. It does not bound the step to the next column.
       - Inside the ring, natural steps of 2-5 blocks stay, for example 69 → 66 at k=2/3 near (683-684, 857), and 72 → 68 at k=3 near (672-673, 882).
       - At the ring's outer edge (k=9 → 10) the step can be anything: 78 → 85 at (629-635, 888-889).
     - The leaf/log skip (`while LEAVES || LOGS: e--`) stops at the first **air** pocket inside a canopy, so `e` is taken at canopy height. The ramp then puts a grass block there, in mid-air. Examples, with nothing below them down to the real ground at y=63-66:
       - (673, 78, 839), (673, 74, 840), (669, 74, 841), (670, 74, 842).
     - Evidence: I read the saved world of run 1 (site 638, 69, 848) with my own region reader.
       - Every column in k=1..9 satisfies |h − floor| ≤ k, so the patch edge itself is fine.
       - The step counts are in the bullets above. 185 floating ground blocks are in the area (this count includes the prefab roofs).
     - None of this broke a run, because the workers stay on the patch and the grove now that the edge is ramped. But the "no edge drops more than one block per step" claim is not true.
   - **Expected.**
     - Find the ground through leaves *and* air above it, for example with `MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES` and then logs skipped.
     - Build the ramp ring by ring from the patch outward, clamping each column to ±1 of its already-placed inner neighbour. This gives a real 1-block-per-step slope to the land, or a 1-block step at the ring's edge.
     - Or else correct the javadoc and the BACKLOG line to say what it does guarantee.
