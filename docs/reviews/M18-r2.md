## Orchestrator questions

**Alarm.**
- `GuardAlarm.raise` runs on `AFTER_DAMAGE` when an `Enemy` hurts a villager in the claim. `refresh` runs every 20 ticks and keeps the alarm up while an in-claim hostile that targets a villager is within 24 blocks of a guard.
- The alarm ends `GUARD_ALARM_HOLD_TICKS` = 600 ticks after the last sign of a threat, so it **does not flap**. A3 shows that the return to bed depends on the alarm ending.
- `GuardSchedule.phase` keeps wounded before raid or alarm, so wounded guards stay in bed (L1 row).

**Lever order, as built.**
- a: the kit has 2 iron chestplates and 2 iron helmets.
- b: `GuardRuntime.patrol` walks to `GuardAlarm.spot` while the alarm is up. `GuardCombat.engage` paths toward the nearest awake guard at ≥ 70 % health within 32 blocks when its own health is below 50 %.
- c: `Balance.GUARD_LEVEL_HEALTH = {30, 40, 50}`. **But** `Guards.onHired` calls `applyPerks(guard, false)` (no heal), so a fresh level-1 guard has **20 of 30** health until it has slept. The duel shows this: the zombie lands 3 hits of 3.0, and the guard ends at 11.18. Every `replace` hire in failure mode A starts at 20. Fix item 2.

**Duel `guardBeatsOneZombie` bound.**
- The test asserts at least 5.0 health (its comment says "a quarter", but 5 is a sixth of 30). Its comments also still say "of 20 health".
- Measured in my run: guard 3 hits of 6.0, zombie 3 hits of 3.0, guard health left **11.18**, done in 103 ticks.
- With item 2 fixed, the guard starts at 30 and ends at about 21. **Tighten the bound to ≥ 50 % of max health (15)**, as asked. At the current 20-health start it would fail (11.18), which is exactly the bug the bound should catch. Fix item 3.

**Can a gate be left open?**
- The keeper closes every gate it opened after at most 400 ticks, and the ledger is saved.
- The r1 BACKLOG gap remains: the keeper ticks only while a village is claimed. Nothing in r2 made this worse.

**Can a guard fall back and drag a zombie into the workers?** Yes, through a wall gate.
- A falling-back guard still has a target, so `GuardCombat.engaged` is true.
- So `GatePaths.mayPass` ignores the hostile near the gate, and `GateKeeper` keeps that gate open while "an engaged guard needs it".
- A guard below half health outside the wall therefore paths through the gate toward a healthy ally inside, and the zombie chasing it can follow it in before the gate closes.
- This is not proven in game. It goes to BACKLOG with the fix: a retreating guard should not count as engaged for gates.
- The fall-back target is another guard, never a worker. With the alarm, the guards gather where the trouble is, so the target is usually there already.

**Does the alarm flap?** No (600-tick hold). Two related problems:
- The alarm is static JVM state (fix item 6).
- A zombie outside the wall, still inside the claim, keeps the alarm up all night, so the guards keep walking toward it (BACKLOG).

**Client classes in main.** None: 0 added lines in `src/main` import `net.minecraft.client` or `dev.villageheart.client`. No new lang keys are missing (`LangCoverageTest` is green).
