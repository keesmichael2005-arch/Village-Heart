## The side-by-side test
- **Fair.** The three groves are asserted to be translations of one another (same seeds, 300 logs each, disjoint, every log inside the claim and at most 12 blocks
  above its ground). Every tick, the sampler checks the claim owner of every claimed tree against the worker of that tree's level. Wear is checked against the
  logs every 100 ticks. No "Needs axe" spell may last over 40 ticks, and each grove must still have 20 or more logs standing at the end, so a run where a
  grove runs out cannot pass.
- **Light bias.** Iron sits on level 0, under both upper groves, so any shade effect works against iron. Hiring order (iron first) is the only asymmetry, and it is
  negligible over 4000 ticks.
- **Wrong-reason pass.** At Hamlet all three workers stay level 1, which is asserted, so the ratio comes from the tool only. The cap-ignored mutant got iron to
  level 2 for its last logs (108 instead of 106-107) and the test went red on the level assertion. The margins are 1.19-1.20 against 1.1 and 1.49-1.51 against
  1.3. Counts are deterministic in every run I saw (wood 71, stone 89, iron 106 or 107).
- **Cleanup.** `clearUpper` runs before the claim and `clearAll` only on the success path. A failed run leaves the two glass platforms, the dark oaks and
  the villagers at +27..+74 above that test's yard (note 3). In my three mutated runs where it failed, no later test in the same run was affected. Each test of a
  run gets its own x/z slot, and level 1's trees are inside `sweepStrayTrees`' +40 range anyway.
