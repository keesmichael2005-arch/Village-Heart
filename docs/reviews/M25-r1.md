# M25 review, round 1 (raids, sieges, wall damage, creeper breaches, builder repairs, random raids)

verdict: FAIL

Reviewed `m25-full` after merging `origin/dev` into it (merge `5494fa5`; conflicts resolved, see "Merge" below), against
`docs/plans/M25.md` (r3.1 + Deviations), the ROADMAP M25 entry and the owner decisions in `docs/LOOP_STATE.md`
(`decisions_by_human`). The design is sound and almost every owner decision is built and proven. The milestone fails on one red full verify
caused by an M25 test, one visible text defect in the new siege title, and one owner rule ("every villager goes indoors" during a
**siege**) whose test cannot tell the mod's shelter from vanilla's bell.

## Fix list

1. **`SiegeLureGameTests.lureBudgetCaps` is flaky and failed the second full verify.**
   `src/gametest/java/dev/villageheart/gametest/SiegeLureGameTests.java:328`. Run 2 (`5494fa5`) failed:
   `siege_lure_game_tests_lure_budget_caps failed ... slots free, holding 19 of 20 on tick 3002`. After the ten discards and
   `LureSlots.resetForTests()`, one of the 20 live zombies was never re-admitted for the rest of the test. Alone it passes (2 of 2), and in
   run 2 its batch ran by itself (`siege_lure_budget` started 05:04:03, failed 05:04:08, and no other environment started in between). So the
   cause is world state left by earlier batches. `LureSlots.consider` drops a zombie only when it has a target or is past `LURE_RADIUS`
   (112), so the stuck zombie had picked up a target (a leftover villager, golem or player from an earlier test is the likely one) or
   wandered out. The builder's own notes list it among the failures before their green run.
   Expected: the test must not depend on what earlier batches left behind. For example, clear targets and non-test villagers, golems
   and players near the yard in setup, and compare against the zombies `LureSlots` can admit (no target, inside the radius). On a
   failure, report the missing zombie's target and distance. Then show 3 consecutive green full L2 runs.
2. **The siege title does not fit the screen.** `ThreatWarnings.SIEGE_TITLE` "The dead are coming!"
   (`src/main/java/dev/villageheart/core/siege/ThreatWarnings.java:36`, lang `message.villageheart.title.siege`). In the L4 shot
   `0047_siege_title.png` (854×480) the first "T" and the final "!" are cut off at the screen edges. The raid title just fits.
   Expected: a shorter title (for example "The dead rise!") or a subtitle split, so it fits at the client-test resolution. Look at the
   re-taken shot. Keep `ThreatWarningsTest` and `LangCoverageTest` in step.
3. **Shelter for everyone during a siege is not proven.** Mutation: `UnhiredShelter.tick`
   (`src/main/java/dev/villageheart/worker/shelter/UnhiredShelter.java:67`) `if (!siege && !raid)` changed to `if (!raid)`, so unhired
   villagers are never held during a siege. It **survives**
   `ThreatShelterGameTests.siegeRingsBellAndSendsVillagersIndoors` and every other test. The bell alone gives vanilla's `HIDE`, which walks
   the villager to the nearby House bed long enough for the one "inside" check to pass. Vanilla's hide is short and does not last a
   night-long siege. The owner rule is "every villager in the claim goes indoors" for a raid **or** a siege.
   Expected: the siege test asserts that the unhired villager is in `WorkerBrain.SHELTER` (like the raid test does) and is still inside
   well past vanilla's hide time (for example 600+ ticks after the bell), and is released when the siege ends. The mutation above must then
   fail.
4. *(minor, L1 covers it)* `SiegeGameTests.vindicatorChopsGate` (`src/gametest/java/dev/villageheart/gametest/SiegeGameTests.java:336`) is
   documented as "chops the gate in two hits", but it only checks that one hit did 4 and that the gate opened eventually. With `HP_GATE` 12
   (three hits) it still passes. `WallHpTest` and `SiegeHitsTest` do catch it.
   Expected: assert that the gate breaks on the second vindicator hit.
5. *(minor, L1 covers it)* `StuckRaiderGameTests.sealedRaiderGlowsThenFlees` checks "not fled yet" only on the tick the glow is first seen.
   A raider that fled 20 ticks after glowing (grace ignored) passes. `StuckRaidersTest` catches it.
   Expected: assert that the raider is still present about `GRACE - 20` ticks after the glow.
6. *(process)* The builder ticked the M25 box in ROADMAP (`0301cb6`) before review. This review sets it back to `[ ]`. The verifier ticks
   it on PASS.
