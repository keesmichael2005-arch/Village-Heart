## Notes (non-blocking; for the orchestrator)

- **A. Missing before/after tables in M26-results.** The builder pace (20 -> 70) and "merchant never strikes" are written up as prose and bullets,
  not as before/after tables.
  - For the builder pace this is acceptable: the sim `build` action finishes a job in one go, so no scenario number moves.
  - For the merchant rule, the only "before" is `full_raids` "not measured". A one-line table (`full_raids` or `full` with and without the rule)
    would close this gap.
- **B. Time bounds moved with the owner's builder pace.**
  - `BuildGameTests.farmPlotBuilt` now allows two days instead of one. Scaling with the pace would give 3.5 days, so two days is the tighter choice.
  - `maxTicks` was raised for `farmPlotBuilt`, `playerBlockAppearsMidBuild` and `twoBuildersTwoBuilds`.
  - `MarketGameTests.dailyCapByLevel` and `ledgerSurvivesReload` got larger `maxTicks`. These follow from the decision and do not hide a failure.
- **C. Narrowed checks.**
  - (i) `twoBuildersTwoBuilds` checks the oldest-build-first claim rule over the first work day only. At the old pace both builds finished on
    day 1, so no coverage that existed before was lost. The day-2 re-claim order is still untested.
  - (ii) `dailyCapByLevel` now tests levels 2 and 3 in-world, and its idle after the cap went from 1200 to 100 ticks. A sale takes 480/400 ticks,
    so the count equality alone no longer proves the stop. The `DAILY_LIMIT` status assertion still does. Level 1 is proven at L1
    (`MarketRulesTest`). In-world it is reached only by `ledgerSurvivesReload`, whose 1200-tick idle runs past the end of the work day.
  - (iii) The rewritten strike test dropped "treasury 0 after the wages". The wage settlement order is still asserted at L1 and L2.
- **D. Economy margin is thin in this run.** My `full` run passed every assertion, but its numbers are worse than the ones in M26-results.md
  (treasury min 16, no strikes):
  - The treasury fell from 36 (day 7) to 0 on days 28-30.
  - A bill of 20-21 against `paid` 11 shows strikers: 1 on days 27-28 and 3 on day 30.
  - `min(treasury) >= 0` holds, and there was no starvation (`zerorun(1 - food.out)` = 0).

  This is one unseeded run and the gate holds. A longer run or a different AI roll may strike widely. Watch it at the v1 gate.
