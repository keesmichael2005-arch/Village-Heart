## Cause: workers freeze at the edge of the sim's forced chunks (evidence)

- **The forced area.** `SimWorld.forceLoad(level, structure)` forces chunks `structure ± LOAD_RADIUS_CHUNKS (4)`. Seed 1's village start is at (640, 0, 816), which is chunk (40, 51).
  - So the forced, entity-ticking chunks are x 36..44 (blocks 576..719) and z 47..55 (blocks 752..895).
  - The neighbours of a forced chunk sit at ticket level 32 (block-ticking, not entity-ticking). An entity there is loaded but not ticked.
- **The site.** It is placed 24-72 blocks from the Heart, at (638, 69, 848), size 44 × 32, so z 848..880.
  - The Farmhouse composter (the farmer's job block) is at (641, 70, 865).
  - The chopping block is at (663, 70, 865).
  - So the south edge of the ticking area is only **31 blocks** south of either job block.
- **Where the villagers ended up.** I read the saved entity regions of every run with a small NBT reader (`scratchpad/verifier-m11-r4/villagers.py`):

| Run | Result | Villagers on a forced-area boundary at the end |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PASS | 3 vanilla villagers at x 575.9-576.0 (west edge) |
| 2 | **FAIL** (`min(work.farmer.harvested) > 0`) | **the M11 farmer at (635.5, 87.0, 896.1)**, a vanilla butcher at (642.5, 83.0, 896.0), 3 vanilla villagers at x 576, 1 at z 751.9 |
| 3 | PASS | a vanilla farmer at (636.3, 86.8, 896.0), a vanilla villager at (664.9, 74.0, 896.1), 4 at x 576 |
| 4 | **FAIL** (4 assertions) | **the M11 farmer at (655.5, 78.0, 896.1)**, 4 vanilla villagers at x 576 |

- **The builder's two "lumberjack" positions are these same spots.** The round-3 builder reported (642, 83, 896) and (635, 87, 896). Those are exactly the spots where villagers pile up in my runs: (642.5, 83.0, 896.0) and (635.5, 87.0, 896.1). Both are on the first block row of chunk z = 56, on the hilltop south of the site.
- **The farmer's last trace (`-Dvillageheart.farm.trace=true`).**
  - Run 2: the last line is `@97041 plan CONTINUE (mature (650, 70, 870))` at the start of the day-5 work period. After that there is no `[farm]` line for the rest of the run, so the job never ticked again.
  - Run 4: there are **no `[farm]` lines at all**. The farmer was hired at tick 200 of day 1 and strolled about 37 blocks south from its door before WORK began at 1000. The farmer harvested 0 on every day, food days left went 1.6 → 0.0, and from day 5 both workers were `too_hungry`. The lumberjack stopped as a consequence: logs flat from day 6.
- **Why the nets miss it.**
  - (b) `FarmJob.stallWatchdog` runs inside the job tick, which a frozen villager never reaches.
  - (c) `[far-worker]` needs a distance above 40, but the farmer is 31.6 and 34.3 blocks from its composter. The builder's second "lumberjack" spot, (635, 87, 896), is 41.8 blocks from the chopping block, which is why net (c) looked like it helped.
  - The worker status stays `ok` for the same reason: nothing that sets it runs.
- **Is this a mod bug a player sees?** Mostly no. With a player in the village, the simulation distance keeps the village entity-ticking, and a frozen worker far from every player is vanilla behaviour. It is a Layer-3 fixture bug.
- **What is still real gameplay.** The WAKE-phase stroll drifts workers 40+ blocks from their workplace every morning. `[far-worker]` logged it 3 times in run 1 and once in run 4, all in `[core, idle]` at day time 560-980. D17 saw the same drift.

## Fix list

### Must fix

1. **`src/gametest/java/dev/villageheart/sim/SimWorld.java` (`forceLoad` / `forcedChunksTicking`, `LOAD_RADIUS_CHUNKS = 4` around the village *start chunk*): the scenario's workers walk out of the ticking area and freeze.**
   - **Expected.** Force the area the village actually uses.
     - At least `Balance.CLAIM_RADIUS` around the Heart/bell, i.e. ±6 chunks, after the bell is found. Or the site bounds plus `LUMBER_RADIUS` and a stroll margin.
     - Wait for that area to tick, as now.
   - **Expected.** Add a sim metric or a hard check: a worker (or any village villager) whose position is not entity-ticking at a day's end is reported (e.g. `workers.not_ticking`), and `starter` asserts it is 0.
     - That makes this failure visible instead of a silent "harvested 0".
   - **Then.** Run `starter` at least 6 times and report the per-day harvests.
2. **`src/main/java/dev/villageheart/worker/WorkerSystem.java` `farFromWork` (net c): it teleports healthy workers.**
   - Builders working at a distant site get teleported back to their table (probe above).
   - Sleeping workers whose House is more than 40 blocks away get teleported out of bed every 2400 ticks, with WARN spam.
   - It also logs a WARN for every ordinary morning stroll.
   - **Expected.** Remove it once item 1 removes its reason. If a lost-worker rescue is kept, it must:
     - act only in the WORK phase;
     - act only on a worker whose job made no progress (not on distance alone);
     - leave out builders (or measure from the build site) and away/hidden workers;
     - log at INFO;
     - never act on a worker that is asleep or has a running trip.
   - Its L2 test must include a negative case: a working builder at a far site, and a sleeper in a far House, are **not** moved.
3. **`src/main/java/dev/villageheart/worker/farm/FarmJob.java` `stallWatchdog` (net b): its "set down beside its spot" teleport is a blind move.**
   - **What it does.** The move goes 2-4 blocks to the nearest free cell with ground, from dy -1 to +3. That can be through the Farmhouse wall, onto a roof, or into a neighbouring (player's) house.
   - **Why it must change.** It was added for a stall that, as shown above, it cannot detect: the job tick does not run for a frozen worker.
   - **Expected.** Keep the `[farm-stall]` diagnostic and the `SITE_UNREACHABLE` status if you like. The status text "Can't reach its site" is an acceptable player-facing reason for "ripe crops, and no harvest for a whole work day". A Storage-full trip status still wins in `Workers.status`, so that reason is not hidden. But drop the teleport, or limit it to `WorkerUnstick`'s rule (only when the body is inside a block).
4. **D19 in `docs/plans/M11.md` and `PROGRESS.md`: record the cause.** State that the cause was the sim's forced area, with the positions above, and say which nets are kept and why.

### BACKLOG (not blocking once 1-4 are done)

5. **The WAKE-phase stroll drifts workers.** `WorkerBrain`'s IDLE uses vanilla `VillageBoundRandomStroll`, bounded only by village sections, so a worker drifts 40+ blocks every morning before work. That is in a natural plains village; the stroll is not bounded by the mod's claim. A stroll around HOME or the job block (`StrollAroundPoi`) would keep workers near their village and make L3 less noisy.
6. **Test hooks are not reset.**
   - `WorkerSystem.setFarBlocksForTest` / `setLostTicksForTest` and `FarmJob.setStallTicksForTest` / `setAllCellsTakenForTest` are not reset in `VillageTestSupport.reset`. A test that fails before its restore line leaves them set for later batches.
   - `setAllCellsTakenForTest` is a test switch inside the production planner; prefer seeding real claims.
7. **`farSince` / `farLogged` are never cleared.** These maps in `WorkerSystem` are not cleared for fired or dead workers. The leak is tiny.
