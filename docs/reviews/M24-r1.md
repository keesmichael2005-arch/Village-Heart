## Non-blocking notes
1. **Withdraw is pending M12, which I accept.** The permission and the action don't exist on `dev`, so there is nothing to
   guard. `PermissionsTest` requires a row for every `Permission` value, so M12 can't add `WITHDRAW_TREASURY` without stating
   that it is Owner-only, and the transfer path already re-reads the owner. Risk: M12 forgets T-L2-6. I added a line to M12's
   roadmap entry and left the item noted as pending under M24.
2. **The double-chest partner check has no shipped test** (mutation 5 survives). It works: my probe protected the outer half
   against both opening and breaking. Suggested GameTest, a copy of my probe:
   - after `BuildingSystem.resetRuntimeForTests()`, place a LEFT/RIGHT chest facing east at `(11,1,19)`/`(11,1,20)` in the yard;
   - expect the Visitor to be refused on `(11,1,20)` for both use and `destroyBlock`;
   - check a single chest at `(11,1,23)` as a control.

   `Members.compoundIsProtected` (closing a double-chest menu on demotion) is also untested.
3. **Two uncovered paths are missing from DESIGN §2's "not stopped" list:**
   - a Visitor's projectile breaking a protected decorated pot (verified: contents drop);
   - redstone on a protected dispenser, dropper or crafter.

   Neither goes through a player open or break, so they are outside v1's stated scope. They should be named in the list, or
   the pot case could be closed with a `ProjectileHit` check.
4. **Snapshot headroom is 31 KiB.** The next list added to the snapshot needs a tighter bound or its own payload, as plan §8
   item 10 says.
5. **`mp_ranks.png`:** vanilla chat lines ("Olga made you a Resident…") show faintly behind the rank button row. The cause is the
   chat overlay under the screen, not our layout; `assertClean` passed. This is cosmetic. The test could clear chat before the
   screenshot, as `ranksTab` does.
6. `MP_SESSION end` is logged before the fake player leaves and the client disconnects, so those expected disconnects don't
   trip the gate. As a result, a server error during shutdown is not caught by `check-mp-log.sh`. The L4 run would still fail
   on a crash.
