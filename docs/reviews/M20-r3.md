## Fix list

1. **The check pass overwrites a player's waterlogged block** (`build/BuildExecutor.cellState`, the new line
   `if (step.kind() == CHECK && !st.getFluidState().isEmpty()) return CellState.FREE;`). A waterlogged stair, chest, slab, fence or similar has a
   non-empty fluid state. So a CHECK cell holding one is reported FREE, and `place` does a plain `setBlock` over it. That goes against the rule on the very next line
   ("a check pass leaves a different block of a player alone").
   Probe (temporary GameTest, removed; House `NONE`, `advance` to the first CHECK step, then three wall cells changed):
   - a dry oak stair: left alone (correct);
   - a waterlogged oak stair: **replaced by planks**;
   - a waterlogged chest holding 5 diamonds: **replaced by planks**.

   Expected: match only a real fluid block, for example `st.getBlock() instanceof LiquidBlock`. I tried that. All three player blocks stay.
   `checkPassDoesNotFloodAFarmland` stays green with the one-action replace disabled, so the repair of flowing water still works. Add a GameTest with a waterlogged player block in a wall cell
   at the check pass.
2. **A waterlogged attachment made after the confirm is broken by the clear** (`build/SiteSupport.dependent`:
   `|| !ns.getFluidState().isEmpty() ||` skips every waterlogged neighbour, so its `canSurvive` is never asked). At confirm the FLUID rule refuses such a site,
   because the waterlogged block counts as water next to the box. The per-step re-check has no such backstop.
   Probe with the real builder, using the layout of `attachmentAfterConfirmBlocksTheBuilder` (House `CLOCKWISE_180`, dirt at (3,4,3)). A **waterlogged oak sign** was set on the dirt right after `confirmSite`.
   After 331 ticks the dirt was cleared (cursor 10), the sign was gone and a water source was left. The build then stopped with "water in the way".
   Probes through `BuildJobs.advance` (the same per-step code) break a waterlogged wall sign, ladder, lantern, standing sign, rail and sea pickle the same way.
   Expected: skip only real fluid blocks (`ns.getBlock() instanceof LiquidBlock`). I tried that: the builder stops with
   "oak_sign in the way at ..." and the sign and dirt stay. Add the waterlogged case to `attachmentAfterConfirmBlocksTheBuilder`, or give it its own test.
