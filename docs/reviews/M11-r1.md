## Fix list

1. **`starter` is not reliable: 3 of 4 ten-day runs FAIL "logs increase every day"** (`sim/scenarios/starter.json`, `src/gametest/java/dev/villageheart/sim/StarterActions.java`, and the worker AI).
   - **Evidence.** Per-run numbers are in the table below. Every failing run has the same signature: worker status `storage_unreachable`, `[trip] deposit trip failed` lines, and stockpile logs flat for one day or more.
   - **Root cause, from the `[trip]` lines plus the saved world.**
     - The 8 grove trees are all felled on day 1, and their saplings need days to grow.
     - From day 1-2 the lumberjack therefore fells the natural village trees north of the site. They are inside its 32-block `LUMBER_RADIUS`: day 2 shows 3-4 trees felled with no grove tree left.
     - Those trees stand on lower ground. The `site` action flattens the 44x32 patch to y=69 (walk level 70) and builds no edge. The natural ground at the north edge is y=65-66 (column dump of rows z=830-850: row z=847 is 66-67, row z=848 is 69).
     - So the worker drops 4-5 blocks off the north side and cannot climb back. It stands at the foot of the cliff:
       - `worker at 661, 66, 847 could not reach the Storage door at 658, 70, 854` (runs 1 and 2);
       - the farmer, likewise: `Farmer … cannot reach the cell (656, 70, 863) … at BlockPos{x=656, y=65, z=847}`, in runs 1, 2 and 3.
     - In run 5 the lumberjack was stuck for 9 days at (675, 69, 837): 217 failed trips, and also "cannot reach the tree" for the grove.
       - That block is inside the oak leaves of a half-felled natural tree: two logs stand at (674, 68-69, 837) with leaves at y=69-72 around the worker's column.
       - So a worker can also be boxed in by a canopy it has cut into, and nothing ever frees it.
     - `max_deviation: 5` (D9) lets the site differ by up to 5 blocks from the land around it, so a cliff like this is expected, not bad luck.
   - **Expected.**
     - `starter` 10 days passes reliably. Suggested acceptance: 4 consecutive 10-day runs exit 0 under the normal machine load.
     - Do not loosen the logs assertion.
     - Plausible fixes, to be chosen by the builder:
       - blend the site's border into the surrounding ground: a 1-block-per-step ramp ring, or terrain fill so no edge is more than 1 block;
       - and/or keep the lumberjack on reachable trees: a tree whose base is not reachable from the camp is skipped;
       - plus a stuck-worker recovery (a worker with no movement for N trips steps or teleports out of leaves, or similar), so a worker boxed in by a canopy recovers.
     - The canopy trap (run 5) is a real-game bug, not a harness artefact, so it needs a fix in the mod as well as in the harness. Add a GameTest at the lowest layer that proves the fix, for example a worker below a 4-block step, or one inside leaves, gets back to the Storage.
2. **"Cooked before raw" (roadmap L1) is untested.**
   - **The mutation.** In `src/main/java/dev/villageheart/core/food/FoodRules.java`, `comparing(s -> s.value().raw())` → `comparing(s -> false)`. Nothing goes red:
     - all 13 `DinnerPlannerTest` tests and all 4 `FoodRulesTest` tests stay green, and so does every other L1 test;
     - `needs_game_tests_dinner_picks_best_food_child_half` passes (1/1).
   - **Why.** Every test pairs raw food with prepared food that is already ahead on nutrition or saturation:
     - raw beef (3, 1.8) against carrot (3, 3.6);
     - raw beef, cod and potato are already last by nutrition.
   - **Expected.**
     - An L1 case where a raw item would win on nutrition alone but must come after a prepared one. Examples: raw beef 3 against a cookie, sweet berries or melon slice at 2, or raw porkchop 3 against dried kelp 1. Both `bestFirst` and `DinnerPlanner.plan` should check it.
     - Better still, the L2 best-food test should hold such a pair too, so the raw item stays in the chest.
