## Item conservation (task question 2)

Read through `SelfCraftJob`, `SelfCrafts`, `SelfCraftSystem`, `GearOrders` and `VillageClaims.releaseAt`. No duplication path found.

- **Commit.**
  - The materials are withdrawn into the saved record, never into the worker's inventory.
  - `commit` is the only insert.
  - Progress writes are update-only (they match worker, key and recipe).
  - `finish` and `refund` remove the record by compare-and-remove.
  - A short withdraw puts everything back. `Stockpiles.deposit` shrinks the stack, so the `isEmpty` checks are right.
- **Two workers in one camp.**
  - Each worker reserves its own concrete `StackKey`s, and `MaterialCensus` subtracts the other workers' reservations.
  - Orders are keyed per (building, need, slot).
  - `twoLumberjacksOneAxe` and `aFinishedAxeSkipsTheWorkerStillCrafting` pass.
- **Reload mid-craft.** The record survives. `SelfCraftRuntime.get` rebuilds `GearOrders` from the records. At most 200 ticks of progress are lost (`craftPausesOvernightAndSurvivesReload`).
- **Reload mid-fetch.** Nothing has moved yet. The reservations are transient.
- **Storage chest broken mid-craft.** The items are in the record, not in the chest, so nothing is lost.
  - On cancel, the refund goes to the remaining Storage.
  - If there is no Storage, it goes to the worker's inventory if the worker is loaded.
  - Otherwise it is dropped at the building marker if that chunk is loaded.
  - Otherwise the record stays with `refund_pending`.
- **Fired while walking to Storage.** `Workers.fire` and `forget` → `SelfCraftJob.forget` → `abortFetch`, which releases the reservations. Nothing had been withdrawn.
- **Fired or dead after the commit.** HIRED_DROPPED twice in a row → refund to Storage. Both cases are tested, both are mutation-red.
- **Fire with a late `stop`.** The late stop is part of `firedWorkerRefunds`.
- **Claim release.** `refundAll` runs before the claim goes.
- **System snapshot.** The system works on a snapshot taken at END_SERVER_TICK, after the jobs' entity ticks, so a record that finished in that tick cannot be refunded again.

Minor loss paths go to BACKLOG; none is a duplication:
- `finish` and `commit` (short) call `v.getInventory().addItem(...)` and ignore the remainder. A full 8-slot villager inventory with no Storage space loses the leftover sticks or planks, or the tool.
- `refundAll` on claim release only logs a warning when a refund is incomplete, and the record goes with the claim. That needs no Storage, an unloaded villager and an unloaded building chunk at the same time.
- `craftTick` does not skip `refund_pending` records. That is not a dup today: the system refunds within 100 ticks and a craft needs at least 2400.

## `CampTools.lease` durability order (task question 5)

The comparator is now rank ↓, remaining ↑, order ↑ for every job, the hunter's ranked lease included.

Side effects:
- Among equal-rank tools, the most damaged one is used first, which is the intended "use the worn one up" rule.
- A non-damageable tool (max 0, remaining 0) is handed out first among equals. Harmless.
- A tool with the Unbreakable component counts as full, so it is used last among equals. Harmless.
- Two lumberjacks with one worn and one fresh axe: while the worn holder crafts, its colleague gets the worn axe (rank tie, less remaining), and the fresh one waits. That is by design.
- The hunter's bow-before-sword rank still wins (L1 `ToolAllocationTest`), and M15's side-by-side test still passes.
- No regression found in the M9, M15, M16 or M17 tests.

## Balance (task question 6): measured and estimated, not a fail

- **Stone axe.** 131 durability, worn below 14, so about 118 logs per axe.
  - The tool-speed numbers apply: `BASE_TICKS_PER_LOG` 80 / stone 2.0 = 40 ticks per log, or 80 with a wooden axe.
  - That is about 4700 pure felling ticks per stone axe.
- **The felling window.** `LUMBER_LAST_FELL` = 5000: no new tree is started after day time 5000. A lumberjack fells only in a 4000-tick morning window, and the afternoon is collect, replant or idle.
- **What the plan's "~45 %" gets wrong.** It is 4800 / (4700 + 4800) and assumes every craft tick displaces felling. In fact the craft starts at the next tree boundary after the axe crosses its worn line, eats the rest of that morning's window, and spills into the free afternoon.
- **Expected cost.** About half a window (≈ 2000 ticks) per axe, so **≈ 25-30 % of felling time** in a dense forest. The worst case, crossing the line at 1000, loses one morning.
- **Wooden phase (day 1).** The 2400-tick first axe takes 60 % of the first morning.
- **Miner.** About 4800 ticks per 12 hauls of about 2500 ticks, ≈ 14 %.
- **Farmer and hunter.** Free (idle-time only), or rare (a sword lasts about 10 trips).
- **Verdict.** Not unplayable. It is a visible slowdown on wood income, which the player removes by putting in any tool. An easy M26 win: a lumberjack whose axe is **WORN** (still 13+ logs left) defers its replacement until `LUMBER_LAST_FELL`, which makes the replacement nearly free (BACKLOG).
- **In the starter sim** the lumberjack has a diamond axe (1561 durability) and never crafted.
