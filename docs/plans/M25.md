## 1. Goal

When a raid starts on the claimed village, the Heart screen shows a red raid alert ("Raid! Wave 2/5, 4 raiders left"), online members of Resident rank and above get a chat line, every guard who is not wounded and not in training comes on duty within 10 seconds, hired workers stop work and go indoors, and golems fight as in vanilla. Every villager death is written to a death log (who, job, cause, day, during a raid or a siege), shown on the Overview tab and in the simulation metrics.

Walls do not make a village immune. Wall and gate blocks of a recorded defence have hit points and the vanilla crack overlay. Gates are the weak point (`HP_GATE` 8: two vindicator hits, four zombie hits). Zombies are lured toward a walled village at night and during a siege and hit the defence block in their way. Creepers walk to a wall and blow a breach in it. Ravagers break a wall block in two hits. The builder repairs the damage with cobblestone and fence gates from Storage. Raids also start on their own, stronger where the village is larger, richer or better walled, and a victory over one of those raids pays the treasury. A Hamlet is not sieged. A young Village with two wooden-sword guards gets a small siege: threatened, and not handed the late-game zombie count. Skeletons and every other hostile stay vanilla. There is no per-tier wall-length cap.

### 2.2 Guards as built (M18) **[verified: origin/dev]**

This is the contract M25 plugs into. M25 does not change shift counts, wound thresholds, ration rules, gate rules or which mobs a guard will fight.

- **Shifts.** `ShiftBalancer.dayCount(n) = n / 4`. One to three guards are all on nights. Four to seven: 1 on days. Eight to eleven: 2 on days. The rest are on nights, and nights stay at least 3 once there are 3 guards. Seniors (level, then XP) take nights. `GuardSchedule` day shift: duty `[0, 12000)`, off `[12000, 13000)`, sleep `[13000, 24000)`. Night shift: off `[0, 1000)`, sleep `[1000, 12000)`, duty `[12000, 24000)`.
- **Phase.** The overload the brain uses is `GuardSchedule.phase(shift, dayTime, raid, alarm, wounded, canWork, training)`. Order: a wounded guard sleeps, even in a raid; a trainee who is not wounded is `OFF` (awake, never on duty, not called by a raid or the alarm); else a raid or the alarm forces `DUTY`; else the clock; a guard who cannot work (`WorkGate.mayWork` false: strike or starving) has `DUTY` turned into `OFF` and still sleeps. A strike does not keep a guard home once the raid or alarm flag is set, because that return happens first.
- **Who sets the flags.** `Guards.phaseOf` passes `RaidCheck.active(level, village)` and `GuardAlarm.active(level)` and `GuardData.training(dayTime)`.
- **`RaidCheck`.** `setSource(Source)` replaces the whole answer (`Source` is `boolean underRaid(ServerLevel, VillageState)`). `null` restores `vanilla`, which uses `getRaidAt(heart)` and counts the raid when it is active and the centre or a raider is in the claim. `setViewSourceForTest` feeds a fake raid to that default. M18's `raidCallsUpAllGuards` (`GuardGameTests`) uses the default and must stay green.
- **`GuardAlarm`.** Static. `raise(level, pos)` holds for `GUARD_ALARM_HOLD_TICKS` (600). `refresh` (every 20 ticks) extends it while a hostile that has a villager as its target is within 24 blocks of a loaded guard. Raised when a hostile hurts a villager in the claim (`AFTER_DAMAGE`) and when a guard dies (`ALLOW_DEATH` inside `Guards.register`). `resetForTests` clears it. It is not saved.
- **Training.** `GuardTraining.qualifiesAt` is dusk (`GUARD_TRAINING_END` 12000) of the hire day. `GuardTraining.mayHire` is false from day time 12000. `GuardTestSupport.hireGuard` clears `trainingUntil` so the guard is already qualified; `hireTrainee` leaves the training day. Both set `Workers.nightGuardHireForTests` around the hire, because a night hire is refused.
- **Health and gear.** `GuardPerks.maxHealth` reads `{20, 30, 40}`. Bonus damage `{0, 1, 2}`. The class javadoc still says 30/40/50; the array is the truth. A new guard is given a wooden sword and no armour (`Guards.onHired`). Better gear is fetched from the Barracks chest and the stockpile. The duel `guardBeatsOneZombie` asserts a win and no health floor (owner, 2026-11-11): a wooden sword is 5 hits and the health left varies.
- **Food.** `GuardRation` cap 12, refill below 6, one point heals 1 HP. `GuardSystem.feed` returns immediately when any `Enemy` is within `GUARD_EAT_SAFE_DISTANCE` (10). Refill runs only then, and only when the guard is not in `GuardActivities.DUTY` or is within 16 blocks of its Barracks marker. Food is `Stockpiles.withdrawFirst` of a stack `FoodCatalog` knows that is not raw (bread counts; raw meat does not). `GuardCombat.fight`, while retreating, calls `GuardRations.bite` once the guard is within 5 blocks of a healthy ally and the target is not in melee with line of sight. That bite does not use the 10-block check. There is no other healing.
- **Wounds and retreat.** `WoundRule`: wounded below 30% of max health, cleared at 80%. A wounded guard sleeps even during a raid. Retreat is separate: below 50%, fall back to within 5 blocks of an ally at 70% or better, fight again at 80%. With no ally the guard stays in the fight.
- **Targets.** `GuardTargets.Kind.CREEPER` and `UNREACHABLE` are never chosen. Guards do not kill creepers. Flying mobs are unreachable. Sight 24. Patrol is the Heart, a point 3 cells outside each valid room's door, then lamp posts and watchtowers.
- **Gates.** `core.defence.GateRules.mayOpen(inClaim, powered, hostileNear, engagedGuard)`. `Balance.GATE_HOSTILE_RANGE` is 8. `GatePaths.mayPass` is what the pathfinding mixin asks. `GateTraffic` opens a gate on the path. `GateKeeper` closes it. `GateLedger` (`villageheart_gates`) remembers open gates across a reload. Mobs and unhired villagers still see a fence. A hostile within 8 blocks keeps a worker's gate shut; an engaged guard may still pass.
- **Activities.** `GuardActivities.DUTY` is `villageheart:guard_duty`, `OFF` is `villageheart:guard_off`. Sleep is vanilla `REST`.
- **Scenarios already on dev.** `sim/scenarios/defended.json`: two guards, wooden swords, empty chest, no walls. Both are on nights (`dayCount(2) == 0`). `defended_walled.json` adds `wall_ring` (a filled two-high cobblestone loop and one fence gate) and `lamps`. That ring is not a `DefenceSite`. The lure and the siege damage do not apply to it. `deaths.mobs <= 1` on those scenarios is an M26 balance target (owner, 2026-11-11). M25 re-runs them and does not change the assertion.

### 3.2 Key decisions

1. **One raid truth.** `VillageRaids` (glue) over `core.raid.RaidTracker` (pure). Installed with `RaidCheck.setSource`. No other consumer calls `getRaidAt` for "are we under attack", except the unhired vanilla brain, which M25 leaves alone.
2. **What counts.** Discovery uses `getRaidAt(heart)` only while nothing is tracked. A raid is relevant when it is not over and not stopped, and the centre is in the claim or a raider is. Once an id is tracked, facts come from `Raids.get(id)` until that raid is over, stopped or gone. `getRaidAt` returns the nearest *active* raid within 96 blocks and would swap or miss ours. A second raid nearby is ignored while one is tracked. Because `Raids.tick` removes a stopped raid in the same tick, the tracked id's status is read **every tick**; waves, raiders and guards are read every `RAID_CHECK_TICKS` (20). The system runs at the end of the server tick, after vanilla `stop()` and in time to see `isStopped` before the next `Raids.tick` removes the raid. A raid is relevant before its first wave: the 10 second call-up is shorter than the 15 second cooldown.
3. **The source also covers the first tick and a siege.** `RaidCheck.setSource` becomes: true when a siege is running, or a raid is tracked, or (nothing tracked yet) the vanilla `RaidCheck` rule would be true. The last clause keeps M18's `raidCallsUpAllGuards` green on the tick the raid is created, before `VillageRaids` has latched it. After the latch, the vanilla clause is not consulted.
4. **The alarm is the other channel.** Starting a tracked raid or a siege also calls `GuardAlarm.raise(level, heart)`. That makes guards gather, which is what an attack does. It does not keep them on duty: the hold is 600 ticks, and `refresh` only extends it while a hostile is hunting a villager within 24 blocks of a guard. A zombie chewing a wall with no villager in sight would drop the alarm. The `raid` flag is what holds duty for the whole raid or siege. T-L2-1 clears the alarm after the call-up and asserts the guards stay on duty.
5. **Trainees and the wounded are not called.** That is `GuardSchedule`, not a second filter M25 invents. `onAllGuardsOnDuty` counts guards with `training == false` and `wounded == false`. A trainee does not delay `raid.callup_ticks`.
6. **Workers indoors: activity `villageheart:shelter`.** Hired workers (not guards) lose `SetRaidStatus` from CORE. `WorkerBrain.activityFor(villager, level)` returns `SHELTER` while `VillageThreats.underAttack` and the worker is not away (`AwayTrips.isAway`). During `SLEEP`, a worker with a `HOME` stays in `REST`. A worker without a `HOME` gets `SHELTER` then too. `UpdateWorkerActivity` sits in the `SHELTER` package so the schedule returns when the threat ends. `PANIC` and `HIDE` still pre-empt and then fall back to `SHELTER`. Unhired villagers keep the vanilla brain. Guards keep `GuardBrain`.
7. **Shelter target** (`core.raid.ShelterPlanner.choose`): (1) the worker's `HOME` when it is a bed of a valid House (`Housing`), (2) else the nearest free-or-own House bed, (3) else the nearest valid room building's inside door cell, (4) else none, and the activity shows "No shelter". The glue walks with the existing `WalkTo`, arrives within `SHELTER_ARRIVE` (1.5), and re-walks past `SHELTER_DRIFT` (4).
8. **Death log in the village**, independent of the sim. `DeathLogSystem` on `AFTER_DEATH` and `MOB_CONVERSION`. Logged: every hired worker of the village, wherever it dies (`WorkerData` is still present at `AFTER_DEATH`), and every other villager inside the claim. `duringRaid` and `duringSiege` are separate booleans. A death and a conversion of the same villager within 1 tick are one entry, cause `zombified`. `MOB_CONVERSION` logs only a `Villager` becoming a `ZombieVillager` (`zombified`) or a `Witch` (`minecraft:lightning_bolt`). A cure logs nothing. The third callback argument (`keepEquipment`) is ignored. Last `DEATH_LOG_MAX` (32) entries saved; `nextSeq` and `inClaimLogged` are monotonic and saved.
9. **The raid alert is first.** `AlertCode.RAID` is inserted at index 0. `SIEGE` then `DEFENCE_DAMAGED` follow it, then the built-ins. `MAX_ALERTS` cannot hide the raid line.
10. **Siege damage is defence blocks only**, behind `villageheart:siege_damage` (default true), independent of `mobGriefing`. `SiegeDamage.hit` is the only writer. A defence block is a cobblestone or fence-gate cell of a recorded Wall Segment, Gate or Watchtower that still holds its material.
11. **Who is given a mod goal.** Zombies, husks and zombie villagers (babies too), and not drowned or zombified piglins: lure and breach. Creepers: seek and breach, and only while `siege_damage` is on and the creeper has no target. Ravagers and vindicators of the **tracked** raid: breach. Skeletons, strays, spiders, witches, pillagers, evokers, vexes, endermen, drowned, zombified piglins: vanilla, no lure, no wall damage.
12. **Rolls are pure.** `ThreatRoll.roll(worldSeed, day, kind)` is a SplitMix64 hash. The same world gets the same raid days. Mob AI after the start is not seeded.
13. **Loaded only.** A random raid or a siege starts only while a non-spectator player is within `THREAT_PLAYER_RANGE` (96) of the Heart and the Heart's chunk is entity-ticking. Any such player counts, member or not (orchestrator default). A won raid roll with nobody near is saved and postponed. A siege roll with nobody near is skipped.
14. **Moats protect walls** (orchestrator default). A mob damages a defence block only when it can touch that block (melee reach and a clear ray from the eye). A 1-wide trench, a ledge, or a player's own block skin in front of the wall takes no siege damage. A creeper breaches only from a free cell beside the block, on its own side of the ring; a trench that denies that cell denies the breach.
15. **No per-tier wall cap.** Not chosen by the owner. `MAX_DEFENCE_SITES` goes from 64 to 128 so a ring fits; that is a site cap, not a length cap.
16. **Early sieges are small.** Section 3.12. The Town raid scenario is not shrunk to match: it is the roadmap's Town, with iron in the chest and a golem.
17. **Rewards and M26.** A treasury reward is paid only for a victory over a random raid, at most one per `RAID_GAP_DAYS`. Bad Omen raids stay vanilla. M26's `full` scenario sets `"threats": true`. That is decided.

### 3.9 Defence blocks and hit points

A recorded site is expanded with `Placement.world` into cells. A defence cell is a palette entry of `minecraft:cobblestone` (`STONE`) or a `#minecraft:fence_gates` block (`GATE`). Planks, fences, ladders, lanterns and air are not defence cells. Counts from `DefenceBlueprints` **[verified by reading `wallText` / `gateText` / the watchtower text]**:

| Blueprint | Defence cells |
|---|---|
| `wall(n)` | `3n` STONE. `wall(32)` is 96. `wall(10)` is 30. |
| `gate(n)` | `3n - 6` STONE and 3 GATE. The opening is three columns, gate on layer 0, air on layer 1, cobblestone lintel on layer 2. `gate(5)` is **9 STONE + 3 GATE** (12 cells). `gate(32)` is 90 + 3. r2's "12 STONE" for a gate of 5 was wrong. |
| watchtower | 38 STONE. The ladder, planks and fence are not defence cells. |
| lamp post | 0 |

A cell counts only while it holds its material. A cell a player broke (`PlayerBlockBreakEvents.AFTER`) is *opened*: not repaired, not targeted, until the material is back. A cell the player rebuilt in another block is the player's.

**Hit points** (`core.siege.WallHp`): `HP_STONE` 12, `HP_GATE` **8**. Damage per hit (`SiegeHits.damage`):

| Attacker | STONE | GATE | Interval (ticks) | Hits to break |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| zombie, husk, zombie villager, baby the same | 1 | 2 | 20 | stone 12, gate **4** |
| vindicator | 2 | 4 | 20 | stone 6, gate **2** |
| ravager | 6 | 4 | 40 | stone 2, gate 2 |
| creeper breach | the target cell and the cell above or below it on the creeper's column take 16; other defence cells at centre distance `d ≤ r` take `round(8 × (1 − d/r))`, `r = 3` (6 when powered) | same | once | a 1-wide, 2-high hole, cracks around it |

`HP_GATE` 8 is the orchestrator default "(gate 8)": the per-hit numbers stay, so a vindicator's 4 damage breaks a gate in two hits. A stone cell still takes six vindicator hits. A gate cell is one block with air already above it, so those two hits open a gap a mob can walk through. A stone wall needs two broken cells (feet and head): one zombie, about 24 seconds; one ravager, about 8 seconds.

**Breaking.** A cell is breakable only when `SiteSupport.dependent` finds nothing outside the defence cells and the block above is not a `FallingBlock`. Otherwise damage stops at HP − 1. At HP a breakable cell is set to air with block updates, no drop, the crack is cleared, and `level.levelEvent(2001, pos, Block.getId(state))` plays the break effect **[verified: `Block.getId` is what 2001 carries]**. The cell is then *breached*.

**Cracks.** `destroyBlockProgress(crackId(pos), pos, stage)`. `crackId` is negative and injective inside the claim box: `−1 − (dx+128) − 256×(dz+128) − 65536×(y − minY)`, dx/dz from the Heart. Stage is `min(9, floor(10 × damage / hp))`, and −1 clears it. `SiegeCracks` re-sends every damaged cell within 32 blocks of a player every `CRACK_RESEND_TICKS` (200).

**Store.** `DefenceDamage`: a sparse map of cracked cells (damage 1..hp−1) and a set of breached cells. At most `MAX_DAMAGED_CELLS` (4096); past that the least-damaged crack is dropped, never a breach. The live store is mutable. It is copied into `ThreatState` every `DAMAGE_FLUSH_TICKS` (200) and on save. A breach writes through at once.

**Index.** `DefenceIndex`, rebuilt when `defences` changes. Built from `DefenceCells.of(site, blueprint)`.

**Slow scan.** Every 20 ticks, one tenth of the sites, loaded chunks only. Only a *seen* cell (the scan or the build has watched it hold its material) can become breached. A seen counting cell that is air and not opened becomes breached (a vanilla creeper, TNT, a piston). A breached cell holding its material again becomes intact. A breached cell holding some other block leaves the set.

### 3.10 Recording walls and gates

`Defences.recorded` becomes true for all four defence blueprint ids. `DefenceSpecs.of` is unchanged, so walls and gates still have no key cell and no patrol point. `DefenceSystem.refresh` therefore never drops a wall for a missing block. For a watchtower, a key cell that is in the breached set counts as present, so a breach does not unregister the tower. `MAX_DEFENCE_SITES` becomes 128. At the cap a finished wall is not recorded and the existing log line says so.

### 3.11 Mob goals

Installed from `ServerEntityEvents.ENTITY_LOAD` for a `Zombie` (not `Drowned`, not `ZombifiedPiglin`), `Creeper`, `Ravager` or `Vindicator`, once. An accessor mixin exposes `Mob.goalSelector` (`protected final`). Classes and packages are the ones in 2.1.

**Lure on** (`core.siege.Lure.active`): at least `LURE_MIN_WALL_CELLS` (150) *seen* wall or gate cells, difficulty not peaceful, and day time in `[13000, 23000)` or a siege is running. `LureBudget` admits at most `MAX_LURED` (24), siege zombies first. Slots free on unload, removal, dimension change, and when `isRemoved` is seen at the next admit.

**`VillageLureGoal`** on zombies, priority 5 (under `ZombieAttackGoal` 3 and `SpearUseGoal` 2, over `MoveThroughVillageGoal` 6). `canUse` every 20 ticks: lure on, within `LURE_RADIUS` (112) of the Heart, no target, a budget slot. With no target, `SpearUseGoal` cannot run, so priority 2 does not block the lure. Goal point: the nearest census villager when the zombie is within `CLAIM_RADIUS + 16`, with no line-of-sight test, else the Heart. Paths are to the next waypoint (≤ 24), via `DefaultRandomPos.getPosTowards`. A partial path that ends on a defence cell hands off to the breach goal. A partial path that ends on a player's house waits and tries another waypoint. The house is not attacked.

**`BreachGoal`.** Zombie priority 4, under `ZombieAttackGoal`. Ravager and vindicator priority 2, above `PathfindToRaidGoal` 3. `VindicatorBreakDoorGoal` is also priority 2 and only `canUse`s on a wooden door, so at a cobblestone wall or a fence gate the breach goal is the one that can run. T-L2-18 asserts it is the running goal at the gate. The pure pick is `BreachChoice.pick`: a counting cell the mob can touch, on the side toward its goal, in the columns its box needs (a ravager is 2 wide and 3 high). Ties prefer GATE, then lower y. Paths take a token from `PathBudget` (`MAX_SIEGE_PATHS_PER_TICK` 2). Then look, swing, and `SiegeDamage.hit` on the interval. Raiders only while they belong to the tracked raid. A ridden ravager keeps its goals (`updateControlFlags`, 2.1). A mob that cannot touch a defence cell does no damage. That is the moat rule.

**`CreeperBreachGoal`.** Priority 4, and only while `target == null`, `siege_damage` is on, and the creeper is farther from the Heart than the cell (a creeper inside the ring does not blow the wall outward). It walks to the nearest counting STONE or GATE cell of a wall or gate within `CREEPER_SEEK_RADIUS` (24), to the free cell beside it. Within 2 blocks it `ignite()`s and tags `villageheart.breacher`. Guards do not target creepers (`GuardTargets`), so a guard on patrol does not stop this. Golems might. The test with a guard present asserts the creeper still ignites and the guard never holds it as a target.

**The breach is not a vanilla crater.** A mixin on `Creeper.explodeCreeper` turns `ExplosionInteraction.MOB` into `NONE` for a breacher (`@ModifyArg` on the `explode` call, whose owner is `ServerLevel` **[verified the call]**). An `@Inject` after it calls `SiegeDamage.blast`. Entities still take damage. Blocks other than defence cells do not. A creeper who is chasing a player keeps the vanilla explosion and `mobGriefing`.

### 3.12 Zombie sieges

Vanilla `VillageSiege` is skipped for a player inside the claim, and `findRandomSpawnPos` returns null for a position inside any claim, so a player just outside cannot pull vanilla zombies in through the walls. With `villageheart:sieges` false the suppression stays: no siege in the claim at all. A Hamlet claim gets no mod siege either (orchestrator default, and `SIEGE_MIN_TIER` is `VILLAGE`). The mod's `ZombieSiege` runs instead, for Village tier and above.

Days are `gameTime / 24000`. Each roll kind stores `lastRolledDay`. A roll fires once when day time *crosses* its time, so a sleep or a `/time set` does not roll twice. A counter saved in the future is reset. A siege that was rolled and never spawned is cleared at dawn.

The roll is after day time 13000, only when a player is near: `p = min(0.20, 0.04 + 0.02 × T)`. No siege in the first `SIEGE_GRACE_DAYS` (5) days after the claim, at least `SIEGE_GAP_DAYS` (3) between sieges, tier at least VILLAGE, `villageheart:sieges` on, not peaceful, `doMobSpawning` on, no raid tracked. The sim harness sets `sieges` false unless the scenario says `"threats": true`.

**Size** (`SiegeSize.zombies(T, qualifiedGuards)`):

- `raw = clamp(floor(4 + 2 × T), 4, 20)`.
- When `qualifiedGuards >= 4`, the result is `raw`.
- When fewer are qualified, the result is `min(raw, max(2, qualifiedGuards + 1))`.

Qualified means hired, not in training, not wounded, at the roll. Two guards face 3 zombies even at a high T. Zero guards face 2. Four guards at T = 0 face 4; at T = 3 they face 10; at T = 10 they face 20. A guard hired that morning is qualified at dusk (`GUARD_TRAINING_END` 12000), and the roll is after 13000, so the first night counts them. One to three guards are already on the night shift at midnight, so the siege finds them on duty; the raid flag still holds them if one is off, and it holds a day guard when there are four or more.

This is the hard start the owner asked for. Three zombies against two wooden swords is the fight `defended` already runs, which the owner left as an M26 target. M25 does not add a "nobody died" assertion on that fight. It does assert the count.

Spawn at day time 18000, outside the defences, at `max(outermost defence, 32)` plus 12, clamped to `[32, CLAIM_RADIUS + 16]`, light ignored, `setPersistenceRequired`, tag `villageheart.siege`. The siege runs until day time 23000 or until its zombies are dead. `sieges` off or peaceful ends it. While it runs, `VillageThreats.underAttack` is true and the SIEGE alert is up ("Zombie siege! %s zombies left").

### 3.13 Random raids

`ThreatScheduler` (`VillageSystem` `"threats"`), rules in `core.siege.ThreatRules`.

Roll once per day at day time 1000: `p = min(0.25, 0.02 + 0.03 × T)`. Conditions: `minecraft:raids` and `villageheart:random_raids` on, not peaceful, tier at least VILLAGE, `RAID_GRACE_DAYS` (7) since the claim, `RAID_GAP_DAYS` (5) since the last raid of any origin ended, nothing tracked, nothing pending. A Hamlet rolls nothing.

A won roll stores `PendingRaid(rolledDay, startTick)` with `startTick` in `[2000, 11000]` of that day. It starts at the first tick at or after `startTick` (on a later day, still inside that window) where a non-spectator player has been within 96 blocks for `RAID_LOADED_TICKS` (600) in a row and the 16-point wave ring is entity-ticking. Any nearby player counts. The warning chat goes out when the 600-tick count begins. Nobody near: it waits, and the Overview shows "A raid is coming". A Bad Omen raid on the village cancels the pending one. Peaceful, or `raids` / `random_raids` off, at the start moment: it stays pending, no chat.

The start calls `createOrExtendRaid(nearestNonSpectator, heart)`, then `setRaidOmenLevel(omen)`, then `setDirty()`. The player gets no Raid Omen, so `absorbRaidOmen` returns false and changes nothing; `setRaidOmenLevel` is what sets the strength. Null (spectator, rule off, or `CAN_START_RAID` false) drops the pending raid with a WARN. An empty POI list does **not** return null: the centre is the Heart, and the action checks that centre is in the claim.

### 3.14 Threat, scaling, rewards

`Threat.score(adults, wealth, defenceCells, tier) = adults/10 + wealth/100 + defenceCells/200 + tierBonus` (HAMLET 0, VILLAGE 0.5, TOWN 1). Wealth is treasury emeralds plus stockpile value from `Stockpiles.totalsById`. `ItemValue`: emerald 1, diamond 2, iron, copper or gold ingot 0.25, any other item 1/64. All tunable.

`RaidScale.of(T)` → omen `clamp(1 + floor(T / 3), 1, 5)`, extra vindicators `min(4, floor(T / 2))` from wave 2, extra ravagers `min(2, floor(T / 4))` from wave 3. Extras are for `RANDOM` origin only. They are spawned with `raid.joinRaid(..., false)` when `getGroupsSpawned()` rises, tagged `villageheart.extra`, at the wave's first raider or on the ring when that spot is inside the walls. The plan, `extrasForWave` and `extraRaidersKilled` are fixed on the record so a reload does not double them. A young village at T under 2 gets omen 1 and no extras: a vanilla Normal raid, which is already the bottom of this scale. M25 does not shrink vanilla waves. That raid can overwhelm two wooden-sword guards; see the open question in section 6.

Reward, random victories only, at most one per `RAID_GAP_DAYS` (`lastRewardDay`): `min(40, 4 + 2 × wavesSpawned + 3 × (omen − 1) + extraRaidersKilled)` emeralds via `Treasury.deposit(server, n, null)`. Chat to Resident and above. A siege pays nothing.

### 3.15 Upkeep

`AlertCode.DEFENCE_DAMAGED`: WARNING "Walls damaged: %s broken, %s cracked", or DANGER "Wall breached! %s holes" when a breach exists during a raid or a siege. Chat on the first breach of a night, at most once per `BREACH_NOTICE_TICKS` (1200), and "The walls of %s are repaired" when the last breach closes.

`RepairPlanner.next`: breached GATE, then breached STONE from the bottom, then a crack at or above half HP, then a lighter crack. Inside a class, nearest the Heart. A breach costs one item of its material (cobblestone, or any `#minecraft:fence_gates`). A heavy crack costs one cobblestone. A light crack is free. The builder prefers repairs to new builds and repairs only in `WORK`, and never while `underAttack` (it is sheltering). The reservation uses one fixed repair UUID. Placement goes through `BuildExecutor.place` with the blueprint's state, so a gate comes back closed and facing the way the blueprint says. Short of material, the status says "Repairs need N more cobblestone" and the breach stays open. No builder: the alert says so. Repairs grant `WorkUnit.BLOCK_PLACED` XP like any placement.

### 3.16 Guards during a raid or a siege

`VillageThreats.underAttack` is true when a raid is tracked or a siege is running. That is the flag workers shelter on, and it is what `RaidCheck`'s source reports (together with the one-tick vanilla discovery in 3.2 #3).

| Situation | Raid flag | Alarm | Who comes |
|---|---|---|---|

A dead guard is not replaced at night (`HireRefusal.GUARD_AT_NIGHT`). The next morning's hire trains until dusk, so it misses a raid that is still running through that day. M25 does not add a night hire.

Food during a long fight: `feed` will not refill and will not bite while any enemy is within 10 blocks. The retreat bite can heal behind an ally. The carried ration is at most 12 HP of healing. A raid that never gives a 10-block lull, and never lets a guard stand within 16 blocks of the Barracks while clear, does not refill. The `raid` scenario's bread is what refills between waves. An empty food stockpile means no healing at all. M25 does not loosen `GuardSystem.feed`.

Creepers: a guard on the wall does not acquire them. The breach test covers a guard standing there. Iron golems are the creeper answer, and M19 is not built yet, so the Town scenario's one golem is summoned.

### 3.17 Gamerules and the harness

Gamerules, category MOBS, default true: `villageheart:siege_damage`, `villageheart:random_raids`, `villageheart:sieges`. Registered with `GameRuleBuilder.forBoolean(true).buildAndRegister(Identifier)`.

`sim/scenarios/siege.json` (4 days, reported, not a roadmap gate): the `raid` layout without the raid action, a `siege` action on day 2 with 8 zombies (the action passes the count; it does not re-roll), natural spawning on for nights 1-3. Asserted: claim held, one siege, `defence.breached == 0` on the last day, `sum(siege.villager_deaths) <= 2`, `lure.max_lured <= 24`, `village_ms.mean < 1`. The early-size rule is not what this scenario tests; T-L1-21 and T-L2-34b test it.

## 6. Risks, out of scope, open questions

**Risks**

1. **The Town raid on Normal.** Five waves, pillagers over the walls, vindicators through a gate in two hits, ravagers through stone in two hits, an evoker in the last wave. Guards have no ranged attack and do not chase vexes. Three of the four guards are asleep at tick 2000 and must wake. The kit is iron and level 2 because the village has it; that is what makes a Town different from the two wooden swords of a young Village. If 3 of 3 fail, report deaths per wave and do not loosen the 75% rule or drop the difficulty. Options for the orchestrator: level 3, a second golem. Not Easy, and not a quieter assertion.
2. **A raider stuck outside the claim** can hold the raid until the 2-day stop, which 3.6 counts as a failure. The summary lists the last raiders' positions. Option, not the default: on-duty guards may chase the tracked raid's raiders 16 blocks past the claim edge.
3. **Rations run out inside a wave.** Cap 12, no refill while an enemy is within 10 blocks. A raid with no lull leaves level-1 guards unhealed. The Town guards are level 2 with iron, and bread is in Storage for the lulls. T-L2-44 stops a "helpful" change to `feed`.
4. **Creepers and guards.** Guards ignore creepers. With one golem, some creepers reach the wall. The repair assertion is what makes that survivable. If the ring is gone faster than the builder replaces it, that shows up as `defence.breached_noon` and is reported.
5. **Merge.** Rebase onto `dev` before step 5. Conflicts are expected in `WorkerBrain`, `VillageCodecs` (`RAW_B`), `HeartFacts`, `ActivityKind` (append only), and `SimActions.ALL`. M19 or M23 may land during the build; A2 and A9 say what to do.
6. **Pathing cost** sits in entity ticks, outside the 1 ms village budget. Cap 24 lured mobs and 2 paths a tick. `siege.paths` and `entity_ms` are reported for M26.
7. **`defended` is not a wall test.** Treating `wall_ring` as a defence would change an M18 scenario. Don't.

**Out of scope.** Changing vanilla wave tables or the bonus-wave rule. A player command to start a raid. Wall-length caps. Hit points on planks, fences, ladders, lanterns, or a hand-built wall. Lure for skeletons or any mob not in 3.2 #11. Teaching guards to attack creepers or to shoot. Guards repairing walls. The builder upgrading a wall to a stronger block. Night hiring of a replacement guard. Archers. A death-log tab. Celebrations for sheltered workers.

**Open questions**

1. A young Village (two wooden-sword guards, T under 2) can still roll a **vanilla Normal raid**: omen 1, no extras, five waves, after the 7-day grace. The owner asked to scale early *sieges*, and the raid scale is already at its minimum, so this plan does not shrink vanilla waves. That raid can wipe those two guards. If the owner wants the first random raid softened as well, that is a follow-up; it is not in the decisions that were written down.
2. M19's golem classes do not exist on `dev` yet. The scenario summons the golem. If the raid scenario should wait for a smith-built golem, step 18 changes when M19 merges.
