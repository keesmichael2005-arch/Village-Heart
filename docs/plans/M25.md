## Changes in r3

What changed from r2, and why (r3.1 keeps all of these except where 7.1 says otherwise):

1. **M18 is built, not a plan.** r2's A1 and A14 described `origin/m18-plan` `5356c21`, which still said gate opening was out of scope. On
   `dev` the guards, the alarm, the training day, the night-heavy shifts and the fence-gate opening are code. Section 2.2 replaces those
   assumptions with the signatures that exist.
2. **Call-up is two channels, and trainees stay off.** `Guards.phaseOf` reads `RaidCheck.active` (the `raid` flag) and `GuardAlarm.active`
   (the `alarm` flag) and `GuardData.training`. r2 set one `RaidCheck` and excused only the wounded. The alarm holds only 600 ticks, so
   the `raid` flag must hold duty for a whole raid or siege. Section 3.16. (r3.1: M25 does **not** raise the alarm itself, 7.1 #2.)
3. **Shifts are night-heavy.** `ShiftBalancer.dayCount(n) = n/4`. Four guards are 1 day and 3 night, not 2 and 2. The morning raid wakes
   three sleepers. T-L2-1 matches that.
4. **The start is hard, on purpose.** Level 1 is 20 HP, a wooden sword and no armour (`Balance.GUARD_LEVEL_HEALTH = {20, 30, 40}`). Healing
   is rations from Storage, and only when no hostile is within 10 blocks (the retreat bite is the exception). Early sieges are capped so a
   young Village with two qualified guards faces 3 zombies, not `4 + 2T`. Hamlets get no siege. (r3.1: early random raids are capped too,
   3.13a.) The Town `raid` scenario still arms its guards, because that village has the gear.
5. **Orchestrator defaults for r2 §7.3 are decisions** (`docs/LOOP_STATE.md` `decisions_by_human`, 2026-11-09). Moats stay protective.
   `HP_GATE` is **8** (a vindicator needs 2 hits). No sieges for Hamlets. Any nearby non-spectator can start a postponed raid. Rewards only
   for random raids. M26 `full` runs with raids and sieges on.
6. **API corrections.** `createOrExtendRaid` still creates a raid when no village POI is occupied (the centre is the passed position).
   `BuildExecutor` lives in `dev.villageheart.build`. `advance` takes a job id. A gate of length 5 is 9 cobblestone and 3 fence gates, not
   12 and 3. `defended` has no recorded walls, so the lure does not touch it. Zombies gained `SpearUseGoal` at priority 2; it only runs
   with a target and a kinetic weapon, so a wall-blind zombie still breaches. (r3 also "corrected" `MOB_CONVERSION`'s third argument to a
   boolean; that was wrong and r3.1 reverts it, 7.1 #13.)
7. **Save shape.** `VillageState` already has 17 components and `VillageCodecs` is already `Codec.mapPair(RAW_A, RAW_B)`. M25 appends two
   keys to `RAW_B`.

## 1. Goal

When a raid starts on the claimed village, the Heart screen shows a red raid alert ("Raid! Wave 2/5, 4 raiders left"), online members of
Resident rank and above get a chat line, every guard who is not wounded and not in training comes on duty within 10 seconds (asleep or
not), hired workers stop work and go indoors, and golems fight as in vanilla. Every villager death is written to a death log (who, job,
cause, day, during a raid or a siege), shown on the Overview tab and in the simulation metrics.

Walls do not make a village immune. Wall and gate blocks of a recorded defence have hit points and the vanilla crack overlay. Gates are
the weak point (`HP_GATE` 8: two vindicator hits, four zombie hits). Zombies are lured toward a walled village at night and during a siege
and hit the defence block in their way. Creepers walk to a wall and blow a breach in it. Ravagers break a wall block in two hits. The
builder repairs the damage with cobblestone and fence gates from Storage. Raids also start on their own, stronger where the village is
larger, richer or better walled, and a victory over one of those raids pays the treasury. A Hamlet is not sieged and not randomly raided.
A young Village with two wooden-sword guards gets a small siege and a small random raid: threatened, and not handed the late-game numbers.
Skeletons and every other hostile stay vanilla. There is no per-tier wall-length cap.

### 2.1 Vanilla raids and sieges in 1.21.11 **[verified: sources]**

- **Start.** `BadOmenMobEffect.applyEffectTick` gives a non-spectator player `RAID_OMEN` for 600 ticks and `setRaidOmenPosition`. With 1
  tick left, `RaidOmenMobEffect.applyEffectTick` calls `level.getRaids().createOrExtendRaid(player, omenPos)`.
- **`Raids.createOrExtendRaid(ServerPlayer, BlockPos)`** (Raids.java:109) returns null when the player is a spectator, `GameRules.RAIDS` is
  off, or `EnvironmentAttributes.CAN_START_RAID` is false at that position (Java default `true`). The centre is the mean of occupied
  `#village` POIs within 64 blocks, **or the passed position when that list is empty**. Then `getOrCreateRaid(level, centre)` **reuses
  `getRaidAt(centre)`** when one exists: an active raid within 96 blocks of the centre is *extended*, not replaced. `absorbRaidOmen` adds
  amplifier + 1, clamped, and returns false when the player has no Raid Omen (no stat, no criterion).
- **Early stop.** A raid whose centre is not `isVillage` is stopped before its first wave (`groupsSpawned == 0`; after a wave it is a
  LOSS). The mod's held POI tickets keep the claim's sections "occupied", but a young village's Heart may stand away from them.
- **`/raid`** is a debug command (`DEBUG_DEV_COMMANDS`). Tests use `RaidStart`, which copies `createOrExtendRaid` + `setRaidOmenLevel` +
  `setDirty`.
- **`Raid` public:** `isOver`, `isVictory`, `isLoss`, `isStopped`, `isStarted`, `isActive`, `isBetweenWaves`, `hasFirstWaveSpawned`,
  `getGroupsSpawned`, `getTotalRaidersAlive`, `getAllRaiders`, `getCenter`, `getRaidOmenLevel`, `setRaidOmenLevel`,
  `getNumGroups(Difficulty)` (a computation: EASY 3, NORMAL 5, HARD 7, not the field), `stop`, `joinRaid(level, wave, raider, pos,
  alreadySpawned)`. The field `numGroups` is `private final`, set in the constructor from the difficulty, and **saved** as `group_count`
  in `Raid.MAP_CODEC`. `hasBonusWave` is private and is `raidOmenLevel > 1`; `hasMoreWaves` and `isFinalWave` read `numGroups`. Private
  fields the gametest accessor needs: `raidCooldownTicks`, `groupsSpawned`, `started` **[verified: all three exist]**.
- **Timing.** A new raid has `raidCooldownTicks = 300`: the first wave spawns 15 s after creation; 300 ticks between waves; after the
  last wave is dead, 40 post-raid ticks then `VICTORY`, then 600 celebration ticks, then `stop()`. A raid stops after 48000 active ticks
  (2 days). LOSS only when the centre stops being a village after a wave spawned.
- **Waves (Normal)** from `RaiderType`: wave 1 is 4 pillagers, wave 2 is 3 pillagers and 2 vindicators, wave 3 is 3 pillagers and a
  ravager, wave 4 is 4 pillagers, a vindicator and 3 witches, wave 5 is 4 pillagers, 4 vindicators, an evoker and a ravager ridden by a
  pillager. Normal adds 0-1 extra pillager and 0-1 extra vindicator per wave, and 0-1 witch in waves 3 and 5 (`getPotentialBonusSpawns`). Wave spawn position:
  `findRandomSpawnPos` on `serverLevel.random` (not seeded), 41-98 blocks from the centre while the cooldown counts down, in
  entity-ticking chunks; the fallback at cooldown 0 is within about 8 blocks of the centre.
- **`joinRaid`** calls `finalizeSpawn(..., EVENT)`, `applyRaidBuffs` and `addFreshEntityWithPassengers` when `alreadySpawned` is false.
  The mod must not finalize again.
- **`Raids.get(id)`, `getId(Raid)`, `getNearbyRaid(pos, distSqr)`.** `ServerLevel.getRaidAt(pos)` is `getNearbyRaid(pos, 9216)` and keeps
  only an **active** raid whose centre is within 96 blocks. `Raids.tick` calls `stop()` when `RAIDS` is off and **removes a stopped raid

### 2.2 Guards as built (M18) **[verified: origin/dev]**

This is the contract M25 plugs into. M25 does not change shift counts, wound thresholds, ration rules, gate rules or which mobs a guard
will fight.

- **Shifts.** `ShiftBalancer.dayCount(n) = n / 4`. One to three guards are all on nights. Four to seven: 1 on days. Eight to eleven: 2 on
  days. Seniors (level, then XP) take nights; ties keep the current side, then hiring order. **Every guard counts, trainees too**, and a
  pinned guard counts toward its side. `GuardSchedule` day shift: duty `[0, 12000)`, off `[12000, 13000)`, sleep `[13000, 24000)`. Night
  shift: off `[0, 1000)`, sleep `[1000, 12000)`, duty `[12000, 24000)`.
- **Phase.** The overload the brain uses is `GuardSchedule.phase(shift, dayTime, raid, alarm, wounded, canWork, training)`. Order: a
  trainee who is not wounded is `OFF` (awake, never on duty, not called by a raid or the alarm); a wounded guard sleeps, even in a raid;
  else a raid or the alarm forces `DUTY`; else the clock; a guard who cannot work (`WorkGate.mayWork` false) has `DUTY` turned into
  `OFF`. A strike does not keep a guard home once the raid or alarm flag is set.
- **Who sets the flags.** `Guards.phaseOf` passes `RaidCheck.active(level, village)` and `GuardAlarm.active(level)` and
  `GuardData.training(dayTime)`. It runs for **every guard on every brain update**, so the `RaidCheck` answer must be cheap.
- **`RaidCheck`.** `setSource(Source)` replaces the whole answer and **returns the previous source** (`Source` is `boolean
  underRaid(ServerLevel, VillageState)`). The default `vanilla` is `private`; `null` restores it. It uses `getRaidAt(heart)` through the
  `ViewSource` and counts the raid when it is active and the centre or a raider is in the claim. `setViewSourceForTest` feeds a fake raid
  to that default. M18's `raidCallsUpAllGuards` (`GuardGameTests`) uses the default with a fake far raid (no wake), then a real raid
  (wake within 200 ticks), then `raid.stop()` and **expects `REST` within 200 ticks**. It must stay green.
- **`GuardAlarm`.** Static. `raise(level, pos)` holds for `GUARD_ALARM_HOLD_TICKS` (600) and sets a spot. `refresh` (every 20 ticks)
  extends it while a hostile that has a villager as its target is within 24 blocks of a loaded guard. Raised by `AFTER_DAMAGE` when a
  hostile hurts a villager in the claim and when a guard dies (`ALLOW_DEATH`). **While the alarm has a spot, an on-duty guard walks to the
  spot instead of patrolling** (`GuardRuntime.patrol`). `resetForTests` / `reset` clear it. Not saved.
- **Training.** `GuardTraining.qualifiesAt` is dusk (`GUARD_TRAINING_END` 12000) of the hire day. `mayHire` is false from day time 12000.
  `GuardTestSupport.hireGuard(h, pos, Optional<Shift> pin)` clears `trainingUntil` so the guard is already qualified; `hireTrainee(h, pos,
  pin)` leaves the training day. Both set `Workers.nightGuardHireForTests` around the hire.
- **Health and gear.** `GuardPerks.maxHealth` reads `{20, 30, 40}`; bonus damage `{0, 1, 2}`; base damage 1. The class javadoc still says
  30/40/50; the array is the truth. A new guard gets a wooden sword and no armour (`Guards.onHired`). Better gear is fetched from the
  Barracks chest and the stockpile. A guard without a Barracks bed is never marked wounded (`GuardSystem`).
- **Food.** `GuardRation` cap 12, refill below 6, one point heals 1 HP, a bite every `GUARD_EAT_TICKS` 40. `GuardSystem.feed` returns
  when any `Enemy` is within `GUARD_EAT_SAFE_DISTANCE` (10). Refill (up to 4 items, remote `Stockpiles.withdrawFirst`, 200-tick back-off
  on a miss) runs only when the guard is not in `GuardActivities.DUTY` or is within 16 blocks of its Barracks marker. `GuardCombat.fight`,
  while retreating, calls `GuardRations.bite` within 5 blocks of a healthy ally; that bite does not use the 10-block check.
- **Wounds and retreat.** `WoundRule`: wounded below 30%, cleared at 80%. Retreat: below 50%, fall back to an ally at 70% or better, fight
  again at 80%. With no ally the guard stays in the fight.
- **Targets.** `GuardTargets.Kind.CREEPER` and `UNREACHABLE` are never chosen. Flying mobs are unreachable. A target must be visible
  unless it hurt the guard, and on duty it must be in the claim. A guard inside a closed 3-high wall does not see a zombie chewing the
  outside of it.
- **Gates.** `core.defence.GateRules.mayOpen(inClaim, powered, hostileNear, engagedGuard)`. `Balance.GATE_HOSTILE_RANGE` 8. `GatePaths`,

### 3.2 Key decisions

1. **One raid truth.** `VillageRaids` (glue) over `core.raid.RaidTracker` (pure). Installed with `RaidCheck.setSource`. No other consumer
   calls `getRaidAt` for "are we under attack", except the unhired vanilla brain, which M25 leaves alone.
2. **What counts.** Discovery uses `getRaidAt(heart)` only while nothing is tracked. A raid is relevant when it is not over and not
   stopped, and the centre is in the claim or a raider is (`RaidFacts.from`, pure). Once an id is tracked, facts come from `Raids.get(id)`
   until that raid is over, stopped or gone; it stays the village's raid even when all its raiders leave the claim, so the alert does not
   flap. `getRaidAt` would swap in a nearer raid or miss ours. A second raid nearby is ignored while one is tracked. Because `Raids.tick`
   removes a stopped raid in the same tick, the tracked id's status is read **every tick** (one map lookup); waves, raiders and guards are
   read every `RAID_CHECK_TICKS` (20). The system runs at the end of the server tick, after vanilla `stop()`, so it sees `isStopped`
   before the next `Raids.tick` removes the raid. A raid is relevant before its first wave: the 10 s call-up is shorter than the 15 s
   cooldown.
3. **The guard source.** At mod init: `Source previous = RaidCheck.setSource(VillageThreats::forGuards)`. `forGuards(level, village)` is
   true when a siege is running, or a raid is tracked, or (nothing tracked yet) `previous.underRaid(level, village)`. The first two are a
   cached boolean (O(1) per guard per brain tick). The last clause keeps M18's `raidCallsUpAllGuards` green on the tick the raid is
   created, before `VillageRaids` has latched it, and it keeps `setViewSourceForTest` meaningful. After the latch it is not consulted.
4. **No explicit alarm.** M25 does **not** call `GuardAlarm.raise` when a raid or siege starts. The `raid` flag is the call-up and holds
   duty for the whole threat. Raising the alarm would (a) keep every guard on duty 600 ticks after a raid is stopped, which breaks M18's
   `raidCallsUpAllGuards` ("REST within 200 ticks of `stop()`") and T-L2-2, and (b) send every on-duty guard to stand at the alarm spot
   (the Heart) for 30 s instead of patrolling. The alarm still rises by itself, as built, when a raider or zombie hurts a villager or a
   guard dies, and then gathers the guards at that spot, which is the useful case. T-L2-1 clears the alarm and asserts duty holds.
5. **Trainees and the wounded are not called.** That is `GuardSchedule`, not a second filter. `onAllGuardsOnDuty` counts **loaded**
   guards in the claim with `training == false` and `wounded == false` at that moment. A trainee does not delay `raid.callup_ticks`; an
   unloaded guard is reported (`raid.guards_unloaded_at_start`), not waited for.
6. **Workers indoors: activity `villageheart:shelter`.** Hired workers (not guards) lose `SetRaidStatus` from CORE. `WorkerBrain
   .activityFor(villager, level)` returns `SHELTER` while `VillageThreats.underAttack` and the worker is not away (`AwayTrips.isAway`).
   During `SLEEP`, a worker with a `HOME` stays in `REST` (already indoors); a worker without a `HOME` gets `SHELTER`. A worker asleep in
   its bed when SLEEP turns into WAKE during a raid is woken by vanilla `WakeUp` and is already at its target: accepted.
   `UpdateWorkerActivity` sits in the `SHELTER` package so the schedule returns when the threat ends. `PANIC` and `HIDE` still pre-empt
   and end in IDLE, and `UpdateWorkerActivity` then puts the worker back in `SHELTER`. The vanilla `PRE_RAID`/`RAID`/`HIDE` packages stay
   registered (harmless). Unhired villagers keep the vanilla brain. Guards keep `GuardBrain`.
7. **Shelter target** (`core.raid.ShelterPlanner.choose`): (1) the worker's `HOME` when it is a bed of a valid House (`Housing`), (2) else
   the nearest free-or-own House bed, (3) else the nearest valid room building's inside door cell, (4) else none, and the activity shows
   "No shelter". Ties by squared distance, then (x, z, y). The glue walks with `WalkTo` (opens doors), arrives within `SHELTER_ARRIVE`
   (1.5), stands looking at the door, re-walks past `SHELTER_DRIFT` (4). An `UNREACHABLE` walk tries the next target in planner order,
   never hangs.
8. **Death log in the village**, independent of the sim. `DeathLogSystem` on `AFTER_DEATH` and `MOB_CONVERSION`. Logged: every hired
   worker of the village, wherever it dies (`WorkerData` is still present at `AFTER_DEATH`), and every other villager inside the claim.
   `duringRaid` and `duringSiege` are separate booleans. A death and a conversion of the same villager within 1 tick are one entry, cause
   `zombified`. `MOB_CONVERSION` logs only a `Villager` becoming a `ZombieVillager` (`zombified`) or a `Witch`
   (`minecraft:lightning_bolt`). A cure logs nothing. The `ConversionParams` argument is ignored. The sim collector today counts a witch
   as zombified; it is aligned to the shared rule (section 8 note). Last `DEATH_LOG_MAX` (32) entries saved; `nextSeq` and
   `inClaimLogged` are monotonic and saved, so a metric never depends on the capped list.
9. **The raid alert is first.** `AlertCode.RAID` is inserted at index 0. `SIEGE` then `DEFENCE_DAMAGED` follow it, then the built-ins.
   `MAX_ALERTS` cannot hide the raid line.
10. **Siege damage is defence blocks only**, behind `villageheart:siege_damage` (default true), independent of `mobGriefing` (servers turn
    `mobGriefing` off to stop craters and would otherwise lose the upkeep loop by accident). `SiegeDamage.hit` is the only writer. A
    defence block is a cobblestone or fence-gate cell of a recorded Wall Segment, Gate or Watchtower that still holds its material.
11. **Who is given a mod goal.** Zombies, husks and zombie villagers (babies too), not drowned (their water AI fights a lure) or zombified
    piglins (neutral): lure and breach. Creepers: seek and breach, only while the **lure is on** (3.11), `siege_damage` is on, the
    creeper has no target and holds a lure slot. Ravagers and vindicators of the **tracked** raid: breach. Skeletons, strays, spiders,
    witches, pillagers (they keep shooting over walls), evokers, vexes, endermen, drowned, zombified piglins: vanilla, no lure, no wall
    damage.
12. **Rolls are pure.** `ThreatRoll.roll(worldSeed, day, kind)` is a SplitMix64 hash, no shared `RandomSource`. The same world gets the
    same raid days; a reload does not re-roll. Mob AI after the start is not seeded.
13. **Loaded only.** A random raid or a siege starts only while a non-spectator player is within `THREAT_PLAYER_RANGE` (96) of the Heart
    and the Heart's chunk is entity-ticking. Any such player counts, member or not (orchestrator default). A won raid roll with nobody
    near is saved and postponed. A siege roll with nobody near is skipped.
14. **Moats protect walls** (orchestrator default). A mob damages a defence block only when it can touch that block (melee reach and a
    clear ray from the eye). A 1-wide trench, a ledge, or a player's own block skin in front of the wall takes no siege damage. A creeper
    breaches only from a free cell beside the block, on its own side of the ring.
15. **No per-tier wall cap.** `MAX_DEFENCE_SITES` goes from 64 to 128 so a ring fits; that is a site cap, not a length cap.
16. **Early threats are small.** Sieges: 3.12. Random raids: 3.13a (r3.1, orchestrator default). The Town `raid` scenario is not shrunk:
    it is the roadmap's Town, with iron in the chest and a golem, and its raid is a TEST raid.
17. **Rewards and M26.** A treasury reward is paid only for a victory over a random raid, at most one per `RAID_GAP_DAYS`. Bad Omen raids
    stay vanilla. M26's `full` scenario sets `"threats": true`.
18. **Raid identity survives a reload.** The tracked raid is stored as its vanilla id (`Raids.getId`) in `threats.raid` with its start
    data. On load `Raids.get(id)` re-finds it; a missing raid closes the record with `GONE`. Notices fire only on tracker transitions, so a
    reload sends no second start line.
19. **Deterministic test raids.** `RaidStart.start(ServerPlayer p, BlockPos at, int omen)` (gametest source set, used by the sim and the
    GameTests): asserts `GameRules.RAIDS` on and difficulty not PEACEFUL (else throws with the reason), asserts `getRaidAt(at)` is null
    (no raid to extend), asserts `level.isVillage(at)`, then `createOrExtendRaid(p, at)`, `setRaidOmenLevel(omen)`, `setDirty()`; null
    throws. In the sim `p` is the creative `SimOwner`; in GameTests a survival test player. `RaidStart.holdWaves(raid)` resets
    `raidCooldownTicks` to 300 every 20 ticks through `RaidTestAccess`, so a test gets no wave it did not ask for; `stopAll(level, pos,
    r)` stops every raid within r and discards its raiders.

### 3.6 What "the village survives" means

All of: the claim exists on every day; the raid has ended by the last day with outcome `VICTORY`; `raid.villager_deaths ≤ 0.25 ×
raid.villagers_at_start`; and `raid.villagers_at_end ≥ 0.75 × raid.villagers_at_start`. Adults only, so babies neither inflate nor dilute
it. Zombified counts as a death. A villager who vanished or left the claim does not count as alive. Victory is stricter than vanilla's
loss rule, which the mod's held POI tickets make rare.

Vanilla wave contents and timing of a Bad Omen raid, the omen bonus wave, the bell, the boss bar, Hero of the Village, and vanilla victory
and loss. The unhired brain. M18's shift math, wounds, rations, targets (creepers stay ignored), patrol points, the alarm and gate rules.
M19's golem rules. M21's placement rules and the blueprints' shapes. Recording *which* blueprints become sites does change (3.10). A
random raid's wave count is capped for a young village (3.13a); that is the mod's own raid.

### 3.9 Defence blocks and hit points

A recorded site is expanded with `Placement.world` into cells. A defence cell is a palette entry of `minecraft:cobblestone` (`STONE`) or
a `#minecraft:fence_gates` block (`GATE`). Planks, fences, ladders, lanterns and air are not defence cells (a creeper never deletes a
watchtower ladder). Counts from `DefenceBlueprints` **[verified: wallText / gateText / the watchtower text]**:

| Blueprint | Defence cells |
|---|---|
| `wall(n)` | `3n` STONE. `wall(32)` is 96. `wall(15)` is 45. `wall(10)` is 30. |
| `gate(n)` | `3n − 6` STONE and 3 GATE. `gate(5)` is **9 STONE + 3 GATE** (12 cells). `gate(32)` is 90 + 3. |
| watchtower | 38 STONE. |
| lamp post | 0 |

A cell counts only while it holds its material. A cell a player broke (`PlayerBlockBreakEvents.AFTER`) is *opened*: not repaired, not
targeted, until the material is back. A cell the player rebuilt in another block is the player's.

**Hit points** (`core.siege.WallHp`): `HP_STONE` 12, `HP_GATE` **8**. Damage per hit (`SiegeHits.damage`):

| Attacker | STONE | GATE | Interval (ticks) | Hits to break |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| zombie, husk, zombie villager, baby the same | 1 | 2 | 20 | stone 12, gate **4** |
| vindicator | 2 | 4 | 20 | stone 6, gate **2** |
| ravager | 6 | 4 | 40 | stone 2, gate 2 |
| creeper breach | the target cell and the cell above or below it on the creeper's column take `CREEPER_CORE_DAMAGE` 16; other defence cells at centre distance `d ≤ r` take `round(8 × (1 − d/r))`, `r = 3` (6 powered) | same | once | a 1-wide, 2-high hole, cracks around it |

A gate cell has air above it, so two vindicator hits open a walk-through gap. A stone wall needs two broken cells (feet and head): one
zombie about 24 s, one ravager about 8 s. A ravager (2 wide, 3 high) needs 6 cells: about 24 s.

**Breaking.** A cell is breakable only when `SiteSupport.dependent` finds nothing outside the defence cells and the block above is not a
`FallingBlock`. Otherwise damage stops at HP − 1. At HP a breakable cell is set to air with `setBlock(pos, AIR, 3)`, no drop, the crack is
cleared, and `level.levelEvent(2001, pos, Block.getId(state))` plays the break effect. The cell is then *breached*.

**Cracks.** `destroyBlockProgress(crackId(pos), pos, stage)`. `crackId = −1 − (dx+128) − 256×(dz+128) − 65536×(y − minY)`, dx/dz from the
Heart: negative and injective inside the claim box. Stage `min(9, floor(10 × damage / hp))`, −1 clears it. `SiegeCracks` re-sends every
damaged cell within 32 blocks of a player every `CRACK_RESEND_TICKS` (200).

**Store.** `DefenceDamage`: a sparse map of cracked cells (damage 1..hp−1) and a set of breached cells. At most `MAX_DAMAGED_CELLS` (4096);
past that the least-damaged crack is dropped, never a breach. The live store is a mutable runtime object: a hit does not rebuild
`VillageState`. It is copied into `ThreatState` every `DAMAGE_FLUSH_TICKS` (200) and on save; a breach writes through at once. A reload
loses at most 200 ticks of cracks, never a breach.

**Index.** `DefenceIndex` (not saved), rebuilt when `defences` changes: a `Long2ObjectMap` from packed position to `DefenceCell(siteId,
kind, expectedState)` plus a per-chunk list for "nearest defence cell within r". About 12 k cells at the 128-site cap. Built from the pure
`DefenceCells.of(site, blueprint)`.

**Slow scan.** Every 20 ticks, one tenth of the sites, loaded chunks only. Only a *seen* cell (the scan or the build has watched it hold
its material) can become breached. A seen counting cell that is air or replaceable and not opened becomes breached (a vanilla creeper,
TNT, a piston). A breached cell holding its material again becomes intact. A breached cell holding some other block leaves the set.
Damage entries of cells that changed are pruned.

### 3.10 Recording walls and gates

`Defences.recorded` becomes true for all four defence blueprint ids. `DefenceSpecs.of` is unchanged, so walls and gates still have no key
cell and no patrol point, and `DefenceSystem.refresh` never drops a wall for a missing block. For a watchtower, a key cell that is in the
breached set counts as present. `MAX_DEFENCE_SITES` becomes 128; at the cap a finished wall is not recorded and the existing log line
says so. `DefenceClientTests` expects 3 recorded sites for its wall + gate + wall + 2 lamps + tower section; with this rule it is 6. Step
9 changes that number and says why in the commit (the recording rule changed by design, the test's intent is "every finished defence
build is recorded"). Not a weakened test.

`/villageheart defences adopt` (operator) records walls built by the pipeline before this milestone. A wall the player placed by hand is
not a defence. A site whose defence cells are all opened by a player is removed.

### 3.11 Mob goals

Installed from `ServerEntityEvents.ENTITY_LOAD` for a `Zombie` (not `Drowned`, not `ZombifiedPiglin`), `Creeper`, `Ravager` or
`Vindicator` in the overworld, through `MobGoalsAccessor` (`Mob.goalSelector`, `protected final`). Once: skipped when
`getAvailableGoals()` already holds one of ours (ENTITY_LOAD fires on every chunk reload). Goals are not saved. Classes and packages: 2.1.

**Lure on** (`core.siege.Lure.active`): at least `LURE_MIN_WALL_CELLS` (150) *seen* wall or gate cells (an enclosure, not one wall
piece), difficulty not peaceful, and day time in `[13000, 23000)` or a siege is running. `LureBudget` admits at most `MAX_LURED` (24)
mobs, siege zombies first, then by distance to the Heart. A slot frees on unload, removal, dimension change, leaving the lure radius,
getting a vanilla target, and when `isRemoved` is seen at the next admit.

**`VillageLureGoal`** on zombies, priority 5 (under `SpearUseGoal` 2, `ZombieAttackGoal` 3, the turtle-egg goal 4; over
`MoveThroughVillageGoal` 6), flag MOVE. `canUse` every 20 ticks (staggered by entity id): lure on, within `LURE_RADIUS` (112) of the
Heart, no target, a budget slot. Goal point: the nearest census villager when the zombie is within `CLAIM_RADIUS + 16`, with no
line-of-sight test, else the Heart. Waypoints: `DefaultRandomPos.getPosTowards(mob, 16, 7, goal, π/2)` while farther than 24, the goal
itself when nearer; re-path every `LURE_REPATH_TICKS` (60). Every path check is against the next waypoint (≤ 24), never the far goal, so
a partial path means a real obstacle. A partial path that ends at a defence cell hands off to the breach goal. A partial path that ends
anywhere else waits 100 ticks and tries another waypoint; a player's house is not attacked.

**`BreachGoal`**, flags MOVE + LOOK. Zombie priority 4. Ravager and vindicator priority 2, above `PathfindToRaidGoal` 3;
`VindicatorBreakDoorGoal` is also 2 and only `canUse`s on a wooden door, so at a cobblestone wall or a fence gate the breach goal is the
one that runs. "Blocked": the mob's goal (vanilla target, else the lure point, else for a raider the raid centre) is not reachable
(`navigation.createPath(goal, 1)` null or `!canReach()`, at most every 40 ticks), the mob stands within 1.5 blocks of the path's end,
and the index has a counting cell the mob can **touch** (face within melee reach, clear eye ray), on the side toward the goal. The pure
pick is `BreachChoice.pick(mobBox, goalPos, cells)`: a counting cell in the columns and layers the mob's box needs (zombie or vindicator
1 × 2, ravager 2 × 3), nearest first, ties GATE, then lower y, then (x, z). Paths take a token from `PathBudget`
(`MAX_SIEGE_PATHS_PER_TICK` 2, shared; no token → retry next interval), and an existing path to the same waypoint is reused. Then look,
swing, and `SiegeDamage.hit` on the interval. It stops when the cell breaks, the mob is pushed more than 3 blocks away, a vanilla target
becomes reachable, or the gamerule is off. Raiders only while they belong to the tracked raid; zombies only while lured or while their
vanilla target is in the claim. A ridden ravager keeps its goals. A mob that cannot touch a defence cell does no damage (the moat rule).

**`CreeperBreachGoal`**, priority 4, only while `target == null`, `siege_damage` is on, **the lure is on** (enclosure, night or siege),
the creeper holds a `LureBudget` slot, and the creeper is farther from the Heart than the cell (a creeper inside the ring does not blow
the wall outward). It walks to the free cell beside the nearest counting STONE or GATE cell of a wall or gate within
`CREEPER_SEEK_RADIUS` (24), paths through `PathBudget`, and within 2 blocks `ignite()`s and tags `villageheart.breacher`. **Nothing in
the village stops it**: guards never target creepers (`GuardTargets`) and iron golems never target creepers (2.1). The answer to a
breacher is the player, the trench rule, and the builder's repairs. T-L2-25b pins that neither a guard nor a golem holds it as a target.

**The breach is not a vanilla crater.** `CreeperExplodeMixin` on `Creeper.explodeCreeper`: `@ModifyArg` on the `ServerLevel.explode` call
turns `ExplosionInteraction.MOB` into `NONE` for a breacher, and an `@Inject` after it calls `SiegeDamage.blast`. Entities still take
damage. Blocks other than defence cells do not. A creeper chasing a player keeps the vanilla explosion and `mobGriefing`; the slow scan
then finds any defence cell it destroyed.

**Cost.** Lure and breach checks run in the mobs' own goal ticks, outside the 1 ms village budget: at most 24 mobs and 2 paths per tick.
`siege.paths` and `entity_ms` are reported.

### 3.12 Zombie sieges

`VillageSiegeMixin`: vanilla `VillageSiege.tryToSetupSiege` skips a player inside the claim, and `findRandomSpawnPos` returns null for a
position inside any claim, so a player just outside cannot pull vanilla zombies in through the walls. With `villageheart:sieges` false the
suppression stays. A Hamlet claim gets no mod siege (`SIEGE_MIN_TIER` VILLAGE). The mod's `ZombieSiege` runs instead.

Days are `gameTime / 24000`. Each roll kind stores `lastRolledDay`. A roll fires once when day time *crosses* its time, so a sleep or a
`/time set` does not roll twice; a skipped window rolls late or not at all. `doDaylightCycle false` stops rolls. A counter saved in the
future is reset. A siege that was rolled and never spawned is cleared at dawn.

The roll is after day time 13000, only when a player is near: `p = min(0.20, 0.04 + 0.02 × T)`. No siege in the first `SIEGE_GRACE_DAYS`
(5) days after the claim, at least `SIEGE_GAP_DAYS` (3) between sieges, at least `THREAT_REST_DAYS` (2) after a raid ended (3.13a), tier
at least VILLAGE, `villageheart:sieges` on, not peaceful, `doMobSpawning` on, no raid tracked, no pending raid due today. The sim harness
sets `sieges` false unless the scenario says `"threats": true`.

**Size** (`SiegeSize.zombies(T, qualifiedGuards)`):

- `raw = clamp(floor(4 + 2 × T), 4, 20)`.
- When `qualifiedGuards >= 4`, the result is `raw`.
- When fewer are qualified, the result is `min(raw, max(2, qualifiedGuards + 1))`.

Qualified means hired, not in training, not wounded, at the roll. Two guards face 3 zombies even at a high T. Zero guards face 2. Four
guards at T = 0 face 4; at T = 3, 10; at T = 10, 20. A guard hired that morning is qualified at dusk (12000), before the roll. One to three
guards are on the night shift at midnight already; the raid flag wakes a day guard when there are four or more. M25 does not add a
"nobody died" assertion on the two-guard fight (it is `defended`'s fight, an M26 target). It asserts the count.

**Spawn.** At day time 18000, `N` zombies (`EntityType.ZOMBIE`, `EVENT`, finalized like vanilla) in 1-3 groups, 2 ticks apart, on the
surface outside the defences: distance `max(outermost defence cell from the Heart, 32) + 12`, clamped to `[32, CLAIM_RADIUS + 16]`, angle
from the seeded roll, 10 tries per group (solid floor, 2 free cells, not water, not inside a room, `isPositionEntityTicking`). Light is
ignored. Each gets `setPersistenceRequired`, tag `villageheart.siege` and a lure slot first. A group with no valid cell is skipped and
logged; the siege runs with fewer.

**Lifecycle.** `SiegeRecord(startDay, zombieIds, spawned, killed, villagerDeaths)` in `threats.siege`. Running from the first spawn until
day time 23000 or until its zombies are dead (alive = loaded, tagged and not removed, or unloaded and not seen dead). At the end, leftovers
64+ blocks from every player are discarded; the rest burn at dawn. `sieges` off or peaceful ends it at once. While it runs,
`VillageThreats.underAttack` is true and the SIEGE alert is up ("Zombie siege! %s zombies left", DANGER). The summary goes to
`threats.lastSiege`.

### 3.13 Random raids

`ThreatScheduler` (`VillageSystem` `"threats"`), rules in `core.siege.ThreatRules`.

Roll once per day at day time 1000: `p = min(0.25, 0.02 + 0.03 × T)`. Conditions: `minecraft:raids` and `villageheart:random_raids` on,
not peaceful, tier at least VILLAGE, `RAID_GRACE_DAYS` (7) since the claim, `RAID_GAP_DAYS` (5) since the last raid of any origin ended,
nothing tracked, nothing pending. A Hamlet rolls nothing. Expected: a Village with T ≈ 2 rolls 8 %/day, about one raid every 12 days with
the gap; a rich walled Town (T ≈ 8) hits the 25 % cap, about one every 9 days.

A won roll stores `PendingRaid(rolledDay, startTick)` with a seeded `startTick` in `[2000, 11000]` of that day. It starts at the first tick
at or after `startTick` (on a later day, still inside that day's window) where a non-spectator player has been within 96 blocks for
`RAID_LOADED_TICKS` (600) in a row, the 16-point wave ring is entity-ticking, at least `THREAT_REST_DAYS` (2) have passed since the last
siege, **`getRaidAt(heart)` is null**, and `level.isVillage(heart)`. The warning chat ("Raiders are gathering near %s!") goes out when the
600-tick count begins. Nobody near: it waits, no expiry, and the Overview shows "A raid is coming". A Bad Omen raid on the village cancels
the pending one. Peaceful, or `raids` / `random_raids` off at the start moment: it stays pending, no chat. Another raid within 96 blocks
(a neighbour's, not tracked): it stays pending, so the mod never extends or re-levels a raid it did not create.

The start calls `createOrExtendRaid(nearestNonSpectator, heart)`. The returned raid must be fresh (`!isStarted()`, `getGroupsSpawned() ==
0`, an id `VillageRaids` has not seen); anything else is left untouched and the pending raid stays. Then the plan (3.13a, 3.14) is applied:
`RaidAccessor.setNumGroups(cap)` when the plan caps waves, `setRaidOmenLevel(omen)`, `setDirty()`. The player gets no Raid Omen, so
`absorbRaidOmen` changes nothing. Null (spectator, rule off, `CAN_START_RAID` false) drops the pending raid with a WARN. An empty POI
list does **not** return null; the centre is the Heart, which is why `isVillage(heart)` is checked first.

### 3.13a Young-village random raids (orchestrator default, r3.1)

r3 left this open: after the 7-day grace a young Village with two wooden-sword guards could roll a vanilla Normal raid (five waves, a
ravager in wave 3, witches in 4, an evoker in 5) that wipes them. The owner wants a hard start, not a hopeless one, and random raids are
the mod's own, so the mod sizes them. Rule, fixed at the start moment and saved in `RaidRecord.plan`:

| Village at the start moment | Waves | Omen | Extras | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Town tier, or ≥ `RAID_FULL_GUARDS` (4) qualified guards | vanilla for the difficulty (Normal 5) | `RaidScale` | `RaidScale` | 3.14 |
| Village tier, 2-3 qualified guards | `EARLY_RAID_WAVES` **2** | 1 | none | 3.14 (2 waves: 8) |
| Village tier, 0-1 qualified guards | **1** | 1 | none | 3.14 (1 wave: 6) |

- Waves 1 and 2 of a Normal raid are vanilla's own: 4-5 pillagers and 0-1 vindicator, then 3-4 pillagers and 2-3 vindicators. No
  ravager (wave 3), no witches (4), no evoker (5). Two vindicators with iron axes still kill a 20-HP wooden-sword guard in two hits: hard.
  A random raid starts only with a player within 96 blocks, who can fight: not hopeless.
- Zero guards is not an exemption: one wave still comes. A village does not stay safe by not hiring guards.
- `THREAT_REST_DAYS` (2) both ways: no random raid starts within 2 days of a siege, no siege is rolled within 2 days of a raid's end. A
  siege night is not followed by a raid morning.
- Mechanism: `RaidAccessor` (main source, `villageheart.mixins.json`) with `@Accessor("numGroups") int villageheart$numGroups()` and
  `@Mutable @Accessor("numGroups") void villageheart$setNumGroups(int)`. Set in the start tick, before the first wave (the cooldown is 300).
  Vanilla saves the field as `group_count`, so a reload keeps the cap, and `hasMoreWaves` / `isFinalWave` read it, so vanilla declares
  VICTORY after the last capped wave and the boss bar and Hero of the Village work unchanged. The getter is also what `RaidFacts
  .wavesTotal` reads, so the alert says "1/2", not "1/5".
- Bad Omen and TEST raids are never capped. Sieges keep 3.12's guard-count cap (no tier term: siege numbers are already small at a low T).
- Metric `raid.early` (1 when the started raid was capped). M26's `full` reports it.

### 3.14 Threat, scaling, rewards

`Threat.score(adults, wealth, defenceCells, tier) = adults/10 + wealth/100 + defenceCells/200 + tierBonus` (HAMLET 0, VILLAGE 0.5, TOWN
1). Wealth is treasury emeralds plus stockpile value from `Stockpiles.totalsById`. `ItemValue`: emerald 1, diamond 2, iron, copper or gold
ingot 0.25, any other item 1/64. All tunable.

`RaidScale.of(T)` → omen `clamp(1 + floor(T / 3), 1, 5)`, extra vindicators `min(4, floor(T / 2))` from wave 2, extra ravagers `min(2,
floor(T / 4))` from wave 3. Omen above 1 also raises vanilla's enchant odds and the Hero amplifier. Example: T = 2 → (1, 1, 0); T = 8 →
(3, 4, 2). Extras are for a full `RANDOM` raid only (not early, not Bad Omen, not TEST unless `scaled`). They are created with
`EntityType.X.create(level, EVENT)`, spawned with `raid.joinRaid(level, wave, raider, pos, false)` when `getGroupsSpawned()` rises,
tagged `villageheart.extra`, at the wave's first raider or on the ring when that spot is inside the outermost defence radius. The plan,
`extrasForWave` and `extraRaidersKilled` are fixed on the record so a reload neither doubles nor skips them.

Reward, random victories only, at most one per `RAID_GAP_DAYS` (`lastRewardDay`): `min(40, 4 + 2 × wavesSpawned + 3 × (omen − 1) +
extraRaidersKilled)` emeralds via `Treasury.deposit(server, n, null)`, recorded in `RaidSummary.reward` and `raid.reward`. Chat to
Resident and above ("Victory! The raid on %s is beaten: +%s emeralds to the treasury"). A siege pays nothing.

### 3.15 Upkeep

`AlertCode.DEFENCE_DAMAGED`: WARNING "Walls damaged: %s broken, %s cracked", or DANGER "Wall breached! %s holes" when a breach exists
during a raid or a siege; a "(no builder)" variant when no builder is hired. Chat on the first breach of a raid, siege or night ("The wall
of %s was breached"), at most once per `BREACH_NOTICE_TICKS` (1200), and "The walls of %s are repaired" when the last breach closes.

`RepairPlanner.next(breached, cracked, cells, heart, stock)`: breached GATE, then breached STONE from the bottom, then a crack at or above
half HP, then a lighter crack. Inside a class nearest the Heart, ties (y, x, z). A breach costs one item of its material (cobblestone, or
any `#minecraft:fence_gates`). A heavy crack costs one cobblestone. A light crack is free. The builder prefers repairs to new builds
(`REPAIR_BEFORE_BUILDS` true), repairs only in `WORK`, and never while `underAttack` (it is sheltering). The reservation uses one fixed
repair UUID; material is fetched from Storage (`FETCH`); the builder walks to a `StandPoints` stand of the cell (outside the wall when the
outside is in the claim, else inside). Placement goes through `BuildExecutor.place` with the blueprint's state, so a gate comes back
closed and facing the way the blueprint says. A cell with a player's block or an entity in it is skipped (M20 BLOCKED / ENTITY; never
broken). Each repair is one builder action (1 s at level 1, M15 level speed) plus the walk. Short of material, the status says "Repairs
need N more cobblestone" (or fence gate) and the breach stays open. Repairs grant `WorkUnit.BLOCK_PLACED` XP.

### 3.16 Guards during a raid or a siege

`VillageThreats.underAttack` is true when a raid is tracked or a siege is running. Workers shelter on it, and `VillageThreats.forGuards`
reports it to `RaidCheck` (with the one-tick vanilla discovery in 3.2 #3).

| Situation | Raid flag | Alarm | Who comes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raid tracked, or siege running | yes, for the whole threat | not raised by M25; rises by itself when a mob hurts a villager or a guard dies | every guard who is not wounded and not training, asleep or not |
| Hostile hurts a villager, or a guard dies, and no raid or siege | no | yes, 600 ticks, extended while a hunter is within 24 of a guard | the same guards, gathering at the alarm spot, then back to the clock |
| Trainee | the schedule returns `OFF` before the raid test | ignored | stays at the yard, or at the Barracks |
| Wounded (below 30%) | the schedule returns `SLEEP` first | ignored | stays in bed; an engaged guard gets `OFF` and still fights |
| 1-3 guards, midnight siege | already on the night shift | — | no sleeper to wake; the flag matters if one is in `[0, 1000)` |
| 4 guards, raid at day time 2000 | the day guard is on duty; three night guards are in `SLEEP` (`[1000, 12000)`) | — | the three wake, and stay in `DUTY` while the raid is tracked |
| 4 guards, day time 12500 | the day guard is `OFF` (handing gear in), three are on duty | — | the day guard comes back on duty |
| Raid stopped or won | flag falls on the tick `VillageRaids` sees it | — | back to the clock within one brain update (M18's 200-tick bound) |

A dead guard is not replaced at night (`HireRefusal.GUARD_AT_NIGHT`). The next morning's hire trains until dusk, so it misses a raid still
running through that day, and counts toward the shift split from the moment it is hired. M25 does not add a night hire.

Food during a long fight: `feed` will not refill and will not bite while any enemy is within 10 blocks; the retreat bite can heal behind
an ally; the carried ration is at most 12 HP. On duty a guard refills only within 16 blocks of the Barracks. An empty food stockpile means
no healing at all. M25 does not loosen `GuardSystem.feed` (T-L2-44).

Walls and sight: a guard inside a closed ring does not see a zombie at the outside face (targets must be visible), so siege zombies are
fought when they break in, at the breach. That is the decided "walls slow, not stop".

Creepers: nobody in the village kills them (2.1, 3.11). Golems (vanilla now, M19's smith-built ones later) fight zombies and raiders,
not creepers.

### 3.17 Gamerules and the harness

Gamerules, category MOBS, default true: `villageheart:siege_damage`, `villageheart:random_raids`, `villageheart:sieges`. Registered with
`GameRuleBuilder.forBoolean(true).category(...).buildAndRegister(Identifier)`. The lure has no gamerule: it only moves mobs, and only
toward enclosed villages.

The sim sets `random_raids` and `sieges` false at setup unless the scenario's top-level `"threats": true` (parsed by `ScenarioParser`,
default false). `siege_damage` stays on. `bare`, `starter`, `defended`, `defended_walled` and `raid` do not opt in. M26 `full` does.

Actions: `siege` `{"day": 2, "zombies": 8}` starts a siege now with the 3.12 spawn rule and the given count (no roll, no size rule);
`threats` `{"random_raids": true, "sieges": true}` sets the two gamerules mid-run.

New metrics: `defence.cells`, `defence.unseen`, `defence.breached`, `defence.cracked` (at the record), `defence.breached_noon` (at day time
9000), `defence.blocks_broken` and `defence.broken.<zombie|ravager|vindicator|creeper|other>`, `defence.repairs`, `defence.repair_items`,
`siege.started`, `siege.zombies`, `siege.killed`, `siege.villager_deaths`, `siege.paths`, `lure.max_lured`, `threat.score` (×100),
`entity_ms` (mean ms per tick of the overworld's entity ticking around `ServerLevel.tickNonPassenger`, same timer as `village_ms`;
reported only).

`sim/scenarios/siege.json` (4 days, reported, not a roadmap gate): the `raid` layout without the raid action, a `siege` action on day 2
with 8 zombies, `doMobSpawning` on. Asserted: `claimed == 1` every day, `sum(siege.started) == 1`, `defence.breached == 0` on the last
day, `sum(siege.villager_deaths) <= 2`, `max(lure.max_lured) <= 24`, `village_ms.mean < 1`. Reported: breaches per cause, repair items,
paths, `entity_ms`.

## 6. Risks, out of scope, open questions

**Risks**

1. **The Town raid on Normal.** Five waves, pillagers over the walls, vindicators through a gate in two hits, ravagers through stone in
   two hits, witches, an evoker whose vexes the guards cannot reach. Three of the four guards are asleep at tick 2000 and must wake. The
   kit is iron and level 2 because the village has it. If 3 of 3 fail, report deaths per wave with cause and position and do not loosen
   the 75% rule or drop the difficulty. Options for the orchestrator: level 3, a second golem. Not Easy, not a quieter assertion.
2. **A raider stuck outside the claim** can hold the raid until the 2-day stop, which 3.6 counts as a failure (guards only fight inside
   the claim). The summary lists the last raiders' positions. Option, not the default: on-duty guards chase the tracked raid's raiders 16
   blocks past the claim edge.
3. **Rations run out inside a wave.** Cap 12, no refill while an enemy is within 10 blocks. T-L2-44 stops a "helpful" change to `feed`.
4. **Creepers are unopposed.** Guards and golems both ignore them (2.1). The lure gate (enclosure, night or siege, budget slot) bounds how
   many seek the wall, and the repair loop is what makes it survivable. If the ring is gone faster than the builder replaces it, that
   shows up as `defence.breached_noon` and is reported; M26 can tune `CREEPER_SEEK_RADIUS` or the budget. A creeper answer (archers) is
   the owner's "maybe" follow-up, not M25.
5. **Merge.** Rebase onto `dev` before steps 5, 12 and 18. Conflicts expected in `WorkerBrain`, `VillageCodecs` (`RAW_B`), `VillageState`,
   `HeartFacts`, `ActivityKind` (append only; M19 appends `BUILDING_GOLEM`), `SimActions.ALL`, `villageheart.mixins.json`. A2 and A9 say
   what to do when M19 or M23 lands.
6. **Pathing cost** sits in entity ticks, outside the 1 ms village budget. Cap 24 lured mobs and 2 paths a tick. `siege.paths` and
   `entity_ms` are reported for M26.
7. **"Blocked by a defence block" is a heuristic** built on vanilla partial paths. If L2 shows zombies milling at the ring without hitting
   it, the fallback is to also treat "no movement for 60 ticks while lured and within 2 blocks of a defence cell" as blocked (section 8).
8. **Raids in GameTests leak** to other batches (96-block reach). `reset` / `done` / `finally` stop raids and discard raiders; a test that
   dies by timeout relies on the next `reset`.
9. **Shelter crowding.** Eight workers walking to beds through doors; `WalkTo`'s false-unreachable history. UNREACHABLE tries the next
   target.
10. **Crack overlays are client-side and transient.** Resent every 200 ticks to players within 32 blocks; a missed one shows late, never a
    wrong breach. T-L4-2's third screenshot.
11. **Gates are the way in.** Two vindicator hits open a gate. If L3 shows walls are pointless, `HP_GATE` is the first tunable (M26).
12. **`defended` is not a wall test.** Treating `wall_ring` as a defence would change an M18 scenario. Don't.
13. **A guard gathering at an alarm spot outside the wall** (a pillager shooting from outside hurts a villager) walks to a gate that a
    hostile within 8 keeps shut. That is M18 behaviour; M25 only makes it more frequent. Reported in T-L3-1 if seen.

**Unknowns.** Whether the SimOwner's simulation distance keeps the 100-block wave ring entity-ticking (the `raid` action checks and forces);
whether the seed-1 centre lands in the claim (the action ERRORs with the position); how long a Normal raid takes there (most of a day);
the site size (T-L2-12).

**Out of scope.** Changing vanilla wave tables or the bonus-wave rule of a Bad Omen raid. A player command to start a raid. Wall-length
caps. Hit points on planks, fences, ladders, lanterns, or a hand-built wall. Lure for skeletons or any mob not in 3.2 #11. Teaching guards
or golems to attack creepers, or guards to shoot or fight vexes. Guards repairing walls. The builder upgrading a wall to a stronger block.
Night hiring of a replacement guard. Archers. Repairing golems mid-raid (M19's rule stays). A death-log tab or export. Celebrations for
sheltered workers. Alerts for night-mob attacks without a raid or siege (DEFENCE_DAMAGED covers what they break). A raid HUD beyond the
boss bar.

appended). M16 "a hidden miner that dies loses its haul" stays open, but the death is logged. **New BACKLOG lines in step 19:** guards
chasing raiders out of the claim (risk 2); a creeper answer (risk 4); worker celebrations; `defences adopt` for walls built before M25; a
stone-brick wall upgrade; guards repairing walls; T-L2-46 if M19 was not merged by step 18.

**Open questions.** None block the build. r3's question 1 (a young Village vs a vanilla-sized random raid) is now the orchestrator
default in 3.13a; the owner may change the numbers (`EARLY_RAID_WAVES`, `RAID_FULL_GUARDS`, `THREAT_REST_DAYS`). r3's question 2 (wait
for a smith-built golem) stays the A2 rule: the scenario summons the golem.
