## 1. Goal

When a raid starts on the claimed village, the Heart screen shows a red **raid alert** ("Raid! Wave 2/5, 4 raiders
left"), online members get a chat line, every guard comes on duty within 10 seconds (asleep or not), hired workers stop work and go
indoors to their bed, and golems fight as in vanilla. Every villager death in the village is written to a **death log** (who, job,
cause, day, during a raid or not), shown on the Overview tab and in the simulation metrics.

*(r2)* **Walls do not make a village immune.** Raids also come by themselves (a seeded daily chance that grows with the village's
population, wealth and defences), and stronger villages get stronger raids with a treasury reward for a victory. On some nights a
**zombie siege** comes. Zombies near a walled village are **lured** towards it even when the walls hide every villager, and attack the
wall where their path is blocked; **creepers seek the wall and blow a breach** in it; **ravagers** smash wall blocks in a few hits and
vindicators chop gates. Wall blocks have hit points with the vanilla crack overlay. Damage raises an alert, and the **builder repairs**
the wall with cobblestone (or fence gates) from Storage. Only blocks of the village's recorded defences are ever damaged by these rules;
everything else (skeletons, spiders, witches, endermen, …) stays vanilla.

### 2.1 Vanilla raids in 1.21.11 (read for this plan, all **[verified]**)

- **Start.** `BadOmenMobEffect.applyEffectTick`: a non-spectator `ServerPlayer` with Bad Omen in a village
  (`ServerLevel.isVillage(pos)`, not peaceful) gets `RAID_OMEN` for 600 ticks and `setRaidOmenPosition(pos)`. When Raid Omen has 1
  tick left, `RaidOmenMobEffect.applyEffectTick` calls `level.getRaids().createOrExtendRaid(player, omenPos)`.
- **`Raids.createOrExtendRaid(ServerPlayer, BlockPos)`** returns null if the player is a spectator, `GameRules.RAIDS` is off or the
  `EnvironmentAttributes.CAN_START_RAID` attribute is false (Nether only). The **centre** is the mean of the `#village` POIs within 64
  blocks with `Occupancy.IS_OCCUPIED` (else `pos`); `getOrCreateRaid` reuses `getRaidAt(centre)`; then `raid.absorbRaidOmen(player)`
  (adds the Raid Omen amplifier + 1, clamped to 5; no effect → no change). It does **not** need the player to be near.
- `/raid start <omen>` (`RaidCommand`) is registered only when `SharedConstants.DEBUG_DEV_COMMANDS || IS_RUNNING_IN_IDE`: **not usable**
  in the sim or GameTests. Its `start` = `createOrExtendRaid(player, player.blockPosition())` + `raid.setRaidOmenLevel(i)` +
  `raids.setDirty()`; M25's test helper copies exactly that.
- **`Raid`** public API: `isOver()`, `isVictory()`, `isLoss()`, `isStopped()`, `isStarted()`, `isActive()`, `isBetweenWaves()`,
  `hasFirstWaveSpawned()`, `getGroupsSpawned()`, `getTotalRaidersAlive()`, `getAllRaiders()`, `getCenter()`, `getRaidOmenLevel()`,
  `setRaidOmenLevel(int)`, `getNumGroups(Difficulty)` (EASY 3, NORMAL 5, HARD 7), `stop()`, `joinRaid(ServerLevel, int wave, Raider,
  BlockPos, boolean alreadySpawned)`. The wave count is fixed at creation from the difficulty; omen level > 1 adds one bonus wave.
- **`Raids`**: `get(int id)`, `getId(Raid) → OptionalInt`, `getNearbyRaid(pos, 9216)`; `ServerLevel.getRaidAt(pos)` = the nearest
  **active** raid whose centre is within 96 blocks. `Raids.tick` removes stopped raids and stops all raids when `RAIDS` is off.
- **Timing.** A new raid has `raidCooldownTicks = 300`: the first wave spawns **15 s after creation**; 300 ticks between waves; after
  the last wave is dead, 40 post-raid ticks then `VICTORY`, then 600 celebration ticks, then `stop()`. A raid stops after 48000 active
  ticks (2 days). `LOSS` only when the centre is no longer a village (`isVillage(centre)` false) after a wave spawned. **The mod's held
  POI tickets (job blocks, Barracks beds) keep sections "occupied", so LOSS almost never happens while mod buildings are valid**; M25
  therefore defines "the village survives" itself (3.6).
- **Wave spawn position**: `findRandomSpawnPos` on `serverLevel.random` (not seeded), 41-98 blocks from the centre while the cooldown
  counts down, in loaded entity-ticking chunks; fallback at cooldown 0 is within ~8 blocks of the centre.
- **`Raider.die`** calls `raid.removeFromRaid(...)` **before** `super.die`: at Fabric `AFTER_DEATH` the raider's `getCurrentRaid()` is
  already null. `ALLOW_DEATH` (fires before `die`, M18 verified) still sees it.
- **Villager brain.** `SetRaidStatus` (random 1/20 per tick): raid at own position → first wave spawned and not between waves → `RAID`,
  else `PRE_RAID` (also sets the default activity). `PRE_RAID` package = `RingBell`, walk to the MEETING point (the bell) / stroll:
  **outdoors**. `RAID` package = `LocateHidingPlace(24)` (walk to a random `HOME` POI within 24 blocks while the raid is active) and
  `CelebrateVillagersSurvivedRaid` after a victory. `ResetRaidStatus` returns to IDLE when the raid is gone, stopped or lost.
  `ReactToBell` (CORE) → `HIDE` for 15 s after a bell. All of this only near the raid centre (`getRaidAt(own pos)`).
- `Vindicator`: `VindicatorBreakDoorGoal` on NORMAL/HARD and `RaiderOpenDoorGoal`: houses are not safe on Normal.

### 3.2 Key decisions

1. **One raid truth: `VillageRaids` (glue) over `core.raid.RaidTracker` (pure).** A `VillageSystem` `raids` runs every 20 ticks
   (`RAID_CHECK_TICKS`) and decides whether the claim is under raid. Everything else reads it: the Heart alert, the guard schedule
   (M25 replaces M18's default `RaidCheck` with `VillageRaids::underRaid`), worker shelter, the chat lines, the death log's
   `duringRaid` flag and the sim metrics. No consumer calls `getRaidAt` itself (one exception: vanilla's own villager brain for
   unhired villagers, which M25 leaves alone).
2. **What counts as "a raid on the claim".** Facts gathered by the glue: `raid = level.getRaidAt(heart)`; it is relevant when it is not
   `isOver()`/`isStopped()` **and** (`ClaimGeometry.inClaim(heart, centre)` **or** at least one of `getAllRaiders()` is in the claim)
   (= M18's rule, critique #15 there). `getRaidAt(heart)` is used **only to discover** a raid while none is tracked. **Latched**: once a
   raid id is tracked, its facts come from `level.getRaids().get(trackedId)` (never from `getRaidAt`, which returns the *nearest active*
   raid within 96 blocks and could swap in another raid, or miss ours when vanilla moves its centre), and it stays the village's raid
   until it is over, stopped or gone (`Raids.get(id) == null`), even when its raiders all leave the claim, so the alert does not flap.
   A second raid nearby is ignored while one is tracked. **Stopped raids live one tick**: `Raids.tick` removes a stopped raid on the
   next tick **[verified]**, so a 20-tick poll would see "gone" instead of "stopped". Therefore the cheap part runs **every tick**:
   `Raids.get(trackedId)` and its status (`isVictory/isLoss/isStopped`), one map lookup; the full facts (waves, raiders in the claim,
   guards) every `RAID_CHECK_TICKS`. Our system runs at the end of the server tick, after the level tick in which vanilla (or a test,
   or the 48000-tick timeout) called `stop()`, and before the next `Raids.tick` removes it. A raid that starts **before
   its first wave** is already relevant (centre in claim): the L2 bound of 10 s is shorter than the 15 s before the first wave.
3. **Raid identity survives a reload.** The tracked raid is stored as its vanilla id (`Raids.getId(raid)`) in `VillageState.raid`
   with its start data (3.4). On load, `Raids.get(id)` re-finds it (vanilla saves raids in its own `raids` saved data, and raiders
   re-join their raid on load). A missing raid on load closes the record with outcome `GONE`.
4. **Workers indoors: a mod activity `villageheart:shelter`, not vanilla `RAID`.** Vanilla's raid behaviour is wrong for workers: it
   only triggers within 96 blocks of the raid centre, and `PRE_RAID` (before the first wave and between waves) sends villagers *out* to
   ring the bell. So the hired-worker brain (non-guards) **drops `SetRaidStatus` from CORE** and gains a `SHELTER` activity:
   `WorkerBrain.activityFor(villager, level)` returns SHELTER while `VillageRaids.underRaid(server)` and the day phase is not SLEEP and
   the worker is not away (`AwayTrips.isAway`). In SLEEP a worker **with a HOME** stays in REST (walking to / sleeping in its own bed:
   already indoors); a worker **without a HOME** gets SHELTER in SLEEP too (REST would leave it standing outdoors). A worker asleep in its
   own bed when SLEEP turns into WAKE during a raid is woken by vanilla `WakeUp` (CORE) and finds itself already at its target (arrived at
   once): accepted, not special-cased.
   `UpdateWorkerActivity` sits in the SHELTER package too, so the worker returns to its schedule once the raid ends. PANIC (hurt or a
   hostile close) still pre-empts SHELTER, and the HIDE package (bell) stays: both end in IDLE, and `UpdateWorkerActivity` then puts the
   worker back in SHELTER. **Unhired villagers keep the untouched vanilla brain** (DESIGN §8: vanilla raids unchanged). Guards have
   their own brain (A1). Away workers stay away (hidden underground or on a hunt: safe). The vanilla `PRE_RAID/RAID/HIDE` packages stay
   registered in the worker brain (HIDE is reached through the bell; the other two are unused and harmless).
5. **Shelter target** (`core.raid.ShelterPlanner.choose`): (1) the worker's `HOME` if it is a bed of a valid House (M11
   `Housing`), (2) else the nearest free-or-own bed of a valid House by path-free distance, (3) else the nearest valid **room**
   building of the village (any type with a room scan: Storage, Kitchen, Smithy, …) and its door's inside cell, (4) else none: the worker
   stays where it is, activity shows "No shelter". The glue walks there with M9's `WalkTo` (opens doors, A8 of M18), arrives within
   `SHELTER_ARRIVE` (1.5) blocks of the target, then stands still, looking at the door; it re-walks if pushed more than
   `SHELTER_DRIFT` (4) blocks away. Vanilla `InteractWithDoor` (CORE) closes doors behind it.
6. **Death log in the village state, independent of the sim.** `DeathLogSystem` (glue) registers Fabric `ServerLivingEntityEvents
   .AFTER_DEATH` and `MOB_CONVERSION` (like `SimMetricsCollector`, whose cause rule moves to core `DeathCauses` so both use the same
   words). Logged: every **hired worker of the village wherever it dies** (read from the entity's `WorkerData` attachment, still present
   at AFTER_DEATH: no dependency on `WorkerSystem`'s handler order) and every other villager **inside the claim**. Entry: `seq` (monotonic
   per village), villager UUID, name (`VillagerNames.of` or the custom name), job id (or ""), cause id, `byMob`, `gameTime`, `day`
   (`dayTime / 24000 + 1`), `inClaim`, `duringRaid`. A death followed by a conversion of the same villager within 1 tick is **one** entry
   with cause `zombified` (the existing sim rule). `MOB_CONVERSION` counts **only** a `Villager` turning into a `ZombieVillager` (cause `zombified`) or a
   `Witch` (lightning, cause `minecraft:lightning_bolt`); a cure (zombie villager → villager) and every other conversion log nothing.
   The rule is `core.death.DeathCauses.conversion(fromType, toType)`, shared with the sim collector (which today counts a witch as
   zombified: aligned, noted in section 8). Kept: the last `DEATH_LOG_MAX` (32) entries, saved with the village; the counters
   `nextSeq` (all entries ever) and `inClaimLogged` (in-claim entries ever) are monotonic and saved, so a metric never depends on
   the capped list. The sim keeps
   its own collector as an independent cross-check (3.7).
7. **Alert first.** The RAID line is DANGER and is always the **first** alert (inserted at index 0, before the built-ins), so the
   `MAX_ALERTS` cut can never hide it.
8. **Deterministic raid start (sim and GameTests).** Helper `RaidStart.start(ServerPlayer p, BlockPos at, int omen)` (gametest source
   set, used by both): checks `GameRules.RAIDS` on and difficulty not PEACEFUL (else throws with the reason), then exactly
   `RaidCommand.start`: `createOrExtendRaid(p, at)`, `setRaidOmenLevel(omen)`, `setDirty()`; null → throw. Omen 1 (= a Bad Omen I raid,
   no bonus wave). In the sim the player is the creative `SimOwner` (`createOrExtendRaid` checks only `isSpectator`) and `at` is the
   Heart; in GameTests a survival test player. GameTests that run past 300 ticks must not get waves they did not ask for: a gametest-only
   accessor mixin `RaidTestAccess` (`@Accessor` get/set on `Raid`'s private `raidCooldownTicks`, `groupsSpawned`, `started`) lets `RaidStart.holdWaves(raid)` keep the
   cooldown at 300 every 20 ticks. A separate test (T-L2-4) uses the real Bad Omen → Raid Omen path.
9. ~~No new vanilla behaviour.~~ **Replaced in r2** (human decision 2026-11-09): the mod now starts raids itself (3.13), adds raiders
   to the waves of a strong village (3.14), pays a reward (3.14), runs zombie sieges in place of vanilla's `VillageSiege` inside the
   claim (3.12), lures zombies, sends creepers at walls and lets mobs damage **defence blocks only** (3.9-3.11). It still never stops or
   shortens a vanilla raid, and the raid's own waves, bell, boss bar, Hero of the Village and victory/loss logic are vanilla.
10. *(r2)* **Siege damage touches defence blocks only, behind a mod gamerule, independent of `mobGriefing`.** A *defence block* is a
    cobblestone or fence-gate cell of a **recorded** Wall Segment, Gate or Watchtower of the village (3.10). Every block change made by
    the siege rules goes through one function, `SiegeDamage.hit(level, pos, amount, cause)`, which first asks the index "is this a
    defence cell, and does it still hold its material?" and does nothing otherwise. Gamerule `villageheart:siege_damage` (boolean,
    default **true**; 1.21.11 game-rule ids are snake_case identifiers like vanilla's `minecraft:mob_griefing` **[verified]**, so the
    human's `siegeDamage` becomes `siege_damage`). Why not `mobGriefing`: it exists to protect terrain and player builds, which siege
    damage never touches; servers often turn it off to stop creeper craters and would then lose the core "walls need upkeep" loop
    without meaning to. Admins who want immune walls set `siege_damage false`. Vanilla effects stay vanilla: a creeper chasing a player
    explodes with `ExplosionInteraction.MOB` and obeys `mobGriefing` as before; the mod's slow scan then finds any defence cell it
    destroyed and queues it for repair (3.15).
11. *(r2)* **Who attacks the village (the motive rule, human amendment).** Only these mob kinds get mod goals:
    | Kind | When | Mod behaviour |
    |---|---|---|
    | `Zombie`, `Husk`, `ZombieVillager` (babies too) | lure on (3.11): night, or a siege, and the village has walls | lured towards the village without line of sight; hits the defence block that blocks its path |
    | `Creeper` | lure on, no target | seeks the nearest wall/gate cell and explodes there (mod breach, 3.11) |
    | `Ravager` | raider of the tracked raid | hits a blocking defence block, heavy damage |
    | `Vindicator` | raider of the tracked raid | hits a blocking defence block, axe bonus on gates |
    | everything else: skeletons, strays, spiders, endermen, witches, pillagers, evokers, vexes, drowned, zombified piglins, … | — | **vanilla, no lure, no wall damage** |
    Drowned are left out (their water AI walks them back to water; a lure would fight it), zombified piglins are neutral. Pillagers keep
    shooting over walls (vanilla). Vanilla zombie → villager targeting still applies once a zombie sees one (`NearestAttackableTargetGoal`
    with `TargetingConditions.forCombat()` checks line of sight **[verified]**: that is exactly why a wall hides villagers today).
12. *(r2)* **Sieges and random raids are seeded and pure.** Every roll is `ThreatRoll.roll(worldSeed, day, kind)` (a SplitMix64 hash,
    no shared `RandomSource`), so the same world gets the same raid days in a re-run, L1 can pin the odds, and a sim reload does not
    re-roll. Mob AI after the start is not seeded.

### 3.6 What "the village survives" means (pinned for L3)

All of: the claim exists and the Heart is in place on every day (`claimed == 1`); the raid ends **by the last day** (no tracked raid at
the last record) with outcome **VICTORY** (no LOSS, no STOPPED by the 2-day timeout, no GONE); and **≥ 75 %** of the adult villagers counted
at the raid's start are alive: `raid.villager_deaths ≤ 0.25 × raid.villagers_at_start` (death-log entries in the claim while the raid is
tracked; zombified counts as a death) **and** `raid.villagers_at_end ≥ 0.75 × raid.villagers_at_start` (adults in the claim, plus away
workers, at the end transition). Adults only in both numbers, so babies born on days 1-3 neither inflate nor dilute the ratio. Victory is stricter than vanilla's "no defeat" (which the mod's POI tickets make
almost impossible, 2.1), and it is the only reading under which a raid that just times out is not a "survived" raid.

### 3.9 Defence blocks and hit points *(r2)*

**Which blocks.** A recorded defence site (3.10) is regenerated into world cells from (blueprint, params, ground, rotation) with M20's
`Placement.world` (the same as a build's steps). Its **defence cells** are the cells whose palette entry places `minecraft:cobblestone`
(kind `STONE`) or a `#minecraft:fence_gates` block (kind `GATE`). Planks, fences, ladders, lanterns and air are not defence cells (a
creeper never deletes a watchtower's ladder, which would also unregister the site under M21's key rule; lamp posts have no defence cells
at all). Counts: a 32-long wall 96 STONE; a 32-long gate 90 STONE + 3 GATE; a watchtower 38 STONE.

A defence cell **counts** only while it holds a block of its material: a cell a player rebuilt in stone bricks, or filled with anything
else, is the player's block and is never damaged or replaced. A cell a **player** broke is *opened* (the player wants a hole there): it
is not repaired and not targeted until the material is back in it (then it counts again). Detected with Fabric
`PlayerBlockBreakEvents.AFTER` on a defence cell.

**Hit points** (`core.siege.WallHp`, all *(tunable)* in `Balance`): `HP_STONE` 12, `HP_GATE` 4. Hit damage per attacker kind
(`core.siege.SiegeHits.damage(attacker, cellKind)`):

| Attacker | vs STONE | vs GATE | interval (ticks) | blocks/min on one cell (STONE) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| zombie (incl. husk, zombie villager; baby the same) | 1 | 2 | 20 | 5 (12 s per block) |
| vindicator | 2 | 4 (axe: a gate in one hit) | 20 | 10 |
| ravager | 6 | 4 | 40 | 15 (2 hits per block) |
| creeper breach (per cell, by distance d from the blast) | round(16 × (1 − d/r)), r = 3 (6 powered) | same | once | point-blank cell and its 1-block neighbours break: a 1-3 cell hole |

A 3-high wall needs 2 broken cells (feet and head) for a zombie to pass: one zombie alone takes about 24 s, a ravager 8 s; a gate cell
2 zombie hits or 1 vindicator hit. That makes gates the weak point and ravagers the wall breakers, as decided.

**Breaking.** At damage ≥ HP the block is removed with `level.setBlock(pos, AIR, 3)` and **no drop** (rubble; the repair costs real
material), the crack is cleared, a `BLOCK_BREAK`-style sound and particles play (`level.levelEvent(2001, pos, Block.getId(state))`, the
vanilla break effect), and the cell becomes *breached*.

**Crack overlay.** `level.destroyBlockProgress(crackId(pos), pos, stage)` **[verified: `ServerLevel.destroyBlockProgress(int, BlockPos,
int)` sends `ClientboundBlockDestructionPacket` to players within 32 blocks whose entity id ≠ the first argument]**. One breaker id per
cell (`crackId` = a negative int from the packed position, so it never equals an entity id and two cells never share one: the client
keys its overlays by that id); stage = `min(9, floor(10 × damage / hp))`, −1 clears it. Because the overlay lives only on clients that
were near, `SiegeCracks` re-sends the stage of every damaged cell within 32 blocks of each player every `CRACK_RESEND_TICKS` (200)
(also covers a client that pruned a stale overlay).

**The store** (`core.siege.DefenceDamage`, saved): a sparse map `packed BlockPos (long) → damage (1..hp−1)` for cracked cells and a set
of breached cells, nothing for intact cells. Saved as two sorted `long` arrays (`[pos, dmg, pos, dmg, …]` and `[pos, …]`) under
`threats.damage`: cheap to write, independent of chunk loading, and it never needs a chunk to be loaded to be read. At most
`MAX_DAMAGED_CELLS` (4096) entries; past it the oldest cracked entries are dropped (a crack forgotten, never a breach). Entries whose
cell is no longer a defence cell (site cancelled, opened, rebuilt by a player) are pruned on the slow scan.

**Runtime index** (not saved, `siege.DefenceIndex`, rebuilt when `VillageState.defences` changes, compared by list identity): `Long2ObjectMap`
from packed position to `DefenceCell(siteId, kind, expectedState)` plus a per-chunk list for "nearest defence cell within r" queries
(look only at the chunks the query box touches). Built from the pure `core.siege.DefenceCells.of(site, blueprint)` (L1: counts above).
12 k cells at the cap (128 sites, 3.10) is about 1 MB of map: acceptable; built once per change.

**Slow scan** (`SiegeScanSystem`, part of the `defence` village system, every 20 ticks one tenth of the sites, so each site every
200 ticks; loaded chunks only): a counting cell that is air or replaceable and not opened → breached (cause: anything not a player:
a vanilla creeper with `mobGriefing`, TNT, a ghast); a breached cell holding its material again (a player put it back) → intact; cracked
entries of cells that changed → pruned.

### 3.10 Recording walls and gates *(r2)*

M21 records only Lamp Posts and Watchtowers (A13). M25: `Defences.recorded(id)` becomes true for all four defence blueprints;
`DefenceSite` gains `BlueprintParams params` (back-compat constructor with `NONE`; codec key `length`, optional, default 0), filled from
`BuildJob.params` in `BuildJobs.finish`; `DefenceSpecs.of` stays as is, so walls and gates still have no key cells and no patrol point
(nothing in M18 changes) and `DefenceSystem.refresh` never drops them for a missing block (a breach must stay a site to be repaired).
`MAX_DEFENCE_SITES` 64 → **128** (a ring of 32-long sections round a 80-radius claim needs about 32; 128 leaves room; at the cap a finished
wall is not recorded and the log says so, M21's existing message). Walls built **before** M25 on a live world were never recorded: the
builder adds a one-shot operator command `/villageheart defences adopt` (Owner/op) that is out of the normal flow; v1 worlds made with
M21 only exist in tests, so this is a convenience, not a migration. A wall the player built by hand is **not** a defence (only builds
are recorded): stated in DESIGN §8.

A defence site is removed (and its damage entries go) when the build is cancelled before it finished (never recorded) or when all its
defence cells are opened by a player (`DefenceSystem` checks on the slow scan): the player tore it down.

### 3.11 Mob goals: lure, breach, creeper *(r2)*

**Installing goals.** Fabric `ServerEntityEvents.ENTITY_LOAD` **[verified, fabric-lifecycle-events-v1 2.6.15]** for a `Zombie` (not
`Drowned`, not `ZombifiedPiglin`), `Creeper`, `Ravager` or `Vindicator` in the overworld: adds the goals through an accessor mixin on
`Mob.goalSelector` (`protected final GoalSelector`, **[verified]**), once (skips when `getAvailableGoals()` already holds one of ours:
ENTITY_LOAD fires on every chunk reload). The goals are not saved; the per-mob state they keep is transient. Classes are in the 1.21.11
packages `net.minecraft.world.entity.monster.zombie.{Zombie, Husk, ZombieVillager, Drowned, ZombifiedPiglin}`,
`monster.Creeper`, `monster.Ravager`, `monster.illager.Vindicator` **[verified]**.

**Lure on** (`core.siege.Lure.active(...)`, pure): the village has at least one recorded Wall Segment or Gate (a village without walls
needs no lure: zombies already see villagers), difficulty not PEACEFUL, and (`dayTime % 24000` in `[LURE_FROM 13000, LURE_TO 23000)` or a
siege is running). Lured mobs are budgeted: `LureBudget` (glue, transient) admits at most `MAX_LURED` (24) mobs at once, siege zombies
first, then by distance to the Heart; a mob that leaves the lure radius, dies or gets a vanilla target frees its slot.

**`VillageLureGoal`** (zombies; goal priority 5: under `ZombieAttackGoal` 3, over `MoveThroughVillageGoal` 6 and strolling 7
**[verified priorities]**; flags MOVE): `canUse` every 20 ticks (staggered by entity id): lure on, the zombie within `LURE_RADIUS` (112) of
the Heart, no target, a budget slot. Goal point: the nearest villager of the census when the zombie is within `CLAIM_RADIUS` + 16 of the
Heart (**no line of sight needed**), else the Heart. It moves by waypoints: `DefaultRandomPos.getPosTowards(mob, 16, 7, goal, π/2)`
**[verified]** while farther than 24, the goal itself when nearer; re-path every `LURE_REPATH_TICKS` (60). When the path is partial
(`!path.canReach()`) and the zombie stands at its end node, it hands over to the breach goal (below) if a defence cell blocks it, else it
waits 100 ticks and tries another waypoint (a player's house in the way is not attacked).

**`BreachGoal`** (zombie, vindicator, ravager; priority 4, flags MOVE + LOOK): "a defence block stands between the mob and its goal"
is decided as: the mob's goal (its vanilla target, else the lure goal point, else for a raider the raid centre) is not reachable
(`navigation.createPath(goal, 1)` null or `!canReach()`, checked at most every 40 ticks), the mob stands within 1.5 blocks of the path's
end node, and the index has a **counting** defence cell within 2 blocks of the mob, at feet or head height, on the side towards the goal
(dot product of (cell − mob) and (goal − mob) > 0). The pure part is `core.siege.BreachChoice.pick(mobPos, goalPos, cells)`: nearest by
distance, ties GATE first, then (y, x, z). Then: look at it, swing the arm, every interval (3.9 table) `SiegeDamage.hit`. It stops when
the cell breaks, the mob is pushed more than 3 blocks away, a vanilla target becomes reachable (a gap opened), or the gamerule is off.
Raiders use it only while they belong to the **tracked** raid (3.2 #2), zombies only while lured or while their vanilla target is a
villager/golem/player in the claim (a zombie that saw a villager over a gate and cannot reach it hits the gate). Vanilla
`VindicatorBreakDoorGoal` and zombies' Hard-mode door breaking are untouched.

**`CreeperBreachGoal`** (priority 4 with `target == null`, so a creeper that sees a player keeps vanilla `MeleeAttackGoal`/`SwellGoal`
**[verified: Creeper goals 1 Float, 2 Swell, 3 Avoid cat/ocelot, 4 MeleeAttack, 5 stroll; targets player only]**): lure on, a budget
slot, the nearest counting **STONE or GATE** cell of a Wall Segment or Gate within `CREEPER_SEEK_RADIUS` (24). It walks to the free cell
next to that block on its own side; within 2 blocks it calls `creeper.ignite()` **[verified: public; `tick()` swells while ignited and
`explodeCreeper()` fires at `maxSwell` 30]** and adds the entity tag `villageheart.breacher`.

**Mod-controlled breach, not a vanilla crater** (decision): a mixin on the private `Creeper.explodeCreeper()` **[verified body:
`serverLevel.explode(this, x, y, z, explosionRadius * f, Level.ExplosionInteraction.MOB)`, then lingering cloud, death effects,
`discard()`]**: `@ModifyArg` on that `explode` call's `ExplosionInteraction` turns MOB into **NONE** for a creeper with the breacher tag
(entities are still hurt, particles and sound as vanilla; `NONE` → `Explosion.BlockInteraction.KEEP` **[verified in
`ServerLevel.explode`]**), and an `@Inject` right after it calls `SiegeDamage.blast(level, centre, r)`: the 3.9 falloff on defence cells
within r only. Why: a vanilla explosion would also crater the player's houses, farms and Storage behind the wall, and would need
`mobGriefing`; the breacher's job is the wall. (The mixin target's owner class is the one `javap -c Creeper` shows for the call:
`ServerLevel` or `Level`; the builder checks it.)

**Cost.** The lure and breach checks run in the mobs' own goal ticks (entity ticking), not in `VillageTicker` (the 1 ms budget
measures village systems only). Bounded by `MAX_LURED` 24 mobs × one path every 40-60 ticks = at most ~0.6 paths per tick; the
`SimMetricsCollector` adds `siege.paths` (count per day) and `entity_ms` is watched in L3 (risk 9).

### 3.12 Zombie sieges *(r2)*

Vanilla's `VillageSiege` (a `CustomSpawner`) **[verified]**: at `dayTime` 18000, 1 in 10 nights, for a player standing where
`isVillage` is true, it spawns 20 zombies (`EntitySpawnReason.EVENT`) about 32 blocks from the player at spots that are **inside** the
village (`isVillage(spot)`) and pass the normal monster spawn rules (dark). In a walled village that puts zombies inside the walls, and
in a lit one it spawns nothing. **Decision: replace it inside the claim, keep it elsewhere.** A mixin on `VillageSiege.tryToSetupSiege`
skips players inside the claim sphere; the mod's own `ZombieSiege` runs instead:

- Roll at `dayTime` 13000 (dusk) each day, only when loaded (3.2 #13): `p = min(SIEGE_P_MAX 0.20, SIEGE_P_BASE 0.04 + SIEGE_P_PER_THREAT
  0.02 × T)` (T: 3.14), no siege in the first `SIEGE_GRACE_DAYS` (5) days after the claim, at least `SIEGE_GAP_DAYS` (3) between sieges,
  tier at least `SIEGE_MIN_TIER` VILLAGE, gamerule `villageheart:sieges` on (default true; the sim harness sets it false unless a
  scenario opts in, 3.17), no raid tracked.
- At `dayTime` 18000 (midnight, as vanilla) it spawns `N = clamp(4 + 2 × T, 4, 20)` zombies (`EntityType.ZOMBIE`, `EVENT`, finalized
  like vanilla) in 1-3 groups, 2 ticks apart, on the surface **outside** the defences: at distance `max(outermost defence cell from the
  Heart) + 12`, clamped to `[32, CLAIM_RADIUS + 16]`, random angle from the seeded roll, 10 tries per group (a cell with a solid floor,
  2 free cells, not water, not inside a room); light is ignored (a siege is an event, like a raid wave). Each gets the tag
  `villageheart.siege` and a lure slot first.
- The siege is *running* from the first spawn until dawn (`dayTime` 23000) or until all its zombies are dead; `SiegeRecord(startDay,
  spawned, killed, villagerDeaths)` in `threats.siege`; summary into `threats.lastSiege`. While running it counts as a threat for the
  guards (3.16) and raises the SIEGE alert ("Zombie siege! %s zombies left", DANGER).

### 3.13 Random raids *(r2)*

`ThreatScheduler` (glue, `VillageSystem "threats"`, pure rules in `core.siege.ThreatRules`):
- **Roll** once per day at `dayTime` 1000, `p = min(RAID_P_MAX 0.25, RAID_P_BASE 0.02 + RAID_P_PER_THREAT 0.03 × T)`; conditions: gamerules
  `minecraft:raids` and `villageheart:random_raids` (new, default true) on, difficulty not PEACEFUL, tier ≥ `RAID_MIN_TIER` VILLAGE (a
  Hamlet has no Barracks), at least `RAID_GRACE_DAYS` (7) days since the claim, at least `RAID_GAP_DAYS` (5) days since the last raid
  of any origin ended, no raid tracked, no pending raid. Expected: a Village of 10 adults, 50 wealth, 100 defence cells (T ≈ 2.0) rolls
  8 %/day: about one raid every 12 days with the gap; a rich walled Town (T ≈ 8) hits the 25 % cap: about one every 9 days.
- A won roll creates `PendingRaid(rolledDay, startTick)` with `startTick` a seeded time in `[2000, 11000]` of that day (daylight: the
  guards' day shift, villagers awake), saved in `threats.pending`. It **starts** at the first tick ≥ `startTick` (any later day too)
  at which the village is loaded (3.2 #13) for `RAID_LOADED_TICKS` (600) in a row; the warning chat "Raiders are gathering near %s!"
  goes out when that 600-tick count begins. Nobody near → it waits (postponed, no expiry: the village is raided when someone comes
  back; the Overview shows "A raid is coming"). A Bad Omen raid on the village while one is pending cancels the pending one.
- **Start** = the same public path as `RaidStart` (3.2 #8): `level.getRaids().createOrExtendRaid(player, heart)` with the nearest
  non-spectator player in range **[verified: needs only a non-spectator player and the `raids` rule; the centre is the mean of
  occupied `#village` POIs within 64 blocks of the Heart]**, then `raid.setRaidOmenLevel(omen)` (3.14) and `setDirty()`. The player gets
  no effect and no stat (`absorbRaidOmen` returns false without the effect **[verified]**). Null (rule off, no POI, Nether) → the
  pending raid is dropped with a WARN. The tracked-raid path (3.2 #2) then treats it like any raid; `RaidRecord` gains `origin`
  (`BAD_OMEN | RANDOM | TEST`).

### 3.14 Threat score, raid scaling and rewards *(r2)*

`core.siege.Threat.score(adults, wealth, defenceCells, tier)` = `adults / 10 + wealth / 100 + defenceCells / 200 + tierBonus`
(`tierBonus` HAMLET 0, VILLAGE 0.5, TOWN 1); **wealth** = treasury emeralds + stockpile value, with
`core.siege.ItemValue` (emerald 1, diamond 2, iron/copper/gold ingot 0.25, any other item 1/64 emerald; all *(tunable)*), from the
stockpile totals the Heart already reads. All weights *(tunable)*.

`core.siege.RaidScale.of(T)` → `RaidPlan(int omen, int extraVindicators, int extraRavagers)`:
- `omen = clamp(1 + floor(T / 3), 1, 5)` (vanilla: omen > 1 adds the bonus wave and raises the enchant odds and the hero amplifier
  **[verified `hasBonusWave`, `getEnchantOdds`, `HERO_OF_THE_VILLAGE` amplifier `omen − 1`]**). Random raids use it; a Bad Omen raid keeps
  its vanilla omen.
- Extra raiders, **every raid tracked by the village** (random and Bad Omen): per wave `extraVindicators = min(4, floor(T / 2))`, from
  wave 2; `extraRavagers = min(2, floor(T / 4))`, on waves 3 and later. When `VillageRaids` sees `getGroupsSpawned()` rise, it spawns them
  with `raid.joinRaid(level, wave, raider, spawnPos, false)` **[verified public]** at the position of that wave's first raider, created
  with `EntityType.X.create(level, EntitySpawnReason.EVENT)` and `finalizeSpawn` like vanilla. Vanilla's wave count stays (it is fixed
  at creation from the difficulty: `numGroups` is final **[verified]**), so "more waves" comes only from the omen bonus wave.
- Example: T = 2 → omen 1, 1 extra vindicator from wave 2, no extra ravager; T = 8 → omen 3 (bonus wave), 4 vindicators, 2 ravagers.
- **Reward** for a VICTORY (any origin): `reward = min(RAID_REWARD_MAX 40, 4 + 2 × wavesSpawned + 3 × (omen − 1) + extraRaidersKilled)`
  emeralds into the treasury (`Treasury.deposit`, A16), a chat line to Resident+ ("Victory! The raid on %s is beaten: +%s emeralds to the
  treasury"), recorded in `RaidSummary.reward` and the metric `raid.reward`. Vanilla Hero of the Village for players stays. A siege
  pays nothing (zombies drop their own loot).

### 3.15 Upkeep: alert and builder repairs *(r2)*

**Alert.** `AlertCode.DEFENCE_DAMAGED` (appended): WARNING "Walls damaged: %s broken, %s cracked" while any counting cell is breached or
cracked; it turns **DANGER** with text "Wall breached! %s holes" when a breach exists during a raid or siege. Ordered after RAID/SIEGE
(3.2 #7 keeps RAID first). Chat to Resident+: "The wall of %s was breached near (%s)" on the first breach of a raid/siege/night (at most
once per `BREACH_NOTICE_TICKS` 1200), and "The walls of %s are repaired" when the last breach is closed.

**Repair job** (M20 builder, A15). `core.siege.RepairPlanner.next(breached, cracked, cells, heart, stock) → Optional<RepairStep>`
(pure): breached GATE cells first, then breached STONE cells from the bottom layer up (feet and head cells close a hole fastest), then
cracked cells with damage ≥ half HP, then lighter cracks; inside a class nearest to the Heart, ties (y, x, z). Costs: a breached cell
**1 item of its material** (cobblestone; any `#minecraft:fence_gates` for a gate, placed with the blueprint's state); a cracked cell with
damage ≥ half HP **1 cobblestone**, a lighter crack free; each repair is one builder action (1 s at level 1, M20 pace and M15 level
speed) plus the walk. The builder **prefers repairs to new builds** (`REPAIR_BEFORE_BUILDS` true) and repairs only in its WORK phase and
while no raid or siege is running (it shelters then, 3.2 #4). A step reuses M20: the reservation is keyed by a fixed per-village repair
UUID, the material is fetched from Storage (`FETCH`), the builder walks to a stand point of the cell (`StandPoints`, from outside the wall
when the outside is in the claim, else inside), and places with `BuildExecutor.place` using the site's blueprint step for that cell, so
the state (gate facing, closed) is the blueprint's. A cell with a player's block or an entity in it is skipped (BLOCKED / ENTITY, M20
rules; never broken). Short of material the builder status says "Repairs need 5 more cobblestone" and the alert stays: **an unrepaired
breach stays open**. Repairs give builder XP like placements (M15 `BLOCK_PLACED`). No builder hired → nothing repairs; the alert says so
("no builder").

### 3.16 Threat flag for guards and workers *(r2)*

`VillageThreats.underAttack(server)` = a raid is tracked **or** a siege is running. M18's `RaidCheck` seam is set to it (guards come on
duty for sieges too); worker shelter (3.2 #4) uses it as well (at night workers are in bed already, so a siege mostly matters for the
night-shift guards and an early dusk). The death log's `duringRaid` flag becomes `duringThreat` with a `threat` id (`raid`, `siege`, or
none); `raid.*` metrics stay raid-only, `siege.*` are separate.

### 3.17 Saved state, gamerules and harness *(r2)*

**Saved:** `VillageState` grows by **two** components only (it has 13; `RecordCodecBuilder.group` allows 16, A9): `DeathLog deaths`
and `ThreatState threats` = `(Optional<RaidRecord> raid, Optional<RaidSummary> lastRaid, Optional<PendingRaid> pending,
Optional<SiegeRecord> siege, Optional<SiegeSummary> lastSiege, int lastRaidEndDay, int lastSiegeDay, DefenceDamage damage)`; one codec
key `threats`, every sub-key lenient optional (3.4). The opened cells are stored per site in `DefenceSite.opened` (a sorted int array of
cell indices; codec key `opened`, optional).

**Gamerules** (Fabric `GameRuleBuilder.forBoolean(true).buildAndRegister(Identifier)` **[verified, fabric-game-rule-api-v1 2.0.3]**,
category MOBS): `villageheart:siege_damage`, `villageheart:random_raids`, `villageheart:sieges`; all default true. The lure has no
gamerule: it only moves mobs, and it is off for villages without walls.

**Harness:** the sim sets `random_raids` and `sieges` to **false** at setup unless the scenario's top-level `"threats": true` (so
`bare`, `starter`, `defended` and `raid` keep exactly the raids they ask for; M26's `full` sets it true). `siege_damage` stays on
everywhere. New actions: `siege` (`{"day": 2, "zombies": 8}`: starts a siege now, the 3.12 spawn rule, bypassing the roll) and
`threats` (`{"random_raids": true, "sieges": true}` mid-run). New metrics (per day, pinned): `defence.cells`, `defence.breached`,
`defence.cracked` (at the record), `defence.blocks_broken` (this day, by cause family `defence.broken.<zombie|ravager|vindicator|creeper|other>`),
`defence.repairs`, `defence.repair_items`, `siege.started`, `siege.zombies`, `siege.killed`, `siege.villager_deaths`, `siege.paths`,
`lure.max_lured`, `raid.origin_random`, `raid.reward`, `raid.extra_raiders`, `threat.score` (T at the record, ×100 as an int),
`entity_ms` (mean ms per tick of the overworld's entity ticking, measured around `ServerLevel.tickNonPassenger` with the same timer as
`village_ms`; reported only, risk 9). `deaths.cause.*` already covers who killed whom. New scenario `sim/scenarios/siege.json` (T-L3-3).

### 3.18 Effect on M26 and the other scenarios *(r2)*

- **M26 `full`** (30 days, no player input, starts at Hamlet, reaches Village): it sets `"threats": true`, so once the village is
  VILLAGE tier, 7 days old and walled, random raids (≈ 3-8 %/day at its likely T of 1-2.5, at least 5 days apart) and sieges (≈ 6-9 %
  of nights, 3 apart) **will** happen: expect 1-2 raids and 1-3 sieges in days 10-30, on reproducible days for a fixed seed (3.2 #12).
  Its criteria (Village tier reached, food never 0 for > 1 day running, treasury never negative, < 1 ms) now have to hold through them:
  the M26 setup needs guards (Village tier unlocks the Barracks) or the deaths will cut food production; the raid reward adds to the
  treasury. The M26 planner should add `sum(raid.victories + raid.losses + raid.stopped) >= …` reporting and decide whether to assert
  survival there; this plan only reports it. If the human prefers a quiet `full`, it sets `"threats": false` (design question 4).
- `bare`, `starter`: no walls → no lure; threats off in the harness → unchanged. `defended`: walls → lure and siege damage at night
  (5.4). `raid`: exactly one raid (the action), lure on.

## 6. Risks, unknowns, out of scope

**Risks** (highest first):
1. **(top) L3 `raid` on Normal against vanilla raiders.** A Normal raid has 5 waves: pillagers (crossbows over walls), vindicators that
   **break doors** (2.1), witches in wave 4, ravagers in waves 3 and 5, an evoker (vexes fly over walls) in wave 5. Guards are villagers
   with swords (M18: no ranged combat, ignore flyers as UNREACHABLE), so vexes are left to the golem. ≥ 75 % of 12 allows 3 deaths;
   victory needs every raider dead, and guards only chase raiders inside the claim. Mitigations in the setup: level-2 guards in full
   iron, the golem, a closed ring with two gates (r2), everyone housed inside the walls, workers sheltering in beds. *(r2)* Harder
   now: vindicators chop the gates in one hit and ravagers open a wall cell in two, so the ring slows the raid rather than stopping it;
   the extra vindicator per wave (T ≈ 3) adds about 4 raiders. The 3-of-3 rule will expose a weak
   defence honestly. If it fails, the builder reports deaths per wave with cause and position and does not loosen anything; options for
   the orchestrator: guards at level 3 / diamond swords in the kit, a second golem, gates instead of gaps; never Easy difficulty or a
   weaker check (both would change the meaning of the test).
2. **Stragglers stop a victory.** A pillager stuck outside the claim (guards do not leave it) can keep the raid alive until vanilla
   drops it (`ticksOutsideRaid`) or the 2-day timeout STOPs the raid, which 3.6 counts as a failure. The summary lists the last raiders'
   positions so this is visible; option: let on-duty guards chase raiders of the tracked raid up to 16 blocks past the claim edge.
3. **Merge order.** M25 needs M18 (hard, including gate opening for the scenario), and for the scenario M14 (tier) and M19 (soft:
   fallbacks in 3.1); M11, M12, M20 and M21 are merged. Rebase on dev before steps 5, 6, 12 and 18; conflicts expected in `WorkerBrain`, `VillageState`/`VillageCodecs` (field count, A9), `HeartFacts`,
   `ActivityKind` (append only) and the harness action lists.
4. **Removing `SetRaidStatus` from the worker brain** changes M7's "vanilla raid behaviours" note in DESIGN §12. Unhired villagers are
   untouched, and the bell still sends workers to HIDE. T-L2-10 pins the split. DESIGN §12 is updated in step 10.
5. **Raids in GameTests leak to other batches** (96-block reach): handled by `reset`/`done`/`finally` stopping raids and discarding
   raiders, plus own environments. A test that dies by timeout still relies on the next `reset` (as M15's side-by-side cleanup does).
6. **Shelter crowding**: 8 workers walking to beds through doors; M9's `WalkTo` false-unreachable history. ShelterBehaviour treats
   UNREACHABLE as "try the next target" (planner order), never as a hang.
7. *(r2)* **"Blocked by a defence block" is a heuristic.** It relies on vanilla partial paths ending next to the obstacle. A zombie
   whose partial path ends at a fence, a river or a player's house hits nothing (good) but also mills about; a wall with a gap 40 blocks
   away gives a reachable path and the zombie walks round (good, that is what a gap is). Mis-hits are bounded by `BreachChoice`'s 2-block,
   facing-the-goal rule and pinned by T-L2-15/T-L2-20. If L2 shows zombies stuck at the ring without hitting it, the fallback is to
   also accept "no movement for 60 ticks while lured" as blocked.
8. *(r2)* **Lure vs the existing scenarios.** Only villages with recorded walls are lured, and sieges/random raids are off in the
   harness by default, so `bare` and `starter` are unaffected (T-L2-23); `defended` (M18) has walls and may change (5.4).
9. *(r2)* **Pathing cost outside the 1 ms budget.** Lured mobs path in entity ticks. Capped at 24 mobs and one path per 40-60 ticks
   each; the sim reports `siege.paths` and `entity_ms` (time in `ServerLevel` entity ticking, measured like `village_ms`), and the
   balance pass (M26) can lower `MAX_LURED`.
10. *(r2)* **Crack overlays are client-side and transient.** Resent every 200 ticks to players within 32 blocks; a client that misses
    one sees a crack late, never a wrong breach (breaches are real blocks). Checked by T-L4-2's third screenshot.
11. *(r2)* **A 1-hit gate** (vindicator) means a raid usually enters through the gates. That is the decided "gates are the weak
    point"; if L3 shows it makes walls pointless, `HP_GATE` is the first tunable to raise (M26), not a rule change.

**Unknowns:** whether the SimOwner's simulation distance keeps the 100-block wave ring entity-ticking (the `raid` action checks and
forces what is missing; `raid.spawn_min_distance` shows a near-centre fallback spawn); whether `createOrExtendRaid`'s centre in the seed-1 village lands inside the claim (the `raid` action checks it and ERRORs

villagers ringing the
bell for the mod; ranged guards or guards fighting vexes/flyers (M18); repairing golems mid-raid (M19 rule stays); celebrations for
workers (they skip vanilla's `CelebrateVillagersSurvivedRaid`); a death log tab or export (the Overview shows 3 rows); alerts for
