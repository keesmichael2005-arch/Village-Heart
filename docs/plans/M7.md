## 1. Goal

The player (through a command for now; M8 adds the Heart screen) hires an adult villager in the claim into a job that has a
free job block inside a valid building. The villager drops its vanilla profession and trades, takes the mod's profession
for that job, and from then on follows the village day: it walks to its job block during work hours, goes home at the end
of the day, sleeps in a bed at night, and carries what it holds to Storage. Vanilla villagers can no longer claim
workstations inside valid buildings, while the same workstation anywhere else stays fully vanilla. Firing turns the
worker back into an ordinary unemployed vanilla villager.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 The spike: brain mixin (a) vs mod task runner (b)

**Decision: (a). Hired villagers get a mod-built brain, installed by one mixin at the head of
`Villager.registerBrainGoals(Brain<Villager>)`. Unhired villagers keep the vanilla brain untouched.**

How the 1.21.11 villager brain works **[verified]**:
- `Villager.makeBrain(Dynamic)` (constructor) and `Villager.refreshBrain(ServerLevel)` (profession change, age boundary,
  `readAdditionalSaveData`) both end in the private `registerBrainGoals(Brain<Villager>)`, which adds the CORE, WORK, MEET,
  REST, IDLE, PANIC, PRE_RAID, RAID and HIDE packages from `VillagerGoalPackages` and calls `brain.setSchedule(EnvironmentAttributes.VILLAGER_ACTIVITY)`.
- **New in 1.21.11:** the schedule is an `EnvironmentAttribute<Activity>` sampled from the data-driven timeline
  `minecraft:villager_schedule` (`Timelines.VILLAGER_SCHEDULE`, keyframes IDLE 10, WORK 2000, MEET 9000, IDLE 11000, REST 12000),
  not the old `Schedule` class. `Brain.updateActivityFromSchedule(EnvironmentAttributeSystem, long, Vec3)` falls back to
  `Activity.IDLE` when no schedule is set. `UpdateActivityFromSchedule`, `VillagerCalmDown`, `ResetRaidStatus`,
  `SetHiddenState` and `CelebrateVillagersSurvivedRaid` all call it.
- Profession management lives entirely in the CORE package: `ValidateNearbyPoi(heldJobSite, JOB_SITE)`,
  `ValidateNearbyPoi(acquirableJobSite, POTENTIAL_JOB_SITE)`, `PoiCompetitorScan`, `AcquirePoi(acquirableJobSite, …)`
  (every 20-40 ticks, 48 blocks, `PoiManager.Occupancy.HAS_SPACE`), `GoToPotentialJobSite`, `YieldJobSite`,
  `AssignProfessionFromJobSite` (first profession in `BuiltInRegistries.VILLAGER_PROFESSION` whose `heldJobSite` matches,
  then `refreshBrain`) and `ResetProfession` (JOB_SITE absent, profession not NONE/NITWIT, xp 0, level ≤ 1 → NONE).
- Door opening is a brain behaviour (`InteractWithDoor` in CORE), not part of navigation.

Spike (8 throwaway GameTests, one environment each, ~800 ticks, results logged; two full `runGameTest` runs, 46/46 green on the second):

| Spike test | Result | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| (a) brain worker in a closed 2×3 cell, closed oak door, composter 5 blocks away, day time 2000 | **reached the job block at tick 60**, stayed there, profession `villageheart:spike_worker`, 0 offers | the mod brain keeps vanilla door use, movement and look; the mod profession sticks |
| (b) runner (brain tick skipped by a `@Redirect` on `Brain.tick` in `customServerAiStep` during work hours, `getNavigation().moveTo` every 20 ticks), same closed door | **never arrived**: stuck at 3.83 blocks in front of the door for 800 ticks | suspending the brain loses `InteractWithDoor`; (b) would have to re-implement doors, panic, raid hiding, sleeping |
| (b) runner, door open | reached at tick 40 | the runner itself works; doors are what break it |
| "naive": mod profession set, vanilla brain kept (no mixin) | **profession reset to `minecraft:none` within 200 ticks** | vanilla fights back: the mod profession has no JOB_SITE memory, so `ResetProfession` fires. (b) has the same problem whenever the brain runs (off-hours) |
| (a) worker hired at day time 13000, bed 5 blocks away | acquired HOME through `AcquirePoi`, **asleep by tick 100**, all night; after `setDayTime(2000)` awake, activity WORK, at the job block (0.75) within 60 ticks | going home and sleeping can reuse vanilla REST behaviours |
| (a) worker saved with `saveWithoutId`, discarded, re-created with `EntityType.create(VILLAGER, TagValueInput, level, LOAD)` | came back with the mod profession and the worker brain (activity WORK at once), walked to the job block | `readAdditionalSaveData → refreshBrain` re-enters the mixin after load |
| unemployed vanilla villager next to a composter whose POI ticket the mod took with `PoiManager.take` | **stayed `minecraft:none` for 1200 ticks** (run 1) | a held ticket is enough to block `AcquirePoi` |
| same, composter not reserved | became `minecraft:farmer` (2 offers) by tick 200 | vanilla is untouched outside reservations |

What fought back, and the fixes now in this plan:
1. `ResetProfession` resets any mod profession that has no JOB_SITE memory → the worker brain omits all vanilla
   profession behaviours (3.5).
2. In run 2 the reservation `take` at test tick 5 **failed** (the POI did not exist yet) and the vanilla villager became a
   farmer. **[verified]** `ServerLevel.updatePOIOnBlockStateChange` adds and removes POIs through `getServer().execute(...)`,
   so a just-placed workstation has no POI for an unknown number of ticks. → Reservation is a reconcile loop that retries and
   evicts vanilla claimants (3.6), and tests wait until the POI exists before reserving or asserting.
3. Calling `GameTestHelper.runAfterDelay` from inside a `runAfterDelay` callback modifies the map that `GameTestInfo.tickInternal`
   iterates: duplicate callbacks, then an NPE in `Object2LongOpenHashMap$MapIterator` that **crashed the server**. → GameTests
   schedule every step up front from the test body, or use `startSequence()`. Added to TESTING.md in step 11.
4. A ticket leaked by a crashed run stays in the POI file (`PoiRecord.Packed` saves `free_tickets` **[verified]**), and the
   GameTest world is reused between runs. → Tests release what they take in `done`, and assert on a fresh position.

Why not (b), summarised: it needs a mixin anyway (to skip `Brain.tick` and to stop `ResetProfession` off-hours), it loses doors,
panic, raid hiding and sleeping, and M9-M19 would rebuild what the brain already does. (a) costs one mixin into one private
method and keeps every vanilla behaviour we want, by picking them into the worker packages. Rejected variant (a'): append mod
behaviours at the tail of the vanilla brain. It keeps `ResetProfession`, `PoiCompetitorScan` and job-site acquisition, and
vanilla's 2000-9000 WORK window instead of §4's 1000-11000.

`JobSlots` (pure, the L1 slot accounting):

### 3.4 Glue: professions, attachment, mixin (`dev.villageheart.worker`)

**Professions** (`ModProfessions.register()`): one per `JobType`, key `villageheart:<id>` in `BuiltInRegistries.VILLAGER_PROFESSION`
**[spike]**:
`Registry.register(BuiltInRegistries.VILLAGER_PROFESSION, key, new VillagerProfession(Component.translatable("entity.villageheart.villager." + id), PoiType.NONE, PoiType.NONE, ImmutableSet.of(), ImmutableSet.of(), null))`.
- `heldJobSite = acquirableJobSite = PoiType.NONE` (always false) **[verified]**: `AssignProfessionFromJobSite`'s registry
  scan never picks a mod profession, and vanilla `AcquirePoi` never runs for one. The mod alone assigns them.
- **No trades** **[verified + spike]**: `Villager.updateTrades` looks the profession key up in `VillagerTrades.TRADES` (and
  `EXPERIMENTAL_TRADES`); a missing key adds nothing, so `getOffers()` is empty, and `mobInteract` with empty offers shakes the
  head and never opens the trade screen. `setVillagerData` with a new profession sets `offers = null`, so old trades are gone.
- Textures **[verified]**: `VillagerProfessionLayer` draws `<ns>:textures/entity/villager/profession/<path>.png` (namespace = the
  profession key's) plus the vanilla level badge. A missing file is the purple/black missing texture, so M7 ships 9 overlays
  `assets/villageheart/textures/entity/villager/profession/<id>.png` (64×64, apron/sash colour + a small job glyph on the chest),
  drawn by `AssetGenerator` (`ProfessionArt`) and pinned by `GeneratedAssetsTest`. No `.mcmeta` (hat = NONE). Zombie
  villagers would look up `zombie_villager/profession/…`; M7 avoids that by resetting the profession on conversion (3.7).
- Lang: `entity.villageheart.villager.<id>` for all 9 (LangCoverageTest extended to `JobType`).

**Attachment** (`WorkerAttachments.WORKER`) **[verified, Fabric data-attachment-api-v1 1.8.48]**:
```java
public static final AttachmentType<WorkerData> WORKER = AttachmentRegistry.create(
        Identifier.fromNamespaceAndPath("villageheart", "worker"), b -> b.persistent(WorkerCodecs.WORKER_DATA));
```
No `copyOnDeath()` (so it is not copied by `MOB_CONVERSION`, which Fabric's own `AttachmentEntrypoint` uses for transfers), no
`syncWith` (M8's screen uses its own packets). `WorkerCodecs` (DFU only, like `VillageCodecs`) with pinned keys
`job, building, job_block, level, xp, needs{fed, hungry_days, housed, paid}`; `level`/`xp`/`needs` are `optionalFieldOf` with defaults.
**Load order [verified]**: Fabric's `EntityMixin` reads attachments at the `INVOKE` of `readAdditionalSaveData` inside
`Entity.load`, i.e. before `Villager.readAdditionalSaveData` calls `refreshBrain`. So the mixin sees the attachment on load
(the spike's reload test proves the same path end to end).

**Mixin** (the mod's first; `src/main/resources/villageheart.mixins.json`, package `dev.villageheart.mixin`, `"required": true`,
`compatibilityLevel JAVA_21`, `injectors.defaultRequire 1`; listed in `fabric.mod.json` `"mixins"`). Loom generates the refmap **[spike]**.
```java
@Mixin(Villager.class)
abstract class VillagerBrainMixin {
    @Inject(method = "registerBrainGoals", at = @At("HEAD"), cancellable = true)
    private void villageheart$workerBrain(Brain<Villager> brain, CallbackInfo ci) {
        Villager self = (Villager) (Object) this;

```
That is the only mixin. `WorkerBrain.install` records the brain in a `Collections.newSetFromMap(new WeakHashMap<>())`
so the worker system can self-heal a worker whose brain is not the worker brain (`refreshBrain`).

### 3.6 Job-block reservation (`JobSiteReservations`, a `VillageSystem`)

Design rule (§3.4): every job block in a **valid** building that has a vanilla POI (`PoiTypes.forState(state).isPresent()`; the
3 mod job blocks have none and need nothing) is reserved, assigned or not. Mechanism **[verified + spike]**: the mod holds
the POI's one ticket (`PoiType` FARMER etc. have `maxTickets = 1`), so `AcquirePoi`'s `Occupancy.HAS_SPACE` search never returns it.

State: `VillageSavedData` gains `reservedJobBlocks : Set<HeartLocation>` (codec key `reserved_job_blocks`, optional, default empty).
Every `RESERVATION_INTERVAL_TICKS`, inside the system's section:
1. `desired` = the job blocks of `JobSiteSource.sites(server, job)` for every `JobType`, filtered to blocks with a POI type.
2. For `p` in `desired − held`: if `poi.getType(p)` is empty (async add pending) → retry next pass. Else **evict** vanilla
   claimants: non-worker villagers within 64 blocks whose `JOB_SITE` or `POTENTIAL_JOB_SITE` memory equals `GlobalPos(dim, p)` get
   that memory erased and `poi.release(p)` once (their ticket). (A vanilla villager that loses its JOB_SITE behaves exactly as when
   a player breaks its workstation: `ResetProfession` if it never traded.) Then `poi.take(h -> true, (h, b) -> b.equals(p), p, 1)`;
   on success add `p` to `held` and `setDirty`.
3. For `p` in `held ∩ desired`: if the POI exists and `getInSquare(h -> true, p, 0, HAS_SPACE)` contains `p` (the record was
   re-created with a free ticket, e.g. the block was replaced) → take again. If the POI is gone (block broken) → drop `p` from `held`.
4. For `p` in `held − desired` (building invalid or unregistered, block outside slots): if the POI exists and is occupied →
   `poi.release(p)`; drop `p` from `held`. `release` throws `IllegalStateException` for a never-registered section **[verified]**,
   so it is only called after `getType(p).isPresent()`.
5. On Heart release (`VillageClaims.releaseAt`): release every held ticket whose POI exists, clear the set (3.7).

Because the set is saved with the village data and the POI tickets with the POI files, both survive a save. A crash between the two
saves can leak a ticket (a workstation vanilla can never claim); this is noted as a risk, not handled.

### 3.7 Hire, fire and lifecycle (`Workers`, `WorkerSystem`)

`Workers.hire(ServerLevel, Villager, JobType, Optional<HeartLocation> preferredBuilding) : HireResult` (server thread):
1. Refuse unless: a claim exists, the villager is alive, adult, in the Heart's dimension and within `CLAIM_RADIUS` of the Heart
   (`core.ClaimGeometry.inClaim`, a sphere; T-L1-9 covers the BACKLOG note that T-L2-2 never tested a box corner), and not already a worker.
2. `JobSlots.decide(sites, workers, …)`; a refusal returns with its reason (and a translatable message).
3. While the old profession is still set: `villager.releasePoi(JOB_SITE)`, `releasePoi(POTENTIAL_JOB_SITE)` **[verified: the
   predicate uses the current profession]**, erase both memories, `setTradingPlayer(null)` if trading.
4. Set the attachment `WorkerData.hired(...)`; append the `WorkerRecord` (`setDirty`).
5. `setVillagerData(data.withProfession(holder).withLevel(1))`, then `refreshBrain(level)` → mixin installs the worker brain.

`Workers.fire(ServerLevel, Villager)`: remove the record and the attachment, `setVillagerData(withProfession(NONE).withLevel(1))`,
`setVillagerXp(0)`, erase WALK_TARGET/LOOK_TARGET, `refreshBrain` → vanilla brain. The slot is free at once; the block stays
reserved while its building is valid. `Workers.fireById(server, UUID)` for unloaded villagers removes only the record.

Command (`/villageheart hire <entity> <job> [building x y z]`, `/villageheart fire <entity>`, `/villageheart workers`; permission
level 2; `CommandRegistrationCallback.EVENT`, `EntityArgument.entity()`, a `StringArgumentType.word()` suggesting `JobType` ids).
It calls the same API. Rank checks are M8's (packets); the command is for tests and ops.

`WorkerSystem` (a `VillageSystem`, every 20 ticks) reconciles registry ↔ entities:
- record whose `building` is no longer a registered building (A4) → fire (the marker was removed). Record whose building is invalid
  or whose job block is no longer a slot → keep; the worker idles near its building (`GoToJobBlock` targets the record's block
  anyway), status reason `WORKPLACE_INVALID` (M8 shows it).
- loaded villager with the attachment but no record (fired while unloaded, Heart broken while unloaded) → restore vanilla (`fire` path).
  Found through `ServerEntityEvents.ENTITY_LOAD` (queued, handled in the next system tick, not inside the load callback).
- loaded worker whose brain is not in the worker-brain set → `refreshBrain`.
Events: `ServerLivingEntityEvents.AFTER_DEATH` (worker died → remove record); `ServerLivingEntityEvents.MOB_CONVERSION`
**[verified]** (worker → zombie villager or witch: remove record; if the result is a `ZombieVillager` with a mod profession, set its
profession to NONE so the cured villager is an ordinary one). Heart release: fire every loaded worker, clear the worker list and
the reservations.

### 3.8 Deposit trips (`DepositTrip`, step 9; needs A5)

"Carrying" = the vanilla villager inventory (`AbstractVillager.getInventory()`, a `SimpleContainer(8)` **[verified]**). Full when
the carried item count reaches `WORKER_CARRY_STACKS` × the first stack's max size. Start condition: full, or phase GO_HOME with
anything carried. Then: `Stockpiles.depositTarget(level, pos, stack)`; none → status `STORAGE_FULL`, keep the items, end; else set
WALK_TARGET to the chest (closeEnough 1); within 2 blocks → `Stockpiles.deposit` for each stack, keep remainders; end. Timeout
1200 ticks (unreachable chest) → end with `STORAGE_UNREACHABLE`. The trip state is transient (not saved); after a reload the
condition is simply re-evaluated.

### 3.9 Not changed
No networking, no screen (M8), no new blocks, no new POI types, no timeline/data-pack files. Assets: 9 profession PNGs + lang only.

## 6. Risks & unknowns, out of scope

**Risks**
1. **(top) Brain ownership.** Other mods or a future Minecraft version may also touch `registerBrainGoals`; a vanilla
   behaviour we reuse may start reading memories the worker brain does not register (`Brain.provider` uses the vanilla
   `MEMORY_TYPES`, so every vanilla memory is registered; a mod memory would need a second mixin into `brainProvider`, not planned).
   Mitigation: one mixin, `require = 1` (fails loudly at start), T-L2-2/3/4/9 cover the brain end to end.
2. **POI asynchrony and races.** POI add/remove is deferred (`server.execute`); a vanilla villager can claim a block in the
   window before the reservation pass. Handled by eviction (T-L2-5c). A crash between the village-data save and the POI save can
   leak one ticket.
3. **Real-time cost of 1-day GameTests.** T-L2-5 runs 24000 ticks; with nothing else in its batch this is seconds to a minute.
   Do not raise its tick count; if the run gets slow, keep the 1-day test and shorten nothing else.
4. **Night tests and mobs.** The GameTest level is NORMAL difficulty; unlit night tests can lose villagers to zombies. Light them.
5. **Unseeded villager AI.** Walking times vary; thresholds have slack (the spike reached in 40-60 ticks against limits of 400-3000).
6. **Dependencies.** Steps 5-9 need M5 (and M6 for step 9). If M5's `BuildingView` differs, only `BuildingJobSites` changes.
7. **Sleeping in someone else's bed.** Vanilla HOME acquisition may pick a House bed another villager uses; M11 decides homes.

**Out of scope (later milestones):** real jobs (M9+), the Heart screen and rank checks (M8), dinner/needs effects (M11), wages and
strikes (M12), XP gain and level effects (M15), guard shifts (M18; guards follow the normal day in M7), preferring House beds (M11),
held-item job identity and work particles (with the real jobs), mod POI types for the new job blocks (not needed: nothing in
vanilla claims them).
