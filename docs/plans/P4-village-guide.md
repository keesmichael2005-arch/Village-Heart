# P4 plan: village guide

The Heart's Guide tab. It starts when the village is claimed. Each row is a few words. The current row has one sentence. Recipes stay in the recipe book.

A row checks off when the village already has that thing, in any order, and stays checked. The book runs through Town: house, storage, emeralds, field, jobs, the builder, mine, hunt, barracks, training yard, guard, market, wall, gate, lamp, smithy, golem, watchtower.

Three needs can show above the list and then go away: emeralds short of tomorrow's wages, a farmer with no planted field, a guard with no Training Yard. Guards already gain no training experience without a yard. The Barracks blueprint places the yard on the barracks wall, with two targets inside a fence. The Builder's Table also has a starter plan for every other workplace, including a Hunting Grove. The Mineshaft stays player-dug.

The page is the achievement list. It is not a Minecraft advancement. The next three steps are plain text on the left. The advancement sound plays when a step is checked off. Click a step for its short instructions and, for a craft, the recipe.

Saved on the world claim as `guide`, a list of done task ids. An unknown id is dropped. Old saves load with an empty list.
