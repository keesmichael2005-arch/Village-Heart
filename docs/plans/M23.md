## 1. Goal

A village that has room and food grows by itself. At each dawn, when a valid House has a free bed, the stockpile holds at least 3 food
days and at least two adults are fed and housed, the mod picks two of them; that morning they walk to each other and have a baby
(vanilla villager breeding, with the hearts). At most one baby is born per day, and the baby gets the free House bed. Without a free
House bed, without food, or without fed and housed adults, no baby is born, even if vanilla alone would allow it. Babies are ordinary
vanilla villagers and can be hired once they are adults.

### 3.2 Rules

1. **Who counts.** An *adult resident* is a loaded villager in `VillageCensus.of` that is not a baby, not `AwayTrips.isAway`, has no
   `NoAI`, and is in the Heart's level. It is **ready** when it is also **fed** (`DietAttachments.read(v, claim).fed()`), **housed**
   (`Housing.housed(server, v, ownBuilding)`, with `ownBuilding` = the worker's building for a worker, empty otherwise) and has
   `getAge() == 0` (vanilla's breeding cooldown is over; a parent is at 6000 for 6000 ticks after a birth). Sleeping does not matter at
   dawn (everyone is asleep then); the courtship (#5) waits until they are awake. Workers and non-workers both count (DESIGN §7 says
   "adult villagers"), so hired workers breed too (M7 kept `VillagerMakeLove` in the worker IDLE package for this).
2. **Food.** `FoodMath.daysLeft(Dinner.nutrition(server), adults, children)` over the same census (all adults and babies, ready or
   not, the Overview's number) must be present and `>= Balance.GROWTH_MIN_FOOD_DAYS` (3.0, inclusive; *tunable*).
3. **Free bed.** A *free House bed* is a bed head in `Housing.beds(server).houseBeds()` (valid Houses only: Barracks and other own-building
   beds do not count, DESIGN §7 "in a valid House") that is loaded, still a bed block, not `OCCUPIED`, has a HOME POI and that POI has a
   free ticket (`BedPoi.hasSpace`). A bed someone holds (a resident, a worker, the mod's own reservation, #6) is not free.
4. **The dawn decision** (`core.growth.GrowthRules.decide`, pure). Inputs: `freeBeds`, `daysLeft`, the ready candidates, today's day
   index, the recently failed pairs. Output `Decision(Optional<Pair>, Reason)`. The checks run in the order `NOBODY` (no adult resident at
   all, so food days are empty), `NO_FREE_BED`, `LOW_FOOD`, `TOO_FEW_READY`; the first failing check is the reason, and `LICENSED` is the
   result when none fails. Pair choice: the two ready candidates with the
   smallest distance between them (they usually sleep in the same House), ties by the lower (UUID, UUID) pair in `UUID.compareTo`
   order; every pair that lapsed with `CANNOT_MEET` within the last `Balance.FAILED_PAIR_DAYS` (3) days is skipped
   while another pair exists (saved list `failed_pairs: [{a, b, day}]`, pruned at each dawn), so two villagers who cannot reach each
   other do not block growth for ever, not even every other day. The decision is made **only at dawn** (DESIGN §7 "at each dawn"); nothing is re-decided
   during the day.
5. **Courtship** (the "make them willing" part, glue `growth.Courtship`, rules in `core.growth.CourtshipWindow` and
   `core.growth.License`). A licence lives from its dawn until the next dawn (or a birth). Its parents are fixed: the licence does not re-apply
   the census rules of #1 (test parents with `NoAI` stay licensed). `GrowthSystem` checks every tick (2 villagers, cheap):
   - **Stale**: `licence.day != DailyClock.dayIndex(dayTime)` (the Heart was unloaded over a dawn, or `/time set` changed the day index
     before the dawn run) → lapse `NEXT_DAWN` at once. The gate (#7) checks the same, so a stale licence never exempts anyone.
   - If a parent is unloaded: wait. Dead, a baby, away (`AwayTrips`) or no longer in the claim: lapse `PARENT_GONE`.
   - **Window**: both must be awake, `brain.isActive(Activity.IDLE)`, and the day phase must leave room for a full `VillagerMakeLove`
     run (≤ 325 ticks after its start **[verified: `275 + nextInt(50)`]**) plus the approach: `CourtshipWindow.canSeed(phase, kindA,
     kindB)` with `Kind(role ∈ {WORKER, UNEMPLOYED, PROFESSIONAL}, hasMeetingPoint)`. Role: a hired worker is WORKER; otherwise a villager
     with a `JOB_SITE` memory is PROFESSIONAL, and one without (unemployed, nitwit) is UNEMPLOYED. An activity whose required memory is
     missing falls back to the default IDLE **[verified: `setActiveActivityIfPossible` → `useDefaultActivity`]**, so the IDLE windows are:
     WORKER [0, 1000); UNEMPLOYED with a meeting point [10, 9000) and [11000, 12000), without one [10, 12000); PROFESSIONAL with a meeting
     point [10, 2000) and [11000, 12000), without one [10, 2000) and [9000, 12000). Seed only while `phase + Balance.COURTSHIP_MARGIN`
     (400) is still inside the same IDLE window of **both**. Outside a window the licence waits (until the approach rule lapses it).
   - **Approach**: if they are farther apart than `Balance.COURTSHIP_SEED_DISTANCE` (4.0 blocks) or not in each other's
     `NEAREST_VISIBLE_LIVING_ENTITIES` (`BehaviorUtils.entityIsVisible` **[verified]**), walk them to each other with
     `BehaviorUtils.setWalkAndLookTargetMemories(a, b, 0.5F, 1)` **[verified, public]** and the reverse. This is re-issued every 10 ticks,
     and only when that villager's `WALK_TARGET` is absent or does not track the partner (an `EntityTracker` on it), so `MoveToTargetSink`
     is not made to re-path every tick; `CANT_REACH_WALK_TARGET_SINCE` is read before any overwrite. IDLE strolls only start when
     `WALK_TARGET` is absent **[verified: `SetWalkTargetFromLookTarget`, `VillageBoundRandomStroll` require it absent]**, so a stroll can
     take a villager off course for at most 10 ticks. The licence lapses with `CANNOT_MEET` (saved, #4) when they have not met after
     `Balance.COURTSHIP_APPROACH_TICKS` (400, below the shortest window of 590 ticks) of window time, when either has
     `CANT_REACH_WALK_TARGET_SINCE` older than 200 ticks, or when today's last seed window closes while they are still approaching.
   - **Seed**: check that the reserved bed (#6) is still valid; top up each parent's vanilla `foodLevel` to
     `Villager.BREEDING_FOOD_THRESHOLD` (12) if it is lower, remembering the old value **on the first seed only** (a re-seed after an abort
     keeps the first remembered value; accessor mixin, 3.3); set `BREED_TARGET` on the **initiator only** (the licence's `a`, the lower
     UUID) to the partner, as vanilla's `InteractWith` does. From the next brain tick vanilla's `VillagerMakeLove` runs in the initiator
     (gate behaviour, IDLE, priority 3) and moves both through `lockGazeAndWalkToEachOther`. One behaviour instance means one
     `birthTimestamp`, one birth tick and one `stop` per seed. Seeding both would run two instances, each with its own birth tick and each
     calling `eatAndDigestFood` on both parents, and one instance's `stop` would look like an abort while the other still gives birth. The food top-up stands for the dinner the village already paid for
     (DESIGN §6; the stockpile's food days are a gate, not a cost: nothing extra is taken from Storage): no bread is taken from the parents' inventories (M10's kept food stays for the worker), and `eatAndDigestFood` then
     digests exactly the 12 points again **[verified: `eatUntilFull` eats only while `foodLevel < 12`, then `digestFood(12)`]**.
   - **Birth** (mixin hook, 3.3): the licence becomes `BORN` (with the child's UUID and the day), the parents' `foodLevel` is what vanilla
     left. One INFO line `[growth] day D: <a> and <b> had a baby <c> (bed x,y,z)`.
   - **Abort** (the initiator's MakeLove stopped without a birth, mixin hook): WARN line with the reason (3.5), `aborts++`; the bed stays
     reserved unless vanilla consumed it (#6), and if the window still allows and `seeds < Balance.COURTSHIP_MAX_SEEDS` (3) it approaches
     and seeds again. After the fix (3.5) an abort is expected to be rare. The regular L2 tests assert the birth and log the abort count;
     the "no more parked-gap aborts" claim is proved by the deterministic T-L2-5 and by the 100-round probe of step 9 (recorded in section
     8), not by one run. So the abort is neither hidden by a retry nor turned into a flaky assertion.
   - **Lapse/expiry** (next dawn, release, `PARENT_GONE`, `CANNOT_MEET`, max seeds): restore each parent's `foodLevel` to
     `min(current, remembered)`, release a held bed, clear `BREED_TARGET` on both, log the reason.
6. **The baby's bed.** **At dawn, when the licence is created**, the mod takes the free House bed nearest to the pair's midpoint
   (`poi.take(t -> t.is(PoiTypes.HOME), (t, p) -> p.equals(bed), bed, 1)` **[verified signature]**; the same call M11's rehome uses).
   Holding the ticket from dawn on keeps a homeless villager's vanilla `AcquirePoi` and M11's rehome (every 100 ticks) off it until the
   birth (both need `HAS_SPACE`); a bed reserved only at seeding could be gone by then. Vanilla's
   `VillagerMakeLove.takeVacantBed` would otherwise take the **first** free HOME POI within 48 blocks in section order (not the nearest,
   not necessarily a House bed) and needs a path to it **[verified: `PoiManager.take` = `getInRange(…HAS_SPACE).filter(…).findFirst()`]**.
   A mixin (3.3) makes `takeVacantBed` return the reserved bed for a licensed initiator, after checking that the block is still a bed
   and its HOME POI still exists (otherwise it returns empty: vanilla's `NO_BED` path, an abort; `Courtship` then reserves another free
   House bed if there is one, else the licence lapses `NO_BED`). From the moment the mixin returns the bed, the licence marks it
   **handed over** and never releases it itself: `giveBedToChild` sets the baby's `HOME` to it, and when `breed` fails vanilla itself
   calls `poi.release(bed)` **[verified]**. Before the hand-over the licence releases its ticket on every lapse (next dawn, release,
   parent gone, cannot meet). The bed is also checked at seeding (broken since dawn → re-reserve or lapse `NO_BED`).
   **Unlicensed breeding just outside the claim** cannot take a House bed inside it either: the same mixin, at RETURN and for an
   unlicensed villager, releases and drops a result that lies inside the claim (vanilla then finds no bed: angry particles).
7. **The gate: no unlicensed breeding in the claim.** A mixin on `Villager.canBreed()` (RETURN) returns `false` for a villager on the
   server whose position is inside the claim (`ClaimGeometry.inClaim`, Heart's dimension) unless it is a parent of today's licence that is
   not yet `BORN`. `canBreed` is what both vanilla `InteractWith(…BREED_TARGET…)` filters and `VillagerMakeLove.isBreedingPossible` read
   **[verified]**, so in the claim vanilla breeding happens only through a licence. This is what makes "1 birth per day" and "no free bed
   → no baby" true: without the gate, villagers that picked up bread from a farmer (vanilla food sharing, `TradeWithVillager`) would
   breed at random with any free bed within 48 blocks, also outside Houses. Outside the claim vanilla is untouched. The gate reads a
   static snapshot (`growth.BreedingGate`: Heart location + dimension + the licensed UUIDs, or none) that `GrowthSystem` refreshes when
   the claim or the licence changes; `canBreed` is called often (filters run for every nearby villager), so the check is a few field reads
   and one sphere test (dimension compare first, then squared distance against a precomputed r²), no map lookups by position. Server
   only (`level() instanceof ServerLevel`). One snapshot is enough because the mod has one village per world (`VillageClaims.current`
   returns at most one claim, M1); if that ever changes the snapshot becomes a list. The snapshot carries the licence day, so a stale
   licence (#5) exempts nobody.
8. **One birth per day.** Structural: one licence per day index, one pair per licence, the licence ends at the birth, and vanilla puts
   both parents at age 6000 (cooldown) **[verified: `breed` → `setAge(6000)` on both]**, and nobody else in the claim can breed (#7).
   Births are **counted** through the birth hook (3.3), not through `ENTITY_LOAD` of a baby (that event also fires for babies loaded from
   disk, spawn eggs and `/summon`). `WorldClaim.growth` keeps `lastBirthDay`, `birthsTotal` (Overview/L3 later) and the licence.
9. **No catch-up, frozen when unloaded** (DESIGN §6): the decision runs in M12's dawn tick, so it obeys its rules. A dawn while the Heart
   is unloaded never happens; when it loads again on a later day M12 runs the dawn once, at whatever time it is: if that is after the
   window, the licence waits and expires unused at the next dawn. `/time set` jumps fire one dawn per day-index change (M12).
10. **Babies.** Normal vanilla villagers (`getBreedOffspring`, `EntitySpawnReason.BREEDING` **[verified]**), age −24000, HOME = the reserved
    House bed. They eat 5 at dinner (M11) and count as children in food days. `Workers.hire` already refuses them (`NOT_ADULT`); once grown
    (age reaches 0) they can be hired like anyone.

### 3.5 The vanilla breeding abort: root cause and fix

Static reading of `VillagerMakeLove` **[verified]**, `MoveToTargetSink` **[verified]** and `Behavior` **[verified]**:

- `start` calls `lockGazeAndWalkToEachOther(villager, partner, 0.5F, 2)`: both get a `WALK_TARGET` on the other with close-enough
  distance **2**. `MoveToTargetSink.reachedTarget` is `target.currentBlockPosition().distManhattan(mob.blockPosition()) <= 2`: the
  **Manhattan distance between block positions**. It erases the walk target when that holds, and the villager stops.
- `tick` only acts when `villager.distanceToSqr(partner) <= 5.0` (**Euclidean**, about 2.24 blocks). Only then does it re-issue the walk
  targets, and only then can it give birth (`l >= birthTimestamp`).
- `canStillUse` is `l <= birthTimestamp && isBreedingPossible`. So **per behaviour instance** there is exactly one tick (`l == birthTimestamp`,
  275-324 ticks after `start`) on which the birth can happen, and it needs the pair within √5 on that tick. Vanilla normally runs one
  instance (only the `InteractWith` initiator gets `BREED_TARGET`); M7's test seeded both, which gave two instances and two chances, so
  its measured rate (~1 in 16 per window) understates the per-instance rate.
- Two villagers that arrive at block offset (2, 0) or (1, 1) are "reached" but can stand 2.24-3.0 blocks apart (e.g. one just crossed
  into block 0 at x ≈ 0.1 while the other stands at block 2's centre, x = 2.5: Manhattan 2, distance 2.4). In that state nothing
  moves them closer: `tick` does nothing above √5, `MoveToTargetSink` considers the target reached, and once `WALK_TARGET` is absent the
  IDLE `RunOne` may even start a stroll that carries one away. On the birth tick they are too far: no birth, `stop` erases
  `BREED_TARGET`, and no food is used (an "abort" with no visible reason). Whether a pair parks inside or outside √5 depends on where each
  was in its block and on the stop slide (~0.2 blocks), which fits a minority failure such as ~1 in 16.
- Secondary causes of the same "no baby" outcome: `takeVacantBed` finds no free HOME POI (another villager took the free bed during the
  ~300 ticks, or the path check `createPath(bed, 1).canReach()` fails), in which case the food *is* digested and angry particles show;
  the partner leaves `NEAREST_VISIBLE_LIVING_ENTITIES` (line of sight); a parent falls asleep or panics.

**Fix** (for licensed parents only, so vanilla outside the claim is unchanged):
- (a) close-enough **1** instead of 2 in `start` and `tick`: arrival at Manhattan ≤ 1 means block offset (1,0,0) or (0,0,1) on flat ground,
  where the Euclidean distance is < √5 by construction (|dx| < 2, |dz| < 1 → d² < 5).
- (b) when `tick` finds the pair farther than √5, it re-issues the walk targets (close-enough 1) instead of doing nothing, so a parked or
  pushed pair closes the gap before the birth tick.
- (c) the reserved House bed (3.2 #6) removes the "bed taken meanwhile" and "first POI in section order is unreachable" causes.
- The remaining causes (visibility, panic, sleep) are reported, not hidden: the stop hook logs the reason, computed at the stop tick in
  this order: `TOO_FAR` (`d² > 5`, with d), `NOT_VISIBLE`, `CANNOT_BREED` (age/sleep/food/gate), `PARTNER_GONE`, `TIMED_OUT`, `NO_BED`
  (`takeVacantBed` empty: angry particles). `Courtship` re-seeds at most 3 times a day; the deterministic T-L2-5 and the 100-round probe (step 9) show the parked-gap abort is gone.

Step 1 confirms this before the fix. The builder adds the stop-hook logging first (no behaviour change) and a probe GameTest
(`breedingAbortProbe`, gametest source set, run by hand with `-Dfabric-api.gametest.filter`, not in the regular suite) that runs 100
rounds: a fresh pair of unemployed adults in a House with a free bed, `BREED_TARGET` on one of them (food given as bread), wait for the
birth or the stop, discard. 20 runs of the old test would be too few: at 1/16 they show no abort 27 % of the time. Result rules: with
at least 5 aborts and `TOO_FAR` the most common reason, go on; with another dominant reason, stop and report before step 4 (the fix
would be aimed at the wrong cause); with fewer than 5 aborts, record the counts, rely on the deterministic reproduction T-L2-5 (which
builds the parked state on purpose) and go on. After step 5 the same probe runs again with the fix (licensed pairs) and must show 0
`TOO_FAR` aborts in 100 rounds (step 9); its counts go into section 8.

## 6. Risks and unknowns

1. **Top risk: the root cause is inferred, not measured.** Static reading says the parked-gap mismatch (3.5) is the abort; step 1's
   100-round probe measures it before any fix, with written result rules, and step 9 measures the fix. If the cause is something else,
   the fix changes, not the tests.
2. **Mixins into a vanilla behaviour with private targets** (`takeVacantBed`, `tryToGiveBirth`, `giveBedToChild`) and a bridge method on
   `tick`/`stop`. `defaultRequire = 1` makes a miss fail at start-up; the full descriptors are given in 3.3.
3. **The gate changes vanilla inside the claim.** Players may expect vanilla breeding (bread to villagers). This is DESIGN §7's intent (the
   cap and the conditions would be meaningless otherwise); documented in DESIGN "Rules as built". Outside the claim nothing changes.
4. **`canBreed` is hot.** The gate must not allocate or scan; a snapshot with a precomputed squared radius. If profiling shows it, cache
   per villager per tick.
5. **Approach in a busy village.** Walk targets are re-issued every tick, but a work package, panic or a door can still delay the meeting;
   `COURTSHIP_APPROACH_TICKS` (400) and the window margins are tunable. Workers have only the 0-1000 window.
6. **`foodLevel` top-up** leaves a lapsed parent with at most its old value (restored), but a parent that leaves the claim mid-courtship
   keeps up to 12 points (one free vanilla breed outside the claim). Accepted, documented.
7. **M12/M14 not built yet** (A3-A5): names may change; the plan lists the fallback for each.
8. **`starter` (M11 L3)** loses vanilla's random births; its assertions do not use population growth, but the run is checked by hand in step 8.

**Out of scope:** Overview/Heart UI for growth (next baby, reason), L3 growth metrics (`growth.births` per day: M26 balance can add it
from `GrowthStats`), choosing parents by job or level, baby names, growth limits by tier (DESIGN §9 is M14's), golem spawning (M19),
children's play or schooling.

### Orchestrator decision: no vanilla breeding in the claim

M23 blocks all vanilla villager breeding inside the claim; growth happens only through the dawn trigger, so bread fed to villagers does nothing there. Three layers, all tested: (1) `Villager.canBreed` RETURN (the plan's gate, #7); (2) `Villager.getBreedOffspring` HEAD returns null for a villager the gate blocks (the one place a villager baby is made, so a caller that skipped `canBreed` still gets nothing); (3) the `takeVacantBed` RETURN hook gives a claim bed back to an unlicensed villager (this is also what stops a pair just outside the claim).
