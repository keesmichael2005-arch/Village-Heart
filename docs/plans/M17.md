Planner: Opus, 2026-11-08. Builder: implement in the step order of section 4; every step ends green on `scripts/verify.sh --fast`.
APIs marked **[verified]** were read in the decompiled 1.21.11 Mojang-mapped sources (Loom `minecraft-common-…-sources.jar`). Nothing
was built or run for this plan (static checks only, as asked: other agents use the CPU).

M17 is planned while **M16 (Miner) is being built on `origin/m16`** (read at `c767f21`: only M16 steps 1-3, the pure core, exist; the
away-trip glue `AwayTrips` is M16 step 5 and not written yet). M17 reuses M16's "go away and come back" machinery. Section 3.1 lists

## 1. Goal

A hunter hired at a Hunting Lodge takes the best bow or sword in the Lodge chest, walks to the Lodge's linked Hunting Ground and
vanishes into it for 3 in-game hours (level 1). It comes back with 2-4 raw meat (beef, porkchop, mutton or chicken, weighted by the
animals that spawn in the biomes around the ground), 1-2 leather, 1-2 feathers and 0-1 rabbit hide, carries them to Storage and goes
out again: 2 trips a day at level 1. With a bow (or crossbow) it takes 1 arrow per trip from the Lodge chest and brings 1 extra meat.
Each trip wears the weapon. Without a weapon it does not hunt ("Needs bow or sword"); while it is out the Heart screen shows it as
"Away (hunting)".

### 3.2 Decisions (DESIGN §5.4 gets a "Rules as built (M17)" paragraph in step 10)

1. **Away machinery = M16's, unchanged.** Arrival → `AwayTrips.hide` (invisible, invulnerable, silent, NoAI, custom name hidden,
   brain walk/look/path memories erased, navigation stopped, prior flags saved); return → `AwayTrips.surface`; interaction callbacks
   refuse trading and hitting; load repair; `restoreVanilla` surfaces a fired or released hunter. The **hold spot is the surface spot**
   (`AwayTrip.hold` = `AwayTrip.surface`): the cell the hunter stood on when it arrived ("vanishes into the woods" where it stands; there
   is no hole to hide in). If another away worker already holds that cell, the hunter takes the nearest free standable cell within 2
   blocks of it that nobody holds and that is not the marker cell (`HuntSites.holdCell`), else its arrival cell, so several hunters do not
   stack on one cell. Each step re-pins it if it drifted > 0.5 blocks (M16 §3.4 #7). Its invisible hitbox catches the crosshair there
   (M16 risk 4; more exposed here than in a pit, so the BACKLOG `isPickable` note is raised to "before v1", §6 risk 2).
2. **Trip length.** A trip costs `HUNTER_TRIP_UNITS` = 3000 units. `HunterSystem` steps every away hunter every `HUNTER_STEP_TICKS` = 20
   ticks (staggered by `(gameTime + id) % 20`, gated on `isPositionEntityTicking` **[verified on ServerLevel]**, like `MinerSystem`) and
   adds `20 × HuntPace.multiplier(...)`. Level 1 with any weapon: 1.0 units per tick → **exactly 3000 ticks = 3 in-game hours** away
   (DESIGN §5.4); level 2: 2400; level 3: 2000. The walk out and the deposit trip are on top (written into DESIGN). Progress belongs to
   one ground (`AwayState.progressShaft`, M16's field, used as "progress site"); a different ground resets it.
3. **Pace does not depend on the weapon tier.** `HuntPace.multiplier(boolean hasWeapon, int level, Needs needs)` =
   `hasWeapon ? LEVEL_SPEED[level-1] × needs.speed() : 0`. Not `WorkSpeed.multiplier` with the tier: that would make a bow (WOOD, ×1.0)
   and a stone sword (×1.25) give different trip lengths and a diamond sword 1.75 × as many trips, against DESIGN's "3 hours, 2 trips a
   day". The tier matters only for which weapon is leased (decision 5) and how long it lasts (wear, decision 6).
4. **Trip schedule** (2 per day at level 1):
   - **Daily cap** `HUNTER_TRIPS_PER_DAY` = {2, 3, 3} by level (index level-1, clamped). Completed trips (surface `HAUL`) are counted
     in `AwayState.doneToday` for `AwayState.doneDay` = `Math.floorDiv(level.getDayTime(), 24000)` **[verified `Level.getDayTime`]**.
     `TripDay.of(savedDay, savedCount, nowDay)`: a different day (earlier or later: `/time set` can go back) → count 0. Pure, L1.
   - **Departure only if the trip can end in the work phase**: `dayTime % 24000 + ceil((HUNTER_TRIP_UNITS − progress) / multiplier) +
     HUNTER_DEPART_MARGIN (300) ≤ Balance.WORK_END (11000)`. At level 1 from zero progress the last departure is day time 7700. So a
     hunter is not caught out at dusk except when its multiplier drops mid-trip.
   - At level 1 with short walks both rules forbid a third trip (two cycles of ≈ 3000 + 600 end near day time 8200, after 7700); the cap
     decides at level 1 when the second cycle ends before 7700 (a trip resumed with saved progress) and at higher levels. Each rule is
     tested on its own (T-L1-9, T-L2-6), not through T-L2-1.
   - The fit is **checked again on arrival**, just before `hide` (the walk may have been long): if it no longer fits there is no hide; the
     hunter goes back to the Lodge (`IDLE_AT_LODGE`), nothing reported.
   - Day end while away (phase not WORK, M16 check 2) → surface `DAY_END`, no yield, **progress kept** for tomorrow (M16 rule).
5. **Weapon.** Leased from the Lodge chests with `CampTools.lease`, but **ranked for the hunter**: any bow or crossbow before any sword,
   swords by tier, ties by container/slot order. Reason: `ToolAllocation` ranks by tier and a bow is WOOD, so a player who puts in a bow,
   arrows and a stone sword would see the sword taken and the arrows never used. A ranged weapon never does worse than a sword (no
   arrows = no bonus, same pace). Implementation: `ToolAllocation.assign(tools, workers, ToIntFunction<Tool<S>> rank)` overload (the old
   `assign` delegates with `tier.ordinal()`), `CampTools.lease(server, villager, kind, ToIntFunction<ItemStack> rank)` overload, rank from
   core `HunterWeapons.rank(boolean ranged, ToolTier tier)` = `ranged ? 100 : tier.ordinal()`. `ranged` = `Items.BOW` or `Items.CROSSBOW`
   (glue `HunterWeapons.isRanged(ItemStack)`; the same items `CampTools.isTool` accepts).
   No lease: no departure, status `NEEDS_TOOL` ("Needs bow or sword", key exists). The lease is re-read each away step (M16 §3.4 #4:
   `NONE` → surface `NO_TOOL`, progress kept; `UNKNOWN` → no progress; 60 unknown steps → `STOPPED_WORKING`) and each planner run.
6. **Wear**: `HUNTER_WEAR_PER_TRIP` = 5 durability per completed trip through `CampTools.use(level, lease, 5)` (`ItemStack.hurtAndBreak(int,
   ServerLevel, ServerPlayer, Consumer<Item>)` **[verified]**; Unbreaking applies). Bow (384) ≈ 76 trips ≈ 38 days, stone sword (131) 26
   trips ≈ 13 days, copper sword (190) 38, iron sword (250) 50, wooden (59) 11, golden (32) 6, crossbow (465) 93. Aborted trips cost nothing. A weapon that breaks on the last trip
   still gives that trip. If `use` returns false (the weapon was swapped in the same tick) the trip is still given (one free trip at most,
   as M16).
7. **Arrows** (taken at the **return**, not at departure, so an aborted trip never costs or refunds an arrow and nothing has to be saved):
   when the trip completes and the leased weapon is ranged, `HunterArrows.take(server, lodge)` removes 1 item from the Lodge chests
   (`CampContainers.of`): the first `minecraft:arrow` in container/slot order, else the first other `#minecraft:arrows` item (tipped,
   spectral; `ItemTags.ARROWS` **[verified]**, the predicate bows use: `ProjectileWeaponItem.ARROW_ONLY` **[verified]**), `shrink(1)`,
   `setChanged()`. One taken → +`HUNT_ARROW_BONUS_MEAT` (1) meat. None → the trip is a no-bonus trip, nothing reported. A sword never
   takes arrows. Infinity is not modelled (an arrow is taken anyway; BACKLOG note). The lease was HELD in this step, so the Lodge chests
   are readable.
8. **Yield table** (core `HuntTable`, numbers in `Balance`). One trip, drawn in this **pinned order** from the trip's generator:
   1. base meat = 2 + `nextInt(3)` (2, 3 or 4);
   2. leather = 1 + `nextInt(2)`;
   3. feathers = 1 + `nextInt(2)`;
   4. rabbit hide = 1 if `nextDouble() < 0.5` else 0;
   5. **five** meat-kind draws, always all five (`MeatWeights.pick(nextInt(total))`, cumulative in the order beef, porkchop, mutton,
      chicken); meat piece k (k = 0 … base+bonus−1) takes kind draw k.
   So with and without a bow the same trip index gives the same base meat, leather, feathers, hide and kinds, and the bow adds exactly one
   meat of the kind of draw `base`: an exact property the L1 test asserts. Item ids: `minecraft:beef`, `minecraft:porkchop`,
   `minecraft:mutton`, `minecraft:chicken`, `minecraft:leather`, `minecraft:feather`, `minecraft:rabbit_hide` **[verified in `Items`]**.
   Means per trip: meat 3 (4 with an arrow), leather 1.5, feathers 1.5, rabbit hide 0.5. Leather, feathers and hide are flat (DESIGN
   gives no biome rule for them).
   Seed: `HaulRng.forHaul(level.getSeed(), uuid, AwayState.rolls)` (M16, unchanged; `rolls` + 1 per completed trip). A reload never
   re-rolls a trip; the L2 tests predict every trip.
9. **Biome weighting** ("weighted by nearby biome"): the weights are **the vanilla creature spawn weights of the biomes around the
   ground**, read from the game, so modded and datapack biomes work too.
   - Glue `HuntSites.meatWeights(level, groundMarker)`: 9 sample columns (the marker and the 8 compass points at `HUNTING_GROUND_RADIUS`
     12 blocks, at the marker's y); a sample whose chunk is not loaded (`LevelReader.hasChunkAt(BlockPos)` **[verified]**) is skipped; for
     each: `level.getBiome(pos)` **[verified `LevelReader.getBiome` → `Holder<Biome>`]** `.value().getMobSettings().getMobs(MobCategory.CREATURE)`
     **[verified: `WeightedList<MobSpawnSettings.SpawnerData>`]** `.unwrap()` **[verified: `List<Weighted<E>>`]**, and for each
     `Weighted(value, weight)` **[verified record]** the key `BuiltInRegistries.ENTITY_TYPE.getKey(value.type())` (`SpawnerData(EntityType<?>
     type, int minCount, int maxCount)` **[verified record]**) → weight, summed into `Map<String, Integer>`.
   - Core `MeatWeights.fromSpawns(Map<String,Integer>)`: `minecraft:cow` and `minecraft:mooshroom` → beef, `minecraft:pig` → porkchop,
     `minecraft:sheep` → mutton, `minecraft:chicken` → chicken; every other mob ignored. Total 0 (desert, snowy plains, oceans: no farm
     animals) → `MeatWeights.DEFAULT` = vanilla `farmAnimals` (beef 8, porkchop 10, mutton 12, chicken 10). Plains → the same numbers ×9;
     mushroom fields → beef only; jungle → chicken-heavy (farm animals + 10 chicken).
   - Cached in `HunterRuntime.weights` (transient, per ground location) **only when all 9 samples were loaded**; a partial result is
     used for that trip and not cached. The entry is dropped whenever `BuildingValidator` re-checks the ground (so a `/fillbiome` is seen
     within one re-check cycle), by `BuildingRuntime.forget` and by `resetRuntimeForTests`. A trip that completes after a restart uses the
     same weights as before it unless a sample chunk is unloaded (documented).
10. **Delivery**: the yield goes into the villager inventory via M16's give helper (A6); overflow stays in `AwayState.pending`, nothing
    is dropped. `JobBehaviours.depositNow(hunter)` = it carries any item of the hunter yield set (the 7 ids above) or `pending` is not
    empty, so `DepositTrip` takes every trip's yield to Storage at once. The job keep (`JobBehaviours.keep`) is 0 for every item; on top
    of it M10's `FoodKeep` top-up keeps up to 12 food points of bread/potato/carrot/beetroot, like every worker. Raw meat is not a
    `FoodKeep` food, so it is always handed in. Only the 7 hunted ids (and `pending`) trigger `depositNow`; bread or seeds do not.
11. **Which ground**: the VALID Hunting Ground whose `link` is the hunter's Lodge, nearest to the Lodge marker
    (`ShaftChoice.pick`, job-neutral despite its name). Several hunters can share one ground (no capacity).
12. **Link range (an M5 rule change).** Today a Hunting Ground links to the nearest Lodge at any distance (`BuildingValidator.siteVerdict`
    passes `Integer.MAX_VALUE` to `Links.nearest` for everything but the Mineshaft), so a ground can be ~250 blocks from its Lodge, while
    `WalkTo` plans at most 48 blocks (`RANGE` **[read]**) and `DepositTrip` walks with `WalkTo` too: a hunter that surfaces far out could
    never bring its meat home and would sit in planner row 2 for good. M17 adds `HUNTING_GROUND_LINK_RANGE` = 40, applied exactly like
    `MINESHAFT_LINK_RANGE` (validator range, and the `BuildingRules.checkSite` range in `Reason.noParent(HUNTING_LODGE, 40)`). A ground
    farther than 40 from every Lodge is INVALID with the existing `noParent` text. This replaces the waypoint "legs" of the draft
    (critique #1, #7, #8). The way out follows M16's rule: a hunter more than `HUNTER_FAR_FROM_GROUND` = 40 blocks from the marker (back
    from a far Storage) first walks to its fletching table, from where the marker is within about 46 blocks (40 + the room). Near the
    marker it walks with reach `HUNT_ENTRY_REACH` 3.0, closest `HUNT_ENTRY_CLOSEST` 6.0 (leaves stop a villager's head, M9 deviation 2).
    UNREACHABLE → `SITE_UNREACHABLE` ("Can't reach the Hunting Ground"), retry after `HUNTER_RETRY_TICKS` 600. The way back (ground →
    Storage) has the same exposure as M16's miner (shaft ≤ 48 from the Mine, Storage anywhere in the claim): a failed deposit shows
    `STORAGE_UNREACHABLE`, never silently (§6 risk 3).
13. **Hunter plan** (core `HunterPlanner.decide`, first row that matches; the glue gathers the inputs):

    | 4 | lease `NONE` | `IDLE_AT_LODGE`, `NEEDS_TOOL` |
    | 5 | no VALID linked ground | `IDLE_AT_LODGE`, `NO_WORK` ("No valid Hunting Ground"). 14. **One away step** = M16's `AwayCheck` with the hunter's numbers: (1) not a registered hunter / trip job not `hunter` → `NOT_A_MINER`

## 6. Risks, unknowns, out of scope

**Risks**
1. **(top) M16's away machinery is not built yet.** M17 depends on `AwayTrips`, `MinerSystem`, `depositNow` and `AwayState` as planned,
   not as built. A1-A6 and A9 name the fallbacks; the worst cases are A3 (`MinerSystem` surfacing every away worker; the 200-tick canary in
   step 5) and an `AwayTrips.hide` that computes a shaft hold spot itself (needs an overload). Step 3 is a checkpoint: re-read M16's code
   and plan §8 before writing hunter glue.
2. **A hidden villager in the open.** Unlike the miner at the bottom of a pit, the hunter hides where players walk: its invisible hitbox
   catches clicks and arrows. Players cannot push it (NoAI skips travel, M16 verified), it is re-pinned each step and hunters spread over
   cells (decision 1). Not fixed in M17 (a client-side `isPickable` mixin would have to guess from synced flags, M16 critique #28); the
   BACKLOG note is marked "before v1".
3. **Ground → Storage walk.** With the 40-block link range the hunter surfaces ≤ about 46 blocks from its Lodge, but Storage may be farther
   (it only has to be in the claim). Then `DepositTrip` reports `STORAGE_UNREACHABLE` and the hunter waits (visible status). Same exposure
   as M16's miner; BACKLOG: give `DepositTrip` a walk-via-the-job-block step for all away jobs.
4. **The link range is an M5 rule change.** A ground placed > 40 from its Lodge turns INVALID (and so the Lodge too). Nobody plays the
   mod yet; the M5 L2 test's ground is ~14 blocks from its Lodge and stays valid. DESIGN §5.4 and the Buildings text say it.
5. **Weapon ranking changes `CampTools.lease`** (shared with lumberjack/miner/farmer). The overload leaves the old path untouched; the
   lumberjack/farmer/miner L2 suites must stay green.
6. **Balance**: wear 5/trip, cap {2,3,3}, flat leather/feathers/hide, Infinity ignored, crossbow = bow. All tunable; levels are M15's.
   Weapons are exactly `#minecraft:swords` (wood, stone, copper 190, gold, iron, diamond, netherite), bow and crossbow (`CampTools.isTool`);
   spears, the mace and the trident are **not** hunter weapons. Written into DESIGN's "Rules as built", not only BACKLOG.
7. **Backlog leniency**: "Hunting Ground inside a house reads valid after a restart until re-scan" (BACKLOG M5 note): a hunter can go out
   to such a ground in the first re-check cycle after a restart. Harmless (one trip at most); left open, noted in DESIGN.

on `DepositTrip`, they stay open). New notes from this plan: walk-via-job-block for `DepositTrip` (risk 3), Infinity bows, the pickable

### Where the build differs from the plan
1. **Names.** The glue class for weapons and arrows is `worker.hunt.HuntGear` (`isRanged`, `rank`, `carriesYield`, `takeArrow`) instead of glue `HunterWeapons` / `HunterArrows` (the core `HunterWeapons.rank` is as planned). `HunterPlanner.PlanInputs` carries `level`, `progressUnits`, `multiplier`, `dayTime` so that row 8 and `fitsNow` are pure.
2. **`fitsNow` is also false before `WORK_START`** (1000): a hunter that leaves at 500 would be surfaced as DAY_END at once (the away check wants the WORK phase).
3. **Hold cell.** The hunter is held where it arrived (the surface spot = the arrival cell) unless another away worker holds that cell or it is a marker cell: then the nearest free standable cell within 2 blocks that nobody holds (`HuntSites.holdCell`), as planned. **Added rule:** a hunter that touches an away villager within 6 blocks of the marker counts as arrived (it walked straight behind the first hunter, a NoAI villager is solid for a walker; under CPU load the second hunter lost up to 2600 ticks, 3 of 8 runs under load, 0 of 8 with the rule). Deterministic test: `hunterBlockedByHiddenHunterGoesOutBesideIt` (35 ticks with the rule, 133 without).
4. **Arrival without a weapon.** If the lease is not HELD when the hunter arrives (weapon taken, chest unreadable), it does not go out (the plan's "too late" check would have read the multiplier 0 as "too late"); the next plan says why.
5. **Existing tests changed, with the reason.** `SiteRulesTest.huntingGround`: "No Hunting Lodge in the village" is now "No Hunting Lodge within 40 blocks" (the approved M5 rule change; a `Balance.HUNTING_GROUND_LINK_RANGE` reason pin was added). `HeartTextsActivityTest.otherJobsFallBackToTheGenericWords` / `siteUnreachableAndNoWorkForTheMiner` used the hunter as their "job with no specific words" example; the hunter has its own words now, so they use an unlisted job id (the same fallback check; a new test pins the hunter's words). `minerHaulsFortyCobblestone` (M16) looked for stray item entities 40 blocks around the test and saw the chests dropped by a neighbouring high-scene test finishing at that moment (age 0; this is the BACKLOG's "unexplained stray item"): it now looks at its own footprint (`AABB.encapsulatingFullBlocks`).
6. **T-L2-9 geometry.** The planned Lodge at the structure corner collides with the bell and the Heart. The far ground is at (26, 0, 26) (its field is in the corner, so its sphere reaches real grass outside the footprint: the scene asserts at least 121 natural cells there, not the literal weights) and the hunter is teleported to (6, 0, -10) on a stone strip (41 blocks from the marker, nothing inside a 32 x 32 footprint can be 40 blocks from a marker that has a field around it). The strip's chunks are forced for the test (an entity outside the structure is not always in an entity-ticking chunk: without it the hunter did nothing in 1 of 3 runs) and the strip is restored block by block.
7. **Extra test** `hunterBlockedByHiddenHunterGoesOutBesideIt` (see 3). `twoHuntersOneGround` waits with an own 460-tick diagnostic print instead of `awaitWithin` so a flake says where both hunters were.
8. **Environments** are empty `all_of` JSONs (as planned: no time, no rules). Mobs can spawn, as in the miner tests; the yard is torch-lit (`lightYard` keeps 2 blocks clear of the ground markers: a torch on the marker cell replaced it in a first version).
