## 1. Goal

A blacksmith hired at a Town-tier Smithy smelts raw iron, copper and gold from the stockpile into ingots at its blast furnace (with
fuel). It **makes the tools and gear the village's workers ask for, and nothing else**: when a hired lumberjack, miner, hunter or farmer
has no usable tool in its job chest (or its tool is about to break), or a guard lacks a sword or an armour piece, the smith makes that one
item from the **best material the stockpile can afford right now** (wood, stone, copper, iron, diamond under a reserve rule), which takes
a long time (a stone tool ≈ 1200 ticks, an iron one a quarter of its day, a diamond one half), and carries it to the worker's chest. A tool
that still works is never replaced, so the village climbs the tiers as tools wear out; a tool the player puts in a chest is used at once
and cancels the smith's order. It repairs damaged tools and armour left in the Smithy chest (and the Barracks chest) with ingots. It builds iron golems from 36 ingots and a carved pumpkin over one working day (at most 1 golem per 5 villagers), and a
golem below half health walks to the Smithy and is patched up with ingots (25 HP each). Villagers inside the claim no longer summon
golems themselves: golems come from the Smithy.

### 3.2 Decisions

1. **Everything the blacksmith makes is a mod-side timed conversion, as in M13** (decision 1 there): items move between the stockpile and
   the smith's inventory, a `WorkClock` turns inputs into outputs while the smith stands at its station, and the vanilla blocks only show
   it. No crafting grid, no `RecipeManager`, no block-entity slots: DESIGN pins the recipes, data packs must not change villagers, the
   table is L1-testable, and nothing ever sits in a furnace slot where a player or hopper can take it mid-job.
   Stations: **smelting** at the job block (blast furnace, `LIT` on while the clock runs); **crafting, gear repair and golem work** at the
   Smithy's anvil (the scan's `#anvil` cell nearest the blast furnace), with the anvil sound `level.levelEvent(LevelEvent.SOUND_ANVIL_USED
   = 1030, anvilPos, 0)` **[verified constant, `LevelAccessor.levelEvent(int, BlockPos, int)` verified]** and `swing(MAIN_HAND)`
   **[verified]** on each finished unit. Golem work also lights the blast furnace (see #9).
2. **Recipes** (core `SmithRecipes`, all ids `minecraft:`, counts **[verified data files]**):

   | Kind | Output ← inputs | Ticks per unit (level 1) |
   |---|---|---|
   | SMELT | iron_ingot ← raw_iron; copper_ingot ← raw_copper; gold_ingot ← raw_gold | 100 (the blast furnace's `cookingtime` 100 **[verified]**) |
   | CRAFT tool, 5 tiers × 5 kinds | `<tier>_axe`, `<tier>_pickaxe` ← 3 material + 2 stick; `<tier>_hoe` ← 2 + 2; `<tier>_shovel` ← 1 + 2; `<tier>_sword` ← 2 + 1 stick, for tier prefix `wooden` / `stone` / `copper` / `iron` / `diamond` **[verified: the 30 recipes `minecraft:{wooden,stone,copper,golden,iron,diamond}_{axe,pickaxe,hoe,shovel,sword,spear}` all exist in the 1.21.11 jar with these counts]** | by tier, `SMITH_TOOL_TICKS` *(tunable)*: wooden 600, stone 1200, copper 1800, iron 2400, diamond 4800 |
   | CRAFT armour, 4 tiers × 4 pieces | `<tier>_helmet` 5, `_chestplate` 8, `_leggings` 7, `_boots` 4 material, for `leather` / `copper` / `iron` / `diamond` **[verified recipes; no chainmail recipe exists]** | by tier, `SMITH_ARMOUR_TICKS` *(tunable)*: leather 600, copper 1800, iron 2400, diamond 4800 per piece |
   | STICKS | 4 stick ← 2 of any `#minecraft:planks` (`minecraft:stick` **[verified]**; made only as a step of a tool order when the stockpile has too few sticks) | 20 |
   | GOLEM | 1 iron golem ← 36 iron_ingot + 1 carved_pumpkin | `GOLEM_BUILD_TICKS` 8000 (3.2 #9) |

   **Materials per tier** (the vanilla item tags, read in the 1.21.11 data **[verified]**): wooden = `#minecraft:wooden_tool_materials`
   = `#minecraft:planks` (any planks, mixed kinds allowed: the smith counts the tag, not one id); stone = `#minecraft:stone_tool_materials`
   = cobblestone, blackstone, cobbled_deepslate (mixed allowed, as in vanilla crafting); copper = `#copper_tool_materials` = copper_ingot;
   iron = `#iron_tool_materials` = iron_ingot; diamond = `#diamond_tool_materials` = diamond. The glue reads the tags (`StackKey.is`), the
   core gets the count per tier material. **Gold**: the `golden_*` recipes exist but the smith **does not make gold tools or armour** in
   v1: gold has 32 durability (iron 250), so 3 gold ingots buy 3 miner hauls; with A9 a gold tool would be the fastest tool in the game
   (×6.0), which makes it a trap for the planner, not a choice. Gold tools the player puts in a chest are used (they satisfy a demand).
   **Netherite**: upgrade-only (`minecraft:netherite_*_smithing`, `smithing_transform` of a diamond item + `netherite_upgrade_smithing_template`
   + `#netherite_tool_materials` **[verified]**): not made by the smith in v1 (a Nether template and netherite ingots are a late-game
   player thing); a player's netherite tool is used. **Spears** (1.21.11 has them) and shovels: recipes are in the table (pinned) but no
   job asks for them in v1, so the smith never makes them. **Why so slow** (owner, 2026-11-09): the recipe counts stay vanilla because
   the player can hand-craft the same tool at any time; the smith's limiter is **time** (wooden tool 600 ticks ≈ 6 % of its ~10 000-tick
   work day, iron 24 %, diamond 48 %), scaled by `WorkSpeed.untooled(level, needs)` (level 3 makes an iron tool in 1600 ticks).

   Only component-free input stacks are taken (`StackKey.patch().isEmpty()`), as in M13. Smelting needs fuel (M13's heat bank, 200
   heat per ingot, so a coal smelts 8: the blast furnace halves both burn and cook time, the ratio is vanilla's); crafting, sticks,
   repair and golem work need none (DESIGN §5.7 names fuel for smelting only). Ore blocks and raw-ore blocks are not smelted in v1.
3. **Smelting = M13's cook pipeline with another recipe table.** Batch of at most `SMITH_BATCH` 8 (`BatchMath` units by input, time,
   slots and fuel), fetch with reservations (`release` → `set(uuid, key, n)` → recompute; `nearestHolding` + `withdrawAt`), load fuel
   into the **blast furnace's** heat bank, clock 100 ticks per ingot × `WorkSpeed.untooled`, outputs kept until the batch ends, then
   `depositNow` → `DepositTrip`. Fuel order: charcoal, coal, logs (M13's `COOK_FUEL_ORDER`, shared as `FUEL_ORDER`). **Fuel competition
   with the cook** (M13 risk 5): when the village has a hired cook, the smith leaves `SMITH_FUEL_RESERVE` = 3200 heat (2 coal) of fuel
   in the stockpile for it (pure: `available heat − reserve`, L1). `NEEDS_FUEL` as in M13: raw ore to smelt, nothing else to do, no fuel.
4. **Gear demand planner (pure core, the roadmap's L1; r2 replaces r1's gear targets and spare stock).** The smith makes a tool or
   armour piece **only for an actual demand** of a hired worker (owner, 2026-11-09). There is no spare stock, nothing for unhired jobs and
   nothing for a job whose building is invalid.
   - **Demands** (`GearDemand(worker UUID, JobType job, Need need, Urgency urgency, HeartLocation destination, Optional<CraftTier>
     current)`, built by the glue `DemandCensus` every 100 ticks and directly before an order starts):
     - *Tool workers*: lumberjack → AXE, miner → PICKAXE, hunter → SWORD (its `BOW_OR_SWORD` need; a bow needs string the village does not
       make, so the smith answers with a sword), farmer → HOE. For each **valid** building of that job: take its hired workers in hiring
       order and the tools of the right kind in its camp containers (`CampContainers.of`, `CampTools.isTool`), and run the existing
       `ToolAllocation.assign` **over the not-worn tools only** (a worn tool is still used by M9's lease; it just does not cover the demand).
       A worker left without a tool is a demand: **MISSING** if the camp has no tool of that kind at all for it (it has stopped, "Needs
       axe"; the farmer still works at ×1.0), else **WORN** (its tool is worn and nothing fresh is left). `current` = the tier of the worn
       tool it holds.
     - *Worn* (`ToolWear.worn(remaining, max, wearPerUnit)`): `remaining < max(ceil(GEAR_MIN_DURABILITY × max), 2 × wearPerUnit)` with
       wear per unit lumberjack 1, farmer 1, miner 10 (`MINER_WEAR_PER_HAUL`), hunter 5 (`HUNTER_WEAR_PER_TRIP`). A wooden pickaxe (59) is
       worn below 20 remaining (two hauls left), an iron axe (250) below 25. The order starts while the old tool still works, so with
       the slow craft the worker loses little time.
     - *Guards* (M18): for guard g = 1..guards and slot in [WEAPON (sword), CHEST, LEGS, HEAD, FEET]: if the usable pieces of that slot
       (M18's usable rule: durability fraction ≥ `GEAR_MIN_DURABILITY`; worn by loaded guards, in the Barracks chests, in the stockpile, in
       the Smithy chest, carried by a smith; a not-loaded guard counts as fully equipped) number < g → a MISSING demand for guard g's slot
       (the guard's UUID in Barracks hiring order; destination = Storage, where M18's guards take gear). A piece below the usable rule that
       is **repairable now** (3.2 #5) counts as usable (it gets repaired, not replaced).
     - **What satisfies a demand** (the player's shortcut, by design): any usable tool of the kind in the job chest, whatever its tier and
       whoever put it there, including gold and netherite (owner: "the smith is automation, not the only source"). A player tool that
       appears while an order is pending or being crafted **cancels** that order (below).
   - **Urgency classes** (first class first; within a class in hiring order, then building order): **U1** a tool worker with a MISSING
     REQUIRED tool (lumberjack, miner, hunter: they cannot work); **U2** a guard's missing sword; **U3** a guard's missing armour piece
     (CHEST, LEGS, HEAD, FEET); **U4** WORN tools of REQUIRED-tool workers; **U5** the farmer's hoe (BONUS tool: MISSING or WORN).
   - **One order per demand**: an order (`GearOrder(demand, recipe, prerequisites)`) is keyed by the worker's UUID and slot; a worker
     with an open order (pending fetch, being crafted, finished and in transit) gets no second one; two smiths never take the same
     demand (the transient `SmithOrders` registry + the saved crafts of 3.6 are checked before a new order).
   - **Tier choice** (`TierChoice.best(need, job, demand, materials, village)`, the rule "quality follows resources"): the **best tier
     the stockpile can afford at that moment** in the order DIAMOND > IRON > COPPER > STONE > WOODEN (tools) or DIAMOND > IRON > COPPER >
     LEATHER (armour). "Afford" = `material available (count − reserved by others) + carried ≥ cost`, and for tools also sticks ≥ cost or
     planks to make them (the STICKS prerequisite, 2 planks → 4 sticks; for a wooden tool the planks must cover head **and** sticks).
     Ingots count **plus the raw ore the smith can smelt now** (raw ore available and fuel for it after the cook reserve of 3.2 #3): then
     the order gets a SMELT prerequisite (n raw → n ingots, 100 ticks each), so a miner is not given a stone pick while raw iron sits in
     Storage. Then:
     - **Never downgrade a working tool**: a WORN demand only takes a tier **≥ its `current` tier**; if the best affordable is lower, no
       order is made yet (the old tool keeps working; when it breaks the demand becomes MISSING and any tier is taken). A MISSING demand
       takes the best affordable, down to WOODEN (a brand-new smith with only planks still equips everyone).
     - **Never a proactive upgrade** (owner, 2026-11-09): a usable tool is never replaced because better material appeared. Progress
       comes through wear: the stone axe wears out, its replacement is iron if iron is there.
     - **Diamond rule** (diamonds are scarce): a DIAMOND item only when (a) the job's pace depends on the tier (lumberjack, miner) or it is
       guard gear (never hunter swords, never hoes); (b) **every other** hired tool worker and guard slot already has an IRON-or-better
       (iron, diamond, netherite; gold does not count) usable item; (c) the diamonds left after the cost are ≥ `SMITH_DIAMOND_RESERVE`
       *(tunable, default 5; the owner can set it high to forbid diamond use)*. Otherwise the diamond tier is skipped and iron is tried.
     - **Leather** (armour only): only when iron and copper (ingots + smeltable raw ore) cannot cover the piece (r1's rule, kept).
     - Nothing affordable → no order; `deficits` reports the cheapest missing material ("Needs planks" / "Needs iron") for the status.
   - **Cancel**: before every unit, and every 100 ticks of a long craft, the order's demand is re-checked against a fresh census. Gone
     (a player's tool in the chest, the worker fired, its building invalid) → the order is cancelled: before commit nothing happened
     (reservations released); after commit the committed materials are refunded (3.6, the golem refund rule) and the progress is lost
     (no tool is ever finished for nobody). A finished item whose demand vanished while it was carried goes to Storage.
   - Pure API: `GearDemandPlanner.next(List<GearDemand> demands, Set<OrderKey> open, Materials m, VillageGear g, SmithSettings s) →
     Optional<GearOrder>`; `GearDemandPlanner.cancelled(GearOrder o, List<GearDemand> demands) → boolean`; `deficits(...)` for the hint.
   - **Effect of the vanilla speeds (A9)**: an iron axe fells 1.5× as fast as stone and a wooden one half as fast, so the best-tier
     choice and the SMELT prerequisite matter a lot (one iron tool is worth half a stone-tooled worker more). That is why the planner
     waits for nothing but takes the best affordable *now*, and why WORN orders start early: a worker without a tool loses all its
     output, a slow tier half.
5. **Gear repair** (core `RepairRules`): a unit restores `maxDamage / 4` (integer division) **[verified: `AnvilMenu` divides
   `getMaxDamage()` by 4]**, the vanilla anvil's material repair; `units(damage, max) = damage / (max / 4)` (floor: never spend an ingot
   for less than a full quarter; this **deliberately differs** from vanilla, which also spends an ingot on a partial last quarter, and
   is written into DESIGN §5.7 as ingot-saving); the material is whatever `ItemStack.isValidRepairItem(material)` **[verified]** accepts among
   `iron_ingot`, `gold_ingot`, `copper_ingot`, `leather` (tags `repairs_iron_armor` / `iron_tool_materials` = iron_ingot,
   `repairs_leather_armor` = leather **[verified]**). Enchantments and names are kept; the anvil's `REPAIR_COST` component is not touched
   (no anvil menu is used). 100 ticks per unit at the anvil.
   - **Smithy chest**: repaired **in place** (the smith stands at the anvil, re-reads the slot before each unit: same item, same damage,
     else the unit is dropped; `setDamageValue`, `setChanged()`). Anything damageable with a known material counts, including the player's
     own tools (that is what the Smithy chest is for, DESIGN §5.7).
   - **Barracks chests** (M18): items with damage ≥ `max / 4` that no guard has claimed (`GuardGear.CLAIMS`) are fetched (at most
     `SMITH_REPAIR_CARRY` 4 per trip), repaired at the anvil, and carried back to the same Barracks chest (kept until then; if it is full,
     the Smithy chest). The pieces are "carried by the smith" for the gear census meanwhile.
   - **Storage** (critique #16): gear pieces in the stockpile with units ≥ 1 and durability fraction < 0.5 are fetched
     the same way (same carry limit, same row 10) and deposited back into Storage after repair.
6. **Pace and time.** Every clock is `WorkClock(ticksPerUnit).tick(WorkSpeed.untooled(level, needs))`. **Smelt batches and repairs**
   start only when they can end by `SMITH_DONE_BY` = `WORK_END − 500` (M13's rule: outputs reach Storage before GO_HOME). **Crafts**
   are too long for that (a diamond pickaxe is half a day), so a craft is **committed** like a golem build: at the anvil the inputs leave
   the smith's inventory into a saved `SmithCraft` (3.6) whose progress is saved every 200 ticks; it pauses outside WORK and continues
   the next day, by this or the next smith of that blast furnace. A craft may start at any WORK time. At GO_HOME the smith keeps nothing
   (keep 0), except items being returned to a Barracks chest and a finished tool on its way to a job chest, which it delivers first.
7. **Smith planner** (`SmithPlanner.decide(SmithFacts)`, pure, first match wins; same shape as M9/M10/M13):

   | # | Condition | Decision |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | Smithy invalid | `Idle(OK)` + returning (M7 shows `WORKPLACE_INVALID`) |
   | 2 | `speed == 0` | `Idle(TOO_HUNGRY)` |
   | 3 | `depositNow` and trip not FULL/UNREACHABLE | `Yield` (DepositTrip) |
   | 4 | a golem waits at this Smithy (3.2 #10) and ingots are carried or available | `RepairGolem(n)` (fetch n first if not carried); any time of the WORK window, not cut off by `SMITH_DONE_BY` |
   | 5 | the smith carries a finished tool for a live demand | `Deliver(destination)` (walk to the job chest, insert; full or unreachable → Storage) |
   | 6 | a saved craft of this blast furnace exists | its demand gone → `CancelCraft` (refund); else `Craft(continue)` |
   | 7 | an open demand of U1-U3 (`GearDemandPlanner.next` restricted to them) | a usable item of that kind is in Storage → `FetchAndDeliver(item)` (no craft: a tool lying in Storage is used first); else `StartCraft(order)` (prerequisites SMELT / STICKS first, fetch, commit at the anvil). **Pre-empts a saved golem build** (the build pauses, saved) |
   | 8 | a golem build of this Smithy is saved and day time < `WORK_END` | `BuildGolem` (continue) |
   | 9 | the Smithy chest holds a repairable item and its material is available | `RepairInPlace(slot, units)` |
   | 10 | a Barracks chest or Storage holds an unclaimed repairable item (3.2 #5) and its material is available | `FetchForRepair(items)` |
   | 11 | an open demand of U4-U5 | as row 7 (`FetchAndDeliver` or `StartCraft`), never pre-empting a golem build |
   | 12 | no open demand of U1-U4, golem allowed (3.2 #9, recounted at this moment), 36 iron + 1 pumpkin available, day time ≤ `GOLEM_LAST_START` | `StartGolem` (fetch) |
   | 13 | raw ore available and fuel covers ≥ 1 unit | `Smelt(batch)`; raw ore but no fuel → remember `NEEDS_FUEL` and go on |
   | 14 | otherwise | `Idle(h)`: `NEEDS_FUEL` if row 13 saw ore without fuel; else `NEEDS_MATERIAL(m)` ("Needs iron" / "Needs planks") if a demand found nothing affordable; else `NEEDS_VILLAGERS` ("Too few villagers for a golem") if only the cap stopped row 12; else `NO_WORK` ("Nothing to smith") |

   **Interruptions**: the planner runs between units only (a craft's unit is the whole item, so it also runs every 100 ticks *inside*
   a craft for the cancel check and row 4). When row 4 (a waiting golem) pre-empts a smelt, craft or repair, the job stays the runtime's
   `current` job, its inputs/fuel/outputs stay kept and reserved (a committed craft is saved anyway), the clock remainder is kept per job,
   and it resumes after the golem; at GO_HOME it ends as any batch does (a committed craft pauses). A craft in progress is never
   pre-empted by a new demand (it is at most 4800 work ticks), only by a golem repair. A saved golem build is pre-empted by U1-U3
   demands only (a worker that cannot work, or a guard without a weapon or armour, matters more than a day of golem work).

   Rows 9-13 are time-gated by `SMITH_DONE_BY` (row 10 by `SMITH_DONE_BY − 1000`, it has two extra walks); rows 5-7 and 11 are not
   (deliveries and committed crafts survive the evening). Demands come before a golem, a golem before smelting for stock.
8. **What the smith carries.** Fetch and deposit reuse M13 3.2 #7/#8 exactly (reservations under the smith's UUID; outputs kept until the
   job ends; `SmithKeep.keep` = inputs, fuel and outputs of the current job, plus Barracks items being repaired, plus a finished tool or a
   Storage tool being delivered; 0 at GO_HOME except those). **Tools go to the worker's job chest** (the camp's first container with
   space, `CampContainers.of(server, building)`), walked there by the smith (`WalkTo` the container, reach as `DepositTrip`); full or
   unreachable (`WalkTo` gives up) → Storage, and the demand stays open (counter `work.blacksmith.delivery_failed`). Guard gear and
   ingots go to Storage (guards take gear from the stockpile, M18 3.7). Repaired Barracks gear goes back to the Barracks chest.
9. **Golem build** (the "day of work").
   - **Cap** (`GolemRules.allowed(population, golemsInClaim, buildsInProgress)`): `golems + builds < population / GOLEM_VILLAGERS_PER`
     (5, integer division: 4 villagers → 0, 5 → 1, 10 → 2). `golemsInClaim` = loaded, alive `IronGolem`s inside the claim sphere, any
     origin (a world-generated village golem counts), counted by `SmithySystem` every 100 ticks with one `level.getEntities(EntityType.
     IRON_GOLEM, box, inClaim)` (same box as `VillageCensus`), **and recounted directly** when row 12 is about to start a build and when a
     golem is spawned (the cache is bumped in the spawn tick), so the 100-tick cache never lets a second build in (critique #5). Any
     origin counts (a player's or a world-generated golem fills a slot): documented; smith-built golems are not tracked while unloaded
     (risk 5).
   - **Start** (row 12): fetch 36 `iron_ingot` + 1 `carved_pumpkin` (reserved; 36 fits one slot), walk to the anvil, then **commit**: the
     items leave the inventory and a saved `GolemBuild(progress 0)` is written for this Smithy (`VillageState.golemBuilds`, 3.6). From
     then on the materials are no longer anywhere in the world: the stockpile is −36 / −1, which is what the roadmap asserts.
     `GOLEM_LAST_START` = `WORK_END − 2000` (no new build late in the day).
   - **Work**: at the anvil, clock `GOLEM_BUILD_TICKS` = 8000 work ticks at level 1 (×1.25/×1.5 with M15 levels), progress saved every
     200 ticks and on `stop()`; it pauses outside WORK and continues the next day (a build started at 1000 ends near 9000 the same day).
     Visuals: the blast furnace `LIT` while the smith works on the golem (no fuel is used: decision, the cast is part of the 36 ingots),
     the anvil sound + swing every `GOLEM_HAMMER_TICKS` 400, `ParticleTypes.LAVA` 3 at the blast furnace mouth each hammer stroke via
     `ServerLevel.sendParticles` **[verified signature, M15]**. Status on the Villagers tab: new `ActivityKind.BUILDING_GOLEM`
     ("Building an iron golem"; appended at the end of the enum, no new snapshot field, BACKLOG headroom items).
   - **Finish**: at progress ≥ 8000 the golem appears at the Smithy's **wait spot** (3.2 #10) with
     `SpawnUtil.trySpawnMob(EntityType.IRON_GOLEM, EntitySpawnReason.MOB_SUMMONED, level, spot, 10, 2, 3, SpawnUtil.Strategy.LEGACY_IRON_GOLEM,
     true)` **[verified signature and constants]** (`checkCollision = true`, so it never lands in the Smithy wall); with no wait spot, or
     no fit there, around the door's outside cell with range 6; then `setPlayerCreated(true)` **[verified]** (it is the owner's golem: it never
     turns on players over villager reputation; `DefendVillageTargetGoal` only runs for non-player-created golems), sound
     `SoundEvents.IRON_GOLEM_REPAIR` **[verified]**, `HAPPY_VILLAGER` particles. The build entry is removed in the same tick. If no spot is
     free the spawn is retried every 100 ticks (status stays `BUILDING_GOLEM`, counter `work.blacksmith.golem_blocked`). Golem
     building does not go through `Villager.spawnGolemIfNeeded`, so the spawn gate (#11) never blocks it.
   - **Refund** (critique #15): a build whose Smithy marker is **unregistered** (marker broken) returns 36 ingots + 1 pumpkin: deposited
     into Storage as far as there is space (`Stockpiles.deposit` per stack, partial allowed), the rest dropped at the marker position, and
     only while that chunk is loaded (otherwise the refund waits; an unloaded marker is not an unregistered one). The entry is removed in
     the same tick as the refund, so it can never run twice. A registered but invalid Smithy (or a Smithy that loses tier) keeps its
     build, paused, until it is valid again. Releasing the claim (`VillageClaims.releaseAt`, Heart broken) refunds every build first, by
     the same rule. Firing the smith keeps the build: the next smith of that Smithy continues it.
   - **Carried items on death or fire** (critique #14): villagers do not drop their inventory on death (`Villager` has no
     `dropEquipment` override **[verified]**). `SmithRuntime.forget` on death drops the job items the smith carries (fetched ingots,
     pumpkin, ore, fuel, gear in transit) at its position; on fire they go to Storage (`Stockpiles.deposit`), else are dropped.
10. **Golem repair.** A golem walks to the Smithy itself (goal, below); the smith comes out to it.

      new scan, `golemNavigation.createPath(spot, 1)` **[verified `createPath(BlockPos, int)`]** from the door's outside cell must
      reach it, else the spot is rejected. Iron golems are 1.4 wide

## 6. Risks & unknowns, out of scope

**Risks** (highest first):
1. **(top) Golem walking and waiting.** A 1.4 × 2.7 golem must path to a spot next to a room, stand there while vanilla goals and the
   targetSelector keep running, and not be pushed away by villagers. The vanilla goals were read (3.2 #10) but their interplay with a
   priority-1 MOVE goal is untested; `MoveBackToVillageGoal` and being shoved can still move it. Mitigations: the smith comes to the
   golem (re-targeted walk, reach 3.0) rather than the other way round, the wait spot is a checked 2×2×3 cell 3 out from the door, the
   waiting golem's navigation is stopped every tick, one golem per Smithy at a time, and T-L2-2/2b/2c/2d/2e check walk, wait, combat,
   queueing and reloads separately. If priority 1 misbehaves (for example `MoveBackToVillageGoal` still wins because the claim has no
   vanilla village POIs), stop the other movers while waiting with `goalSelector.disableControlFlag(Goal.Flag.MOVE)` (re-enabled in `stop()`) **[verified `GoalSelector.disableControlFlag/enableControlFlag`]**, and record it in §8.
2b. **The gate counts on `wantsToSpawnGolem` matching vanilla's own filter.** If T-L2-3's pre-round assertion fails (fewer than 5
   villagers want a golem) the test is wrong, not the gate: fix the setup, never relax the assertion.
2. **T-L2-1 runs ~9000+ ticks (and is bounded at 24 000)**: slow (several minutes) and exposed to M9-style walking flakes. The build
   itself is a fixed clock; only the fetch walk varies. Keep the Storage 4-6 blocks from the Smithy door.
3. **Three unmerged dependencies** (M13 pipeline, M18 guards/Barracks, M14/M15 numbers). Each has a fallback in 3.1; the build gate is
   M13 + M18 on dev. If M13's shape differs, step 8 adapts and records it rather than writing a second fetch system.
4. **Mixins** into `Villager.spawnGolemIfNeeded` and `IronGolem.registerGoals`: another mod doing the same could conflict; both are
   small HEAD/TAIL injects (`defaultRequire 1` makes a mismatch fail at start-up, not silently).
5. **Gear census blind spots**: unloaded guards count as fully equipped; golems in unloaded chunks are not counted (the cap can be
   exceeded by golems that wandered out of loaded chunks; golems stroll within the village, so this is rare). Both documented.
6. **Ingot competition**: worker and guard demands first, then a golem. A village with many guards and little iron gets no golem until
   the guards are equipped; that is the intended priority, but tuning (M26) may want a golem earlier.
7. **(r2, second highest) The Smithy is Town tier, so the smith cannot bootstrap a new village.** "Wooden tools need only planks so a
   brand-new village can bootstrap" holds for the planner, but a village only gets a smith after population 12 / 8 workers; until then
   every tool comes from the player (which the 2026-11-09 balance rationale accepts: the player's tools are always welcome). If the owner
   wants smith-made tools earlier, that is a tier change of the Smithy (or a lower-tier "tool bench" job), not an M19 change; flagged in
   PROGRESS for the orchestrator.
8. **(r2) Throughput.** One smith makes about 4 iron tools a day at level 1; a Town with 8+ tool workers and 2+ guards needs ~10 tools
   plus 2 × (sword + 4 armour) = 20 iron items just to start, ≈ 5 days of smithing. That is the intended slowness, but U1 workers may stand
   idle for days at the first Town tier-up if the player supplies nothing. Mitigation: U1 first, WORN orders start two units early,
   Storage tools are delivered without crafting, several blast furnaces = several smiths. M26 tunes the times.
9. **(r2) Demand census cost and blind spots.** The census reads every tool building's chests every 100 ticks (≤ ~10 buildings × 27 slots);
   an unloaded or unscanned camp gives no demand (never craft blind). A worker fired mid-craft cancels and refunds, which wastes the
   smith's time but never materials.
10. **(r2) Gold under A9.** With vanilla speeds a gold tool is ×6.0 (3× stone) but lasts 32 uses. The smith never makes gold, but
   `ToolAllocation` ranks by `ToolTier` ordinal (GOLD between COPPER and IRON), so a player's golden axe is handed out after iron ones; the
   tool-speed follow-up should decide whether to re-rank. M19 does not depend on it (the demand rule ignores tier).

**Out of scope**: a Heart UI for the diamond reserve or craft times (Balance only); spare stock of any kind (owner, 2026-11-09);
proactive upgrades of working tools; gold, chainmail and netherite crafting (netherite is upgrade-only with a smithing template);
shovels and spears (no job needs them); bows (string); repairing a worn tool **in a camp chest** instead of replacing it (a later
option: one ingot per quarter is cheaper than 3 for a new tool; v1 replaces); repairing items in job chests other than the Smithy and
Barracks; the tool-speed change itself (A9, a separate follow-up); smelting ore blocks,
raw-ore blocks or other furnace recipes; enchanting; anvil `REPAIR_COST`; golems following orders, patrol routes or names; golems for
other villages; a player's own golems being refused (only villager-made spawns are gated).
