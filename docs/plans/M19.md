## 1. Goal

A blacksmith hired at a Town-tier Smithy smelts raw iron, copper and gold from the stockpile into ingots at its blast furnace (with
fuel), makes iron tools, iron armour and leather armour until the village has its gear target (one sword and one armour set per guard,
plus 2 spare iron axes, 2 pickaxes and 1 hoe), and repairs damaged tools and armour left in the Smithy chest (and the Barracks chest)
with ingots. It builds iron golems from 36 ingots and a carved pumpkin over one working day (at most 1 golem per 5 villagers), and a
golem below half health walks to the Smithy and is patched up with ingots (25 HP each). Villagers inside the claim no longer summon
golems themselves: golems come from the Smithy.

### 3.2 Decisions

1. **Everything the blacksmith makes is a mod-side timed conversion, as in M13** (decision 1 there): items move between the stockpile and
   the smith's inventory, a `WorkClock` turns inputs into outputs while the smith stands at its station, and the vanilla blocks only show
   it. No crafting grid, no `RecipeManager`, no block-entity slots: DESIGN pins the recipes, data packs must not change villagers, the
   table is L1-testable, and nothing ever sits in a furnace slot where a player or hopper can take it mid-job.
   Stations: **smelting** at the job block (blast furnace, `LIT` on while the clock runs); **crafting, gear repair and golem work** at the
   Smithy's anvil (the scan's `#anvil` cell nearest the blast furnace), with the anvil sound `level.levelEvent(LevelEvent.SOUND_ANVIL_USED
   = 1030, anvilPos, 0)` **[verified constant, `LevelAccessor.levelEvent(int, BlockPos, int)` verified]** and `swing(MAIN_HAND)`
   **[verified]** on each finished unit. Golem work also lights the blast furnace (see #9).
2. **Recipes** (core `SmithRecipes`, all ids `minecraft:`, counts **[verified data files]**):

   | Kind | Output ← inputs | Ticks per unit (level 1) |
   |---|---|---|
   | SMELT | iron_ingot ← raw_iron; copper_ingot ← raw_copper; gold_ingot ← raw_gold | 100 (the blast furnace's `cookingtime` 100 **[verified]**) |
   | CRAFT tool | iron_axe, iron_pickaxe ← 3 iron_ingot + 2 stick; iron_hoe ← 2 + 2; iron_shovel ← 1 + 2; iron_sword ← 2 + 1 stick | 200 |
   | CRAFT armour | iron_helmet 5, iron_chestplate 8, iron_leggings 7, iron_boots 4 iron_ingot; leather_* the same counts of leather | 200 |
   | STICKS | 4 stick ← 2 of any `#minecraft:planks` (made only as a step of a tool order when the stockpile has too few sticks) | 20 |
   | GOLEM | 1 iron golem ← 36 iron_ingot + 1 carved_pumpkin | `GOLEM_BUILD_TICKS` 8000 (3.2 #9) |

   Only component-free input stacks are taken (`StackKey.patch().isEmpty()`), as in M13. Smelting needs fuel (M13's heat bank, 200
   heat per ingot, so a coal smelts 8: the blast furnace halves both burn and cook time, the ratio is vanilla's); crafting, sticks,
   repair and golem work need none (DESIGN §5.7 names fuel for smelting only). Ore blocks and raw-ore blocks are not smelted in v1.
3. **Smelting = M13's cook pipeline with another recipe table.** Batch of at most `SMITH_BATCH` 8 (`BatchMath` units by input, time,
   slots and fuel), fetch with reservations (`release` → `set(uuid, key, n)` → recompute; `nearestHolding` + `withdrawAt`), load fuel
   into the **blast furnace's** heat bank, clock 100 ticks per ingot × `WorkSpeed.untooled`, outputs kept until the batch ends, then
   `depositNow` → `DepositTrip`. Fuel order: charcoal, coal, logs (M13's `COOK_FUEL_ORDER`, shared as `FUEL_ORDER`). **Fuel competition
   with the cook** (M13 risk 5): when the village has a hired cook, the smith leaves `SMITH_FUEL_RESERVE` = 3200 heat (2 coal) of fuel
   in the stockpile for it (pure: `available heat − reserve`, L1). `NEEDS_FUEL` as in M13: raw ore to smelt, nothing else to do, no fuel.
4. **Gear target planner (pure core, the roadmap's L1).** `GearTargetPlanner.next(GearTargets, GearStock) → Optional<CraftOrder>`, one
   item per call (the planner runs again after each item, so the stock is always fresh):
   - **Targets** (`GearTargets`, defaults in `Balance`, tunable; no Heart UI in M19): per guard 1 sword + 1 armour set (helmet, chest,
     legs, boots); spares `iron_axe` 2, `iron_pickaxe` 2, `iron_hoe` 1 (`iron_shovel` 0).
   - **Slot and quality** (`GearQuality.of(id) → Optional<(GearSlot, quality)>`, an explicit id table in core, never "anything
     equippable in that slot"): helmets/chestplates/leggings/boots of leather 1 < gold 2 = copper 2 < chainmail 3 < iron 4 < diamond 5 <
     netherite 6 (copper armour exists in 1.21.11 **[verified recipe `copper_chestplate`]**); WEAPON = swords only (wood 1, stone 2, gold 2,
     copper 3, iron 4, diamond 5, netherite 6), as M18 3.7. Not gear: carved pumpkins (a golem input), heads, turtle shells, elytra, spears
     (1.21.11 has them **[verified `iron_spear` recipe]**; M18 does not wield them), maces, axes. Unknown ids do not count.
   - **What counts as "have"** (`GearStock.have`, per id, filled by the glue `GearCensus`): usable pieces (M18's rule: durability fraction
     ≥ `GEAR_MIN_DURABILITY` 0.1) in the stockpile, the Smithy chest, every valid Barracks chest, worn by loaded guards, and carried by the
     smith. A guard that is not loaded counts as fully equipped (no over-production after a restart; risk 5). Spare tools count only in the
     stockpile and the Smithy chest (a tool in a Lumber Camp chest is in use, not spare). A piece below the usable rule that is
     **repairable now** (3.2 #5: units ≥ 1 and its material available) counts as "have" (pending repair), so a broken helmet is repaired,
     not replaced (critique #16).
   - **Order** (first match wins): (a) for guard g = 1..guards, slot in [WEAPON, CHEST, LEGS, HEAD, FEET]: if pieces of quality ≥ IRON in
     that slot < g → the iron item if the materials allow it; else, armour slots only, if pieces of any quality < g and the leather allows
     it **and iron cannot cover the slot soon** (ingots + smeltable raw iron in the stockpile < the iron cost) → the leather item; else go
     on to the next slot (a missing material never blocks the other guard slots); (b) golem (row 9 of the smith planner, not part of this
     class); (c) spares in the target list's order (a `List<Spare(id, n)>`, filled one item at a time: axe, axe, pickaxe, pickaxe, hoe),
     count < target → the item if the materials allow it. **(b) and (c) run only when `deficits` of the guard part is empty** (a guard
     still missing an 8-ingot chestplate is never out-bid by a 3-ingot spare axe or a golem; critique #12).
   - **Materials** = stockpile available to the smith (`count − reserved by others`) + carried. A tool needs sticks; if sticks are short
     and planks are available, the order is the STICKS step first (`CraftOrder.prerequisite`).
   - Also `deficits(targets, stock)` → the unmet targets with the missing material, for the status text ("Needs iron").
5. **Gear repair** (core `RepairRules`): a unit restores `maxDamage / 4` (integer division) **[verified: `AnvilMenu` divides
   `getMaxDamage()` by 4]**, the vanilla anvil's material repair; `units(damage, max) = damage / (max / 4)` (floor: never spend an ingot
   for less than a full quarter; this **deliberately differs** from vanilla, which also spends an ingot on a partial last quarter, and
   is written into DESIGN §5.7 as ingot-saving); the material is whatever `ItemStack.isValidRepairItem(material)` **[verified]** accepts among
   `iron_ingot`, `gold_ingot`, `copper_ingot`, `leather` (tags `repairs_iron_armor` / `iron_tool_materials` = iron_ingot,
   `repairs_leather_armor` = leather **[verified]**). Enchantments and names are kept; the anvil's `REPAIR_COST` component is not touched
   (no anvil menu is used). 100 ticks per unit at the anvil.
   - **Smithy chest**: repaired **in place** (the smith stands at the anvil, re-reads the slot before each unit: same item, same damage,
     else the unit is dropped; `setDamageValue`, `setChanged()`). Anything damageable with a known material counts, including the player's
     own tools (that is what the Smithy chest is for, DESIGN §5.7).
   - **Barracks chests** (M18): items with damage ≥ `max / 4` that no guard has claimed (`GuardGear.CLAIMS`) are fetched (at most
     `SMITH_REPAIR_CARRY` 4 per trip), repaired at the anvil, and carried back to the same Barracks chest (kept until then; if it is full,
     the Smithy chest). The pieces are "carried by the smith" for the gear census meanwhile.
   - **Storage** (critique #16): gear pieces in the stockpile with units ≥ 1 and durability fraction < 0.5 are fetched
     the same way (same carry limit, same row 7) and deposited back into Storage after repair.
6. **Pace and time.** Every clock is `WorkClock(ticksPerUnit).tick(WorkSpeed.untooled(level, needs))`. New batches (smelt, craft, repair)
   start only when they can end by `SMITH_DONE_BY` = `WORK_END − 500` (M13's rule: outputs reach Storage before GO_HOME). At GO_HOME the
   smith keeps nothing (keep 0), except items being returned to a Barracks chest, which it delivers first.
7. **Smith planner** (`SmithPlanner.decide(SmithFacts)`, pure, first match wins; same shape as M9/M10/M13):

   | # | Condition | Decision |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | Smithy invalid | `Idle(OK)` + returning (M7 shows `WORKPLACE_INVALID`) |
   | 2 | `speed == 0` | `Idle(TOO_HUNGRY)` |
   | 3 | `depositNow` and trip not FULL/UNREACHABLE | `Yield` (DepositTrip) |
   | 4 | a golem waits at this Smithy (3.2 #10) and ingots are carried or available | `RepairGolem(n)` (fetch n first if not carried); any time of the WORK window, not cut off by `SMITH_DONE_BY` |
   | 5 | a golem build of this Smithy is saved and day time < `WORK_END` | `BuildGolem` (continue) |
   | 6 | the Smithy chest holds a repairable item and its material is available | `RepairInPlace(slot, units)` |
   | 7 | a Barracks chest or Storage holds an unclaimed repairable item (3.2 #5) and its material is available | `FetchForRepair(items)` |
   | 8 | `GearTargetPlanner.nextForGuards` gives an order | `Craft(order)` (fetch first) |
   | 9 | guard deficits empty, golem allowed (3.2 #9, recounted at this moment), 36 iron + 1 pumpkin available, day time ≤ `GOLEM_LAST_START` | `StartGolem` (fetch) |
   | 10 | raw ore available and fuel covers ≥ 1 unit | `Smelt(batch)`; raw ore but no fuel → remember `NEEDS_FUEL` and go on |
   | 11 | guard deficits empty and `nextSpare` gives an order | `Craft(order)` |
   | 12 | otherwise | `Idle(h)`: `NEEDS_FUEL` if row 10 saw ore without fuel; else `NEEDS_VILLAGERS` ("Too few villagers for a golem") if only the cap stopped row 9; else `NEEDS_IRON` with guard deficits; else `NO_WORK` ("Nothing to smith") |

   **Interruptions** (critique #23): the planner runs between units only. When row 4 (a waiting golem) pre-empts a smelt, craft or
   repair batch, the batch stays the runtime's `current` job, its inputs/fuel/outputs stay kept and reserved, the clock remainder is kept
   per job, and the batch resumes after the golem; at GO_HOME it ends as any batch does. Row 5 above row 8 means a running golem build
   delays a new guard's gear by up to a working day: accepted and written into DESIGN (critique #26).

   Rows 6-11 are also time-gated by `SMITH_DONE_BY` (row 7 by `SMITH_DONE_BY − 1000`, it has two extra walks). Guard gear comes before
   a golem, a golem before spares: a 1-guard village spends 26 ingots on the guard first (DESIGN priority "help the village survive").
8. **What the smith carries.** Fetch and deposit reuse M13 3.2 #7/#8 exactly (reservations under the smith's UUID; outputs kept until the
   batch ends; `SmithKeep.keep` = inputs, fuel and outputs of the current job, plus Barracks items being repaired; 0 at GO_HOME except
   those). Crafted gear and ingots go to Storage (guards take gear from the stockpile, M18 3.7). Repaired Barracks gear goes back to the
   Barracks chest. Spare tools stay in Storage (delivering them to camp chests is out of scope).
9. **Golem build** (the "day of work").
   - **Cap** (`GolemRules.allowed(population, golemsInClaim, buildsInProgress)`): `golems + builds < population / GOLEM_VILLAGERS_PER`
     (5, integer division: 4 villagers → 0, 5 → 1, 10 → 2). `golemsInClaim` = loaded, alive `IronGolem`s inside the claim sphere, any
     origin (a world-generated village golem counts), counted by `SmithySystem` every 100 ticks with one `level.getEntities(EntityType.
     IRON_GOLEM, box, inClaim)` (same box as `VillageCensus`), **and recounted directly** when row 9 is about to start a build and when a
     golem is spawned (the cache is bumped in the spawn tick), so the 100-tick cache never lets a second build in (critique #5). Any
     origin counts (a player's or a world-generated golem fills a slot): documented; smith-built golems are not tracked while unloaded
     (risk 5).
   - **Start** (row 9): fetch 36 `iron_ingot` + 1 `carved_pumpkin` (reserved; 36 fits one slot), walk to the anvil, then **commit**: the
     items leave the inventory and a saved `GolemBuild(progress 0)` is written for this Smithy (`VillageState.golemBuilds`, 3.6). From
     then on the materials are no longer anywhere in the world: the stockpile is −36 / −1, which is what the roadmap asserts.
     `GOLEM_LAST_START` = `WORK_END − 2000` (no new build late in the day).
   - **Work**: at the anvil, clock `GOLEM_BUILD_TICKS` = 8000 work ticks at level 1 (×1.25/×1.5 with M15 levels), progress saved every
     200 ticks and on `stop()`; it pauses outside WORK and continues the next day (a build started at 1000 ends near 9000 the same day).
     Visuals: the blast furnace `LIT` while the smith works on the golem (no fuel is used: decision, the cast is part of the 36 ingots),
     the anvil sound + swing every `GOLEM_HAMMER_TICKS` 400, `ParticleTypes.LAVA` 3 at the blast furnace mouth each hammer stroke via
     `ServerLevel.sendParticles` **[verified signature, M15]**. Status on the Villagers tab: new `ActivityKind.BUILDING_GOLEM`
     ("Building an iron golem"; appended at the end of the enum, no new snapshot field, BACKLOG headroom items).
   - **Finish**: at progress ≥ 8000 the golem appears at the Smithy's **wait spot** (3.2 #10) with
     `SpawnUtil.trySpawnMob(EntityType.IRON_GOLEM, EntitySpawnReason.MOB_SUMMONED, level, spot, 10, 2, 3, SpawnUtil.Strategy.LEGACY_IRON_GOLEM,
     true)` **[verified signature and constants]** (`checkCollision = true`, so it never lands in the Smithy wall); with no wait spot, or
     no fit there, around the door's outside cell with range 6; then `setPlayerCreated(true)` **[verified]** (it is the owner's golem: it never
     turns on players over villager reputation; `DefendVillageTargetGoal` only runs for non-player-created golems), sound
     `SoundEvents.IRON_GOLEM_REPAIR` **[verified]**, `HAPPY_VILLAGER` particles. The build entry is removed in the same tick. If no spot is
     free the spawn is retried every 100 ticks (status stays `BUILDING_GOLEM`, counter `work.blacksmith.golem_blocked`). Golem
     building does not go through `Villager.spawnGolemIfNeeded`, so the spawn gate (#11) never blocks it.
   - **Refund** (critique #15): a build whose Smithy marker is **unregistered** (marker broken) returns 36 ingots + 1 pumpkin: deposited
     into Storage as far as there is space (`Stockpiles.deposit` per stack, partial allowed), the rest dropped at the marker position, and
     only while that chunk is loaded (otherwise the refund waits; an unloaded marker is not an unregistered one). The entry is removed in
     the same tick as the refund, so it can never run twice. A registered but invalid Smithy (or a Smithy that loses tier) keeps its
     build, paused, until it is valid again. Releasing the claim (`VillageClaims.releaseAt`, Heart broken) refunds every build first, by
     the same rule. Firing the smith keeps the build: the next smith of that Smithy continues it.
   - **Carried items on death or fire** (critique #14): villagers do not drop their inventory on death (`Villager` has no
     `dropEquipment` override **[verified]**). `SmithRuntime.forget` on death drops the job items the smith carries (fetched ingots,
     pumpkin, ore, fuel, gear in transit) at its position; on fire they go to Storage (`Stockpiles.deposit`), else are dropped.
10. **Golem repair.** A golem walks to the Smithy itself (goal, below); the smith comes out to it.
    - **Wait spot** of a Smithy = the scan's door, 3 blocks out (outward = door − start, normalised to a horizontal direction), on the
      ground at door y: a **2×2×3 air volume on solid ground** (the golem is 1.4 wide; critique #8), else the next such cell along the
      same line up to 6 out; none → the Smithy offers no repairs (no heightmap lookup: a spot on a roof would be unreachable). Once per
      new scan, `golemNavigation.createPath(spot, 1)` **[verified `createPath(BlockPos, int)`]** from the door's outside cell must
      reach it, else the spot is rejected. Iron golems are 1.4 wide
      and 2.7 tall and do not fit through a door, which is why they wait outside, and 3 out so they do not block the doorway.
    - **Goal** `SmithyRepairGoal` (glue `dev.villageheart.golem`), added to **every** iron golem by a mixin into
      `IronGolem.registerGoals` at TAIL (`registerGoals` runs only on a `ServerLevel` **[verified Mob constructor bytecode: `instanceof
      ServerLevel` before `registerGoals`]**), `goalSelector.addGoal(1, goal)` **[verified `GoalSelector.addGoal(int, Goal)`]**, flags
      MOVE + LOOK. Priority 1 so that `MoveBackToVillageGoal` (2) and the strolls (4) never take it over; it gives way to combat by
      itself: `canUse`/`canContinueToUse` are false while `golem.getTarget() != null` **[verified `Mob.getTarget()`]**, and the vanilla
      `MeleeAttackGoal` (also 1) then starts. Vanilla iron golem goals **[verified bytecode of `registerGoals`]**: 1 MeleeAttack,
      2 MoveTowardsTarget, 2 MoveBackToVillage, 4 GolemRandomStrollInVillage, 5 OfferFlower, 7 LookAtPlayer, 8 RandomLookAround;
      targets 1 DefendVillage, 2 HurtBy, 3 NearestAttackable (players, mobs), 4 ResetUniversalAnger.

## 6. Risks & unknowns, out of scope

**Risks** (highest first):
1. **(top) Golem walking and waiting.** A 1.4 × 2.7 golem must path to a spot next to a room, stand there while vanilla goals and the
   targetSelector keep running, and not be pushed away by villagers. The vanilla goals were read (3.2 #10) but their interplay with a
   priority-1 MOVE goal is untested; `MoveBackToVillageGoal` and being shoved can still move it. Mitigations: the smith comes to the
   golem (re-targeted walk, reach 3.0) rather than the other way round, the wait spot is a checked 2×2×3 cell 3 out from the door, the
   waiting golem's navigation is stopped every tick, one golem per Smithy at a time, and T-L2-2/2b/2c/2d/2e check walk, wait, combat,
   queueing and reloads separately. If priority 1 misbehaves (for example `MoveBackToVillageGoal` still wins because the claim has no
   vanilla village POIs), stop the other movers while waiting with `goalSelector.disableControlFlag(Goal.Flag.MOVE)` (re-enabled in `stop()`) **[verified `GoalSelector.disableControlFlag/enableControlFlag`]**, and record it in §8.
2b. **The gate counts on `wantsToSpawnGolem` matching vanilla's own filter.** If T-L2-3's pre-round assertion fails (fewer than 5
   villagers want a golem) the test is wrong, not the gate: fix the setup, never relax the assertion.
2. **T-L2-1 runs ~9000+ ticks (and is bounded at 24 000)**: slow (several minutes) and exposed to M9-style walking flakes. The build
   itself is a fixed clock; only the fetch walk varies. Keep the Storage 4-6 blocks from the Smithy door.
3. **Three unmerged dependencies** (M13 pipeline, M18 guards/Barracks, M14/M15 numbers). Each has a fallback in 3.1; the build gate is
   M13 + M18 on dev. If M13's shape differs, step 8 adapts and records it rather than writing a second fetch system.
4. **Mixins** into `Villager.spawnGolemIfNeeded` and `IronGolem.registerGoals`: another mod doing the same could conflict; both are
   small HEAD/TAIL injects (`defaultRequire 1` makes a mismatch fail at start-up, not silently).
5. **Gear census blind spots**: unloaded guards count as fully equipped; golems in unloaded chunks are not counted (the cap can be
   exceeded by golems that wandered out of loaded chunks; golems stroll within the village, so this is rare). Both documented.
6. **Ingot competition**: guard gear first, then a golem, then spares. A village with many guards and little iron gets no golem until
   the guards are equipped; that is the intended priority, but tuning (M26) may want a golem earlier.

**Out of scope**: a Heart UI to change gear targets (Balance only); delivering spare tools to Lumber Camp/Farmhouse/Mine chests;
repairing items in job chests other than the Smithy and Barracks; diamond/netherite/copper/chainmail crafting; smelting ore blocks,
raw-ore blocks or other furnace recipes; enchanting; anvil `REPAIR_COST`; golems following orders, patrol routes or names; golems for
other villages; a player's own golems being refused (only villager-made spawns are gated).
