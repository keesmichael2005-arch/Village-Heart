## 1. Goal

A farmer hired at a Farmhouse spends the work day on the farmland within 24 blocks of its composter: it harvests mature wheat,
carrots, potatoes and beetroots and replants each cell straight away from the yield, turns surplus wheat and beetroot seeds into
bone meal in its composter, uses the bone meal on young crops, and carries the harvest to Storage. It never makes new fields and
never touches immature crops, other plants, or farmland outside its area. No tool is needed; a hoe in the Farmhouse chest makes it
work ×1.25 faster (and wears out).

### 3.2 Decisions (pinned here; DESIGN §5.2 gets a "Rules as built (M10)" paragraph in step 13)

1. **Own behaviour, not vanilla's.** Vanilla farming lives in `HarvestFarmland`, `UseBonemeal` and `WorkAtComposter` **[verified]**. None
   of them can run for a mod worker, and none matches §5.2:
   - `HarvestFarmland.checkExtraStartConditions` requires `profession().is(VillagerProfession.FARMER)` and `GameRules.MOB_GRIEFING`, and the
     memory `SECONDARY_JOB_SITE` (filled by vanilla's secondary-POI sensor). The worker profession is `villageheart:farmer`; giving workers
     the vanilla FARMER profession brings back vanilla trades and the job-site/`ResetProfession` fight M7 removed (M7 plan 3.1).
   - It only looks at the 3×3×3 cells around the villager, picks a target with `serverLevel.getRandom()`, works in 200-tick sessions, drops
     the yield on the ground (`destroyBlock(pos, true, villager)`, so it depends on vanilla looting, which needs `mob_griefing`), and plants
     any `#villager_plantable_seeds` (includes torchflower seeds and pitcher pods) on any farmland, with no area limit and no pace hook.
   - `UseBonemeal` picks a random crop in 3×3 and puts a bone meal in MAINHAND (a death drop at `DropChances` 0.085 would duplicate it; M9
     rule: no held items). `WorkAtComposter` needs `JOB_SITE` (removed in the worker brain), turns wheat into bread (the Cook's job, M13; wheat
     must reach Storage) and composts with the composter's random 0.3 chance.
   So M10 writes `FarmerWork` (one `Behavior<Villager>`, the M9 pattern) and reuses vanilla's **static** pieces: `CropBlock.isMaxAge`,
   `Block.getDrops(state, level, pos, null, villager, tool)`, `BoneMealItem.growCrop(stack, level, pos)`, `ComposterBlock.LEVEL/READY`,
   level events 1500 (composter fill) and 1505 (bone meal particles), sounds `CROP_PLANTED`, `COMPOSTER_FILL`, `COMPOSTER_EMPTY` **[verified]**.
   The worker brain stays M7's; `FarmerWork` is the FARMER branch of `JobBehaviours.work`.

2. **Crops** (core `CropKind`, L1). Exactly four, matched by block id, not by `instanceof CropBlock` (torchflower crop extends `CropBlock`
   **[verified]**):

   | Kind | Block | Age property / mature | Product | Seed (replant item) | Mature drops **[verified loot]** |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | WHEAT | `minecraft:wheat` | `CropBlock.AGE` (AGE_7) = 7 | wheat | wheat_seeds | 1 wheat + (1 + Bin(3+fortune, 0.5714)) seeds |
   | CARROTS | `minecraft:carrots` | AGE_7 = 7 (`CarrotBlock` does not override) | carrot | carrot | 1 + (1 + Bin(3, 0.5714)) carrots |
   | POTATOES | `minecraft:potatoes` | AGE_7 = 7 | potato | potato | 1 + (1 + Bin(3, 0.5714)) potatoes, 2% poisonous potato |
   | BEETROOTS | `minecraft:beetroots` | `BeetrootBlock.AGE` (AGE_3) = 3 | beetroot | beetroot_seeds | 1 beetroot + (1 + Bin(3, 0.5714)) seeds |

   Mature = `crop.isMaxAge(state)` (glue) and `age >= maxAge(kind)` (core). The **product count is deterministic** (exactly 1 wheat or
   beetroot per mature crop; ≥ 2 carrots/potatoes); only seed and extra-root counts are random. Immature crops are never broken.

3. **One work unit = one atomic cell action**, done in a single server tick after the `WorkClock` completes:
   - HARVEST (mature crop): `Block.getDrops(state, level, pos, null, villager, hoeOrEmpty)` (skipped when `GameRules.BLOCK_DROPS` is false),
     `level.destroyBlock(pos, false, villager)` (break particles, `BLOCK_DESTROY` event), drops into the villager inventory
     (`SimpleContainer.addItem`; overflow `Block.popResource`, collected later), then **in the same tick** replant the same kind at age 0
     (`level.setBlock(pos, kind.defaultBlockState(), 3)`, `gameEvent(BLOCK_PLACE)`, `CROP_PLANTED`), taking one seed item from the
     inventory. So a field cell is never left empty between two ticks while a seed exists, and nothing has to be resumed after a reload,
     firing or death. Replanting requires `cropState.canSurvive(level, pos)` (light ≥ 8 and farmland below **[verified `CropBlock.canSurvive`]**);
     else the cell stays empty (an empty cell is planted later, #5).
   - PLANT (empty farmland, `isAir()` above `minecraft:farmland`): same placement as the replant.
   - BONEMEAL: `BoneMealItem.growCrop(stack, level, pos)` (shrinks the stack; `CropBlock.performBonemeal` adds `nextInt(2, 5)` ages,
     beetroot that `/3`, i.e. 0 or 1 **[verified]**), then `level.levelEvent(1505, pos, 15)`.
   - COMPOST: insert one surplus seed (#7).
   Unit costs (`Balance`, tunable): `FARM_TICKS_HARVEST` = 20, `FARM_TICKS_PLANT` = 10, `FARM_TICKS_BONEMEAL` = 20, `FARM_TICKS_COMPOST` = 10.
   With `WorkSpeed` ×1.0 an 80-cell field takes 1 600 work ticks plus walking (≈ 2 500 ticks, finished well before noon).

4. **Work area**: a farmland cell whose crop cell (farmland y + 1) is within 24 blocks horizontally (Euclidean xz, inclusive:
   `dx² + dz² ≤ 576`) of the worker's **job block** (its composter) and within ±8 in y. DESIGN says "of the Farmhouse"; the composter is
   inside the Farmhouse and is the same anchor M9 uses (Chopping Block), so two Farmhouse composters 6 blocks apart give slightly different
   areas (accepted). Not limited to the claim. Farmland inside rooms counts (DESIGN has no exclusion).

5. **Planting empty farmland** (does not make fields: the farmland already exists). Kind = the majority kind among the 8 neighbouring crop
   cells at the same y; ties and no neighbours → first available in WHEAT, CARROTS, POTATOES, BEETROOTS order; only kinds the farmer can get
   a seed for. Seed source: inventory, else the Farmhouse chest (`CampContainers`, the worker walks within 2 blocks and takes up to the
   number of open cells of that kind: `FETCH`). Storage is not searched for seeds in v1 (§6 out of scope).

6. **Hoe = flat ×1.25, no hoe = ×1.0.** DESIGN §5.2 ("No tool is needed (a hoe gives ×1.25)") overrides the §4 default ×0.5. M9's
   `ToolRequirement` gets a third mode: `ToolRequirement(ToolKind kind, Mode mode)`, `Mode {REQUIRED, OPTIONAL, BONUS}`, keeping
   `required()` as `mode == REQUIRED` for M9 callers. `WorkSpeed` for BONUS: tool present → `Balance.HOE_BONUS` = 1.25 (any tier, wood
   included), missing → 1.0, never `NEEDS_TOOL`. FARMER = `(HOE, BONUS)`. The hoe is leased in place (`CampTools`, `#minecraft:hoes`
   **[verified tag]**) and loses 1 durability per HARVEST (not per plant, bone meal or compost).

7. **Composting is deterministic** (core `CompostRule`). Vanilla `ComposterBlock.addItem` raises the level on the first item always and
   then with chance 0.3 per seed **[verified]** (mean 1 + 6/0.3 = 21 seeds per bone meal). The mod keeps that mean without randomness:
   at level 0 a seed raises the level to 1; above 0 each seed adds 3 tenths and every 10 tenths raise one level (exactly 21 seeds per bone
   meal). The tenths remainder lives per composter in `FarmRuntime` (transient: a restart loses at most 9 tenths = 3 seeds). Glue mirrors
   vanilla's package-private `addItem`/`empty`: `setBlock(pos, state.setValue(LEVEL, n), 3)`, `gameEvent(BLOCK_CHANGE)`,
   `levelEvent(1500, pos, changed ? 1 : 0)`, and at level 7 `level.scheduleTick(pos, Blocks.COMPOSTER, 20)`; vanilla's `tick` then makes it
   READY (8). At READY the farmer takes it: level 0 (same three calls), `COMPOSTER_EMPTY`, **one bone meal straight into the inventory**
   (no item entity). Only `#wheat_seeds` and `#beetroot_seeds` items are composted (the two vanilla's `WorkAtComposter` uses), only into the
   worker's own composter, and only while the block is `minecraft:composter` with level < 7. A composter a player or hopper filled is used
   the same way (READY → taken).
   *Surplus* = seeds above `SEED_RESERVE` = 16 per seed item. The planner composts when surplus ≥ `COMPOST_BATCH` = 8, or when nothing
   else is left to do and surplus > 0; once at the composter it inserts all surplus (one per unit), waiting at level 7 for READY.

8. **Bone meal targets**: a young crop (`age < max`) in the area, unclaimed. Order: non-beetroot first (a beetroot gains 0-1 age per bone
   meal), then lowest `age / max`, then nearest to the composter (dist²), then `Pos`. Bone meal is used whenever it is carried and a target
   exists; it is never deposited (kept, #9).

9. **Keep rule** (what `DepositTrip` leaves in the inventory), core `FarmKeep` + shared `FoodKeep`:
   - FARMER job keep: wheat seeds and beetroot seeds: all (surplus is composted, never deposited); carrots and potatoes: `min(have,
     SEED_RESERVE)`; bone meal: all; everything else (wheat, beetroot, the rest of the carrots/potatoes, poisonous potatoes, other pickups): 0.
   - **Shared food top-up (changes M7, A3)**: after the job keep, food in `Villager.FOOD_POINTS` (bread 4, potato 1, carrot 1, beetroot 1
     **[verified]**) is additionally kept until the kept food points reach `WORKER_FOOD_KEEP` = 12 (= `Villager.BREEDING_FOOD_THRESHOLD`
     **[verified]**, what `canBreed` needs), bread first, then potato, carrot, beetroot. Job-kept carrots/potatoes count toward the 12.
     The rest is deposited. For every non-farmer the result is "keep up to 12 food points" instead of "keep all food": M7's breeding test
     (12+ points each) keeps passing; a lumberjack holding 5 bread keeps 3 and deposits 2.

10. **Pickup**: drops that did not fit (popped) and crop items lying in the area are collected with M9's `ItemPickup` (wheat, wheat seeds,
    carrot, potato, poisonous potato, beetroot, beetroot seeds, bone meal), `COLLECT_RADIUS` 12 of the worker and inside the area.

11. **Chunks**: never loads or forces a chunk. All reads through `getChunkNow` (`LevelFieldView`, like M9's `LevelTreeView`); UNLOADED cells
    are skipped; a cell is a target only when `level.isPositionEntityTicking(pos)` **[verified on ServerLevel]**. Every action re-reads its
    cell first (the player may have harvested it).

12. **Rules shared with M9**: `mob_griefing` does not stop the farmer (hired; also: vanilla `FarmBlock.fallOn` trample needs `mob_griefing`,
    but villagers walking on a flat field fall < 0.5 blocks **[verified]**); `block_drops = false` gives no yield (the farmer still replants
    from carried seeds). While the worker's trip status is `STORAGE_FULL` it does not harvest (DESIGN §6 "stop gathering"); planting, bone
    meal and composting continue (they add nothing to the load).

13. **No saved data.** Harvest/replant is atomic, the composter level is block state, claims and the field index are transient
    (`FarmRuntime`). M10 adds no codec key.

### 3.4 The farmer's plan (`FarmerPlanner`, first match wins)

| # | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | workplace invalid | `IDLE_AT_FARMHOUSE` (status `WORKPLACE_INVALID`, M7) |
| 2 | `DepositTrip.wanted` | `YIELD` |
| 3 | an action in progress (walking to / working a cell, at the composter) whose target is still valid | continue |
| 4 | trip status not `STORAGE_FULL`, a mature crop unclaimed | `HARVEST(nearest to the worker; ties Pos)` |
| 5 | a wanted item entity (3.2 #10) | `COLLECT(nearest)` |
| 6 | empty farmland unclaimed and `PlantChoice` finds a kind with a seed in the inventory, or in the Farmhouse chest | `PLANT(nearest)` (`FETCH` first when the seed is only in the chest) |
| 7 | composter READY, or surplus ≥ `COMPOST_BATCH` | `COMPOST` |
| 8 | bone meal carried and `BoneMealTargets.pick` finds a crop | `BONEMEAL(target)` |
| 9 | surplus > 0 | `COMPOST` |
| 10 | otherwise | `IDLE_AT_FARMHOUSE`; status `NO_WORK` ("No farmland within 24 blocks") only when a complete sweep found no farmland at all (sticky until farmland is found); waiting for crops to grow is `OK` |

The planner is consulted after every unit and every 20 ticks while idle. The `WorkClock` lives in `FarmRuntime` per worker (not in the
behaviour), reset on a change of target. Claims (cell → worker UUID) are server-wide in `FarmRuntime`, released when the unit ends, the
worker leaves WORK, is fired or unloads; a cell claimed by another farmer is skipped (two Farmhouses may overlap).

### 3.5 Glue (`dev.villageheart.worker.farm`)

- `LevelFieldView implements FieldView`: `getChunkNow` + cached chunk; CROP when the block id maps to a `CropKind` **and** the block below
  is `minecraft:farmland` (age from `CropBlock.getAge`); EMPTY_FARMLAND when `isAir()` above farmland; else OTHER; no chunk → UNLOADED.
- `FarmRuntime` (per server, cleared on claim change): field index per job block (one composter = one farmer slot), claims, composter tenths, per-worker clock/target.
- `FarmerWork extends Behavior<Villager>` (WORK priority 3): entry `WALK_TARGET` REGISTERED, `LOOK_TARGET` REGISTERED, long-running like
  `LumberjackWork`. Each tick inside `VillageTicker.section()`: spend index budget, re-plan when needed, run the action: walk with M9's
  `WalkTo(target, closeEnough 1)` until within `FARM_REACH` (3D to `atBottomCenterOf(cell)`), look at the cell, `WorkClock.tick(WorkSpeed…)`,
  on a completed unit do the atomic action of 3.2 #3 / #7, count it (3.8), stamp `LAST_WORKED_AT_POI`. A unit whose cell changed
  (no longer mature / no longer empty / not loaded) is dropped without effect. Unreachable target: M9's watchdog (no progress ≥ 1 block in
  200 ticks or 800 total) → the cell is skipped for 1 200 ticks.
- `JobBehaviours.work(v)` for FARMER = `[DepositTrip (1), FarmerWork (3), StandAtJobBlock (4)]`; `JobBehaviours.keep` FARMER branch =
  `FarmKeep` + `FoodKeep`.
- `FarmerStatus` texts: `status.villageheart.no_work.farmer` = "No farmland within 24 blocks" (en_us).
- Hoe: `CampTools.lease(server, v, HOE)` before each HARVEST unit; `use(lease, 1)` after it. `UNKNOWN` (chest unreadable) counts as no hoe
  (×1.0; there is no "needs" status to protect for a BONUS tool).

### 3.7 GameTest determinism (goes into TESTING.md "Farmer GameTests")
- **Crop ages are set by the test and frozen**: every farmer test has its own environment
  `villageheart-gametest:farm_<name>` = `{"type": "minecraft:game_rules", "rules": {"minecraft:random_tick_speed": 0}}`. `GameTestRunner`
  runs environments as separate batches, one after another, calling `setup` before and `teardown` (which resets each listed rule to its
  default) after **[verified `GameTestRunner.runBatch`, `TestEnvironmentDefinition.SetGameRules`]**. With 0 random ticks crops never grow,
  farmland never dries or reverts **[verified `FarmBlock.randomTick`]**, so every age in a test comes from the setup, the farmer's replant
  (age 0) or its bone meal. Each test asserts `getGameRules().get(RANDOM_TICK_SPEED) == 0` first (a mis-parsed environment fails loudly).
- Fields are built farmland first (`MOISTURE` 7), then crops with `setBlock(..., 2)`, under open sky at day time 1000 (light ≥ 8).
- Remaining randomness: seed/root counts (loot), bone meal growth (2-5, beetroot 0-1), walking. Assertions use the deterministic parts
  exactly (product counts, cell states, unit gaps while standing still, bone meal and seed counts with deterministic composting) and
  **measured bounds** for time: `farmBound(units, dist) = WALK_TICKS_PER_BLOCK (10) × dist + Σ unit ticks + 20 × units (re-targeting)
  + 200`. Every roadmap test logs its tick counts (`[m10] …`); the builder copies them into PROGRESS.
- Stray state: `FarmTestSupport.sweepStrayCrops(h, 40)` removes crops, farmland and crop items within 40 blocks outside the test's own bounds.

### 3.8 L3 metrics (for M11's `starter`: food from crops)

## 6. Risks, unknowns, out of scope

**Risks**
1. **(top) Dependency on unbuilt M9 code.** Every glue step reuses M9's toolkit (`CampTools`, `WalkTo`, `ItemPickup`, `JobBehaviours.keep`,
   `DepositTrip.wanted`, `WorkStats`) and changes two of its pieces (`ToolRequirement` gets `BONUS`; `DepositTrip` gets the capped food
   keep). If M9 lands with different shapes, steps 4-7 adapt; if M9 is not merged, M10 stops after step 3 rather than copy any of it.
2. **Changing M7's food keep** affects every job. Mitigated: the cap equals vanilla's breeding threshold, M7's breeding test and M9's keep test
   re-run unchanged in step 5, T-L2-9 has a lumberjack control.
3. **Environment game rules.** The determinism of every farmer test rests on `random_tick_speed 0` per environment. Verified in the sources
   (sequential batches, teardown resets); each test asserts the rule first, so a parse or ordering surprise fails loudly instead of flaking.
   No other gamerule is touched.
4. **Pathing on fields**: villagers can step into the centre water of a 9×9 field or path around it; partial paths (FOLLOW_RANGE 16) are handled
   by `WalkTo`. Trampling needs a fall > 0.5 blocks plus `mob_griefing` **[verified `FarmBlock.fallOn`]**; a jump onto farmland could still
   trample a cell. T-L2-3 (b) would show it; if it happens, that is a design signal (e.g. walk targets on non-farmland), not a reason to loosen (b).
5. **Composter shared with players/hoppers**: a hopper pulling from a READY composter races the farmer; whoever comes first gets the bone meal.
   Accepted. The tenths remainder is lost on restart (≤ 3 seeds).
6. **Vanilla farmers elsewhere** (unemployed villagers that took a free composter outside a building) can still harvest the mod's fields with
   `HarvestFarmland`. Not changed in v1 (vanilla outside buildings stays vanilla, DESIGN §3.4).
7. **Tick cost**: index sweep/refresh are budgeted and counted (T-L1-5); all work inside `VillageTicker.section()`.

**Out of scope**: tilling or making fields (DESIGN), watering/hydration, fetching seeds from Storage (BACKLOG candidate: DESIGN §4 "inputs are
fetched the same way"), melons, pumpkins, sugar cane, sweet berries, torchflowers, pitcher plants, nether wart, cocoa, bread making (M13),
held-item visuals, XP/levels (M15), needs effects (M11), the `starter` scenario (M11).
