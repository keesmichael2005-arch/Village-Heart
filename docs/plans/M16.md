## 1. Goal

A miner hired at a Mine takes the best pickaxe in the Mine chest, walks to the Mine's linked Mineshaft and vanishes into it. Every 2
in-game hours of work (stone pickaxe, level 1) it comes back out with one haul from the table of the shaft's deepest point (Y ≥ 40,
0 ≤ Y < 40, Y < 0), carries it to Storage and goes back down. Diamonds only come with an iron pickaxe or better from below Y 0. Each haul
wears the pickaxe. Without a pickaxe it does not mine ("Needs pickaxe"); when the shaft stops being valid it comes out and stops, and the
Heart screen says why. While it is underground the Heart screen shows it as "Away (mining)".

### 3.2 Decisions (DESIGN §5.3 gets a "Rules as built (M16)" paragraph in step 12)

1. **"Disappears inside" = the villager stays a loaded entity, hidden in the shaft.** On arrival at the entrance the miner is moved to
   the *hold spot* and made `setInvisible(true)`, `setInvulnerable(true)`, `setSilent(true)`, `setNoAi(true)` **[verified on
   `Entity`/`Mob`]**. Why this and not removing the entity or a far holding spot:
   - it keeps its UUID, attachments, POI tickets (bed, job block), brain memories and inventory; nothing has to be re-created, nothing
     can be duplicated by a crash between a remove and a re-add;
   - it is saved with its chunk like any villager (all four flags are vanilla NBT: `Invisible`, `Invulnerable`, `Silent`, `NoAI`), and our
     state is a persistent attachment on the same entity, so the two are always saved together;
   - **`NoAI` freezes the vanilla brain**: `LivingEntity.aiStep` calls `serverAiStep` only when `isEffectiveAi()`, which `Mob` makes
     false with `isNoAi()` **[verified]**; `travel` is skipped the same way, so it is not pushed, does not fall and does not walk off.
     No vanilla behaviour (sleep, panic, raid hide, meet, gossip, POI acquisition, trading restock in `customServerAiStep`) can move or
     change it while it is away;
   - `Invulnerable` also hides it from mobs: `LivingEntity.canBeSeenAsEnemy()` = `!isInvulnerable() && canBeSeenByAnyone()`
     **[verified]**, so zombies and raiders never target it (no conversion, no raid death). Only `/kill` and the void bypass it;
   - the Heart screen still has its name and data (it is loaded), and shows `AWAY_WORKING` ("Away (mining)", §3.6).
   **Hold spot** = the shaft's deepest cell: `ShaftScanner.ShaftSurvey` gains `deepestCell` (the first visited open cell at `deepestY`,
   pure change, L1 T-L1-11), stored with `deepestY` (decision 8). If that cell or the one above it holds a fluid, the hold spot moves up
   that column to the first cell pair that is air or non-fluid and has no full collision shape (ladders, rails and torches are fine:
   NoAI means the villager never collides or moves). **Never the marker cell** (it would block clicks on the marker). The deepest cell is
   out of the player's way at the entrance; it can be in another chunk than the marker (the fill spreads 16 sideways), which only means
   the hidden miner freezes when that chunk is not entity-ticking (step gate, 3.4). The *surface spot* is where the miner stood when it
   arrived (a block it could path to); both are saved in the trip. Each underground step re-pins the villager to the hold spot if it
   drifted more than 0.5 blocks (pistons move entities directly).
   At hide time the brain's `WALK_TARGET`, `LOOK_TARGET` and `PATH` memories are erased and `getNavigation().stop()` is called, so
   nothing stale resumes when the brain wakes up (it is frozen from the next tick on, so `canStillUse` alone would be too late).
   `CustomNameVisible` is also saved and cleared (a named villager would otherwise show a floating name tag).
   Players cannot trade with or hit a hidden worker: Fabric `UseEntityCallback.EVENT` and `AttackEntityCallback.EVENT` **[verified,
   fabric-events-interaction-v0 4.1.1]** return `InteractionResult.FAIL` for an entity with an away trip. Its hitbox still catches the
   player's crosshair in the deepest cell (accepted, risk 4). Its shadow is not drawn (invisible).
2. **Coming up** ("surfacing") restores the flags to the values saved at hide time (`PriorFlags`), clears the trip, and teleports
   to the surface spot (`Entity.teleportTo(x, y, z)` **[verified]**) if its feet and head cells are free and its chunk is loaded; else to
   the first free standable cell within 2 blocks of the marker. If there is none it **stays hidden** with the haul pending, status
   `SITE_UNREACHABLE`, and retries every step (never left visible at the bottom of a pit). Surfacing is always done from a server tick
   (the `MinerSystem` step, or M7's `processLoaded` batch at `END_SERVER_TICK`), never inside the ENTITY_LOAD callback itself, so no entity
   is moved while its chunk is loading. A test hook `AwayTrips.lastSurface(uuid)` records (game time, position, reason) of the last
   surfacing; the reason is also kept in the attachment (`lastReason`) for the L2 tests and the logs. The brain resumes the activity of
   the current day time within 20 ticks (M7's `UpdateWorkerActivity`).
3. **Haul timing**: underground progress is counted in *units*. A haul costs `MINER_HAUL_UNITS` = 2500 units. The `MinerSystem` step runs
   every `MINER_STEP_TICKS` = 20 ticks (staggered by entity id) and adds `20 × WorkSpeed.multiplier(tier, PICKAXE required, level, needs)`.
   Stone ×1.25 at level 1 → 1.25 units per tick (25 per step) → exactly **2000 ticks = 2 in-game hours** per haul (DESIGN §5.3); wood
   2500 ticks, iron 1667, diamond 1429, netherite 1250. "Every 2 in-game hours" is **underground work time**: the walk to the shaft and the
   deposit trip are on top, so a real cycle is about 2300-2600 ticks (written into DESIGN in step 12). Progress belongs to one shaft:
   `AwayState.progressShaft` records it, and progress is reset whenever the miner goes down a different shaft (no HIGH progress cashed in
   as DEEP). Progress is saved in the attachment; the fractional remainder carries to the next haul
   (`AwayProgress`, pure, the same carry rule as M9's `WorkClock`, which is not used directly because it is transient and per tick).
   No new descent after `MINER_LAST_DESCENT` = day time 10400 (less than 600 work ticks left). At 11000 an underground miner comes up
   **without** a haul and **keeps** its progress for the next day.
4. **Pickaxe**: leased with M9's `CampTools.lease(server, villager, PICKAXE)` from the Mine chest (tools stay in the chest, M9 decision
   4). Without a lease: no descent, status `NEEDS_TOOL` ("Needs pickaxe"). The lease is re-read on every underground step: the tier of
   that step sets the pace (a better pickaxe put in mid-trip speeds up the rest); `NONE` → come up at once, no haul, progress kept,
   `NEEDS_TOOL`; `UNKNOWN` (chest chunk unloaded or no scan after a restart) → no progress this step, stay down; after
   `MINER_UNKNOWN_LIMIT` = 60 steps (1200 ticks) of `UNKNOWN` in a row it comes up (`STOPPED_WORKING`, progress kept, logged once), so a
   Mine that is never rescanned cannot hold a miner underground all day with an `OK` status. On the surface the lease is re-read at every
   planner run (every 20 ticks), so a pickaxe put into the chest is seen within 20 ticks.
   **Wear**: `MINER_WEAR_PER_HAUL` = 10 durability per completed haul, through `CampTools.use(level, lease, 10)` (Unbreaking applies).
   Stone pickaxe (131) ≈ 13 hauls ≈ 3 days; wood (59) 5; iron (250) 25; diamond (1561) 156. A pickaxe that breaks on the last haul still
   gives that haul. If `use` returns false (the player swapped the pickaxe in the same tick) the haul is still given and the new tool is
   not charged (one free haul at most, accepted).
5. **Haul table** (core `HaulTable`, tunable in `Balance`), chosen by `DepthBand.of(deepestY)`:
   `HIGH` Y ≥ 40, `MID` 0 ≤ Y < 40, `DEEP` Y < 0 (boundaries 40 and 0 belong to the upper band).

   | Band | Fixed | Chance entries (count when it hits) |
   |---|---|---|
   | HIGH | 16 cobblestone, 2 coal, 1 raw copper | 30% 1 raw iron |
   | MID | 12 cobblestone, 2 coal, 2 raw copper, 2 raw iron | 20% 4 redstone, 10% 4 lapis lazuli |
   | DEEP | 12 cobbled deepslate, 1 coal, 3 raw iron | 30% 4 redstone, 20% 4 lapis lazuli, 20% 1 raw gold, 2% 1 diamond (**iron tier or better**) |

   DESIGN gives no count for redstone and lapis; 4 (the vanilla minimum drop of one ore block) is pinned here, 1 for gold and diamond.
   "Iron or better" = `IRON`, `DIAMOND`, `NETHERITE`; `GOLD` and `COPPER` are below iron (`ToolTier` order) and get no diamonds. The
   pickaxe tier changes the pace (decision 3) and the diamond gate only; the wooden pickaxe uses the stone table (DESIGN lists stone;
   vanilla's "wood cannot mine iron" is not modelled, risk 5).
6. **Seeded RNG**: `HaulRng.forHaul(worldSeed, villagerUuid, haulIndex)` = `new SplittableRandom(s)` with
   `s = mix(mix(mix(worldSeed ^ uuid.getMostSignificantBits()) ^ uuid.getLeastSignificantBits()) ^ haulIndex)`, where `mix` is the
   SplitMix64 finaliser (`z = (z ^ (z >>> 30)) * 0xBF58476D1CE4E5B9L; z = (z ^ (z >>> 27)) * 0x94D049BB133111EBL; return z ^ (z >>> 31)`),
   pinned so it is reproducible across refactors (T-L1-4 pins three literal seeds). Pure Java, `java.util.random.RandomGenerator`. `haulIndex` is a per-worker counter saved in the
   attachment (`rolls`), incremented once per haul. `worldSeed` = `ServerLevel.getSeed()` **[verified]** (0 in the GameTest world:
   `GameTestServer.WORLD_OPTIONS = new WorldOptions(0L, …)` **[verified]**). Every chance entry draws exactly one `nextDouble()` in table
   order, gated or not, so a stone and an iron pickaxe give the same non-diamond results for the same index (pinned in T-L1-3). Effects:
   L1 tests are exact; L2 tests can predict every haul; a reload never re-rolls a haul differently.
7. **Which shaft**: the VALID Mineshaft whose saved `link` is the miner's Mine, nearest to the Mine marker (ties: `VillageState.locationOrder`).
   Several miners may be in one shaft at once (no capacity). A Mine with no such shaft is INVALID anyway (M5); during the up-to-one-cycle
   lag before the Mine is re-checked the miner reports `NO_WORK` ("No valid Mineshaft"). A shaft whose record is gone (marker broken)
   counts as invalid.
8. **Depth is runtime data**: `SiteSurveys.Survey` gains `Optional<ShaftDepth(int deepestY, Pos deepestCell)>` (MINESHAFT only);
   `BuildingValidator` stores it in a new `BuildingRuntime.shaftDeepest` map when the survey is complete (`forget` clears it);
   `Buildings.shaftDepth(server, loc) : Optional<ShaftDepth>`. After a restart it is unknown until the shaft's first re-check (all buildings are queued at priority on restart, M5); a
   miner whose shaft depth is unknown does not descend (status stays `OK`), and an underground miner whose haul is due waits (progress
   capped at one haul) until it is known. Not saved, so no codec change.
9. **Delivery**: the haul goes into the villager inventory (`SimpleContainer.addItem`). What does not fit (kept food in several slots,
   M10's FoodKeep, plus up to 7 haul kinds can exceed 8 slots) is **not dropped**: it stays in `AwayState.pending` (saved) and is put into
   the inventory as soon as there is room after the deposit trip (planner row 2 covers "pending not empty"), so nothing is lost or left
   on the ground. `JobBehaviours.depositNow(miner)` is true while it carries any non-food item, so M7's `DepositTrip` takes the haul to
   Storage straight away, whatever its size. Miner keep = 0. A miner killed on its deposit trip loses what it carries (vanilla villagers
   drop no inventory); the `work.miner.<item>` counters count what was mined, not what was delivered (documented).
10. **Miner plan** (core `MinerPlanner.decide`, first row that matches; the glue gathers the inputs):

    | # | Condition | Action / status |
    |---|---|---|
    | 1 | no slot (`Workers.status` = `WORKPLACE_INVALID`) | `IDLE_AT_MINE` (walk to the stonecutter) |
    | 2 | carries a non-food item (`depositNow`), or `pending` is not empty | `YIELD` (DepositTrip runs; if its status is `STORAGE_FULL`/`UNREACHABLE` the miner waits at the Mine, DESIGN §6 "stop gathering") |
    | 3 | lease `UNKNOWN` | `IDLE_AT_MINE`, status unchanged |
    | 4 | lease `NONE` | `IDLE_AT_MINE`, `NEEDS_TOOL` |
    | 5 | no VALID linked shaft | `IDLE_AT_MINE`, `NO_WORK` ("No valid Mineshaft") |
    | 6 | shaft depth unknown, or day time ≥ `MINER_LAST_DESCENT`, or the shaft was unreachable less than `MINER_RETRY_TICKS` = 600 ago | `IDLE_AT_MINE` (status `SITE_UNREACHABLE` stays while the retry waits) |
    | 7 | otherwise | `GO_DOWN(shaft)`: if the miner is more than 40 blocks from the marker (it can come back from a Storage up to ~80 blocks from the Heart, beyond `WalkTo`'s 48-block path range) it first walks to the stonecutter, then `WalkTo(marker, reach 2.5, closest 5.0)`; ARRIVED → hide (decision 1), `descents +1`; UNREACHABLE → `SITE_UNREACHABLE` ("Can't reach the Mineshaft"), retry after 600 |

### 3.4 One underground step (`MinerSystem`, every 20 ticks per away miner; pure order in `AwayCheck`)

1. Not a registered MINER of the current village, or the trip's job is not `miner` → surface `NOT_A_MINER` (repair).
2. Day phase is not WORK → surface `DAY_END`, progress kept.
3. The trip's shaft record is gone or not VALID, or its link is no longer the miner's Mine, or the miner holds no slot → surface `SITE_INVALID`,
   **progress reset to 0** (the shaft changed), status `NO_WORK` (`WORKPLACE_INVALID` takes over once the Mine is re-checked, M7 merge
   order).
4. Lease `NONE` → surface `NO_TOOL`, progress kept, `NEEDS_TOOL`. Lease `UNKNOWN` → no progress, stay, `unknownSteps + 1`; at
   `MINER_UNKNOWN_LIMIT` (60) → surface `STOPPED_WORKING`, progress kept. A known lease resets `unknownSteps`.
5. Multiplier 0 (M11: on strike, starving) → surface `STOPPED_WORKING`, progress kept.
6. `progress.add(20, multiplier)`; when a haul is due: depth unknown → hold at one haul and stay; else roll
   `HaulTable.roll(DepthBand.of(deepestY), lease.tier, HaulRng.forHaul(seed, uuid, rolls))`, `rolls + 1`, `CampTools.use(…, 10)`,
   surface `HAUL` with the items, counters. (Depth unknown for 60 steps in a row with a haul due → also `STOPPED_WORKING`, the haul
   progress kept.)
7. Before the checks: if the villager drifted more than 0.5 blocks from the hold spot, it is teleported back.
Every surface clears the trip and writes the attachment once; steps without a surface write the attachment only when progress changed
(once per 20 ticks, a small record).

## 6. Risks, unknowns, out of scope

**Risks**
1. **(top) Hiding a live villager with NoAI.** Anything that later toggles `NoAI`/`Invisible` on workers, or mod code that assumes a
   loaded worker's brain ticks (M9's `WalkTo` clocks, M7's reconcile, M11 dinner, M18), can disturb an away miner. Mitigations: the
   prior-flag restore, the ENTITY_LOAD and step repairs (3.4 #1), `MinerWork` ending itself at hide time, `WalkTo`'s "not ticked for a
   while" reset (M9), T-L2-6/7/9. A miner left hidden by a bug is fixed by the repair the next time it loads; an uninstalled mod leaves a
   hidden villager (documented: `/data merge entity … {NoAI:0b,Invisible:0b,Invulnerable:0b}`).
2. **The GameTest world is at y ≈ -59 (A5).** A shaft in the normal yard is in the DEEP band, so the roadmap's "≥ 40 cobblestone" needs
   the high scene. Its blocks lie outside the structure box: the test must clear them, and its scene must stay within 80 blocks of its
   own Heart (built in the same high box). If the high scene proves flaky (light, spawning, chunk sections), the fallback is a high scene
   with fewer blocks (stone only under the shaft), **never** asserting cobbled deepslate instead of cobblestone for the roadmap test.
3. **Walking to a hole.** Villagers do not path off drops (max fall 3), so `WalkTo` ends near the rim (`closest` 5.0). A shaft whose
   rim cannot be reached gives `SITE_UNREACHABLE`; a villager pushed into the hole before hiding is accepted (it hides from where it
   lands if within reach, else it reports unreachable).
4. **Hitbox at the shaft bottom**: the invisible villager in the deepest cell catches the crosshair there (placing or breaking rails at
   the bottom while a miner is down; the player can wait or fire it). Accepted for v1 because the hold spot is the bottom, not the
   entrance or the marker; a
   `Villager.isPickable` mixin is the fix if players complain (BACKLOG note).
5. **Table balance**: wooden pickaxe mines iron and lapis (vanilla does not); redstone/lapis counts (4) are planner choices. Tunable.
6. **Lightning** at an open vertical shaft can hit the hidden miner (vanilla converts villagers to witches regardless of
   invulnerability, `Villager.thunderHit` **[verified]**). The MOB_CONVERSION fix restores the witch's flags; the miner is lost as in
   vanilla. M7's MOB_CONVERSION handler fires the record.
7. **Timing bounds** (T-L2-1 needs 3 hauls in one work day: ≈ 3 × 2600 ticks of 10 000). With slow walks it still has ~2 000 ticks of
   slack; the builder logs the measured haul times.

**BACKLOG scanned.** Nothing open is in M16's code path except: "Heart snapshot worst case 481 KiB of 512 KiB" (M16 adds no list or
field to the snapshot, only enum values: noted, nothing to tick) and M7-r1/r2 "deterministic regression tests for the deposit-trip
guards" (M16 relies on `DepositTrip`; T-L2-3 covers its use by the miner, the guard tests stay open). New BACKLOG notes from this plan:
the pickable hitbox (risk 4), the wooden-pickaxe rule (risk 5).

**Out of scope**: the cosmetic minecart (deferred by the roadmap), real tunnelling, levels/XP (M15, read through `WorkSpeed`), needs
effects (M11, read through `Needs.speed`), the L3 scenario (M11; M16 only adds counters), a held-pickaxe visual, cave-in or danger
events, shaft capacity limits, ore by biome.

### Interface and structure differences
- `AwayState`, `AwayTrip`, `PriorFlags` are pure records in `core.mine` (not glue); their codec is the new `world/AwayCodecs` (not `WorkerCodecs`), so the shared codec file is untouched. `ShaftDepth` is nested in `ShaftScanner` (`ShaftScanner.ShaftDepth`), `ShaftSurvey` gained `deepestCell` and `shaftDepth()`.
- The three haul tables live in `HaulTable` (only the band boundaries, wear, pace and retry numbers are in `Balance`), to keep the shared `Balance` diff small.
- `MinerSystem` finds away miners through the village's worker records (`Workers.findLoaded`); villagers with a trip but no record are handled by `AwayTrips` itself (load repair, `restoreVanilla`, retry set).
- `Workers.restoreVanilla` calls `AwayTrips.release` (comes up with reason NOT_A_MINER, drops what was still pending, forgets progress, keeps the haul counter); `Workers.fire/fireById` also call `MinerJob.forget`.
- Added beyond the plan: an L2 test `minerReportsUnreachableShaft` (a gap in the staircase: `SITE_UNREACHABLE`, "Can't reach the Mineshaft", retry after `MINER_RETRY_TICKS`), and a mutation-checked fix for what it uncovered (below).

### Things the plan got wrong (found by the tests)
1. **`Invisible` is not saved with a villager** (vanilla saves Invulnerable, Silent, NoAI and CustomNameVisible, but `Invisible` only as a potion effect). A hidden miner that was reloaded came back visible at the bottom of the shaft. `AwayTrips` sets it again in the ENTITY_LOAD callback (a flag change only, no movement). `awayMinerSurvivesReload` was red before the fix.
2. **The Heart screen threw for a registered outdoor site** (`HeartFacts` called `BuildingRequirements.of(MINESHAFT)`, "has no room requirements", inside the server tick: a crash as soon as a Mineshaft existed and someone opened the screen or a snapshot was built; M8 had no site in its tests). Fixed minimally: `DetailKind.NONE` (appended, empty text `detail.none`) for non-room buildings, the Buildings tab shows nothing at the right of such a row. Covered by `minerStopsWhenShaftInvalid` (a shaft row in the snapshot) and by `HeartCodecsTest`/`LangCoverageTest`, which iterate the enum. The same crash would have hit M17's Hunting Ground and M18's Training Yard.

- T-L2-3, T-L2-0 and T-L2-7 use the low scene; **every other miner test uses the high scene** (no staircase to climb), except `minerReportsUnreachableShaft` (low, it needs the tower). The plan's "low scene" for 4/5/6/8 would have worked too; the high scene is used wherever the band does not matter.
- Building counts in the bounds are 3 (Mine, Storage, shaft) and 4 in the two-shaft test, not 2.
