## 1. Goal

A Steward hires a villager as a **Merchant** at a Market Stall. During the working day the merchant stands at its stall and, about every
30 seconds, sells one lot of the village's **surplus** (for example 32 cobblestone) for 1 emerald, which goes straight into the treasury
and can end a wage strike. Surplus is what the stockpile holds above a reserve: 3 days of food, and 64 of every other item; the player sets
both reserves on the Heart screen's Stockpile tab, which also shows how much of each item is for sale and what the market sold today.
A merchant sells at most 8 / 12 / 16 emeralds a day (level 1 / 2 / 3) and earns 2 XP per sale.

### 3.2 Decisions

1. **A stall job: goods are drawn from the stockpile at the moment of sale.** The merchant walks to its Market Stall (M7's `GoToJobBlock`)
   and stands there (`StandAtJobBlock`). A new `MerchantWork` behaviour (priority 3) runs while the merchant is within `JOB_BLOCK_REACH`
   of its stall in the WORK phase. It ticks a `WorkClock(Balance.MERCHANT_SALE_UNITS = 600)` at `WorkSpeed.untooled(level, needs)`
   (600 ticks per sale at level 1, 480 at 2, 400 at 3) **only while a sale is possible** (the last plan was `Sell`); when a unit completes it
   re-plans on a fresh stockpile view and executes the sale in the same tick. No item is carried: a sale is an exchange with off-screen
   traders, like the miner's table-made hauls (DESIGN §5 hybrid style). Rejected: fetch trips to Storage and back (four walks for the
   roadmap case, more AI risk, and items in a villager's inventory are outside the stockpile's reserve while carried). Visible signs:
   `HAPPY_VILLAGER` particles above the merchant and `SoundEvents.VILLAGER_YES` at the stall on each sale **[verified]**
   (`ServerLevel.sendParticles(T, double, double, double, int, double, double, double, double)`, `Level.playSound(Entity, BlockPos,
   SoundEvent, SoundSource, float, float)` **[verified]**), the Stockpile tab and the treasury.
2. **The surplus rule** (`core.market.Surplus`, pure). Inputs: one `StockItem` per StackKey (`id`, `plain` = empty component patch, `count`,
   `reservedByOthers`, `nutritionEach` = 0 unless village food (A5)), `Reserves(foodDays, itemReserve)`, and the daily food demand
   `demand = adults × 10 + babies × 5` (A6).
   - Only **plain** stacks are ever sold (a renamed, enchanted or damaged item is the player's), and only items in the price table (#3).
   - **Non-food item**: `surplus = max(0, count − reservedByOthers − itemReserve)`. Reservations (M20 builds, M13 cooks) are items that
     will leave, the reserve is what must stay, so both are subtracted (M6 3.2.4).
   - **Food** (any item with village nutrition, priced or not, counts to the pool). The pool and the demand are **M11's own numbers**, so the
     merchant and the Overview's "food days left" can never disagree: `pool = FoodMath.nutrition(Dinner.stock(server, true))` (net of
     reservations, `neverEat` left out; M11's `raw` flag does not weight nutrition), `demand = adults × Balance.ADULT_NUTRITION + babies ×
     Balance.CHILD_NUTRITION`; equivalently a food lot may be sold only if `FoodMath.daysLeft(pool − lotNutrition, adults, babies) ≥ foodDays`
     (empty = nobody to feed = allowed). In core: `foodReserve = foodDays × demand`, `spare = max(0, pool − foodReserve)`; a priced food item's surplus is
     `min(count − reservedByOthers, floor(spare / nutritionEach))`. Food is **not** also held to the 64 item reserve (DESIGN: "3 days of food,
     and 64 of everything else"). The per-food surpluses share one `spare`, so they are not additive: the planner recomputes after each lot.
     Crops (carrot, potato, beetroot) are food, so with a low food reserve they can be sold out: the farmer replants from its own inventory
     (M10 keeps `SEED_RESERVE` = 16 of each, verified in `Balance`), never from Storage, so this costs no harvest. Wheat has no `FOOD`
     component, so M11's `FoodCatalog` does not count it (read on `origin/m11`): it is held to the item reserve.
   - Nobody to feed (`demand == 0`) → `foodReserve = 0`. Long arithmetic, saturating; negative counts are rejected.
   - A sale never takes the stock of any key below its reserve, and never takes the food pool below `foodReserve` (L1 property test).
3. **The price table** (`core.market.PriceTable`, tunable, pinned by L1, checked against the game by L2). One lot = 1 emerald. DESIGN §5.9
   examples kept exactly; the rest follow vanilla villager buy prices. Each item id is its own lot (16 oak logs, not a mix of woods):

   | Items | Lot | Food |
   |---|---|---|
   | oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak `_log` (the lumberjack's natural logs) | 16 | no |
   | `wheat` | 20 | no |
   | `carrot` 22, `potato` 26, `beetroot` 15 | as listed | yes |
   | `cobblestone`, `cobbled_deepslate` | 32 | no |
   | `coal` | 15 | no |
   | `raw_iron` 8, `raw_copper` 20 | as listed | no |
   | `iron_ingot` | 4 | no |
   | `leather` | 6 | no |
   | `cooked_beef`, `cooked_porkchop`, `cooked_mutton`, `cooked_chicken`, `cooked_rabbit`, `cooked_cod`, `cooked_salmon` | 10 | yes |

   Not sold in v1: bread (the staple food), raw meat and fish (the cook's input), gold, diamonds, lapis, redstone, saplings, seeds, tools,
   anything with components. `PriceTable.lot(id) : OptionalInt`; entries are in table order, ids unique, lots in `1..64`.
4. **Which lot is sold next** (`core.market.MarketPlanner.decide`, pure): first the stop reasons, in this order:
   `DAILY_LIMIT` (the cap of #5 is reached), `TREASURY_FULL` (`TreasuryMath.depositable(treasury, 1) == 0`), `NOTHING_TO_SELL` (no priced
   plain item has `surplus ≥ lot`). Otherwise `Sell(key, lot)` for the item with the **most whole lots of surplus**; ties in table order, then
   id. (Selling the most overstocked item first spreads sales and keeps every stock near its reserve.)
5. **Daily cap and the sales ledger.** Cap by level `Balance.MERCHANT_DAILY_CAP = {8, 12, 16}` emeralds (level clamped to 1..3).
   `core.market.SalesLedger(long day, Map<UUID, Integer> byMerchant, Map<HeartLocation, Integer> byStall)` (saved). A merchant may sell iff
   `max(soldBy(merchant), soldAt(stall)) < cap(level)`. Counting both closes two exploits: firing and re-hiring (or swapping in another
   villager) at the same stall, and moving a capped merchant to another stall. A new day (`dayIndex(heartLevel.getDayTime()) != ledger.day`,
   `!=` as in M12, so `/time set` backwards also starts a new day; `Level.getDayTime()` **[verified]**; with `advance_time` off the day never
   changes and the cap never resets, documented) empties both maps on the next sale or status check. The cap is about emeralds, and one sale
   is one emerald. The cap uses the merchant's **current** level (the loaded attachment's `WorkerData.level`, the one M15 writes; never the
   registry mirror, which may lag 20 ticks), read at every plan: a level-up in the middle of a day raises that day's cap at once.
   **Village-wide cap:** besides the per-merchant cap, the village sells at most `Balance.MARKET_VILLAGE_DAILY_CAP = 32` emeralds a day
   (two level-3 merchants; tunable in M26), checked as `ledger.soldToday() < 32`, so a cobblestone generator plus many stalls is not an
   unbounded emerald farm once the Owner can withdraw (M12). Stop reason `DAILY_LIMIT` covers both.
6. **XP and counters.** Each sale: `WorkXp.award(v, WorkUnit.TRADE_MADE, 1)` (2 XP, M15), `WorkStats.add("merchant", "sales", 1)` and
   `WorkStats.add("merchant", "emeralds", 1)`; the L3 JSON gets `work.merchant.sales` and `work.merchant.emeralds` for free (no per-item
   counters: metric names stay a small fixed set).
7. **Gates.** Strike (M12) and "hungry 2+ days" (M11) give `needs.speed() == 0`: no clock progress, and M12's `GatedWork` keeps
   `MerchantWork` from starting (A2). Workplace invalid → M7 already stops the job. Not at the stall → no clock progress; after
   `Balance.MERCHANT_STALL_PATIENCE = 600` WORK ticks in a row away from the stall the status is `SITE_UNREACHABLE` with the job text
   `activity.site_unreachable.merchant` "Can't reach its stall" (M16's value, reused), cleared when it arrives. Outside the WORK phase
   ([`WORK_START` 1000, `WORK_END` 11000), M7 `DaySchedule`, verified on dev) the WORK activity is not running: no sale. The job status is
   cleared when `MerchantWork` stops, so outside work hours the Villagers tab shows the vanilla activity (going home, sleeping).
8. **Write order (no loss, no duplication)**, all in one server tick on the server thread. (`VillageTicker.section()` only **measures**
   time for the 1 ms budget; it never skips or splits work, verified on dev, so a sale is never cut in half.)
   1. plan on a **fully fresh** view (#4: new snapshot, reservations, food and a fresh population count, no cache); `Stop` → set the status,
   store this plan as the merchant's cached plan, keep the pending unit (3.4) frozen until the next 20-tick refresh says `Sell`; so a stopped
   merchant builds at most one fresh view per 20 ticks (counter `Markets.freshViews`, asserted in T-L2-1);
   2. `taken = Stockpiles.withdraw(level, stallPos, key, lot, merchantUuid)`;
   3. if `taken < lot` (a Storage just became unknown or unloaded between snapshot and withdraw, or the test seam), the taken items go back
   with `Stockpiles.deposit(level, stallPos, stack)`; whatever that cannot place goes into the **merchant's inventory**
   (`villager.getInventory().addItem`), from where M7's `DepositTrip` hands it in later, and what even that cannot hold is dropped as an
   `ItemEntity` at the stall (WARN log). The status becomes `STORAGE_UNREACHABLE` and the merchant backs off until the next 20-tick refresh
   (no withdraw churn every tick). The sale does not happen and the pending unit is kept;
   4. `Treasury.deposit(server, 1, null)`; if `accepted == 0` (impossible after step 1's check in the same tick, but cheap), the items go back
   the same way as in 3; 5. ledger + XP + counters + particles + sound, pending unit cleared. Conservation (stock + carried + item entities +
   lot × treasury) is asserted every tick in T-L2-1 and T-L2-14.
9. **Reserve settings.** `core.market.Reserves(int foodDays, int itemReserve)`, default `(Balance.FOOD_RESERVE_DAYS = 3,
   Balance.ITEM_RESERVE = 64)`, ranges `foodDays 0..30`, `itemReserve 0..1024` (`Balance.MAX_FOOD_RESERVE_DAYS`, `MAX_ITEM_RESERVE`).
   Changed on the Stockpile tab with `[−]`/`[+]` buttons (food ±1 day, items ±16; the client sends the **absolute** new pair so a double click
   cannot drift). New `Permission.SET_RESERVES(STEWARD)`: Owner and Steward change them; Residents see them (they see the tab, `VIEW_DETAILS`);
   Visitors see nothing (their snapshot has no stock). `HeartAction.SetReserves(int foodDays, int itemReserve)` with `malformed()` →
   new `Rejection.BAD_RESERVE` when out of range (appended; ordinal codecs are append-safe). Payload `SetReservesPayload(containerId,
   foodDays, itemReserve)`, C2S through M8's validated gate (rate limit, open menu, epoch, village, rank re-read). Reserves apply to the next
   plan (≤ 20 ticks for the status, at once for a sale). Admin/sim: `/villageheart market reserves <foodDays> <items>` and
   `/villageheart market status` (op level 2, `WorkerCommands` style).
10. **Statuses.** `NOTHING_TO_SELL` → existing `WorkerStatus.NO_WORK` with the job text `activity.no_work.merchant` = "Nothing to sell
    (all stock is within the reserve)". New, appended: `WorkerStatus.DAILY_LIMIT` / `ActivityKind.DAILY_LIMIT` "Sold today's limit" and
    `WorkerStatus.TREASURY_FULL` / `ActivityKind.TREASURY_FULL` "Treasury full". Job statuses, so they rank where `NO_WORK` ranks in
    `Workers.status` (below `WORKPLACE_INVALID`, `ON_STRIKE`, `TOO_HUNGRY`). `MerchantWork` sets the status every 20 ticks while at the stall
    and clears it on a sale. `HeartFacts.collect`'s status → activity switch gains the two cases.
11. **Shared view, bounded cost.** `market.Markets.view(server)` builds `MarketInputs` from one `Stockpiles.snapshot`, the reservations,
    `Dinner.stock`'s food values, the population (A6) and the saved reserves. The status refresh uses a view cached for 20 ticks per server
    (several merchants share it), with a population count cached for 100 ticks (an entity scan); a sale always builds a fully fresh view,
    population included (#8). All of it runs inside `VillageTicker.section()` so the tick budget sees it.
12. **Saved data lives with the claim.** `WorldClaim.market : MarketState(Reserves reserves, SalesLedger ledger)`, reset by `releaseAt`
    and `clear` (a new village starts with default reserves and no sales). Ownership transfer (M24) does not touch it.
13. **Unemployed villagers keep their vanilla trades** (DESIGN §5.9): nothing to build; T-L2-10 pins it (a librarian in the claim keeps
    its offers while the merchant sells; the merchant itself has 0 offers).

  `checkExtraStartConditions` = the worker is a merchant; `canStillUse` = still a merchant and `brain.isActive(Activity.WORK)`; it sets no

  part-unit on restart costs < 1 sale.

## 6. Risks and unknowns

1. **Two unmerged dependencies (top risk).** M12 (treasury deposit path, strike gate, `DailyClock`) is a plan only, and M11 (food catalog,
   census, test hooks) is in review. M22 is written against their planned/branch names (3.1). Mitigation: steps 1-2 need neither; each
   assumption has a fallback; the builder re-checks A1-A6 on `dev` before step 3 and records differences in section 8.
2. **Census cost and correctness.** The food reserve depends on the population; `VillageCensus` scans entities. Cached 100 ticks; a change
   in population moves the reserve within 5 s (T-L2-2 waits 1 200 ticks). If the extraction from `HeartFacts` (A6) is awkward, the market
   uses `HeartFacts.cached(server)`'s `adults/babies` (a deviation; heavier, but cached per tick).
3. **Merchant reaching the stall.** M7's `GoToJobBlock` is proven, but a stall in a cramped Market may be reached only diagonally. Within
   `JOB_BLOCK_REACH` (1.5) is required to sell; T-L2-1 logs the first sale tick so slack is measured. If flaky, the reach for selling becomes
   2.5 (`Balance.MERCHANT_REACH`), recorded in section 8, and T-L2-13's pen is then moved so the merchant is held beyond the new reach.
4. **Stockpile tab space.** The reserve bar takes 24 px from the item list; with the Market line in place of the (usually empty) alert line
   the header does not grow. The L4 layout assertions catch overflow; fallback: the bar's labels shorten to "Food 3 d" / "Items 64".
5. **Concurrent stock writers.** Players, builders and cooks change the stock between ticks; each sale plans and withdraws in one tick, so the
   reserve holds at every tick boundary (T-L2-1 samples every tick). A player taking items out can of course take stock below the reserve
   themselves; the merchant then simply stops.

**Out of scope** (later or post-v1): buying (the market never spends emeralds); dynamic prices, supply and demand, wandering-trader events;
per-item reserves or a "never sell" list (the two global reserves are DESIGN's v1 setting; a high item reserve stops selling); selling items
with components; the Market chest (required by the building, unused in v1); a Market Stall right-click report; a sales history UI; showing
