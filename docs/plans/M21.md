# M21 plan: Defence blueprints (Wall Segment, Gate, Lamp Post, Watchtower)

Planner: Opus, 2026-11-08. Builder: implement in the step order of section 4; every step ends green on `scripts/verify.sh --fast`.
APIs marked **[verified]** were read in the decompiled 1.21.11 Mojang-mapped sources (Loom `minecraft-common-…-sources.jar`) or in the 1.21.11
data files of the common jar. Nothing was built or run for this plan (static checks only, no gradle).

M21 builds on M20 (builder and blueprints), merged on `dev` (read at 545d2e8). M20 was made with M21 in mind: `Blueprint.kind` (BUILDING/DEFENCE),
`Blueprint.mode` (`PlacementMode.FOOTPRINT`, `LINE` reserved), `BlueprintParams.length` (saved as `length` in the build codec) and
`Blueprints.resolve(id, params)`. **Every M20 safety rule applies unchanged to every M21 blueprint**, because M21 only adds blueprints and a
second click flow: the same `SiteRules` (incl. rule 11b `SiteSupport` attachments), the same `BuildExecutor.cellState` (only a `LiquidBlock`
counts as a fluid to repair, so waterlogged player blocks stay), the same one-action replace of a natural block in a PLACE/CHECK cell, the
same "never break a block that is not natural", the same stand ring outside the box. Section 3.1 lists the assumptions.

## 1. Goal

At the Builder's Table a Steward takes a Wall Segment, Gate, Lamp Post or (at Town tier) Watchtower blueprint. A wall or gate is laid out with
two clicks, start and end, straight along x or z and at most 32 blocks long; the outline shows green or red and a third click confirms. The
builder then builds it like any other blueprint: a 3-high cobblestone wall, a wall with a 3-wide fence-gate opening that opens, a fence post
with a lantern that keeps monsters from spawning within 12 blocks on level ground, and a 3×3, 7-high watchtower with a ladder and a railed
platform. Finished lamp posts and watchtowers become patrol points for guards (M18).

### 3.2 Decisions (pinned; DESIGN §5.8 gets "Rules as built (M21)" in step 11)

1. **LINE placement mode (two clicks).** Applies to `wall_segment` and `gate` (`mode LINE`). With a LINE Blueprint item:
   - **Click 1** (no pending site): the clicked ground (`BlueprintUse.groundOf`, same rule as M20) becomes the **start**. The stack gets
     `BlueprintSite(start, NONE, ok=true, length=0, reason "Click the end of the wall: straight along x or z, up to 32 blocks")`, unless the
     rank lacks `START_BLUEPRINT` (then `ok=false`, rank text). The preview draws a 3-high particle column on the start.
   - **Click 2** (pending, length 0): the clicked ground is the **end**. `WallLines.of(start, end, minLength, MAX_WALL_LENGTH)` (core):
     `dx = end.x − start.x`, `dz = end.z − start.z`; both non-zero → `NOT_STRAIGHT` "A wall must run straight along x or z (%s off the line)"
     (`min(|dx|,|dz|)`); `length = |dx| + |dz| + 1`; `length > 32` → `TOO_LONG` "Too long: %s blocks (max 32)"; `length < minLength` →
     `TOO_SHORT` "Too short: %s blocks (min %s)" (gate 5, wall 1). A line problem keeps the start (`length 0`, `ok=false`, the text), so the
     next click is another end. A good line resolves the blueprint for that length and runs the full M20 site check (`SiteValidation`) →
     `BlueprintSite(start, rotation, ok, length, reason)`.
   - **Click 3**: a click whose ground is a column of the shown box with y in `[start.y − 1, box.maxY]` (M20's `inShownBox`) **confirms**
     (full re-check, then `BuildJobs.start(…, new BlueprintParams(length), …)`, the stack shrinks). Any other click is a **new end** (the start
     stays). **Sneak-click** (on a block or in the air) clears the pending site ("Wall cleared: click the start"); there is nothing to rotate.
   - **Axis and direction.** The generated blueprint runs along local +x from local (0, 0) (anchor (0, 0) = the start column), front NORTH
     (local −z). `rotation = Rotation4.turning(EAST, dir)` where `dir` is the world direction start→end (EAST NONE, SOUTH CLOCKWISE_90, WEST
     CLOCKWISE_180, NORTH COUNTERCLOCKWISE_90). Length 1 (start = end column): NONE.
   - **No terrain following (v1).** The whole line is level at the **start's** ground y (the end click only gives its column; the ok text says
     "Wall: 20 blocks, level at y 65"). Higher ground in the line is natural → cleared (M20 rules; a player block there refuses). Lower ground
     is refused by the new **FOOTING** rule (3.2 #5). A sloped wall is several segments. Reason: steps are recomputed from (blueprint, params,
     ground, rotation) only, so a terrain-shaped wall would need the terrain in the saved params; not worth it for v1 (section 6).
   - `Balance.MAX_WALL_LENGTH = 32`, `Balance.GATE_MIN_LENGTH = 5`.
2. **Generated blueprints.** `core.blueprint.DefenceBlueprints` builds the text of a wall or gate of length n and parses it with the normal
   `BlueprintParser` (so every M20 validation applies), all lengths at class init (wall 1..32, gate 5..32: 60 small parses), kept in arrays.
   - Wall `wall_segment` (n): `tier VILLAGE`, `kind DEFENCE`, `mode LINE`, `builds none`, `base 1`, `anchor 0 0`, `front NORTH`, `size n 3 1`;
     palette `C minecraft:cobblestone <- minecraft:cobblestone "cobblestone" "cobblestone"`; layers 0-2 all `C`. Materials: 3n cobblestone
     (20 → 60). Full blocks: 3 high cannot be jumped; one item each.
   - Gate `gate` (n ≥ 5): same header; the opening is the 3 columns `o..o+2`, `o = (n − 3) / 2` (integer division: n 5 → 1..3, 6 → 1..3,
     7 → 2..4); layer 0: `C` with `G` in the opening; layer 1: `C` with `_` (air) in the opening; layer 2: all `C` (a lintel). So the
     passage is 2 high (gate + air), a player fits, and a closed gate's 1.5-block collision cannot be jumped. `G =
     minecraft:oak_fence_gate[facing=north,open=false] <- #minecraft:fence_gates "fence gate"` (facing NORTH = the gate spans local x, along the
     wall; rotations turn it with `BlockState.rotate`, M20 A8). Materials: cobblestone 3n − 6, fence gate 3 (n 7 → 15 + 3).
   - `Blueprints.all()` (menu order): Small House, Storage Shed, Farm Plot, **Wall Segment, Gate, Lamp Post, Watchtower**. For a LINE id,
     `all()` and `byId(id)` hold the **shortest** instance (wall 1, gate 5): used for the name, tier, kind, mode, the table and the tooltip,
     never for a site. `resolve(id, params)`: FOOTPRINT → `byId` (params ignored, as M20); LINE → the generated instance when
     `min ≤ length ≤ 32`, else empty (so a saved build with length 33 or 0 is dropped by the codec, logged, the village still loads).
3. **Lamp Post** (`lamp_post`, FOOTPRINT, `tier VILLAGE`, `kind DEFENCE`, `builds none`, `base 1`, `anchor 0 0`, `front NORTH`, `size 1 3 1`):
   layers 0-1 `f = minecraft:oak_fence <- #minecraft:wooden_fences "fence"`; layer 2 `L = minecraft:lantern[hanging=false] <- minecraft:lantern
   "lantern" pass=attached cell=open`. Materials: fence 2, lantern 1. The lantern stands on the fence: `LanternBlock.canSurvive` =
   `Block.canSupportCenter(below, UP)` **[verified]**, which a fence post satisfies (vanilla); T-L2-2 asserts the lantern is still there 20 ticks
   after placement in every rotation. Pass `attached` so it is placed after both fences.
   - **Spawn-stopping (vanilla, nothing added).** `Monster.isDarkEnoughToSpawn` returns false when `getBrightness(LightLayer.BLOCK, pos) >
     dimensionType().monsterSpawnBlockLightLimit()` **[verified Monster.java:86-101]**; the overworld's limit is **0** **[verified
     `data/minecraft/dimension_type/overworld.json` `monster_spawn_block_light_limit: 0`]**. Every natural spawn of a type registered with
     `Monster::checkMonsterSpawnRules` or `checkSurfaceMonstersSpawnRules` (zombie, skeleton, creeper, spider, enderman, witch, zombie villager,
     bogged, husk, parched, camel husk, cave spider, creaking … **[verified SpawnPlacements]**) goes through it (`NaturalSpawner.isValidSpawnPostitionForType`
     → `SpawnPlacements.checkSpawnRules(type, level, NATURAL, pos, random)` **[verified]**). So a mob cannot spawn with its feet in a cell of
     block light ≥ 1.
   - **Radius.** The lantern emits 15 **[verified `Blocks.LANTERN` `lightLevel(15)`]**; block light drops by 1 per step through cells whose
     `getLightBlock` is 0 (air, and every non-full shape such as a fence: `propagatesSkylightDown` for a non-full shape **[verified
     BlockBehaviour.getLightBlock / propagatesSkylightDown]**). In open air the block light at P is `max(0, 15 − |dx| − |dy| − |dz|)` from the
     lantern cell. The lantern is 2 above the feet cell of a mob standing on the post's ground (feet at ground+1, lantern at ground+3), so
     on level ground light ≥ 1 exactly where `|dx| + |dz| ≤ 12` from the post's column: **radius 12 (a diamond)**. Core
     `LampLight.radius(emission, lanternAboveFeet) = emission − 1 − lanternAboveFeet` (= 12) and `LampLight.blockLight(dx, dy, dz)`; the
     tooltip and DESIGN say "Keeps most monsters from spawning within 12 blocks (on level ground; walls and houses shade it)".
   - **Not stopped (documented):** pillager patrols (`checkPatrollingMonsterSpawnRules` allows block light ≤ 8 **[verified]**), slimes
     (swamp spawns use raw brightness ≤ `nextInt(8)`; slime chunks ignore light **[verified Slime.checkSlimeSpawnRules]**), phantoms
     (`PhantomSpawner`, not light-based), drowned in water, spawners, raids, and mobs walking in from outside the radius.
4. **Watchtower** (`watchtower`, FOOTPRINT, **`tier TOWN`**, `kind DEFENCE`, `builds none`, `base 1`, `anchor 2 2`, `front NORTH`, `size 5 7 5`).
   A 3×3 cobblestone shaft (local x, z 1..3) 5 high with the ladder inside, a 5×5 plank platform on top and a fence rail round it; 7 layers.
   Symbols: `C` cobblestone, `P minecraft:oak_planks <- #minecraft:planks "planks" "planks"`, `H minecraft:ladder[facing=north] <- minecraft:ladder
   "ladder" pass=attached cell=open` (facing NORTH = it hangs on the block to its south, `LadderBlock.canSurvive` = the face of
   `pos.relative(facing.getOpposite())` is sturdy **[verified LadderBlock]**), `f` fence (`#minecraft:wooden_fences`), `_` air, `.` any.
   Rows are z = 0 (front) to z = 4:
   ```
   layers 0-1        layers 2-4        layer 5 (platform)   layer 6 (rail)
   .....             .....             PPPPP                fffff
   .C_C.   entrance  .CCC.             PPPPP                f___f
   .CHC.   ladder    .CHC.             PPHPP  ladder hole   f___f
   .CCC.             .CCC.             PPPPP                f___f
   .....             .....             PPPPP                fffff
   ```
   The ladder (centre column (2, y, 2), layers 0-5) hangs on the back cell (2, y, 3): cobblestone in layers 0-4, a plank in layer 5. The
   entrance is the front cell (2, 0..1, 1), two high. You climb to layer 5 and step onto the platform (feet in layer 6, inside the rail).
   Materials (first-use order): cobblestone 38 (7 + 7 + 8 + 8 + 8), planks 24, fence 16, ladder 6. Height 7 (ground + 1 to ground + 7).
   The box is 5 × 7 × 5; layers 0-4 outside the shaft are `.` (untouched). Rule 12 (stand point within `BUILD_REACH` 7.0) holds: the
   farthest cell from the ring is the ladder at layer 5 (3 horizontal, 5.5 up: 6.26) — T-L1-4 pins it with `StandPoints`.
   The first click turns the entrance towards the player (M20 rule for `front`).
5. **FOOTING (new site rule 11c, DEFENCE only).** For a blueprint of `kind DEFENCE`, every layer-0 BLOCK cell must stand on a solid block:
   `SiteView.solid(Pos below)` (new default method `cell(p) != AIR && cell(p) != FLUID`; glue = `SiteValidation.solid` of the state: no fluid
   and a non-empty collision shape, so dirt paths and farmland count, short grass and snow layer 1 do not). Else `FOOTING` "No ground under
   %s: defences need level ground" naming the layer-0 cell. Checked after 11b and before 12. BUILDING blueprints are unchanged (a house may
   still bridge a dip, as in M20). Why: a wall over a 1-block dip leaves a gap mobs (baby zombies, spiders) crawl through; a lamp or tower on
   air looks broken.
6. **Rule 8 for defences.** Two pending builds that are **both** `kind DEFENCE` may touch (their boxes adjacent, gap 0) but never overlap;
   any pair with a BUILDING keeps M20's "boxes grown by 1 do not intersect". So a player can queue wall, gate, wall end to end, and a corner
   (the second wall starts beside the first one's end). `SiteRules.Box` gains `boolean defence` (with a back-compat `Box(min, max)` =
   false); `SiteValidation` fills it from `BuildJobs.blueprint(j).kind()`. Safety: a stand point of build A inside pending build B's box is
   only stood on before B is built; once B's blocks are there the column is not standable (`standable` finds no free feet cell) and the
   builder picks another (M20 `BuilderJob` recomputes the ring per work session); a builder standing in B's cell makes B wait (IN_THE_WAY,
   M20). Finished walls are cobblestone = not natural, so a new line overlapping one is refused by rule 11 ("cobblestone in the way").
7. **New codes** are **appended** to `SiteRules.Code` (`FOOTING, NOT_STRAIGHT, TOO_LONG, TOO_SHORT`), so the existing ordinals and the
   `ordinal() >= OVERLAP` UNCHECKED override stay as they are (FOOTING is after OVERLAP, as it should be; line codes never reach
   `SiteValidation`). Lang keys `build.villageheart.site.footing|not_straight|too_long|too_short`.
8. **Defence sites (saved, for patrol points).** When a DEFENCE build finishes (`BuildJobs.finish`, the one place both the builder and
   `advance` end), a lamp post or watchtower is recorded in `VillageState.defences : List<DefenceSite>` (new last component, codec key
   `defences`, `optionalFieldOf` default empty, back-compat constructor): `DefenceSite(UUID id /*the build id*/, String blueprintId,
   HeartLocation ground, Rotation4 rotation, long builtAt)`. Walls and gates are not recorded (no patrol point, nothing reads them).
   Capped at `Balance.MAX_DEFENCE_SITES = 64` (beyond it a lamp still lights but is not a patrol point; logged once).
   - Core `DefenceSpecs.of(blueprintId)` → `Optional<Spec(int[] patrolLocal, List<KeyCell> keys)>`, `KeyCell(int x, int y, int z, String blockId)`:
     lamp: patrol = local (0, 0, −1) (the cell in front of the post at feet level), key = (0, 2, 0) `minecraft:lantern`; watchtower: patrol =
     (2, 0, 0) (in front of the entrance), keys = the bottom and top ladder (2, 0, 2), (2, 5, 2) `minecraft:ladder`. World cells through
     `Placement.world` (rotation-aware). `Defences.patrolPoints(List<DefenceSite>)` (core) → `List<Pos>` in record order.
   - Glue `DefenceSystem` (`VillageSystem`, every `Balance.DEFENCE_REFRESH_TICKS` = 200, inside the ticker section): for each site whose key
     chunks are loaded (`getChunkNow`; unloaded = kept), if any key cell no longer holds its block id, the site is removed (logged "Lamp Post at
     … is gone"). `Defences.patrolPoints(MinecraftServer) → List<BlockPos>` = the core list for the claimed village's dimension; M18 consumes it (A3).
9. **Commands** (op, rank Owner, like M20's `build start`): `/villageheart build line <blueprint> <from> <to>` runs `WallLines` + the site
   check and starts the build (line problems and site problems are sent as failures); `build start` refuses a LINE blueprint ("use build line").
10. **Table and tooltip.** No wire change: the client has core and reads `Blueprints.byId(id).mode()`. For a LINE blueprint the table detail shows
    "Two clicks: start and end, straight, up to 32 blocks" and the per-block cost from the unit instance ("3 cobblestone per block"; gate:
    "at 5 blocks: 9 cobblestone, 3 fence gates; +3 cobblestone per extra block"); the Take button works as for any blueprint. The Watchtower row
    is locked below Town ("Requires Town tier"). `BlueprintItem.appendHoverText` uses the same lines; the Lamp Post adds the radius line.
11. **Villagers and gates.** Vanilla villagers open doors, not fence gates (`InteractWithDoor` handles `DoorBlock` only; the walk evaluator treats a
    closed gate as a fence). A ring of wall with only gates cuts workers off from sites outside it. M21 does not change villager pathing; DESIGN
    and the table detail say "Villagers cannot open fence gates: leave one open, or leave a gap". BACKLOG item for a later milestone (section 6).

## 6. Risks, unknowns, out of scope

**Risks**
1. **Level-only walls on real terrain (top risk).** Without terrain following many lines in a hilly village are refused by FOOTING or cut
   into hillsides (clearing a lot of natural dirt/stone). Mitigations: the refusal names the column; the ok text gives the wall's height;
   segments can step (queue end to end, rule 8). If players find it unusable, a later milestone saves per-column heights in `BlueprintParams`
   (the codec already has a `length` field; a `heights` list would be added beside it).
2. **The lamp test proves the vanilla rule, not a live spawn.** Natural spawning needs a player within 128 blocks and > 24 away
   (`NaturalSpawner.spawnCategoryForPosition` → `getNearestPlayer`, `isRightDistanceToPlayerAndSpawnPoint` **[verified]**), which GameTest
   does not have, and it would be random. T-L2-5 therefore calls the exact predicate the spawner calls (`SpawnPlacements.checkSpawnRules` and
   `isSpawnPositionOk`) at every cell over a real night, with a seeded random and a control set that must see spawns. A later spawner change
   in vanilla that bypasses `checkSpawnRules` would not be caught (none in 1.21.11).
3. **Light engine timing in GameTest.** Light updates run off-thread after `setBlock`; the test waits for the lantern cell to read 15 and then
   checks the whole map, so a slow update shows as a bounded wait, not a false result.
4. **Villagers cannot open fence gates** (3.2 #11): a closed ring isolates workers and (M18) guards. Documented; BACKLOG item.
5. **Rule 8 relaxation** lets one build's stand ring lie in another pending defence box (3.2 #6). Covered by M20's IN_THE_WAY wait and the
   per-session ring; T-L2-9 runs it with one builder via `advance`; two builders on touching walls are not tested (logged as a gap).
6. **Merge conflicts** in `VillageState`/`VillageCodecs`/`SiteRules.Code`/`Blueprints.all()` with M14 and M18. Append only, keep
   back-compat constructors.
7. **Rule 9 (no fluid next to the site)** refuses walls along a river or a pond edge. Accepted (M20 rule; flooding protection matters more).

**Out of scope (M21)**: terrain-following walls, diagonal or curved walls, walls longer than 32 in one blueprint, cutting a gate into an
existing wall (a gate is its own line section; finished walls are not natural and are never broken), villager-opened gates, guards on the
tower platform (patrol points are at ground level; M18 walks there), mob AI changes, stopping patrols/slimes/phantoms, repairing finished
defences, demolishing, cobblestone-wall (`#walls`) blocks or other materials, a Heart screen list of defences.

**Backlog scanned** ("From M20" and others). Folded in: the M20-r2 item "`SiteSupport` touched-cell exemption has no L2 test" (T-L2-8 runs the
check pass with the tower's own ladders on its own cells). Kept open, not M21's: falling non-natural blocks above a cleared cell (walls cut
into hillsides may meet falling gravel, which is natural and handled by the one-action replace; anvils/concrete powder stay untested),
`checkPassLeavesWaterloggedPlayerBlocks` spare stack, "Needs materials" fallback text, `BuildSystem.refresh` orphaned reservations, the
known "player dirt looks natural" gap (walls clearing a hillside inherit it, documented), the M9/M10 `workerOpensDoors` flake items.
New BACKLOG items from this plan: villager-opened gates; M18 registration of `Defences::patrolPoints` (if M18 is not merged first); two
builders on touching defence builds untested.
