## 1. Goal

A lumberjack hired at a Lumber Camp takes the best axe from the camp chest and, during work hours, walks to the nearest natural
tree within 32 blocks of its Chopping Block, fells the whole tree log by log (about 1 log per 2 s with a stone axe), picks up the
saplings, sticks and apples that fall as the leaves decay, replants a sapling on the stump, and carries the logs to Storage.
Logs the player placed (walls, cabins, logs in valid rooms, "trees" with player-placed leaves) are left alone; the one documented
exception is a lone log post on soil that touches 3 or more natural leaves (§6 risk 3). Without an axe
the lumberjack does no work and the Villagers tab reason is "Needs axe". The axe in the chest wears out as it is used.

### 3.2 Decisions (pinned here; DESIGN §4/§5.1 get one "Rules as built (M9)" paragraph in step 12)

1. **Natural tree** (core, 3.4): a 26-connected component of natural logs (`#minecraft:overworld_natural_logs` **[verified]**:
   oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, pale oak, mangrove, cherry; not stripped logs, wood blocks or nether stems) that
   (a) has ≥ `TREE_MIN_LEAF_CONTACTS` = 3 face contacts with **non-persistent** leaves (`LeavesBlock.PERSISTENT` false **[verified]**;
   player-placed leaves are persistent), (b) stands on soil (`#minecraft:dirt` **[verified]**: dirt, grass, podzol, coarse dirt,
   mycelium, rooted dirt, moss, pale moss, mud, muddy mangrove roots) with all its soil-resting logs ("base") inside one 2×2 column
   footprint at the component's lowest y, (c) has no log inside a valid building's room (`RoomScan.occupies`, interior or shell, any
   room type), (d) is at most `MAX_TREE_LOGS` = 192 logs, 17 blocks wide (x and z) and 48 tall. Rule (b) is what protects log walls
   and cabins (many soil-resting logs) even when a tree touches them; rule (a) protects decorative trees.
   Mangroves (trunk on roots, not soil) are not trees in v1.
2. **Whole-tree fell, top-down**: logs are removed highest first, the base last, so an interrupted fell is resumable by flooding
   from the recorded base (3.6). The worker stands within `CHOP_REACH` = 3.0 of the base and removes logs at any height (no
   climbing). Each removal: `level.destroyBlock(pos, false, villager)` **[verified: plays the break effect (levelEvent 2001), sets
   the fluid's block with flag 3 so leaves update their distance, fires `GameEvent.BLOCK_DESTROY`]**, then the drops
   `Block.getDrops(state, level, pos, null, villager, axe)` **[verified 6-arg overload]** go straight into the villager inventory;
   what does not fit is dropped with `Block.popResource` and collected later. Drops are skipped when `GameRules.BLOCK_DROPS`
   **[verified `block_drops`]** is false (same as vanilla). Crack overlay: `level.destroyBlockProgress(villager.getId(), pos, stage)`
   **[verified]** with stage = progress × 10, `-1` when the log goes or the worker stops.
3. **Pace**: `WorkClock` adds `WorkSpeed.multiplier` every work tick; a log falls each `BASE_TICKS_PER_LOG` = 50 units. Multipliers
   (`Balance`, tunable): tool wood 1.0, **stone 1.25** (→ 40 ticks = 2 s, DESIGN §5.1), copper 1.375, iron 1.5, gold 1.0,
   diamond 1.75, netherite 2.0 (DESIGN lists wood/stone/iron/diamond; copper, gold and netherite are pinned here); level ×1.0/1.25/1.5
   (`Balance.LEVEL_SPEED`, M15 raises levels); needs from `Needs` (M7 default = 1.0; M11 sets ×0.5/×0.75/0). The fractional
   remainder carries over, so the long-run rate is exact.
4. **Tools stay in the chest.** The lumberjack uses an axe **in place** in a container of its own camp's latest scan (`#chest`
   positions, read with `StorageContainers.at`, never loading). It is never moved to the villager, so it cannot be lost, duplicated
   by a death drop (vanilla `DropChances.DEFAULT` is 0.085 per hand slot **[verified]**, and there is no public setter for 0) or
   carried off by a zombie conversion. Each felled log costs 1 durability: `stack.hurtAndBreak(1, level, null, item -> {})`
   **[verified: applies Unbreaking through `EnchantmentHelper.processDurabilityChange`; a broken stack shrinks to empty]**, then
   `container.setChanged()`. Axes = `#minecraft:axes` **[verified: wooden, stone, copper, golden, iron, diamond, netherite]**; an
   unknown modded axe counts as wood. `StorageContainers.at` returns each chest half's own `ChestBlockEntity` (27 slots), never the
   `CompoundContainer` **[verified]**, so an axe in a double chest is seen exactly once. With several lumberjacks in one camp, axes are allotted best-first in hiring order
   (`ToolAllocation`); a worker without an allotted axe has status `NEEDS_TOOL`. No held-item visual in M9 (out of scope, 6).
5. **Drop pickup is mod code**, not vanilla looting: vanilla `Mob.aiStep` picks up only `wantsToPickUp` items
   (`#villager_picks_up` or the profession's `requestedItems`) and only with `mob_griefing` **[verified]**. The lumberjack collects
   `ItemEntity`s of `#minecraft:logs`, `#minecraft:saplings`, sticks and apples within `COLLECT_RADIUS` = 12 of itself and inside its
   work area, not `hasPickUpDelay()`, walking to each and taking it within 1.5 blocks (`SimpleContainer.addItem`, `villager.take(item,
   n)` **[verified `LivingEntity.take`]**, discard when empty). Item entities despawn after 6000 ticks **[verified `ItemEntity.LIFETIME`]**,
   so no new tree is started after `LUMBER_LAST_FELL` = day time 5000. Each leaf decays on a random tick once its distance is 7
   (mean ≈ 4096/3 ≈ 1 365 ticks at `random_tick_speed` 3), so a 60-leaf canopy is gone after ≈ 1 365 × ln 60 ≈ 5 600 ticks; the last
   6 000 work ticks leave time to collect those drops before night (night lasts 14 000 ticks, longer than the item lifetime). Random
   ticks run in every entity-ticking chunk with no player needed (`ServerChunkCache.tickChunks` → `ChunkMap.forEachBlockTickingChunk`
   → `forEachEntityTickingChunk` **[verified]**), so this also holds in GameTests. A fell already in progress at 5000 is finished.
6. **Replanting**: on the base cells, after the last log falls. Sapling species = log species (oak logs under mostly azalea leaves →
   azalea). 1×1 trees need one sapling; dark oak and pale oak (they only grow from 2×2 **[verified `TreeGrower` mega/2×2 rule]**) and
   2×2 jungle/spruce get one per base cell. Source order: own inventory, then the camp chest (the worker walks to the chest, within 2
   blocks, and takes what the open cells need: `FETCH`); after `REPLANT_FALLBACK_TICKS` = 6000
   pending, any 1×1 sapling from those that `canSurvive` there. Plant only when the cell `canBeReplaced()` and the sapling block's
   `canSurvive` **[verified; saplings accept `#dirt` or farmland]**; a cell now built over is dropped. Pending spots expire after
   `REPLANT_EXPIRY_DAYS` = 3 (logged once).
7. **Work area**: tree base within 32 blocks horizontally (Euclidean xz, inclusive) of the Chopping Block and within ±16 in y; the
   nearest tree to the Chopping Block first (ties: angle order from the ring table, deterministic). Not limited to the claim.
8. **Pathfinding**: villagers have `FOLLOW_RANGE` 16 (the `Mob` default; `Villager.createAttributes` only sets speed
   **[verified]**), so a path is at most ~16 blocks; `MoveToTargetSink` walks the partial path, then `stop()` erases `WALK_TARGET`
   **[verified]**. M9's `WalkTo` helper re-sets `WALK_TARGET(base, 0.5F, closeEnough 2)` whenever it is absent, until within
   `CHOP_REACH` (3D, no line-of-sight check: a worker can chop through a one-block gap or a pane, which is accepted). Unreachable = best distance not improved by ≥ 1 block in 200 ticks, or 800 ticks total; the tree is then skipped
   for 6000 ticks (per camp). Attributes are not changed (FOLLOW_RANGE also sizes the villager's sensors).
9. **Chunk rules**: never load or force a chunk. All reads go through `getChunkNow` (a `LevelTreeView` like `LevelBlockView`); an
   UNLOADED cell skips that column in the search and makes a flood "unknown, retry later". A tree is a target only when
   `level.isPositionEntityTicking(base)` and `level.shouldTickBlocksAt(base)` **[verified on `ServerLevel`/`Level`]**, so the worker
   can walk there and leaves decay. Every break/plant re-checks `getChunkNow` first. The camp chest is read with
   `StorageContainers.at`; when it is not readable (unloaded, or no scan after a restart) the tool is *unknown*: no work and no
   `NEEDS_TOOL`. When the village's chunks unload, everything freezes (DESIGN §6).
10. Without an axe the lumberjack still replants and collects drops (no tool needed); it only does not fell.
11. The camp chest is not stockpile (M6: a chest inside another room building's scan is excluded), so `Stockpiles.count` never sees
    the tools or camp saplings, and deposits never go there.
12. `mob_griefing` does **not** stop the lumberjack (the player hired it; M10's farmer follows the same rule). `block_drops` does.

### 3.4 Core trees (`dev.villageheart.core.tree`, pure, L1)

```java
public enum Species { OAK, SPRUCE, BIRCH, JUNGLE, ACACIA, DARK_OAK, PALE_OAK, MANGROVE, CHERRY, AZALEA;
    public String saplingId();          // "minecraft:oak_sapling" …, AZALEA → "minecraft:azalea", MANGROVE → "minecraft:mangrove_propagule"
    public boolean needsTwoByTwo(); }   // DARK_OAK, PALE_OAK
public record TreeCell(Kind kind, @Nullable Species species) {   // shared instances, no allocation per read
    public enum Kind { AIR, LOG, NATURAL_LEAVES, PLACED_LEAVES, SOIL, OTHER, UNLOADED } }
public interface TreeView {
    TreeCell cell(int x, int y, int z);
    int top(int x, int z);              // y of the top non-air block (WORLD_SURFACE heightmap), Integer.MIN_VALUE when unloaded
    boolean inRoom(Pos p);              // a valid building's scan occupies p
}
public sealed interface TreeScan {
    record Tree(Species species, List<Pos> base, List<Pos> fellOrder, int leafContacts) implements TreeScan {}
    record NotATree(Why why) implements TreeScan {}           // (not core.building.Reason) NO_LEAVES, NO_GROUND, BASE_NOT_TREE_SHAPE, IN_ROOM, TOO_BIG, UNKNOWN
}
public final class TreeScanner {
    public static TreeScan scan(TreeView v, Pos anyLog);                        // natural-tree rules (3.2 #1)
    public static TreeScan resume(TreeView v, List<Pos> recordedBase);          // a fell in progress: flood from the base, only IN_ROOM/TOO_BIG apply
}
public final class TreeFinder {                                                 // nearest-first, resumable, budgeted
    public TreeFinder(Pos centre, int radius, int dy);                          // ring offsets sorted by (dist², angle) once, shared per radius
    public Result step(TreeView v, int readBudget, Predicate<Pos> skipBase);    // Found(Tree, basePos) | Paused | Exhausted
}
public final class FellOrder { static List<Pos> topDown(Set<Pos> logs, List<Pos> base); }  // y desc, then xz distance to base desc, then Pos; base last
```
Search per column: from `top(x, z)` go down through AIR and both leaf kinds (≤ 48 cells); at a LOG, `scan` from it (components are
de-duplicated by their min base `Pos`), then keep descending below that log (a small tree under a big tree's branch is still found);
an OTHER cell that is not a log (cocoa, bee nest, a roof) is skipped over in the same way while the column is still above the camp's
y − 16; SOIL or the y limit ends the column. Flood: 26-neighbours over LOG cells inside the extent
caps; leaf contacts counted on the 6 faces; any UNLOADED cell → `UNKNOWN`. A cost test counts `cell()` calls (like
`RoomScannerCostTest`): a 192-log tree ≤ 192×(26+6) reads; a full radius-32 sweep of a dense fake forest ≤ 40 000 reads. Glue spends
`TREE_SEARCH_READS_PER_TICK` = 2048 per worker tick inside the timer section; a sweep (~3 200 columns) takes ~10-20 ticks.
Found tree bases are cached per camp for `TREE_INDEX_TTL` = 1200 ticks; every candidate is re-scanned when chosen.

`LevelTreeView` (glue, `dev.villageheart.worker.lumber`): `getChunkNow` + cached chunk (like `LevelBlockView`); LOG = state in
`#overworld_natural_logs` with species by block; NATURAL/PLACED_LEAVES = `#leaves` by `PERSISTENT`; SOIL = `#dirt` or farmland;
AIR = `isAir()` or `canBeReplaced()` (grass, snow layer, vines, leaf litter); `top` = `LevelChunk.getHeight(WORLD_SURFACE, x&15, z&15)`;
`inRoom` = any `Buildings.valid` view's latest scan `occupies` (snapshot of the scans taken once per search, not per read).

### 3.5 The lumberjack's plan (core `dev.villageheart.core.lumber.LumberjackPlanner`, L1)

Pure decision from a snapshot, first match wins:

| # | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | camp invalid (`WORKPLACE_INVALID`) | `IDLE_AT_CAMP` |
| 2 | `DepositTrip.wanted` (full by load or slots, excluding kept saplings) | `YIELD` (DepositTrip runs) |
| 3 | a FELL in progress (between two logs) | continue `FELL` (rows 5-6 never interrupt a tree; rows 1-2 and a lost tool do) |
| 4 | tool UNKNOWN or NONE | only rows 5-6 below may run; otherwise `IDLE_AT_CAMP`, status `NEEDS_TOOL` when NONE (UNKNOWN: status unchanged) |
| 5 | a pending replant of this camp that the worker can plant now (sapling in inventory, or in the camp chest: then `FETCH` first, walk to the chest within 2 blocks and take what the open REPLANT cells need) | `REPLANT(spot)` (nearest) |
| 6 | a wanted item entity within reach rules | `COLLECT(item)` (nearest) |
| 7 | (tool present) a FELLING task of this camp with no live claimant, or the worker's own | `FELL(task)` |
| 8 | (tool present) day time < `LUMBER_LAST_FELL` and the finder has a tree | `FELL(new)` (claims it, writes the FELLING task) |
| 9 | finder paused | `SEARCH` (spend budget) |
| 10 | otherwise | `IDLE_AT_CAMP`, status `NO_WORK` ("No trees within 32 blocks") when the finder is exhausted and no task remains |

Statuses are sticky: `NO_WORK` stays through the periodic re-sweeps (TTL 1200) until a tree is actually found; `NEEDS_TOOL` stays
until a lease succeeds. During `FELL` the planner is consulted only after each log (or when the walk ends), never on a timer, so the
log gaps stay exact. The `WorkClock` lives in `LumberRuntime` per worker and task (not in the behaviour instance): a behaviour restart
(max duration) keeps it; only a change of task resets it and clears the crack overlay.

### 3.6 Saved data: tree tasks (core `VillageState`, codec in `VillageCodecs`)

```java
public record TreeTask(HeartLocation building, Stage stage, List<HeartLocation> base, String sapling, long since,
                       Pos boxMin, Pos boxMax) {     // the tree's extent when the fell started; resume never floods outside it
    public enum Stage { FELLING, REPLANT }   // REPLANT.base = cells still to plant
}
```
`VillageState.treeTasks : List<TreeTask>` (codec key `tree_tasks`, `optionalFieldOf` default empty; keys `building, stage, base,
sapling, since`), ops `withTreeTask`, `withoutTreeTask(base0)`, `treeTasksAt(building)`; `withTreeTask` refuses a task that shares a base
cell with another. The **codec** does not fail on such data: it keeps the first task, drops the later one and logs it, so a bad
entry can never stop the whole village from loading (keys add `box_min`, `box_max`). FELLING is written when a fell starts; when the last base log falls it becomes REPLANT with all base
cells; planting removes cells; empty → removed. So a fell survives panic, night, reload, firing and death: any lumberjack of
that camp resumes it (`TreeScanner.resume`: no leaf or base rule, since the leaves may have decayed, but clipped to the saved box, so
logs a player puts against a half-felled stump outside the original extent are not taken). Claims ("which worker is felling which
task/tree right now") are transient (`LumberRuntime`, per server, cleared on claim change), released when the worker stops,
leaves WORK, is fired or unloads.

`LumberSystem` (a `VillageSystem`, every 100 ticks): drop tasks whose building is no longer registered (`JobSiteSource.isRegistered`);
FELLING tasks whose base cells hold no log any more → REPLANT; REPLANT older than `REPLANT_EXPIRY_DAYS` → dropped with one log line.
Heart release drops all (the state goes with the village).

### 3.7 The behaviour (`worker.lumber.LumberjackWork extends Behavior<Villager>`)

`JobBehaviours.work(v)` for LUMBERJACK = `[LumberjackWork (3), StandAtJobBlock (4)]` (+ M7's DepositTrip at 1). M7's
`StandAtJobBlock` only stamps `LAST_WORKED_AT_POI` when within 1.5 of the job block and never sets a walk target (read on origin/m7),
so it cannot pull a felling worker away. `GoToJobBlock` is **not** in the lumberjack list (it would fight `WalkTo` for `WALK_TARGET` between logs); `IDLE_AT_CAMP` walks to the Chopping Block
itself. Entry: `WALK_TARGET` REGISTERED, `LOOK_TARGET` REGISTERED; long-running (max duration `Integer.MAX_VALUE / 2`; it stops when WORK
ends or `canStillUse` is false; all state it needs is in `LumberRuntime` or saved). Each tick inside `VillageTicker.section()`: re-plan every 20 ticks or when the action ends,
then run the action:
- `FELL`: `WalkTo(base)`; in reach: look at the current log, `WorkClock.tick(WorkSpeed.multiplier(lease…))`; per completed unit: the next
  log in `fellOrder` still a LOG of the species? → break it (3.2 #2), `CampTools.use(lease, 1)`, `WorkStats.add(lumberjack, logs, 1)`,
  `LAST_WORKED_AT_POI` stamp; else skip it. Base gone → task REPLANT, `trees +1`. The lease is re-read before each log; gone → stop, `NEEDS_TOOL`.
- `REPLANT`: walk, plant (3.2 #6), `saplings_planted +1`.
- `COLLECT`: walk to the item (re-target each 20 ticks, it moves), take it.
- `SEARCH`: `TreeFinder.step(view, 2048, skip = claimed ∪ blacklisted ∪ bases of tasks)`.
`stop()` clears the crack overlay and the claim.

**Keep rule** (`JobBehaviours.keep` for LUMBERJACK): per sapling item, keep min(count, saplings needed by this camp's REPLANT
cells of that species + `SAPLING_RESERVE` = 4); everything else is deposited.

### 3.8 Unchanged
No new blocks, items, POIs, mixins, packets or textures. Assets: lang only.

## 6. Risks, unknowns, out of scope

**Risks**
1. **(top) DepositTrip interplay (M7 step 9 not built yet).** The lumberjack's work and M7's deposit trip both drive `WALK_TARGET`.
   M9 relies on DepositTrip running alone while it is `wanted` and on three small hooks (M7-4/5). If step 9 lands with a different
   shape, step 6 changes; if it is not merged, M9 must wait rather than build its own trip. Check at step 5 and record in §8.
2. **Pathing over 32 blocks.** `FOLLOW_RANGE` 16 means partial paths; forests (dense dark oak, leaves at head height) and water can
   strand a worker. Mitigated by the progress watchdog + per-camp blacklist (T-L2-12); measured bounds in tests.
3. **False positives of the natural-tree rule**: a single player log post standing on soil and touching ≥ 3 natural leaves would be
   felled. Rules (a)-(d) make this rare; it is documented in DESIGN. False negatives: mangroves, trees on non-dirt (sand), trees
   merged with player logs are skipped (safe side).
4. **Leaf drops are random** (T-L2-6 b). With 6 oaks the expected sapling count is ~18 (≈ 60 leaves × 5%). Because new fells stop
   at 5000 and a canopy decays in ≈ 5 600 ticks, nearly all drops land during work hours; P(fewer than 6 collected) is about 1e-3.
   If T-L2-6 flakes on this, that is a design signal (e.g. collect during GO_HOME too), never a reason to loosen the assertion.
5. **Tick cost**: search and floods are budgeted (3.4) and inside `VillageTicker.section()`; T-L1-7 pins read counts. The GameTest
   server does not pace ticks (`GameTestServer.waitUntilNextTick` only runs tasks **[verified]**), so T-L2-6 (≤ 50 000 ticks) and
   T-L2-7 (≈ 25 000) cost CPU time, not 40 minutes of wall time; the builder logs each test's wall time in PROGRESS. Each lumber test
   has its own environment (one batch each), so they run one after another.
6. **Hunting Ground interaction**: felling trees can push a Hunting Ground (≥ 40 grass/leaf/log within 12) below its minimum. Left to M17.
7. **Bee nests** on birch/oak stay floating after the fell (not logs); vanilla bees are not angered by it. Accepted.

**Out of scope**: held-axe visual and job particles (no safe way to hold a copy, 3.2 #4), XP and levels (M15; M9 reads `level`),
needs effects (M11), the L3 `starter` scenario (M11; M9 only adds the metrics), Builder-planted forests, mangroves, shearing leaves,
nether fungi, stripping logs into planks (none of it in DESIGN), the Heart screen (M8).

**Reuse map for later jobs**: M10 Farmer = `core.job` (`WorkClock`, optional HOE via `ToolRequirement`), `CampContainers`,
`ItemPickup`, `WalkTo`, the planner pattern, `keep` (seeds), `work.farmer.*` counters. M15 = `WorkSpeed`/`LEVEL_SPEED` inputs and XP per
unit (`WorkClock` units). M16 Miner/M17 Hunter = `CampTools` (required PICKAXE/BOW_OR_SWORD, `NEEDS_TOOL`), counters.
