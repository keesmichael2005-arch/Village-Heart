# M18 plan: Guards (Barracks, shifts, sleep, training, combat)

Planner: Opus, 2026-11-07. Builder: implement in the step order of section 4; every step ends green on `scripts/verify.sh --fast`.
APIs marked **[verified]** were read in the decompiled 1.21.11 Mojang-mapped sources (Loom `minecraft-common-…-sources.jar`,
`minecraft-clientOnly-…-sources.jar`) or the Fabric API 0.141.6 sources jars. No gradle was run for this plan (static checks only).

M18 is built after M9-M17 in roadmap order, but only M1-M8 and M24 are on `dev` while this is written. Section 3.1 lists every
assumption about unmerged milestones; the builder checks each one against `dev` before the step that needs it and records any
difference in section 8 (Deviations).

## 1. Goal

A Steward hires villagers as guards into a Barracks (one grindstone and one bed per guard). Guards are split into a day and a night
shift (auto-balanced, a Steward can pin a guard to a shift on the Villagers tab). On shift a guard takes the best sword and armour
from the Barracks chest and the stockpile, patrols the village and kills hostile mobs and raiders inside the claim; off shift it hands
its gear back, spars at the Training Yard for XP, and sleeps in its own Barracks bed, so the night shift sleeps by day. Levels raise
its max health (20/30/40) and melee damage (+0/+1/+2). During a raid every guard comes on duty.

### 3.2 The key technical decisions

1. **How a villager fights: a mod behaviour plus a real `ATTACK_DAMAGE` attribute.** Vanilla `MeleeAttack`/`StartAttacking`
   cannot run in a villager brain: they need the `ATTACK_TARGET` and `ATTACK_COOLING_DOWN` memories, which are not in
   `Villager.MEMORY_TYPES` **[verified]**, and `Brain.setMemoryInternal` silently ignores an unregistered memory while `checkMemory`
   returns false for it even for `VALUE_ABSENT` **[verified]**, so those behaviours would never start. Adding memories means a second
   mixin into `Villager.brainProvider` for every villager; not worth it. Instead the guard's own behaviours keep the target in a
   field (one behaviour instance per brain, as M7/M9 do). The hit itself is vanilla `Mob.doHurtTarget(level, target)` **[verified]**,
   which reads `Attributes.ATTACK_DAMAGE` (a villager has none: `Villager.createAttributes()` = mob attributes + speed **[verified]**;
   `getAttributeValue` on a missing attribute throws). So at mod init: `FabricDefaultAttributeRegistry.register(EntityType.VILLAGER,
   Villager.createAttributes().add(Attributes.ATTACK_DAMAGE, Balance.GUARD_BASE_DAMAGE))` **[verified: it replaces the supplier in
   `DefaultAttributes`, debug log only]**. Every villager then has the attribute (base 1.0, unused by vanilla villager code). The win:
   the sword's own `ATTRIBUTE_MODIFIERS` (iron sword +5) are applied by vanilla equipment tracking, and `doHurtTarget` gives
   enchantments, knockback, `setLastHurtMob` and the kill credit for free. Mob weapons never wear in vanilla (`postHurtEnemy` is
   player-only **[verified]**), so after a successful hit the guard calls `weapon.hurtAndBreak(1, guard, EquipmentSlot.MAINHAND)`
   **[verified signature]**. Main entrypoint, so client and server agree.
2. **No panic, no bell, no raid hiding for guards: a guard brain.** `WorkerBrain.install` dispatches to a new `GuardBrain.install` when
   the job is GUARD. Its CORE has Swim, InteractWithDoor, LookAtTargetSink, WakeUp, MoveToTargetSink and the MEETING `AcquirePoi`;
   it leaves out `VillagerPanicTrigger` (would flee from the zombie it must fight: PANIC on `HURT_BY` or `NEAREST_HOSTILE`
   **[verified]**), `ReactToBell` and `SetRaidStatus` (both send villagers into the RAID/HIDE activities) and the HOME `AcquirePoi`
   (a guard gets its Barracks bed from `GuardBeds`, 3.5). No PANIC/RAID/HIDE packages. The brain is registered in WorkerBrain's
   `INSTALLED` set so `WorkerSystem`'s "brain is not the worker brain" repair still works.
3. **Three guard activities, one mod behaviour each.** `GUARD_DUTY` (`villageheart:guard_duty`), `GUARD_OFF` (`villageheart:guard_off`)
   and vanilla `REST`. Each non-REST activity has a single mod behaviour (`GuardDutyBehaviour`, `GuardOffBehaviour`) that owns walking
   and decides its mode with the pure `GuardPlanner` (3.4), like M9's single `LumberjackWork`, so no two behaviours fight over the
   navigation. Plus the look package and an `UpdateWorkerActivity` that now asks `WorkerBrain.activityFor(villager, level)` (guards:
   `GuardSchedule`, others: `DaySchedule` as before).
4. **Sleeping by day works with vanilla `SleepInBed`.** `SleepInBed` has no time-of-day check: it starts when `HOME` is within 2
   blocks and the bed is free, and runs while REST is the active activity **[verified]**; `WakeUp` stops sleeping only when REST is not
   active **[verified]**; nothing in `Villager`/`LivingEntity` wakes a sleeper by daylight (only a missing bed or damage
   **[verified]**). So the night shift sleeps by day as soon as `GuardSchedule` returns REST at 6000. A guard that is hurt while asleep
   wakes up (vanilla `hurt` → `stopSleeping` **[verified]**) and defends itself (3.6).
5. **Gear is worn on the villager.** Armour and the sword go into the villager's equipment slots (`setItemSlot`) with
   `setGuaranteedDrop(slot)` **[verified]**, so a guard killed drops its gear intact; a guard converted by a zombie passes it, with the
   drop chances, to the zombie villager (`ConversionType.SINGLE` with `keepEquipment` **[verified]**), which drops it when killed.
   This differs from M9's "tools stay in the camp chest" on purpose: armour only protects when worn. Off shift the gear goes back to
   the Barracks chest, so one set can serve both shifts and damaged gear sits where M19's blacksmith will look. Villagers do not
   damage their own armour (`LivingEntity.hurtArmor` is empty, only players override it **[verified]**), so the mod wears it like a
   player's: on `ServerLivingEntityEvents.AFTER_DAMAGE` **[verified]** for a guard, each worn armour piece loses
   `max(1, damage / 4)` (vanilla `doHurtEquipment`'s rule) unless the source bypasses armour.
6. **Visuals: the sword shows, armour does not.** `VillagerRenderer` has `CustomHeadLayer`, the profession layer and
   `CrossedArmsItemLayer`, which draws the main-hand item in the crossed arms **[verified]**; equipment is synced to clients by vanilla.
   There is no armour layer, and the head layer skips items that `HumanoidArmorLayer.shouldRender` would draw **[verified]**, so a
   worn helmet is invisible too. Rendering armour on the villager model would need custom model work, which DESIGN §11 rules out
   ("vanilla models + held items + particles"). Decision: armour is invisible in v1 (the Villagers tab lists it, 3.9); the held sword
   is the visual. Recorded in DESIGN §5.6.
7. **Health and damage perks are permanent attribute modifiers** (`AttributeInstance.addOrReplacePermanentModifier` **[verified]**),
   ids `villageheart:guard_health` (MAX_HEALTH, ADD_VALUE 0/+10/+20) and `villageheart:guard_damage` (ATTACK_DAMAGE, 0/+1/+2).
   Permanent modifiers are saved with the entity's attributes, so after a reload the max health is already 30 when the saved health
   (≤ 30) is applied (a transient modifier would let the load clamp health to 20). Applied on hire, on level change and by the
   20-tick repair in `WorkerSystem`; removed in `Workers.restoreVanilla`. On level-up the guard heals by the max-health gain.
8. **Villagers do not regenerate health** (no heal path in `Villager` **[verified]**). Guards heal while asleep in their Barracks bed
   (`GUARD_SLEEP_HEAL_TICKS` per half heart), and a wounded guard retreats to bed even on shift (3.3), otherwise a guard would bleed
   out over the 10-day `defended` run.

### 3.3 Core (pure Java, `dev.villageheart.core.guard`, L1-tested)

All numbers are new `Balance` entries (section 3.11).

- `enum Shift { DAY, NIGHT }` (ids `day`/`night`, part of the save format).
- `ShiftBalancer.assign(List<UUID> guardsInHiringOrder, Map<UUID, Shift> pins) → Map<UUID, Shift>`: pinned guards keep their pin;
  then unpinned guards in hiring order each go to the side with fewer guards so far (pins counted), a tie goes to **NIGHT** (night is
  when mobs come; one guard = night shift). Appending a guard never moves an earlier one (property tested). Pins of non-guards are
  ignored.
- `enum GuardPhase { DUTY, OFF, SLEEP }` and `GuardSchedule.phase(Shift, long dayTime, boolean raid, boolean wounded, boolean canWork)`:
  - DAY shift: DUTY `[0, 12000)`, OFF `[12000, 13000)` (hand in gear, spar), SLEEP `[13000, 24000)`.
  - NIGHT shift: SLEEP `[0, 11000)`, OFF `[11000, 12000)`, DUTY `[12000, 24000)`.
  - Overrides, first wins: `wounded` → SLEEP (retreat and heal, even in a raid); `!canWork` (needs speed 0: strike or starving) → OFF
    (self-defence only, no sparring); `raid` → DUTY.
  - So a night guard is asleep at 6000 and on duty at 18000 (the roadmap test), and every guard has 12000 duty, 1000 off, 11000 sleep.
- `WoundRule`: `wounded(prev, health, max)`: becomes true below `GUARD_WOUNDED_BELOW` (0.3) of max, false again at or above
  `GUARD_HEALED_AT` (0.8): hysteresis, so a guard at 31 % does not flap.
- `GuardPerks.maxHealth(level)` = 20/30/40, `bonusDamage(level)` = 0/1/2 (`GUARD_LEVEL_HEALTH`, `GUARD_LEVEL_DAMAGE`); level < 1
  throws, level > 3 uses 3.
- `Leveling.levelFor(int xp, Tier tier)` only if A3 is false (see A3).
- `GuardXp`: `KILL_XP` per hostile killed in the claim, `1` XP per `SPAR_TICKS_PER_XP` ticks of sparring paced by M9's `WorkClock`
  at `Needs.speed()` (hungry guards learn at half speed).
- **Gear** (`GearItem`, `GearRanking`, `GearPlanner`):
  - `GearItem(String id, GearSlot slot, double armor, double toughness, double attackDamage, int damage, int maxDamage, Source source,
    int order)`, `GearSlot { HEAD, CHEST, LEGS, FEET, WEAPON }`, `Source { WORN, BARRACKS, STOCKPILE }`. The glue fills the numbers
    from the item's own components (3.7), so leather < copper < chainmail = gold < iron < diamond < netherite falls out of the armour
    values instead of a hard-coded material list.
  - Rank within a slot: armour by (armor, toughness, durability fraction), weapon by (attack damage, durability fraction); ties: WORN,
    then BARRACKS, then STOCKPILE, then lower `order`. Not usable: armour with `armor <= 0` (pumpkins, heads, elytra), anything whose
    durability fraction is below `GEAR_MIN_DURABILITY` (0.1: left for repair).
  - `GearPlanner.plan(List<GearItem> worn, List<GearItem> available) → List<GearItem>`: for each slot the best usable item if it beats
    what is worn (strictly), else nothing. The glue passes only what is *available* to this guard (stockpile count minus M6
    reservations of other holders), so two guards never plan the same chestplate.
- `GuardTargets.choose(List<Candidate> c, Mode mode) → Optional<Candidate>`: `Candidate(int id, Kind kind, double distSq, boolean
  inClaim, boolean visible, boolean targetsVillager, boolean hurtMe)`, `Kind { HOSTILE, RAIDER, CREEPER, NEUTRAL }`.
  Rules: never CREEPER (guards are melee; vanilla iron golems leave creepers alone too); NEUTRAL (endermen, zombified piglins) only
  when it targets a villager or hurt this guard; DUTY mode: in the claim (or `hurtMe`); OFF/self-defence mode: only `hurtMe`, or a
  HOSTILE/RAIDER within `GUARD_SELF_DEFENCE_RANGE` that targets a villager. Must be visible unless `hurtMe`. Priority: `hurtMe`,
  then `targetsVillager`, then nearest, then lower id.
- `GuardPlanner.duty(DutyFacts) → DutyMode { FIGHT, EQUIP, PATROL }` and `off(OffFacts) → OffMode { DEFEND, RETURN_GEAR, SPAR, IDLE }`:
  a precedence table (FIGHT > EQUIP > PATROL; DEFEND > RETURN_GEAR > SPAR > IDLE). EQUIP only while a planned upgrade exists and
  its source is reachable (not on cooldown); SPAR only with a valid Training Yard linked to the guard's Barracks and `canWork`.
- `PatrolRoute`: points in a fixed order (Heart first, then building doors in `locationOrder`); guard *i* of *n* on its shift starts at
  `i * points / n`; `next(current)` skips points marked unreachable within the last `PATROL_SKIP_TICKS`; dwell `PATROL_DWELL_TICKS`
  at each point.
- `GuardBeds.assign(List<Pos> beds, List<UUID> guardsInHiringOrder, Map<UUID, Pos> current) → Map<UUID, Pos>`: a guard keeps its bed
  while it is still a bed of its Barracks, the others get the free beds in position order. Beds ≥ guards always holds
  (`slots = min(grindstones, beds)`), but a guard with no bed left is simply absent (status "No bed").

### 3.5 Barracks beds (`worker/guard/GuardBeds` glue, `JobSiteReservations`)

- `BuildingJobSites.reservable()` also returns the `#bed` head positions of every valid Barracks scan. The existing reservation pass
  then holds their POI tickets (bed POIs exist only on the head half, `PoiTypes.HOME` **[verified by M11's plan; builder re-checks]**),
  so vanilla `AcquirePoi(HOME)` never gives a Barracks bed to an ordinary villager. `JobSiteReservations.evictFrom` and
  `WorkerSystem.hasJobSiteMemory` also look at `HOME`, so a villager that already slept there loses it (ticket released, then taken
  by the mod). Doc comments: "job blocks and Barracks beds".
- `GuardBedSystem` (a `VillageSystem`, every 20 ticks): `GuardBeds.assign` per Barracks; for each loaded guard set
  `HOME = GlobalPos.of(dim, bed)` when it differs. The mod keeps the ticket; the guard does not need one to sleep (`SleepInBed` only
  checks the block state). On hire a guard first drops its old `HOME` (`releasePoi(HOME)` + erase), so its House bed frees up.
  On death vanilla `releaseAllPois` frees the ticket; the next reservation pass takes it back (its `hasFreeTicket` branch).
- REST for guards: `SetWalkTargetFromBlockMemory(HOME)` + `SleepInBed` (vanilla), no `ValidateNearbyPoi` (it erases HOME when the bed
  is occupied by someone else **[verified]**; the mod owns a guard's HOME and re-sets it every 20 ticks, so it is not needed).
- Healing: while `isSleeping()` and the sleeping pos is its assigned bed, `heal(1)` every `GUARD_SLEEP_HEAL_TICKS`.

### 3.6 Combat (`worker/guard/GuardCombat`, used by both behaviours)

- Scan every `GUARD_SCAN_TICKS` (10, spread by entity id): `level.getEntitiesOfClass(Mob.class, box(GUARD_SIGHT=24), alive)`,
  classified: `Creeper` → CREEPER; `Raider` → RAIDER; `NeutralMob` → NEUTRAL; other `Enemy` → HOSTILE; anything else ignored.
  `inClaim` via `ClaimGeometry.inClaim`, `visible` via `guard.getSensing().hasLineOfSight(e)` **[verified]**, `targetsVillager` via
  `e.getTarget() instanceof Villager` (+ the guard itself), `hurtMe` = `guard.getLastHurtByMob() == e` within 100 ticks. Pure choice by
  `GuardTargets`.
- Chase: re-path every 10 ticks with `nav.createPath(target.blockPosition(), 1, 32)` **[verified signature]** + `nav.moveTo(path,
  GUARD_CHASE_SPEED)`, publishing `PATH` for doors. Hit when `guard.isWithinMeleeAttackRange(target)` **[verified]** and visible, at most
  every `GUARD_ATTACK_COOLDOWN` (20) ticks: `swing(MAIN_HAND)`, `doHurtTarget`, then weapon wear. Give up (target dropped for 200 ticks)
  when it is dead/removed, left the claim + 8 in DUTY mode, or the distance did not shrink by a block in `GUARD_GIVE_UP_TICKS` (200),
  the same 200-tick rule as vanilla `StopAttackingIfTargetInvalid` **[verified]**.
- Kill credit: `ServerLivingEntityEvents.AFTER_DEATH`: when the source's entity is a guard worker and the victim was an `Enemy`
  inside the claim: `KILL_XP`, `WorkStats.add("guard", "kills", 1)`.
- Armour wear on `AFTER_DAMAGE` (3.2 #5). Wounded check after every hit taken (3.3 `WoundRule`).

### 3.7 Gear (`worker/guard/GuardGear`)

- Reading: `GearItem` from an `ItemStack`: slot from `DataComponents.EQUIPPABLE` (`Equippable.slot()` **[verified]**, HEAD/CHEST/LEGS/FEET
  only; BODY = horse/wolf armour ignored) or WEAPON for `ItemTags.SWORDS` (swords only: axes stay for lumberjacks, no spears, maces,
  bows or tridents in v1); armour/toughness/attack damage summed from `stack.forEachModifier(slot, …)` **[verified]** for the ADD_VALUE
  modifiers of ARMOR / ARMOR_TOUGHNESS / ATTACK_DAMAGE; damage and max damage from the stack.
- Available: the Barracks chest (`CampContainers.of`, A1) and the stockpile (`Stockpiles.snapshot` totals, `StackKey.toStack(1)`, minus
  `reservations` held by others). Each damaged piece is its own `StackKey` (components), so durability is exact.
- **EQUIP trip** (DUTY mode EQUIP): runs at the first duty tick of a shift and again every `GEAR_RECHECK_TICKS` (1200) while on duty
  (so gear the other shift hands in at 12000 is picked up by 13200). Plan with `GearPlanner`; reserve stockpile picks
  (`Reservations.set(guard, key, 1)`); walk to the Barracks marker door first if any pick is there, take, equip; then to the nearest
  Storage that holds the next pick (new `Stockpile.nearestWith(from, key, holder)` + facade `Stockpiles.nearestWith`, L1 + used by L2),
  `Stockpiles.withdraw(level, door, key, 1, guard)`, equip. Replaced pieces go into the Barracks chest (or, if it is full, into the
  guard's inventory and are returned by the next RETURN_GEAR). Release the reservations at the end. A source that is UNREACHABLE or
  empty on arrival is skipped until the next recheck.
- **RETURN_GEAR** (OFF mode): walk to the Barracks door, put every worn piece into the Barracks chest (empty slots, chest order);
  what does not fit goes to Storage with the M7 deposit rule (`nearestWithSpace` + `depositHere`); if neither has room the guard keeps
  it (status `STORAGE_FULL`). Also runs on fire (gear is moved to the Barracks chest, else Storage, else dropped at the villager's feet)
  so firing never deletes gear.
- Not saved: trip state. After a reload the planner simply runs again.

### 3.8 Patrol and training (`worker/guard/GuardDutyBehaviour`, `GuardOffBehaviour`, `PatrolPoints`)

- `PatrolPoints.of(server)`: the Heart, then the door (`RoomScan.door`, else the marker) of every VALID room building in the Heart's
  dimension, in `locationOrder`; plus anything registered with `PatrolPoints.register(Source)` (M21). Rebuilt every 200 ticks.
- PATROL: `WalkTo.begin(point, 2.5, 6.0)`; ARRIVED → dwell `PATROL_DWELL_TICKS` looking around, then `PatrolRoute.next`;
  UNREACHABLE → mark the point, next. Counter `WorkStats.add("guard", "duty_samples", 1)` every 100 ticks of duty (the L3 non-vacuity
  check).
- SPAR (OFF mode): the Training Yard linked to the guard's Barracks (`BuildingRecord.link` of a VALID TRAINING_YARD). Walk to its marker;
  if another guard of the same Barracks is sparring there, pair up (stand 2 blocks apart, face each other); otherwise stand at the
  nearest dummy (target block within the yard radius, or armor stand). Each tick at the spot: `WorkClock.tick(Needs.speed())`;
  each unit = 1 XP (`GuardXp`), a swing, a `CRIT` particle and no damage (an armor stand is never hit for real). Counter
  `work.guard.spar_xp`.
- Levels: XP is added to `WorkerData` (A3); when the level changes the perks are applied (3.2 #7) and a level-up sound plays.

## 6. Risks, unknowns, out of scope

**Risks** (highest first):
1. **(top) L3 `defended` in an unseeded real village.** It needs M11's unbuilt harness and `starter`, and "≤ 1 mob death in 10 days"
   depends on vanilla villagers' own night behaviour (a villager caught outside at dusk, a zombie at a door) that two guards cannot fully
   control, and on creepers, which guards ignore. Mitigations: one guard per shift, patrol points at building doors, wounded retreat and
   sleep healing, iron swords in the kit, `duty_samples` proving the guards were active. If it fails, the builder reports deaths per day
   with cause and position and does **not** loosen the assertion; options for the orchestrator: a third guard or night-shift tie rules
   (tunable), never a weaker check.
2. **Combat feel and pathing.** A villager walks at 0.5 × 0.6; a zombie at 0.23: chasing works, but a guard could lose targets behind
   fences or water (the 200-tick give-up). WalkTo's false-unreachable bug (A1) affects patrol and gear trips, which all skip and retry
   instead of failing. T-L2-5 uses a flat lit yard; the L3 run is the real check.
3. **The `ATTACK_DAMAGE` registration replaces the villager attribute supplier** (debug log only). Another mod doing the same would win
   or lose by load order. Low risk in v1; noted in DESIGN §12.
4. **Brain switches at shift boundaries and loads.** `UpdateWorkerActivity` re-asserts the guard activity every 20 ticks; a guard loaded
   mid-trip simply re-plans. Risk of flapping between OFF and SLEEP for a wounded guard is handled by the `WoundRule` hysteresis.
5. **M11 rehome vs Barracks beds.** If M11's rehome runs before `GuardBedSystem` sets HOME, a new guard could take a House bed. Mitigation:
   rehome skips guards (A2) and hiring a guard drops its old HOME; T-L2-3/T-L2-4 check the bed.
6. **Merge conflicts** with M9 (`VillageState` components, `JobBehaviours`, `VillagerRow`/codecs) and M11 (`NeedsSystem`, harness).
   Rebase on dev before steps 3, 4, 11 and 12.

**Unknowns:** whether `Raids.createOrExtendRaid` can run inside a GameTest (T-L2-14 falls back to the seam); exact `defended` site
positions (T-L2-13 checks them).

**Out of scope (M18):** visible armour on the villager model; bows, crossbows, spears, shields and any ranged combat (DESIGN §11);
fighting creepers; the raid alert, "workers indoors" and the death log (M25); golems (M19); watchtower and lamp-post patrol points (M21,
seam only); gear repair (M19); per-guard patrol routes chosen by the player; XP for other jobs (M15).

## 7. Critique addressed

A remote critique session (`session_0184ToXiEtQfezxvHDBvhuai`) was started with only this plan and the roadmap entry. It had sent no answer
when the planner had to hand in, so it was archived and the points below are the planner's own review. **Orchestrator: a fresh critique
is still owed before the build starts.**

1. **Invalid Barracks.** When a guard's Barracks turns invalid (status `WORKPLACE_INVALID`), the guard keeps its schedule and its current
   HOME, still patrols and defends, but skips EQUIP and SPAR, and its bed is no longer reserved (`reservable` lists valid Barracks only).
   This follows M7's "idle near the building" rule without leaving the village unguarded. Test: T-L2-9 gets an extra step (break the
   grindstone: the guard is still on `GUARD_DUTY`, status Workplace invalid).
2. **Several Barracks.** `GuardBeds` and gear sharing work per Barracks (the guard's `building`). `ShiftBalancer` balances over the
   whole village, not per Barracks. T-L1-1 gets a two-Barracks case.
3. **Day-time jumps in L2.** `setDayTime` jumps past boundaries, so every L2 assertion waits on a condition with a bound and never on
   an exact tick (T-L2-4 "6000 ± 20" means the phase check runs once the day time reaches 6000). `WorkerTestSupport.begin/end` restores
   the time.
4. **Babies and conversion.** Babies cannot be hired (M7). A converted guard's zombie villager keeps the gear and the permanent
   modifiers. A cured one is a vanilla villager with no record, and `WorkerSystem`'s orphan restore must also strip the two modifier
   ids. Added to step 9 and T-L2-9.
5. **Difficulty.** GameTests run on NORMAL. On HARD a guard killed by a zombie always converts. L3 runs on NORMAL like `starter`.
