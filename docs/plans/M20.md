## 1. Goal

At a Builder's Table a Steward picks one of three blueprints (Small House, Storage Shed, Farm Plot) and takes a **Blueprint item**. Clicking
the ground with it shows the footprint as green (or red) particles; sneak-clicking rotates it, a second click inside the outline confirms the
site after the server has checked it (inside the claim, not over any room or registered building, only natural blocks in the way). The hired
builder reserves the materials in the stockpile, fetches them, clears the natural blocks (drops go to Storage) and places the building layer by
layer at 1 block per second. A Small House or Storage Shed includes its marker and furniture, so it turns green by itself when the last block is
placed. When materials run out it pauses with "Needs 12 more planks" on the Buildings tab and resumes when they arrive.

### 3.2 Decisions (pinned; DESIGN §5.8 gets a "Rules as built (M20)" paragraph in step 12)

1. **"5×5 room" = 5×5 floor inside the walls** (7×7 outside). A 5×5 outside gives 9 floor cells, below the House minimum of 12. Both building
   blueprints use this shell.
2. **"Player-placed" (no placement tracking exists).** A block may be cleared only if it is *natural*: in the data tag
   `#villageheart:builder_clearable` (below) and, for leaves, `PERSISTENT == false` (M9's heuristic: player-placed leaves are persistent),
   and it holds no fluid (`state.getFluidState().isEmpty()`) and no block entity. Everything else is "player-placed" and is **never broken**: at
   confirm the site is refused, and if such a block appears later the build stops on it (BLOCKED, retried every 5 s). Natural logs are not
   clearable either ("Tree in the way at …: fell it first"). Independently of block kinds, **no touched cell may be inside the interior or shell of
   any registered building's latest scan** (`RoomScan.occupies`, M4; valid or not) or be a registered marker or its support block, so dirt or stone a
   player used for a room is protected by the overlap rule. Known gap (documented): dirt, stone, sand or gravel a player placed outside any marked room
   is indistinguishable from terrain and can be cleared (it is cheap, and its drops go to Storage).
   `builder_clearable.json` = `#minecraft:dirt`, `minecraft:dirt_path`, `#minecraft:base_stone_overworld`, `#minecraft:sand`, `minecraft:gravel`,
   `minecraft:clay`, `minecraft:sandstone`, `#minecraft:snow`, `minecraft:snow`, `#minecraft:replaceable` (minus fluids, by the fluid rule), `#minecraft:flowers`,
   `#minecraft:saplings`, `#minecraft:leaves` (persistent ones excluded in code), `#minecraft:coal_ores`, `#minecraft:iron_ores`, `#minecraft:copper_ores`,
   `minecraft:brown_mushroom`, `minecraft:red_mushroom`, `minecraft:sweet_berry_bush`, `minecraft:pumpkin`, `minecraft:melon`. **[verified tag names and
   contents of `dirt`, `base_stone_overworld`, `replaceable` (includes water/lava, hence the fluid rule)]**. Crops and farmland are not in it.
3. **Origin, anchor, rotation.** The clicked block is the *ground* (if it `canBeReplaced()`, e.g. short grass, the block below is). The blueprint's
   anchor cell (local x, z) sits over the ground; layer `i` is at `ground.y + base + i` (`base` = 1 for the two houses: they stand on the ground;
   0 for the Farm Plot: its farmland replaces the ground). Rotation is about the anchor, in 90° clockwise steps, with the same position rule as
   `StructureTemplate.transform` (CW90: `(dx, dz) → (−dz, dx)` **[verified]**), and block states are turned by vanilla `BlockState.rotate(Rotation)`
   **[verified]**. `core.blueprint.Rotation4` names equal `net.minecraft.world.level.block.Rotation`'s (`NONE, CLOCKWISE_90, CLOCKWISE_180,
   COUNTERCLOCKWISE_90`), mapped by name. The first click picks the rotation that turns the blueprint's front (door side) towards the player.
4. **Materials** are exact items (`minecraft:torch`) or item tags (`#minecraft:planks`) with an English name used in texts ("planks"). For a tag
   material the placed block is `Block.byItem(item)` of the item actually used, with every property of the template state that it has copied over
   (`StateHolder.trySetValue` **[verified]**); exact materials place the template state as written. Only plain stacks count (`StackKey` with an empty
   patch), so named or enchanted items are never consumed. Water is placed from a `minecraft:water_bucket`, which `returns` a `minecraft:bucket`
   to the builder (deposited like any drop).
5. **Pace**: one *action* (clearing one non-air cell, or placing one step: a block, a door, a bed) per `BUILD_TICKS_PER_BLOCK` = 20 work-clock units:
   `WorkClock(20)` ticked with `WorkSpeed.untooled(level, needs)` (1.0 at level 1 → 1 block/s, DESIGN §5.8). Cells that are already right cost
   nothing. Walking and fetching are not paced by the clock.
6. **The builder never enters its own site.** It works from *stand points* on the ring of columns one block outside the site box (feet on a solid
   block, two passable cells), within `BUILD_REACH` = 7.0 (3D, feet to cell centre, no line of sight, like M9's `CHOP_REACH`) of the step's
   cell. So a roof, a closing wall or a fence ring can never trap it, and it never places a block into itself. Another entity in the cell
   (`level.isUnobstructed(state, pos, CollisionContext.empty())` false **[verified on `CollisionGetter`]**) → wait (status IN_THE_WAY after 100 ticks).
   Confirm refuses a site where some touched cell has no stand point within reach.
7. **Order of work** (core `BuildPlan`, deterministic): (a) CLEAR every touched cell, top layer first; (b) PLACE layer by layer from the bottom,
   `MAIN` pass entries only, each layer in perimeter order (angle around the box centre, then distance) so the builder walks round once per layer;
   a two-block unit (door, bed) is one step at its primary cell and places its parts in the same tick; (c) `ATTACHED` entries (torch, marker,
   things that need a supporting block), the **marker last**; (d) `LAST` entries (water). Then (e) one **check pass**: every PLACE step whose cell
   no longer holds its block is placed again if the cell is air or natural (a player who broke a wall block mid-build gets it back; costs
   material); a different non-natural block is left alone. Then DONE.
8. **Placement in the world** (glue `BuildExecutor`): `level.setBlock(pos, state, Block.UPDATE_ALL)`; doors lower then upper and beds foot then
   head, both with flag 3, as `DoorBlock.setPlacedBy` and `BedBlock.setPlacedBy` do **[verified]**; then
   `Block.updateFromNeighbourShapes(state, level, pos)` **[verified static]** for blocks whose shape depends on neighbours (fences, panes), set
   again only if it changed. Chests are placed as `type=single`, never adjacent (so they cannot merge). The marker is a plain `setBlock` of
   `<type>_marker[facing=…,status=unchecked]`; `MarkerBlock.onPlace` registers it **[verified]**, then `Buildings.request(server, loc)` asks for an
   immediate check. Clearing: `level.destroyBlock(pos, false, builder)` and drops `Block.getDrops(state, level, pos, null, builder, tool)` **[verified
   6-arg overload]** with `tool` = a plain `Items.STONE_PICKAXE` stack (the builder's basic tools: stone gives cobblestone, ores drop), skipped when
   `GameRules.BLOCK_DROPS` **[verified `block_drops`]** is false; drops go into the builder's inventory (`SimpleContainer.addItem`), the rest is
   popped at the cell (`Block.popResource`). Never load a chunk: every read and write first checks `getChunkNow`.
9. **Reservations.** Holder = the build id. `BuildSystem` (every `BUILD_REFRESH_TICKS` = 100) works out each build's remaining need per material
   (PLACE steps at or after the cursor, check pass excluded, minus what the builder carries for it) and reserves concrete stack keys from one
   stockpile snapshot: keys it already holds first, then the largest stock, ties by item id (so one wood type is used when possible). Oldest
   build first. `missing(m)` = need − carried − reserved. Reservations are transient (M6), so a restart just recomputes them on the next refresh.
10. **Fetching.** The builder takes a *batch*: the materials of the next PLACE steps, in step order, until the batch is one stack's worth
    (Σ count/maxStack ≥ `BUILDER_FETCH_LOAD` = 1.0) or uses `BUILDER_FETCH_SLOTS` = 4 inventory slots, taking only what is reserved for its
    build. It walks (`WalkTo`) to the nearest Storage holding the first item (A7), and within 2 blocks withdraws each batch item with
    `Stockpiles.withdraw(level, from, key, n, buildId)` (M6 takes from any Storage, nearest first). What it took is the build's **carried** map
    (saved). `JobBehaviours.keep(builder, item)` = carried count of that item, so `DepositTrip` hands in only spoil (drops, the empty bucket).
    The first item of the batch has nothing reserved or carried → the build **pauses**: NEEDS_MATERIALS with the first material in step order
    whose `missing > 0` ("Needs 12 more planks"). It resumes by itself after a refresh finds stock (re-plan every 20 ticks).
11. **Who builds what.** Builds are worked oldest first. Each builder (hiring order) claims the oldest build with no live claimant (transient
    claim in `BuildRuntime`, like M9's camp claims). A build whose `carrier` is a loaded or merely unloaded worker waits for that worker; only if the
    carrier's record is gone is it free for others. Firing a builder deposits its carried materials straight into the stockpile
    (`Stockpiles.deposit`, the rest dropped at its feet) and zeroes `carried`; a dead builder's carried items are lost (vanilla villagers never drop
    their inventory: `AbstractVillager` has an 8-slot `SimpleContainer` and no death drop **[verified]**), `carried` is zeroed and the shortfall is
    simply reserved again.
12. **Ranks and tier.** New `Permission.START_BLUEPRINT` (Owner, Steward): take a blueprint, confirm a site, cancel a build. Every blueprint of M20
    is `Tier.VILLAGE` (DESIGN §9); a locked one shows "Requires Village tier" and cannot be taken or confirmed. Anyone in the claim may open the table
    and read it.
13. **Limits** (`Balance`, tunable): `MAX_ACTIVE_BUILDS` = 8, `BLUEPRINT_TAKE_COOLDOWN` 10 ticks per player, `BUILD_BLOCKED_RETRY_TICKS` 100,
    `BUILD_IN_THE_WAY_TICKS` 100, `BUILD_UNREACHABLE_RETRY_TICKS` 600.

### 3.3 Blueprint data format (core `dev.villageheart.core.blueprint`, pure, L1)

Blueprints are Java text blocks in `Blueprints` (no IO), parsed once by `BlueprintParser`. The format:

```
id small_house
name Small House
tier VILLAGE
kind BUILDING            # BUILDING (M20) | DEFENCE (M21)
builds house             # BuildingType id whose room this makes, or "none"
base 1                   # layer 0 is this many blocks above the ground block
anchor 3 4               # local x z over the clicked ground block
front NORTH              # local direction the door faces (−z); rotations turn it
palette
. any                                                    # not touched
_ air                                                    # cleared, stays air
C minecraft:cobblestone                    <- minecraft:cobblestone "cobblestone"
P minecraft:oak_planks                     <- #minecraft:planks "planks"
D minecraft:oak_door[facing=south,half=lower,hinge=left,open=false] <- #minecraft:wooden_doors "door" cell=solid +#door
d minecraft:oak_door[facing=south,half=upper,hinge=left,open=false] part=DOWN
B minecraft:white_bed[facing=west,part=foot] <- #minecraft:beds "bed"
b minecraft:white_bed[facing=west,part=head] part=EAST +#bed
T minecraft:wall_torch[facing=north]        <- minecraft:torch "torch" pass=attached cell=open +#light
M villageheart:house_marker[facing=north,status=unchecked] <- villageheart:house_marker "House marker" pass=attached cell=open
layer 0
.......
CCCCCCC
...
end
```

- `PaletteEntry(char symbol, Kind kind /*ANY, AIR, BLOCK*/, String state, @Nullable Material material, Pass pass /*MAIN, ATTACHED, LAST*/,
  @Nullable Dir partOf, CellKind cell, List<String> contents)`. `part=DIR` marks the second half of a unit: no material, placed with the primary
  found one step in DIR. `cell=` and `+#key` describe how the room scanner will see the block (defaults: AIR/ANY → OPEN, BLOCK → SOLID; contents
  = the block id plus the listed groups). They let core prove the room valid (below); an L2 test pins them against `RoomCells.of(state)`.
- `Material(String id, String name, @Nullable String returns)`; `isTag()` = id starts with `#`.
- `Blueprint(id, name, tier, kind, @Nullable String buildsTypeId, int base, int anchorX, int anchorZ, Dir front, int width, int height, int depth,
  Map<Character, PaletteEntry> palette, List<List<String>> layers)`; `entry(x, y, z)`, `materials()` → ordered `MaterialList` (first use in
  step order), `touched()` (non-ANY cells).
- `BlueprintParser.parse(text)` → `Blueprint` or `BlueprintSyntaxException(line, message)`: unknown key, unknown or duplicate symbol, ragged rows,
  layer count/size mismatch, a BLOCK without a material (unless `part=`), a `part=` that does not point at a primary of the same block id, an
  anchor outside the footprint, a `builds` type without exactly one marker entry, two entries for one cell.
- `Rotation4` (`apply(dx, dz)`, `apply(Dir)`, `next()`), `Placement(Pos ground, Rotation4 r)` with `world(bp, lx, ly, lz)`, `local(...)` (inverse),
  `box(bp)` (min/max corners), `front(bp)`.
- `BlueprintView implements BlockView`: the finished blueprint at a placement, over solid ground (y < ground.y + base) and air elsewhere, using each
  entry's `cell` and `contents` (door half from the state's `half`, bed partner from `part=`). With it, `RoomScanner.scanFromMarker` +
  `BuildingRules.checkRoom` prove in L1 that each building blueprint is a valid room of its type in all four rotations.
- `Blueprints` (registry): `SMALL_HOUSE`, `STORAGE_SHED`, `FARM_PLOT`; `all()`, `byId(id)`; `resolve(id, params)` with `BlueprintParams(int length)`
  (unused by M20, length 0; M21's Wall Segment generates a `Blueprint` from it). `PlacementMode {FOOTPRINT, LINE}` on `Blueprint` (M20: FOOTPRINT
  only; M21 adds LINE's two-click flow).

### 3.5 Site rules (core `SiteRules`, pure, L1)

`SiteFacts(Blueprint bp, Placement p, HeartLocation heart, String dimension, Tier tier, Rank rank, int minY, int maxY, SiteView view,
List<Protected> protectedAreas, List<Box> otherBuilds, int activeBuilds, StandView stand)` where `SiteView.cell(Pos)` → `SiteCell {AIR, CLEARABLE,
LOG, FLUID, BLOCKED, UNLOADED}` plus `blockId(Pos)`, and `Protected(String typeId, Pos marker, LongSet cells)` (scan interior + shell, the marker and
its support). `SiteRules.check(f)` → `Optional<SiteProblem>`, the first failing rule in this order (each a `TextLine`):

| # | Rule | Text (fallback) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | rank has `START_BLUEPRINT` | "Only a Steward or the Owner can start a blueprint" |
| 2 | tier ≥ blueprint tier | "Requires Village tier" |
| 3 | same dimension as the Heart; box inside the world height | "Not in this village's dimension" / "Too high or too low" |
| 4 | all 8 box corners inside the claim sphere (a sphere is convex, so the whole box is) | "Outside the village: %s blocks from the Heart (max 80)" |
| 5 | `activeBuilds < MAX_ACTIVE_BUILDS` | "Too many builds at once (max 8)" |
| 6 | every box cell loaded | "Part of the site is not loaded" |
| 7 | no touched cell in a `Protected` area | "Overlaps the %s at %s" (type name, marker pos) |
| 8 | box does not intersect another build's box | "Overlaps the build at %s" |
| 9 | no touched cell, and no 6-neighbour of a touched cell, holds a fluid | "Water or lava at %s" |
| 10 | no touched cell is LOG | "Tree in the way at %s: fell it first" |
| 11 | no touched cell is BLOCKED | "%s in the way at %s" (block id) |
| 12 | every touched cell has a stand point within reach (`StandPoints`) | "No place to stand near %s" |

Order is fixed so a test can reach each rule with everything before it passing. A registered building with no scan yet (after a restart) whose
marker is within 16 blocks of the box makes the glue request a check and report rule 7 as "The %s at %s has not been checked yet; try again"
(`Buildings.request`), so an unknown room is never built over.

### 3.6 Build plan, statuses and saved data (core)

- `BuildPlan.of(bp, placement)` → `List<Step>`, `Step(int index, Kind {CLEAR, PLACE, CHECK}, Pos pos, PaletteEntry entry, List<Pos> parts)`; order
  of 3.2 #7. `StandPoints.ring(box)`, `StandPoints.nearest(cell, standable, reach)`.
- `FetchBatch.plan(steps, cursor, carried, reservedByMe, stackSize)` → list of (key, n). `MaterialPlanner.plan(needs, stock, holder)` → new
  reservation map + `missing` per material; generic over the key type (the glue gives each stock key the material ids it satisfies, computed with
  `StackKey.is(tag)` or the item id).
- `BuilderPlanner.next(BuilderFacts)` → `Action`: `IDLE` (no build: walk to the table, status NO_WORK "No blueprint to build"), `YIELD`
  (`DepositTrip.wanted`), `FETCH(batch)`, `WALK(stand)`, `CLEAR(step)`, `PLACE(step)`, `WAIT(reason)` (NEEDS_MATERIALS, BLOCKED, IN_THE_WAY,
  UNREACHABLE, NOT_LOADED), `FINISH`. First match wins; consulted after each action and every 20 ticks, never inside a clock unit.
- `BuildStatus {WAITING_FOR_BUILDER, CLEARING, BUILDING, CHECKING, NEEDS_MATERIALS, BLOCKED, IN_THE_WAY, UNREACHABLE, NOT_LOADED}` with
  `BuildTexts.status(...)` → `TextLine`: "Waiting for a builder", "Clearing the site", "Building: %s%%", "Needs %s more %s", "%s in the way at %s",
  "Something is in the way at %s", "Can't reach %s", "Site not loaded". `BuildTexts.missing(material, n)` = key `build.villageheart.needs`,
  fallback "Needs %s more %s", args `[n, material.name()]` → **"Needs 12 more planks"**.
- **Saved** (`VillageState.builds : List<BuildJob>`, codec key `builds`, `optionalFieldOf` default empty):
  `BuildJob(UUID id, String blueprintId, BlueprintParams params, HeartLocation ground, Rotation4 rotation, long createdAt, UUID startedBy,
  int cursor, Map<String, Integer> carried, @Nullable UUID carrier)`; ops `withBuild`, `withoutBuild(id)`, `build(id)`, `withCursor`, `withCarried`.
  The codec drops (and logs) a build whose blueprint id is unknown or whose cursor is out of range, never failing the whole village load.
  Steps are recomputed from (blueprint, params, ground, rotation), so only the cursor is stored. On load, `carried` is clamped to what the carrier
  actually holds once it is seen.
- `WorkerStatus` gains `NEEDS_MATERIALS` ("Needs materials") and `BLOCKED` ("Build blocked"); the exact text is on the build row.

## 6. Risks, unknowns, out of scope

**Risks**
1. **Builder movement (top risk).** Everything rests on `WalkTo` getting the builder from the hut to Storage to stand points around the site.
   Village terrain (slopes, fences, crops, other villagers) can make stand points unreachable. Mitigations: stand points outside the box only,
   reach 7.0, three nearest stand points tried before UNREACHABLE, retry after 600 ticks, confirm refuses sites with no stand point. If T-L2-7
   is flaky, that is a design signal (e.g. more stand-point candidates), never a reason to loosen it. Far sites (claim radius 80 vs `WalkTo` range
   48) rely on WalkTo's repathing; T-L2 sites are within 20 blocks, so far sites are untested in L2 (logged as a gap).
2. **Block states across rotations** (doors, beds, wall torches, markers, fences). Mitigated by T-L1-4 (core proof) + T-L2-1 (core annotations =
   real cells) + T-L2-2 (real placement, all rotations).
3. **"Player-placed" is a heuristic** (3.2 #2): terrain-like blocks a player placed outside any marked room can be cleared. Accepted and
   documented; the overlap rule covers every marked room.
4. **Transient reservations.** After a restart, until the first refresh (≤ 100 ticks) other consumers could take a build's materials. Accepted
   (the build then pauses and shows what is missing).
5. **Villager death loses carried materials** (vanilla villagers drop no inventory). At most one fetch batch (≤ 1 stack's worth).
6. **Merge conflicts in `VillageState`/`VillageCodecs`/`HeartSnapshot`** with M10-M19 adding fields. Keep back-compat constructors; resolve by
   adding, never reordering codec keys.
7. **Gravity blocks and fluids**: sand/gravel above the box can fall in after clearing (handled: a natural block in a PLACE cell is cleared first);
   fluids next to the site are refused at confirm, but a player could pour water later (the cell then reads BLOCKED: fluids are never removed).
8. **M14 not merged**: in normal play every blueprint stays locked at Hamlet. Tests set the tier, so M20 is testable either way (A4).

**Out of scope (M20)**: defence blueprints and the two-click line flow (M21; the format has `kind`, `PlacementMode.LINE` and `BlueprintParams`),
several builders on one build, builder XP/levels (M15; `WorkStats` counts actions), scaffolding or climbing, repairing finished buildings,
player-made or imported blueprints, mirroring, undo, real placement tracking, a cost for the Blueprint item, a held-tool visual.

**Backlog scanned.** No open item is about blueprints or the builder. Adopted: BACKLOG M5-r1 (chat reads assert non-empty before `get(0)`) as a rule
for the new tests. The M7 deposit-trip guard items stay open (M20 relies on `DepositTrip` but does not change its trigger).
